# Vine-Choked Lizardfolk Shrine

## Shrine Guardian Spirit
*Medium undead, neutral*

**Armor Class** 13  
**Hit Points** 32 (5d8+10)  
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 6(-2) | 16(+3) | 14(+2) | 10(+0) | 15(+2) | 14(+2) |

**Damage Resistances** acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** necrotic, poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Incorporeal Movement.** The spirit can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

### Actions

**Withering Touch.** *Melee Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6+2) necrotic damage.

**Ancestral Warning (1/Day).** All creatures within 30 feet of the spirit must make a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, creatures receive visions of ancient territorial conflicts and are frightened for 1 minute. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

---

### Encounter Information

**Environment:** Ancient lizardfolk shrine overgrown by jungle vegetation  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random lost world discovery (#3 on d8)  
**Discovery Method:** DC 15 Investigation or Religion to recognize worked stone and ritual significance

### Shrine Description

**Physical Structure**
- Stone platform with carved draconic and lizardfolk symbols
- Central altar stone topped with weathered dragon sculpture
- Arranged standing stones forming ritual circle around platform
- Vine-covered offering alcoves containing ancient tribal artifacts

**Cultural Elements**
- Primitive but sophisticated stone carving techniques
- Mixture of draconic religious symbolism and lizardfolk tribal totems
- Evidence of regular use by multiple generations of lizardfolk
- Offerings of weapons, jewelry, and preserved organic materials

**Current State**
- Jungle vines have partially engulfed structure but shrine remains intact
- Some standing stones have toppled but ritual space is still recognizable
- Guardian spirit manifests when non-lizardfolk approach sacred areas
- Ambient magical energy from centuries of ritual worship

### Roleplay Information

**Guardian Spirit Personality**
- Ancient lizardfolk shaman bound to protect tribal sacred site
- Speaks mixture of broken Common and ritual Draconic
- Suspicious of outsiders but not immediately hostile

**Spiritual Duties**
- Protects shrine from desecration by non-believers
- Maintains connection between living tribe members and ancestral spirits
- Guards tribal treasures and sacred objects from theft

**Cultural Knowledge**
- Remembers generations of lizardfolk history and territorial boundaries
- Knows location of other tribal sites and seasonal gathering places
- Understands relationship between local lizardfolk clans and their traditions

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Manifestation:**
> "Warm-bloods approach sacred ground of the Scale-Born. Why do you disturb the resting place of ancestors? Speak purpose or face judgment of the Old Spirits."

**When Questioned About History:**
> "Many seasons have passed since the tribe gathered here. Wars came, territories shifted, young ones forgot the old ways. But the ancestors remember. We always remember."

**If Treated with Respect:**
> "You show honor to the old traditions. Few warm-bloods understand the ways of the Scale-Born. Perhaps the ancestors smile upon you."

### Knowledge

**What the Spirit Knows**
- Detailed history of local lizardfolk tribal politics and territorial conflicts
- Location of other sacred sites and seasonal gathering places
- Traditional knowledge about local flora, fauna, and seasonal patterns
- Information about recent changes in tribal behavior and external threats

**Sacred Offerings Available**
- Ancient weapons blessed by tribal shamans
- Carved totems providing protection against specific regional dangers
- Maps showing safe waterways and tribal territory boundaries
- Ritual knowledge for communicating with local lizardfolk communities

**Tribal Relations**
- Names and characteristics of current lizardfolk tribal leaders
- Protocol for respectful interaction with different lizardfolk clans
- Sacred taboos and customs that must be observed in tribal territories
- Recent conflicts and alliances between tribes and outside forces

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine shrine's current spiritual activity: recently used vs. long abandoned
- Note any recent offerings or signs of ongoing tribal visitation
- Consider relationship between guardian and local living lizardfolk
- Check party's previous interactions with lizardfolk tribes

**Opening Scene Options**

**Sacred Discovery:**
> *Ancient stonework emerges from the jungle tangle—carefully arranged in patterns that speak of religious significance. A platform of worked stone supports weathered sculptures depicting dragons and lizardfolk in ritual poses. As you approach, the air grows cooler, and whispers seem to echo from the vine-covered alcoves.*

**Guardian Awakening:**
> *The moment you step onto the stone platform, the temperature drops noticeably. Mist coalesces near the central altar, forming the translucent figure of an ancient lizardfolk shaman. Ritual scars cover the spirit's scaled hide, and ceremonial jewelry gleams with spectral light. "Warm-bloods approach sacred ground..."*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Spiritual Challenge (Social/Cultural Primary)**
- Guardian manifests to assess party's intentions and cultural awareness
- Tests knowledge of lizardfolk customs and respect for sacred sites
- May demand explanation for presence or proof of peaceful intentions
- **Religion Check (DC 13):** Recognize significance of shrine elements and proper protocols
- **History Check (DC 15):** Know lizardfolk tribal customs and respectful behavior
- **Insight Check (DC 12):** Understand guardian is protective but not necessarily hostile

**Phase 2: Cultural Exchange**
- If party shows appropriate respect, guardian becomes informative
- Shares knowledge about tribal history and regional dangers
- May offer blessings or sacred items to respectful visitors
- **Persuasion Check (DC 14):** Gain guardian's trust through respectful conversation
- **Investigation Check (DC 13):** Examine shrine offerings and artifacts with permission
- **Nature Check (DC 12):** Understand environmental knowledge guardian possesses

**Phase 3: Alliance, Warning, or Conflict**
- Successful interaction leads to valuable cultural knowledge and possible blessings
- Disrespectful behavior results in warning visions and potential spiritual attack
- Guardian may provide ongoing guidance for interacting with living lizardfolk

**Cultural Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Speaking Draconic (sacred language of lizardfolk spirituality)
- Offering appropriate gifts: weapons, crafted items, or food
- Showing knowledge of lizardfolk customs and territorial respect
- Demonstrating understanding of ancestral worship and spiritual traditions

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Touching sacred objects without permission
- Showing disrespect for lizardfolk culture or dismissing "primitive" beliefs
- Attempting to take offerings or artifacts from shrine
- Making threatening gestures or displaying obvious weapons

**Sacred Site Protocols**
- Remove weapons when approaching central altar (sign of peaceful intent)
- Speak quietly and respectfully in presence of spiritual manifestations
- Ask permission before examining artifacts or ritual objects
- Leave appropriate offerings when departing (food, crafted items, or small treasures)

**Guardian's Responses**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Complete historical knowledge shared including secret tribal locations
- Sacred blessing providing advantage on interactions with lizardfolk
- Access to tribal artifacts including blessed weapons or protective totems
- Possible introduction to living tribal members through spiritual mediation

**Success**
- Basic cultural information and respectful acknowledgment
- Safe passage through tribal territories if encountered
- Minor blessing providing insight into regional dangers

**Failure**
- Guardian remains suspicious and provides minimal information
- No spiritual assistance or cultural guidance offered
- Potential negative reaction from living lizardfolk due to disrespect

**Critical Failure**
- Ancestral Warning triggered, causing fear and spiritual disturbance
- Active hostility from guardian and possible combat
- Cursed by ancestral spirits (disadvantage on social interactions with lizardfolk)

**Shrine Exploration**

**Sacred Artifacts**
- **Tribal Weapons:** Spears and clubs blessed by shamans (+1 weapons)
- **Protective Totems:** Carved items providing specific regional protections
- **Ritual Maps:** Showing tribal territories, sacred sites, and seasonal routes
- **Ancient Jewelry:** Ceremonial items marking tribal status and achievements

**Historical Documents**
- Carved stone records of tribal genealogies and territorial agreements
- Ritual instructions for seasonal ceremonies and spiritual practices
- Warnings about regional dangers recorded in stone and organic materials
- Trade agreements and alliance records with other tribes and outside groups

**Spiritual Benefits**

**Ancestral Blessing**
- Advantage on all social interactions with lizardfolk for 30 days
- Ability to understand basic Draconic when spoken by lizardfolk
- Spiritual guidance providing insight into regional dangers
- Dreams showing safe paths through swampland and tribal territories

**Cultural Knowledge Rewards**
- Detailed maps of lizardfolk territorial boundaries
- Protocol for approaching tribal settlements safely
- Information about seasonal gatherings and trade opportunities
- Understanding of tribal politics and current conflicts

**Long-term Consequences**

**If Shrine Respected**
- Guardian remembers party favorably for future encounters
- Positive reputation spreads through spiritual network to living tribes
- Access to ongoing cultural consultation and regional intelligence
- Possible invitation to participate in seasonal tribal gatherings

**If Shrine Desecrated**
- Guardian becomes permanently hostile to party
- Living lizardfolk tribes warned about disrespectful outsiders
- Spiritual curse affects all interactions with scaled humanoids
- Loss of potential tribal alliances and safe passage agreements

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Guardian has witnessed events relevant to party's quest
- Tribal conflicts create opportunities for mediation or alliance
- Sacred site contains prophecy or map pointing to important locations
- Living lizardfolk seek party's help based on guardian's recommendation

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Guardian more trusting, cultural barriers lower
- **Harder:** Multiple spirits or angry ancestral manifestations
- **Environmental:** Shrine actively used by living tribe members
- **Political:** Shrine's location strategic for regional territorial conflicts
