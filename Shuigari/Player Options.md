## Bushido Code

**The Oath of Bushido.** The Oath of Bushido is a sacred vow taken by the samurai and honor-bound warriors of Shugane. It is a path of discipline, duty, and honor that reflects the ancient principles of the warrior code. Those who take this oath—whether they are paladins, samurai, or others who walk the path of honor—are bound to uphold these tenets, no matter the cost. To forsake this oath is to abandon one’s honor, a fate worse than death for those who serve under its banner.<br />The Bushido Code is more than a set of rules; it is a way of life, a path that dictates every action, every word, and every thought of those who swear to it. Though it demands much, it grants strength, courage, and clarity in return. The tenets of the code are considered sacred vows, binding the spirit as much as the body.<br />[h2]Tenets of Bushido[/h2]<br />**Gi - Righteousness.** “I shall act with absolute integrity and justice in all things.” A true warrior holds themselves to the highest moral standard. They strive to make the right choice, even when the path is difficult. Honor and justice must guide every action.<br /><br />**Yu - Courage.** “I shall be brave, and fear neither death nor defeat.” Courage is not the absence of fear but the strength to overcome it. A warrior does not shy away from danger, nor do they act recklessly. True courage is the will to act rightly in the face of adversity.<br /><br />**Jin - Benevolence.** “I shall show compassion and protect those who cannot protect themselves.” The samurai’s strength is a tool for the betterment of others. They must temper their might with mercy, using their power to defend the helpless and alleviate suffering wherever it is found.<br /><br />**Rei - Respect.** “I shall honor the spirit in all things and show courtesy, even to my enemies.” Respect is the foundation of true strength. To honor others, to treat them with dignity, and to show restraint in the use of power are all marks of a true warrior.<br /><br />**Makoto - Honesty.** “I shall speak only truth and remain true to my word.” Deceit and falsehood have no place in the soul of a warrior. The samurai’s word is their bond, and they must strive to be a living example of honesty and sincerity in all they do.<br /><br />**Meiyo - Honor.** “I shall live and die by the code, and I shall bring no shame upon myself or my family.” The warrior’s honor is their most valued possession. They must strive to live a life of integrity, bringing honor to their name and the name of their ancestors. To fail in this is to invite dishonor, which is a fate worse than death.<br /><br />**Chu - Duty and Loyalty.** “I shall serve my lord, my clan, and the Shogunate, unto death.” Loyalty to one’s lord and one’s purpose is the highest virtue. A warrior must be willing to give everything—wealth, power, and even life itself—in service to their oath and duty. To betray one’s lord is to become lost, a ronin without purpose or honor.<br /><br />[h2]Oath Spells[/h2]<br /><br />[table][tr][th]Level[/th][th]Spells[/th][/tr]<br />[tr][td]3rd[/td]<br />[td]Heroism, Shield of Faith[/td]<br />[/tr]<br />[tr][td]5th[/td]<br />[td]Zone of Truth, Spiritual Weapon[/td]<br />[/tr]<br />[tr][td]9th[/td]<br />[td]Aura of Vitality, Crusader&#39;s Mantle[/td]<br />[/tr]<br />[tr][td]13th[/td]<br />[td]Aura of Purity, Death Ward[/td]<br />[/tr]<br />[tr][td]17th[/td]<br />[td]Circle of Power, Commune[/td]<br />[/tr][/table]<br />[h2]Channel Divinity[/h2]<br />When you take this oath at 3rd level, you gain the following Channel Divinity options:<br /><br />**Righteous Fury.** As an action, you can use your Channel Divinity to inspire fear and awe in those around you. All hostile creatures within 30 feet must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of your next turn. Allies within the same radius are filled with resolve, gaining temporary hit points equal to your paladin level.<br /><br />**Stand Against Defeat. **When a creature within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to invoke your Channel Divinity and inspire your allies to persevere. For the next 1 minute, whenever a friendly creature within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 hit points, they can make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier). On a success, they are instead reduced to 1 hit point. After the effect ends, any creature that was saved by this feature gains 1 level of exhaustion for each time they were saved.<br />[h2]Aura of Discipline[/h2]<br />Starting at 7th level, your unwavering presence enforces strict focus and control on the battlefield. You and friendly creatures within 10 feet cannot be affected by advantage on attack rolls or disadvantage on saving throws while you are conscious. At 18th level, this aura extends to 30 feet.<br />[h2]Unbreakable Will[/h2]<br />At 15th level, your indomitable spirit cannot be shaken by external forces. Whenever you are subjected to an effect that would cause you to become paralyzed, stunned, or incapacitated, you can use your reaction to end the effect immediately.<br />[h2]Living Paragon[/h2]<br />At 20th level, you become a true embodiment of the Bushido Code. As an action, you can emanate an aura of radiant energy for 1 minute. While this aura is active, you gain the following benefits:<br /><br />[li]Allies within 30 feet of you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed and gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier at the start of each of their turns.[/li]<br />[li]You and all friendly creatures within 30 feet of you deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage with weapon attacks.[/li]<br />[li]If a creature is reduced to 0 hit points by an attack made by you or your allies, they must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier) or be frightened until the end of your next turn.[/li]<br /><br />Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.
