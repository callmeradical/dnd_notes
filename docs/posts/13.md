_We are at the train station..._

## Overview

The Lanterns respond to sounds of conflict at a fog-shrouded train station, discovering a locomotive under assault by massive spiders. What begins as a rescue mission quickly turns sinister when they uncover the train's cargo—a victim of Crown Alchemical Society experimentation, grotesquely transformed and amplified beyond recognition.

As the party fights their way through the arachnid swarm that has overrun the train cars, they face mounting danger from both the creatures and the dark forest beyond. The session culminates in a desperate battle as a colossal broodmother spider emerges from the mist, leaving the Lanterns' survival uncertain as they stand against this monstrous matriarch in the fog-choked valley.

## During the Session (recap)

The Lanterns arrived at the train station to a scene of chaos—gunshots echoing through the fog and whistles piercing the air. Following the tracks deeper into the mist, they discovered a train under siege by massive spiders that had emerged from the fog-shrouded valley.

Beyond the besieged locomotive, a dark forest blanketed the rest of the valley. Something enormous moved between the trees, its presence felt more than seen. The party witnessed a lone soldier being overwhelmed by three of the arachnid attackers, giving Ish'ta a crucial moment to potentially intervene and save the man's life.

The train's cargo proved to be far more sinister than expected—not goods or supplies, but a person, or rather what had once been a person. This was revealed to be the result of Crown Alchemical Society experimentation, a being whose existing powers had been horrifically amplified through their twisted research.

The situation deteriorated rapidly as the creatures tore through the train car's door, allowing spiders to pour into the confined space. The Lanterns found themselves locked in desperate combat with the arachnid swarm, but their troubles were far from over.

As the session reached its climax, the true threat revealed itself—a massive broodmother spider emerging to join the fray. The session ended on a cliffhanger, with the party's survival hanging in the balance as they face this monstrous matriarch in the fog-choked valley.
