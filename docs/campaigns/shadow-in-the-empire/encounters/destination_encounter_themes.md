These hoards and encounters tie directly to the **destinations near
Adderport**. Each provides both a **treasure cache** and a **thematic
encounter/plot beat** suitable for a level 13 party.

---

## Castle Garodya (45 miles)

**Encounter/Theme:** _The Dragon's Shadow._ As the party explores the melted
vaults, a wyrm circles overhead. It doesn't attack — it is sleeping nearby, its
very presence creating dread. The challenge is stealth, timing, and survival
under its looming shadow.

**Treasure Hoard:**

- ~15,000 gp worth of fused plate, goblets, relics (labor required to extract)
- **Dragonbone Weapon (+2):** Choose weapon type on attunement. Resonates
  faintly near dragons
- **Potion of Dragon's Majesty (homebrew):** For 1 minute, gain Frightful
  Presence & resistance to a dragon's breath weapon
- **Scroll of Gatekeeper's Ward:** One use; halts a planar or draconic entity
  for 1 round

---

## Newthrone (80 miles)

**Encounter/Theme:** _The Fractured Court._ The city is ruled by three
factions: collaborators with wyrm cults, mercenary bands, and desperate
refugees. PCs must navigate a tense council where a single spark could ignite
bloodshed. The encounter is social/political, with combat as the consequence of
failure.

**Treasure Hoard:**

- ~12,000 gp in Brelish regalia and seized tributes
- **Siberys Shard Crown (rare wondrous):** Advantage on Persuasion checks with
  dragonmarked scions
- **Amulet of Health:** Once worn by a Brelish governor
- **Ledger of Chains:** A magical book that records debts, contracts, and oaths
  — may tie to surviving factions

---

## Pitchwall (80 miles)

**Encounter/Theme:** _Collapse of Industry._ The mines are partially collapsed,
home to an awakened earth elemental bound by old Cannith wards. It is enraged,
lashing out at all who trespass. PCs can fight, banish, or try to reason with
the elemental to reach the hoard.

**Treasure Hoard:**

- ~10,000 gp in uncut dragonshards and raw ore
- **Elemental Gem (Earth):** Still bound in Cannith housing
- **Cannith Extraction Tools (rare wondrous):** Grant advantage on checks to
  mine or manipulate dragonshards
- **Gauntlets of Ogre Power:** A relic left behind by a Cannith foreman

---

## Duskwing Clanhold (120 miles)

**Encounter/Theme:** _Trial of the Heirloom._ The Duskwings demand the PCs
prove themselves through a gladiatorial trial — not to the death, but brutal
enough to test resolve. Success grants access to the vault. Failure means exile
or worse.

**Treasure Hoard:**

- ~20,000 gp in carved dragonshard idols and ceremonial arms
- **Dragon Mask (rare/very rare):** Grants resistance to one breath type and
  advantage on Intimidation checks
- **Horn of Valenar Warband:** Once per day, summon 2d4 spirit cavalry for 1
  hour
- **Manual of Bodily Health (or equivalent tome):** Hidden among shrine
  offerings

---

## Stormhorn Clanhold (100 miles)

**Encounter/Theme:** \*Siege in the Mountains.\* The clanhold is under attack
by wyrm cultists backed by a young dragon. PCs arrive in the middle of the
siege. They must choose to intervene (earning Stormhorn respect) or leave
(earning suspicion but conserving strength).

**Treasure Hoard:**

- ~14,000 gp in weapons, armor, and trophies
- **Dragonlance (rare weapon):** Forged for dragonborn champions, deals bonus
  damage vs. dragons
- **Ring of Protection:** Belonged to a clan chieftain
- **Potion of Heroism (2):** Clan-brewed elixirs that inspire courage

---

## Flamebrow Clanhold (100 miles)

**Encounter/Theme:** _Pact of Fire._ The Flamebrow leaders have secretly struck
a bargain with a red wyrm. PCs uncover the ritual in progress — a pact that
could doom the region. They can attempt to break it, sabotage it, or manipulate
it for their own ends.

**Treasure Hoard:**

- ~18,000 gp in shards, ceremonial armor, and tribute
- **Dragon Mask (Red variant):** Grants fire resistance and draconic
  intimidation
- **3 Potions of Storm's Fury (homebrew):** Resistance to lightning & thunder;
  can cast _shatter_ once while active
- **Fragment of a Siberys Shard:** Socket into a spell focus; grants +1 to
  spell attack & DC against dragons

## GM Guidance

### Encounter Themes

- Each destination has a **thematic encounter** and **treasure hoard**:
  - Garodya = ruin & looming wyrm + wyrm-touched artifacts
  - Newthrone = fractured politics + Brelish regalia & political tools
  - Pitchwall = Cannith industry collapse + dragonshards & mining tools
  - Duskwing = trials of worth + dragonborn heirlooms & ceremonial items
  - Stormhorn = martial siege + weapons & armor
  - Flamebrow = wyrm pact + shard wealth & fiery artifacts
- Encounters can be tuned to combat, social, or exploration focus depending on
  session pacing

### Treasure Design

- Each **destination** provides rewards appropriate for a Tier 3 (level 13)
  party
- Balance gold/art (for leverage in a collapsed economy) with unique wyrm-tied
  relics
- Use treasure to reinforce **session themes** and drop **story hooks**
  (Gatekeeper wards, Cannith tools, clan relics)
- Treasure serves both as **reward** and as **story hook** for larger arcs

⚡ **This way, whichever path the party chooses, there's a thematic hoard and
encounter that feels tailored to the destination.**
