# Kagemi no Oni (Mirror-Shadow Revenant)

*Medium undead (yokai), neutral evil*

---

## Lore

The Kagemi is not born — it **coalesces**. When a Shadow Register event thins the Mithal long enough, the pressure differential between the material plane and spirit realm forces reality to develop something like scar tissue. The Kagemi is that scar given hunger. It is an entity that exists in the membrane between worlds, visible only to those with spirit-sight, and actively hostile to any mind that dares to perceive it.

In pre-Shogunate texts, Tsukuyomi's priests recorded contact with similar entities near places where the boundary between the seen and unseen worlds had been damaged by violence or grief. They named them "the ones who resent being seen," and their remedies were simple: do not look, do not use spirit-sight near places of great pressure, and if you must know the invisible world, carry mirrored paper and never look directly.

The Kagemi does not hunt by aggression. It simply exists in the liminal space, and those who open the spirit-eye in its presence are exposed to the full, unfiltered weight of something that should not be perceived. The mind breaks not because the Kagemi attacks it, but because the mind was not designed to hold what it finds there.

A Kagemi that has fed on enough perception events becomes more active, developing a craving for the sensation of being witnessed, and beginning to stalk individuals with active spirit-sight abilities.

## Appearance

To those without magical perception, the Kagemi is completely invisible. The only physical clue of its presence is a faint pressure in the ears, the kind felt before a storm, and the way reflective surfaces (still water, polished armor, lacquered screens) show a smear of darkness that doesn't correspond to anything in the room.

To someone using Detect Magic, See Invisibility, Truesight, or any ability that pierces mundane sight, the Kagemi appears as a vaguely humanoid shape whose proportions shift and rearrange continuously. It has too many joints. Its face is a smooth oval of dark mirror-glass that reflects not the viewer's face, but the viewer's worst memory. Its hands trail threads of something between smoke and hair, floating in still air as if underwater. It does not move so much as it is suddenly elsewhere, leaving afterimage traceries in spirit-sight.

> *"In the direction your magic is pointing, there is something shaped approximately like a person, but it keeps having too many elbows, and its face is a mirror. Your reflection in that mirror is not making the same expression you are."*

## Traits

### Threshold Form
The Kagemi exists simultaneously in the material plane and the spirit realm. It is invisible to all creatures that do not have magical means of sight (Detect Magic, See Invisibility, Truesight, Blindsight through magical senses, or equivalent). Mundane Darkvision does not reveal it. A creature may make a DC 18 Wisdom (Perception) check to detect the Kagemi's presence through environmental signs (pressure sensation, mirror distortions) but cannot determine its exact location this way.

### Spirit-Sight Revulsion
When a creature uses a magical ability or spell that would let it perceive the Kagemi (Detect Magic, See Invisibility, True Seeing, Arcane Eye, or any ability explicitly described as allowing spirit-sight or seeing invisible/hidden creatures), that creature must immediately make a **DC 17 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, roll on the Threshold Madness table and apply the result. On a success, the creature perceives the Kagemi normally but has disadvantage on all saving throws against the Kagemi's other abilities until the end of its next turn.

This saving throw occurs the moment the Kagemi enters range of the perceiving ability, or the moment the ability is activated while the Kagemi is already in range. It also triggers again at the start of each turn during which the creature both maintains the perceiving ability and can see the Kagemi.

### Liminal Displacement
The Kagemi cannot be targeted by opportunity attacks. When it takes damage, it can use its reaction to shift partially into the spirit realm, reducing the damage by 2d10.

### Mirror Hunger
Whenever a creature fails a saving throw against Spirit-Sight Revulsion, the Kagemi regains 10 hit points.

### Spirit Tongue
The Kagemi can communicate with creatures that are currently suffering a madness effect (from any source) through dreams, intrusive visions, or whispered words audible only to the affected creature. It cannot be compelled to stop this communication by magical silence.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Kagemi makes two Veil Lash attacks.

### Veil Lash
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) psychic damage. A creature that takes this damage must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn as its mind briefly processes the physical contact with something that shouldn't exist.

*This attack can only be made against creatures that can currently perceive the Kagemi.*

### Reflection Pull (Recharge 5-6)
The Kagemi manifests a reflection of a creature within 30 feet that can currently perceive it. That creature must make a **DC 17 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, the creature is paralyzed until the end of its next turn as it stares into the reflection, which shows the creature's own death. On a successful save, the creature is frightened of the Kagemi until the end of its next turn.

### Mirror Step
The Kagemi teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. This movement is instantaneous and does not provoke opportunity attacks. It can pass through any reflective surface (still water, a polished shield, a lacquered panel) as if that surface were a door, emerging from any other reflective surface within 60 feet.

## Legendary Actions (3/round)

**Threshold Pressure (1 action).** Each creature currently perceiving the Kagemi must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the start of the Kagemi's next turn.

**Shift (1 action).** The Kagemi uses Mirror Step.

**Veil Lash (2 actions).** The Kagemi makes one Veil Lash attack.

## Reactions

### Revulsion Feedback
When a creature attempts to target the Kagemi with a spell of 1st level or higher, the Kagemi can force that creature to make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the spell slot is expended but the spell has no effect. This reaction can be used once per round.

---

## Weaknesses

### Mithal Anchors
At a properly maintained shrine to Tsukuyomi or a location where a kami's blessing is active, the Kagemi cannot use Spirit-Sight Revulsion and its Damage Resistances are suppressed.

### Mirrored Paper (Ofuda Variant)
A specially prepared ofuda incorporating a mirrored interior, created with a DC 15 Arcana or Religion check and materials worth 25 gp, can be burned as an action to force the Kagemi to make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it is banished to the spirit realm for 1 minute and cannot return during that time.

### Spirit Ward
The spell Dispel Evil and Good, when cast at the Kagemi's current location, forces it to make a Charisma saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or be banished as if by the Banishment spell (no concentration required).

## DM Notes

### The Core Tension
Do you turn off Detect Magic and go blind, or keep it running and keep rolling madness saves?

### Entry into Combat
Don't start with the Kagemi attacking. Start with an NPC guard — someone posted with a warding scroll, or a priest of Tsukuyomi with active spirit-sight — already in a madness state, muttering, weapon drawn against nothing. The party has to figure out what happened before they realize what's still there with them.

### Running the Madness Table
Roll in the open, read the result aloud, and then ask the player: "What does your character see in the mirror?" The Kagemi's reflection shows the character's worst memory or feared death — but you don't decide what that is. The player does.

### Shadow Register Context
In the current Takashima situation, Kagemi are a direct consequence of the partial Shadowfell overlay. They are not controlled by the Oni Mage or Tsurogami — they are a symptom of the damage. Destroying a Kagemi doesn't advance the enemy's agenda. It just removes environmental pressure. This makes the encounter feel like a world in pain rather than an ambush.

### Sound Design
When the Kagemi uses Veil Lash, the perceiving character hears a sound like paper tearing very close to their ear. Nobody else hears anything.

### Signature Behavior
It will stop moving and face a perceiving creature fully, waiting. It does not flee from damage. It retreats only when Mirror Hunger is satisfied or when it is close to destruction.

### Loot
A destroyed Kagemi leaves behind a fragment of dark mirrored material, about the size of a hand, that shows reflections of the spirit realm rather than the material world. It functions as a one-use Scrying focus that reaches into spirit-realm locations, and could provide a vision of the Oni Mage's location if used correctly.
