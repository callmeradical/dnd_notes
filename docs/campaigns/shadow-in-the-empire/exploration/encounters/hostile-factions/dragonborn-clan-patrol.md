# Dragonborn Clan Patrol

## Dragonborn Warrior
*Medium humanoid (dragonborn), lawful neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (Scale Mail)  
**Hit Points** 58 (9d8+18)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16(+3) | 12(+1) | 15(+2) | 10(+0) | 13(+1) | 12(+1) |

**Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +4  
**Skills** Intimidation +3, Perception +3, Survival +3  
**Damage Resistance** Fire (or other based on draconic ancestry)  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Breath Weapon (Recharge 5-6).** The dragonborn exhales destructive energy in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The dragonborn makes two weapon attacks.

**Longsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10+3) slashing damage if used with two hands.

**Javelin.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing damage.

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 2-4 Dragonborn Warriors  
**Environment:** Clan territories, ancient ruins, sacred sites  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#2 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Honor-bound warriors who value courage and directness in conversation
- Suspicious of outsiders but respect those who show proper courtesy
- Deeply connected to their draconic heritage and clan traditions

**Ideals**

- Clan Honor: Actions reflect on the entire clan, must maintain reputation
- Draconic Pride: Dragons are the pinnacle of natural order and power
- Territorial Sovereignty: Clan lands must be protected from all intruders

**Bonds**

- Sworn to protect clan territory and sacred sites from desecration
- Loyal to clan elders and the memory of their draconic ancestors
- Consider other dragonborn clans as rivals but potential allies

**Flaws**

- Prideful: Cannot back down from direct challenges to honor or strength
- Traditional: Slow to accept new ideas or methods
- Xenophobic: Distrustful of non-dragonborn, especially humans

**Mannerisms**

- Speak in clipped, formal sentences with military bearing
- Unconsciously bare teeth when agitated or suspicious
- Touch clan medallions or weapons when invoking honor or tradition

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Challenge:**
> "Halt! You trespass on the hunting grounds of the [Color] Scale Clan. State your business or prepare to face the consequences of your intrusion."

**When Respected:**
> "You show proper courtesy, travelers. Perhaps you are not as ignorant as most of your kind. Speak your purpose here."

**In Combat:**
> "For the glory of the clan and the memory of the Ancient Ones! Your bones will mark our territory!"

### Tactics

**Initial Approach**
- Form defensive line blocking path or surrounding party
- Demand explanation for presence in clan territory
- Use intimidation and display of draconic heritage

**Combat Approach**
- Use breath weapons in opening round to maximize damage
- Coordinate attacks to focus fire on most dangerous opponents
- Fight with disciplined military tactics, protecting wounded allies
- Use javelins for ranged attacks before closing to melee

**Retreat Conditions**
- Never flee from direct combat unless ordered by clan superior
- May withdraw to alert clan of serious threats
- Fight to the death if protecting sacred sites or clan honor

### Knowledge

**What They Know**
- Detailed knowledge of clan territory boundaries and safe passages
- Locations of other dragonborn clans and their current relations
- Recent draconic activity in the region and its significance
- Ancient ruins and their historical importance to dragonborn

**What They Respect**
- Direct, honest communication without deception
- Displays of personal courage and martial skill
- Knowledge of draconic lore and proper customs
- Respect for territorial boundaries and clan traditions

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine clan type: Red (aggressive), Brass (talkative), Silver (lawful), etc.
- Roll for patrol size: 2-4 warriors based on threat level in area
- Note encounter location: border patrol, sacred site, or territorial challenge
- Consider clan's current disposition: hostile due to recent conflicts or neutral

**Opening Scene Options**

**Border Challenge:**
> *Four imposing figures emerge from the jungle undergrowth—dragonborn in scale armor that matches their metallic hide. Their leader steps forward, longsword at the ready, reptilian eyes fixed on your group with ancient authority. "Halt! You trespass on the hunting grounds of the Copper Scale Clan."*

**Sacred Site Guardian:**
> *Ancient carved stones rise from the jungle floor, their draconic runes still glowing faintly after centuries. Between you and the ruins stand two dragonborn warriors, their bronze-scaled faces set in grim determination. "This place is sacred to our ancestors. Turn back now, or face their wrath."*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Territorial Challenge (Social Primary)**
- Dragonborn challenge party's right to be in clan territory
- Demand explanation and assess threat level
- Test party's knowledge of proper protocols and respect
- **History Check (DC 14):** Know appropriate diplomatic protocols for dragonborn clans
- **Insight Check (DC 12):** Recognize they're testing courage, not seeking immediate violence
- **Persuasion Check (DC 13):** Explain legitimate purpose and request passage

**Phase 2: Honor Assessment**
- If party shows respect, dragonborn become more receptive
- May issue challenge of courage or skill instead of combat
- Willing to negotiate if party demonstrates worthiness
- **Intimidation Check (DC 15):** Challenge dragonborn authority (high risk/reward)
- **Performance Check (DC 16):** Demonstrate knowledge of draconic customs
- **Athletics/Acrobatics Check (DC 14):** Accept physical challenge to prove worth

**Phase 3: Resolution**
- Combat only if party fails to show proper respect or threatens clan interests
- Successful negotiation may lead to information exchange or safe passage
- Honor challenges can create respect and potential alliance
- **Religion Check (DC 13):** Understand significance of clan totems and sacred sites

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Speaking Draconic fluently
- Showing knowledge of dragonborn customs and history
- Demonstrating personal courage or martial skill
- Offering tribute or respect to clan ancestors

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Lying or attempting deception (dragonborn value honesty)
- Showing disrespect for draconic heritage or clan traditions
- Backing down from direct challenges or showing cowardice
- Comparing dragonborn unfavorably to other races

**Cultural Considerations**
- Dragonborn respect directness over diplomatic subtlety
- Personal combat prowess is highly valued
- Clan honor supersedes individual concerns
- Ancient draconic traditions guide all decision-making

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Safe passage through entire clan territory
- Information about regional threats and opportunities
- Possible invitation to meet with clan elders
- Respect that may influence other dragonborn encounters

**Success**
- Safe passage for immediate area
- Basic information about territorial boundaries
- Neutral standing with clan (not friend, not enemy)

**Failure**
- Must pay tribute or face combat
- Restricted from certain areas of clan territory
- Marked as potential threats for future encounters

**Critical Failure**
- Immediate combat with full clan response
- Clan war party may pursue beyond territory
- Other dragonborn clans warned about disrespectful outsiders

**Combat Tactics Deep Dive**

**Breath Weapon Strategy**
- Open combat with coordinated breath attacks to maximize damage
- Position to avoid friendly fire while catching multiple enemies
- Save recharge for critical moments or when surrounded
- Different ancestry types use different tactics (fire = direct, cold = area denial)

**Formation Fighting**
- Fight as disciplined unit with clear roles
- Protect wounded allies and maintain battle line
- Use javelins to soften targets before melee engagement
- Coordinate multiattacks to focus down priority targets

**Honor Combat Rules**
- May accept single combat challenges from worthy opponents
- Fight to unconsciousness rather than death if honor is satisfied
- Respect enemies who show courage and skill in battle
- May spare defeated opponents who fought with honor

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Combat Occurred**
- Other clan members investigate if patrol doesn't return
- Clan may seek revenge or demand weregild (blood money)
- Reputation as clan-killers spreads among dragonborn
- Possible escalation to larger territorial conflict

**If Resolved Peacefully**
- Clan remembers party favorably for future encounters
- Information network about regional dragonborn politics
- Possible trade opportunities or military alliances
- Access to clan knowledge about ancient draconic sites

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Clan guards access to important ruins party needs to explore
- Inter-clan conflict requires neutral mediators
- Ancient prophecy involves cooperation between clans and outsiders
- Clan elder has information about party's quest but requires proof of worth

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce numbers, use younger/less experienced warriors
- **Harder:** Add clan champion or draconic sorcerer support
- **Environmental:** Encounter at sacred site with magical wards or guardians
- **Social:** Complex clan politics require careful navigation
