## City Districts and Major Areas

- **Upper Citadel** — Seat of rule, vault beneath, festival focus.  
  Source: [Takashima City - Districts and Locations](../../shuigari/Takashima%20City%20-%20Districts%20and%20Locations.md)
- **Middle Terrace** — Primary operating tier in Arc 2.  
  Source: [Takashima City - Districts and Locations](../../shuigari/Takashima%20City%20-%20Districts%20and%20Locations.md)
  - **Smiths' Quarter**  
  - **Temple District**  
  - **Market District**
  - **Temple District (Major Temples)**  
    - **Temple of Izanami** — Fire and creation rites; forge-blessings and smithing oaths.  
      - **NPCs:**  
        - **High Forgemother Saeko** — stern priest-smith who blesses blades before the Festival of Ascending Iron.  
        - **Acolyte Ren** — anxious clerk who tracks tithe ledgers and fears Circle tampering.  
      - **What They Know:**  
        - **Vault Entrances:** Temple archives mention **eight sealed approaches**; two are described as “forge-warm paths” tied to smithing guild tunnels.  
        - **Hikari’s Grove:** Saeko records a temple legend: the grove is **hidden within the vault’s maze**, appearing only to those who fight beyond exhaustion and capacity—true heroes.  
        - **Stream of the One Tree:** Ren heard rumors of a **silver-thread stream** used to temper soulsteel; source noted as “beneath the old distillery.”  
      - **Quests & Hooks:**  
        - Missing blessing seals were stolen from the forge altar; recover them before the festival.  
        - A sacred anvil cools with black lacquer; cleanse it or risk corrupted weapon oaths.  
    - **Shrine of Hachiman** — War and honor; samurai vows, militia rites, and vigil watch.  
      - **NPCs:**  
        - **Captain Jiro Takeda** — veteran shrine-guard, distrusts nobles, respects honorable acts.  
        - **Novice Yui** — messenger with a network of militia rumors.  
      - **What They Know:**  
        - **Vault Entrances:** Jiro knows of **eight entrance codes** used by citadel patrols; only two are still in active rotation.  
        - **Hikari’s Grove:** Yui repeats a vow from old drillmasters: the grove **only appears within the vault’s maze** to those who push beyond their limits.  
        - **Stream of the One Tree:** Old war maps mark a **spring-fed channel** used for siege water; “One Tree” is scribbled in the margin.  
      - **Quests & Hooks:**  
        - A disgraced guard seeks public redemption through a dangerous vigil; escort him.  
        - A cache of oath-scrolls vanished from the shrine armory; track the thief.  
    - **Sanctuary of Susanoo** — Storms and chaos; storm-watching, warding against tempests.  
      - **NPCs:**  
        - **Stormwatcher Mikoto** — seer who reads wind patterns from the citadel heights.  
        - **Tide-Keeper Hanae** — caretaker of storm wards, secretly buying black-market reagents.  
      - **What They Know:**  
        - **Vault Entrances:** Mikoto saw **eight storm-ward spirals** on an old survey—entrances aligned to wind directions.  
        - **Hikari’s Grove:** Hanae keeps a whispered clue: **follow a white moth that appears only when you are spent to the edge of collapse**—it flutters through the maze and vanishes at the grove’s veil. The trials beyond remain unchanged.  
        - **Stream of the One Tree / Distillery:** Smugglers whisper about a **distillery** that draws from the One Tree stream, hidden below the Terrace.  
      - **Quests & Hooks:**  
        - Sudden storm surges break warding lines; gather rare reagents before the next squall.  
        - A storm-omened relic is buried in the harbor mud; retrieve it before a rival cult.
- **Lower Yards** — Industrial and lower-tier neighborhoods.
