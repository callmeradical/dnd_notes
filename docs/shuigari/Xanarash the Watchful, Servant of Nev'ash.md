# Xanarash the Watchful, Servant of Nev'ash

## Background

Xanarash, a beholder known for his cunning and fierce independence, once roamed the Underdark, guarding his treasure-laden lair and his beloved pet cat, Quondi. His life took a drastic turn when Quelantheril Nev'ash captured Quondi's soul, compelling Xanarash into servitude. Bound by his love for Quondi, Xanarash now serves Nev'ash with unwavering loyalty.

## Alignment

Lawful Neutral

## Appearance

Xanarash is a typical beholder with a floating, spherical body covered in dark purple, mottled skin. His large central eye glows with an intense blue light, and his maw is filled with sharp teeth. Ten eyestalks, each ending in a smaller eye, protrude from his body, each capable of emitting a deadly ray.

## Abilities

- **Antimagic Cone (Central Eye):** Xanarash can project a cone of antimagic, negating magical effects within its range.
- **Eyestalk Rays:** His ten eyestalks can unleash various magical rays, including disintegration, petrification, telekinesis, and more.
- **Levitation:** Xanarash floats effortlessly through the air, moving in any direction he chooses.

## Personality

Xanarash is intelligent, calculating, and fiercely protective of Quondi's soul. While he harbors resentment towards Nev'ash for holding Quondi's soul hostage, he serves with a meticulous dedication, knowing that any failure could endanger Quondi.

## Role and Motivations

- **Guardian of Zephyr's Reach:** Xanarash patrols the skies and hidden pathways of Zephyr's Reach, using his powers to protect the city from threats.
- **Enforced Servitude:** He follows Nev'ash's commands without question, his every action motivated by the desire to protect Quondi.
- **Advisor and Enforcer:** Xanarash offers strategic counsel to Nev'ash and enforces his will, using his formidable abilities to eliminate threats and maintain order.

## Goals

- **Protect Quondi's Soul:** Above all, Xanarash's primary goal is to ensure the safety of Quondi's soul.
- **Fulfill Nev'ash's Commands:** He works tirelessly to carry out Nev'ash's orders, hoping to one day earn Quondi's freedom.
- **Expand Influence:** Despite his servitude, Xanarash seeks to expand his influence and power within Zephyr's Reach.

## Plot Hooks

- **Liberation Mission:** Xanarash secretly seeks adventurers who might help him free Quondi's soul from Nev'ash's grasp.
- **Artifact Retrieval:** Xanarash learns of an artifact that could potentially break Nev'ash's hold over Quondi's soul and needs adventurers to retrieve it.
- **Internal Threat:** Xanarash discovers a plot against Nev'ash and must enlist the help of adventurers to root out the traitors.

## Interactions with the Party

- **Reluctant Ally:** While initially wary, Xanarash can become a powerful ally, providing support with his abilities and knowledge in exchange for the party's help in freeing Quondi.
- **Quest Giver:** He can offer various quests, some directly benefiting Nev'ash's goals and others aimed at securing Quondi's safety.
- **Complex Relationship:** Xanarash's servitude and his concealed resentment towards Nev'ash can create a complex dynamic with the party, who may sympathize with his plight or question his true intentions.
