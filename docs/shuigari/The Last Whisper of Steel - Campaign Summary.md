## Session Highlights
### 000 – Temple of Daichi-no-kame
- Cleansed the corrupted shrine and defeated **The Drowned Mistress**.
- Learned of **Shindarasu** (the Cracked Shell) and the need to cleanse all six shrines before confronting it.

### 001–002 – Nagano Village & Funeral
- Arrived on the eve of the Moonlit Reverence festival.  
- Met key NPCs: **Haruto**, **Renji**, **Lady Hana Arashi**, **Lord Ichiro Kagemura**.  
- Master Hideo’s funeral disrupted by ghoul attack and raven yokai omen; Renji apprehended.

### 003–005 – The Obsidian Circle Exposed
- Discovered Circle ritual kit and coded directives referencing the “boy’s blood key”.  
- Learned Circle targets shrines and plans to weaponise Hideo’s legacy.  
- Encountered three Circle generals; bargained with Renji about the **Grey Tide**.

### 013–015 – Breathless Hollow & Shrine Three
- Traversed the **Hollowed Breath**, suffering memory manipulation and corruption.  
- Retrieved Soulsteel blueprints, forge hammer, and **Shard #3** of Daichi-no-kame’s seal.  
- Met **Minari, the Ashen Widow**; uncovered ritual requirements for sanctifying shards.

### 014–016 – Defending the Lock Shrine
- Allied with **Kadei** and **Yoji** to defend Shrine Three (a lock powered by the **Seven Binders**).  
- Captured Circle spies and discovered warband led by **General Kaien, the Smiling Storm**.  
- Chose a pre-emptive strike on the warcamp rather than a siege defense.

### 017 – Warcamp Infiltration (Current)
- Infiltrating Circle warcamp; leaders present include **Kaien**, **Korha**, **Rakuya Saan**, **Vel Tain**, **Brother Mioshi**.  
- Rescued **Kazuo Himura**, retainer of Lady Arashi, who warns: *“The boy is not safe; Circle agents already hold Takashima.”*
- Discovered orders converging on Takashima and targeting the boy’s blood as key to the seal.

## Major Factions & Fronts
- **The Obsidian Circle:** Seeks to “beautifully unmake” reality under **Tsurogami**. Infiltrates shrines, guilds, and nobility; uses false miracles and corruption.
- **Civil War & Succession:** The Daimyo’s illness, Lady Hana Arashi’s machinations, and the boy heir’s miracles ignite competing claims.
- **The Ronin’s Curse:** The Twice-Cursed Samurai transitions from quarry to patron, but his curse bleeds into every mission, threatening instability.

## Spiritual & Mystical Discoveries
- **Seven Binders** power the shrine lock; their remnants (Minari, Masaru, others) offer guidance or peril.
- **Breathless Hollow** travel induces corruption; Shindarasu implants false memories (e.g., Kenshin).
- **Sanctification Rituals** require multi-skill challenges and rare reagents to stabilise Soulsteel shards.

## Status at Session 017
- **Shrines Cleansed/Defended:** Shrine 1 (cleansed), Shrine 3 (defended/locked). Others unknown.
- **Soulsteel Assets:** Forge hammer, blueprint cache, shard fragments (pending sanctification).
- **Circle Threat:** Warband poised to strike Takashima; Circle embeds within the capital.
- **Next Moves:** Complete the warcamp raid, secure Kazuo’s testimony, and relocate operations to Takashima—the stage for Arc 2.
