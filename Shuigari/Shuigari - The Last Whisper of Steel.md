## Campaign Information
- Experience is awarded based on treasure found and encounters cleared.
- The campaign is set in Shuigari, with early play rooted in the village of Nagano within Takashima Prefecture.
- Sessions open on the 21st day of Tsukinami (Autumn), 2302 CP, during the Moonlit Reverence festival for Tsukuyomi.

## Character Creation Guidelines
- **Ability Scores:** Standard array.
- **Primary Sourcebook:** *Player’s Handbook 2024*.
- **Hit Points:** Take the average at each level.
- **Backgrounds & Species:** Select options from the Core rulebook.
- **Starting Wealth:** Use the standard allotment for the chosen background.
- **Backstory prompts:**
  - **Who You Are:** brief background, profession, origin, or personality.
  - **Why You’re Adventuring:** reason for embracing danger.
  - **Quirk or Flaw:** simple, distinctive trait (e.g., afraid of rats, loves shiny objects).

## Campaign Summary
- **Opening Scene:** Master Hideo Nakata’s funeral in the Whispering Glade, as cherry blossoms swirl through a cold northern breeze.
- **Central Conflict:** Hideo’s death leaves a power vacuum that draws rivals, uneasy allies, and hidden enemies into play.
- **First Adventure:** *The Twice Cursed*.
- **Primary Themes:** Secrets revealed under moonlight, fractured loyalties, and the struggle to inherit a legacy without being consumed by it.

21st Day of Moonlight Path (Tsukinami), 2302 Common Period—Moonlit Reverence. The campaign opens at Master Hideo Nakata’s funeral in the Whispering Glade near Nagano, Takashima prefecture. Cherry blossoms drift in an unnaturally cold breeze, glowing in filtered sunlight. Beneath the beauty lies unease.

Master Hideo, the “Saint of the Silver Road,” forged soul-embodying weapons and was revered across Shuigari. His mysterious death creates a power vacuum drawing out enemies and uneasy allies. Mourners speak in hushed, loaded tones; whispers of betrayal, stolen legacies, and debts swirl through the glade. The forest itself seems to wait. Destinies entwine and the seeds of conflict take root at the Frostwood’s edge.
