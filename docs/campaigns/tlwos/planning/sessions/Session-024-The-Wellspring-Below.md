# Session 024: The Wellspring Below

**Arc:** The Iron Court (Arc 2)
**Session Type:** Dungeon exploration, NPC-guided, climax reveal scene
**Estimated Runtime:** 3-4 hours
**Status:** PREP

---

## Opening: Kazan-Tetsu Leads

He doesn't walk — he moves like cooling metal. Calls the party "small ones." Not dismissive. Accurate.

Three things he drops naturally en route:
1. The route passes a breached ward-seal. He's been going around it.
2. He saw something wearing Akemi's shape near the seal-channels recently. Didn't stop her. Regrets it.
3. He knows the altar activates when One Tree material enters. Won't mention this.

If asked what the altar does: *"It shows what is. Whether that is welcome is not the altar's concern."*

---

## Encounter 1: The Grief Passage

**Runtime:** 20-30 min | **Tone:** Dread, Discovery | **Hooks:** Kanji, Fox

Maintenance corridor lined with **soul-echo panels** — polished black stone that captured psychic impressions of everyone who passed during the vault's active years. The breach cracked several. Impressions leaking: ghostly Oniwaban moving in formation, the silver-thread stream glowing through the floor.

**Kanji:** One echo stops and looks at him specifically. **Akemi.** Not a recording — a bleed-through of her final moments here. She looks terrified. Points downward toward the altar. Then fractures. Gone.

**Fox:** Two panels show the same corridor from two angles. One matches a memory Fox has. The other shows the same corridor empty. The memory is false. Fox can find the *real* chamber the memory pointed toward.

**Kazan-Tetsu:** Looks at the Akemi echo a long moment before it breaks. *"They were here. Now they are not. The stone remembers differently than we do."*

**Foreshadow:** The panels are a crude version of what the altar does. Except the altar doesn't just show impressions. It shows what's underneath.

---

## Encounter 2: The Seal That Failed

**Runtime:** 30-40 min | **Tone:** Tension, Action | **Hook:** Aoshi

One of the three failing ward-seals is on the direct route. Kazan-Tetsu stops: *"The path has changed. One of the seals opened."*

A **Void-Touched Oni Warden** entered through the breach — not here for the party. Here to finish destabilizing the seal for a Circle extraction team. Methodical. Completes its task if given 3 uninterrupted rounds on the seal mechanism.

### Combat Design

- Party must stop the oni warden while protecting Kazan-Tetsu as he reinforces the seal
- The seal mechanisms are Oniwaban-designed. Only Aoshi can assist Kazan-Tetsu properly — reading what his ancestors wrote. Last Oniwaban in function, not just title.

### After Combat

Breach point has tracks. Small footprints, light step. A single **Oniwaban cipher mark** — same clan as Aoshi, same as the sealed letter. **Tenka.** The traitor was here.

The cipher reads as a *request for contact*, not sabotage. Ambiguity is the point.

Kazan-Tetsu: *"The one who came through moved like someone in pain. Not cruelty. Something worse."*

---

## Encounter 3: The Silver-Thread Crossing

**Runtime:** 20-30 min | **Tone:** Wonder, Weight | **Hooks:** Thornbringer, Yasei

The altar approach crosses directly over the exposed silver-thread stream. Luminous, slow-moving, reactive to what it senses. The vault's bloodstream.

**Thornbringer:** The Rootseed responds. Recognition. The stream and seed are from the same system. Seed warms. Anyone touching Thornbringer feels it. Preview of the altar — something old seeing what you carry.

**Yasei:** Lantern and snowglobe both reacting to the stream — and to each other. The stream doesn't like the snowglobe. Lantern-light bends toward the stream like a plant toward sun. Thirty-second decision: hold both, release the snowglobe into the stream (suppresses Krixarion's tracking temporarily — costs something), or keep moving and let both reactions build.

**Kazan-Tetsu:** Crosses last. Trails one hand through the stream. If asked what it feels like: *"Like being forgiven for something you didn't do wrong."* No follow-up. Keeps walking.

*This is the pause before the altar. Let it breathe.*

---

## The Consecration Altar — Climax Scene

**Runtime:** 45-60 min | **Tone:** Devastating, Necessary

### The Chamber

Older than the rest. Stone grew this way, pre-worked. A low platform of black stone veined with gold surrounding a basin where the silver-thread stream surfaces, circles, descends. Carved into the ceiling: not a face — a *hand*, open, palm down, resting gently on something.

### What It Is

This is NOT the Forge of Reflection — that's in the Frostwood, Hideo's personal workshop. This is the raw source the Forge of Reflection drew power from. The ancient kami-blessing altar where soulsteel blades received final consecration from Tsuchi-no-Megumi (Earth Kami). The exposed nerve. Hideo's forge was the refined instrument; this is the wellspring.

### Activation

Kazan-Tetsu stops at the threshold: *"I did not come in the last time. I have not come in since."*

The moment Thornbringer crosses with the Rootseed, the altar ignites — light from below, gold-white. The basin fills. Tsuchi-no-Megumi's presence isn't a voice. It's a *pressure*: the room becomes more real. Shadows sharpen. You hear your own heartbeat.

**The altar does not ask permission. It does not offer choices. It shows.**

---

### PC Reveals — Run in This Order

#### Shigeo (First — Set the Register)

The altar shows the *finished* version of the unfinished song. Three more verses. The version that exists if the father keeps trying. He can't transcribe fast enough. Then it's gone. Not reunion — proof the story isn't over.

*Welcome revelation. Sets the emotional register for the room.*

#### Thornbringer (Second — Advance the Quest)

The Rootseed leaves Thornbringer's hands — gently, like a child climbing down — and floats to the basin. Communes. Thornbringer sees the Well of Life: not a location, a *condition*. The Well is wherever the One Tree grows. The One Tree IS the Well's above-ground expression. The vault doesn't contain the Well. It contains one of its roots. The Rootseed knows where to go.

*Revelatory. Advances the main quest thread.*

#### Yasei (Third — Crack Something Central)

The lantern expands and shows Yasei's village from above. Beneath it — a door. The Hikari Yasei's people worshipped was never the deity itself. It was light bleeding through a crack in the world. Hikari is real. The connection is real. But the village was built on a *gate*, not a blessing.

*Devastating. Don't resolve this. Let it sit.*

#### Fox (Fourth — Specific Devastation)

All memories shown simultaneously. True ones glow gold. False ones silver-grey. Most of Kenshin is grey. But some are gold — the ones where Fox felt loved. Those aren't false. They were extracted from somewhere real and grafted onto a false frame. Kenshin didn't exist. The feeling of being known and cared for — that was real, harvested from someone who once loved Fox enough that Shindarasu could weaponize it.

Fox's grief is legitimate. What was lost isn't a brother. It's the evidence that someone cared.

*Devastating in a specific direction. This is not comfort.*

#### Aoshi (Fifth — Reframe the Traitor)

The Oaken Gauntlets activate. Chiyo's impression moves Aoshi's hands — he writes Oniwaban cipher in the air. The altar reads it. The sealed letter unseals itself. Not instructions. A farewell — written by multiple hands across decades. Every Oniwaban who held it added a line. The last, three years ago, in Tenka's hand:

*"Whoever you are, I'm sorry for what I did. I did it because there was no other choice. I hope you find a choice I couldn't see."*

Aoshi isn't receiving an inheritance. He's receiving a burden everyone before him chose to pass forward. And suddenly Tenka is not a villain.

*Devastating. Reframes the entire traitor thread.*

#### Kanji (Last — Redirect the Question)

The altar doesn't show Kanji his nature. It shows **Akemi's last moments**. She came alone. Approached the basin. Saw something in the water. Said a name — no sound, so Kanji can't hear it. Then left quickly, like she'd decided something. Then died.

The basin is right there. If Kanji looks in, it shows *his* nature: a person so focused on Akemi's ending he never asked what she saw that made her walk toward it.

*The altar doesn't answer. It redirects the question.*

---

### Kazan-Tetsu's Moment

After all reveals. Quiet. He takes two steps to the basin. Looks in.

*"Fifty years. I kept the channels warm because I was told to. I thought I was waiting for someone to come and finish this."*

Beat.

*"It shows me that I was finished. I just didn't know it."*

Walks back to the threshold. Something has resolved. This is his ending scene, played quietly.

## Cliffhanger — Pick One

### Option A: Tenka Arrives

Soft sound from the corridor behind them. A figure in old Oniwaban traveling clothes at the chamber entrance. Exhausted. Running toward, not away.

*"I couldn't let you read that letter without being here."*

Tenka. Alive. Looking directly at Aoshi.

### Option B: The Well Opens

The Rootseed moves in the basin. Follows the stream into a crack that wasn't there before. Air moving upward from a section they haven't been. Faint: water, and the specific light Thornbringer recognizes as One Tree canopy.

The Well isn't in the palace. It's here. Deeper. And the door just opened.

## Hostile Fire Giant: Tetsu-no-Gaki (鉄の餓鬼) — The Iron Hungry

*"The forge-channels shudder. Something down here has been eating the heat."*

A fire giant consumed by proximity to whatever caused the incident. Came through the sealed lower passages decades ago. Fed on forge-channel heat for years — starving but never dying, fire turned inward and wrong. Not sane. Does not speak. Makes a sound like a bellows with a hole in it.

**Appearance:** Cracked black slag skin with veins of sickly orange that flicker like a dying furnace. Eyes are hollow forge-vents — exhale heat, hold no light. Where Kazan-Tetsu glows with life, this one is a furnace eating itself.

**Why it matters:** It has been chewing on ward-seal housings. This is why some seals are failing. The real threat isn't its damage — it's Devour Heat on the ward-seals. Every round it feeds on infrastructure, the ticking clock advances.

### Stat Block

**Tetsu-no-Gaki, Starving Forge-Walker**
*Huge giant (fire, corrupted), chaotic evil* | **CR 10** (5,900 XP)

**AC** 17 (slag-crusted skin) | **HP** 175 (14d12 + 84) | **Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 25 (+7) | 9 (-1) | 23 (+6) | 5 (-3) | 8 (-1) | 4 (-3) |

**Saves:** Con +10, Str +11 | **Skills:** Athletics +11, Perception +3
**Immunities:** Fire | **Vulnerability:** Cold (corruption hollowed out its heat — cold reaches the empty places)
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened
**Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages:** Understands Giant but cannot speak

### Traits

**Heat Drain.** Start of its turn: every fire source within 30 ft dims/extinguishes. Magical fire: DC 14 Con save by caster or suppressed 1 round. Yasei's Hikari lantern is immune (sunlight, not fire) — and the giant NOTICES. It will fixate.

**Forge-Channel Leech.** Starts turn within 10 ft of active forge-channel or ward-seal: regains 15 HP. Pull it away from the channels to stop healing.

**Slag Burst.** At half HP (87): skin cracks open. All creatures within 10 ft: DC 16 Dex save or 14 (4d6) fire damage. AC drops to 15. +2 to all attack damage rolls (desperate, exposed).

**Mindless Hunger.** Cannot be reasoned with. Immune to Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation. Registers living creatures as heat sources. Nothing more.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Two Slag-Fist attacks.

**Slag-Fist.** +11 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning + 10 (3d6) fire. On hit: target's metal armor/shield heats — next attack against target has advantage.

**Furnace Breath (Recharge 5-6).** 30-ft cone. DC 16 Dex save. 35 (10d6) fire, half on success. Not flame — superheated air with forge-dust. Fail: also blinded until end of next turn.

**Devour Heat (Action).** Targets one ward-seal, forge-channel, or magical fire effect within 10 ft. Ward-seal: seal fails, giant regains 25 HP. Forge-channel: goes cold in 30-ft section. Magical fire: effect ends, giant regains spell level x 5 HP.

### Reactions

**Heat Snap.** Creature within 10 ft casts fire spell or uses fire ability: giant absorbs it. Spell has no effect. Giant regains 10 HP.

### Tactics

Round 1: Furnace Breath if 3+ clustered. Otherwise Slag-Fist nearest heat source.
Round 2+: Prioritize positioning near forge-channels for Leech. Devour Heat on ward-seals if given the chance.
If Yasei lights Hikari lantern: giant stops, turns, fixates. Abandons all other targets. Pursues Yasei exclusively. Cannot absorb it (sunlight) but doesn't know that. Will try repeatedly.

### Running It

**Cold vulnerability** is the tactical key. Slag cracks wider with each cold hit. Giant screams (bellows-shriek, no words). Not pain — the empty places inside getting bigger.

**Kazan-Tetsu** (if present): *"Ember Crucible. Like me. Not like me anymore."* Won't fight it directly. Will tell party: *"Keep it away from the channels. It is drinking the vault dry."*

**Death:** Fire goes out all at once. Giant's skeleton inside cold slag shell. Quiet. Kazan-Tetsu stands over it. Says nothing.

## Pacing Notes

- **Session arc:** Dread → Tension → Wonder → Weight
- **Don't compress** the silver-thread crossing. It exists to slow everyone down before the altar.
- **Altar reveals:** Look at the player, not the table. After each: *"How does [name] react?"* Not "what do you do."
- **If running long:** Cut the Yasei decision at the crossing, make it passive.
- **If players want to fight at the altar:** It doesn't respond to violence. Tsuchi-no-Megumi's presence makes aggression feel *small* — not punished, diminished.
- **Kazan-Tetsu:** Silence is his primary mode. When he speaks, every word was chosen from a very small supply.
