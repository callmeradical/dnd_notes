DM Tips
=======

Starting the Game
-----------------

-   Forego the Recap, in favor of…

-   When last we left our heroes…

-   Set a stage for the players...

    -   **Describe the sights, sounds, and smells of the environment.**

    -   **The actions of other people around (Notable NPCs).**

Rolls and When or When the aren’t Required
------------------------------------------

-   Example: Straight up numbers for people around - no check needed

-   Say yes more often, particularly if there is no mechanical
    > difference to the action.

-   Before using passive perception scores… When traveling, ask players
    > what they are doing, and rule accordingly. (adv/disadv for
    > focusing on task)

-   Roll Initiative to maintain balance in interactions

Pacing the Story
----------------

-   Encourage Role Play between characters, no need to make it hastey,
    > the players are having fun.

Three Pillars!
--------------

-   Exploration

-   **Social Interaction (More of this)**

-   Combat

Combat
------

-   Combat should last around 3-5 Rounds

-   Should still be challenging

-   Encounters, set a DC for sneak attacks or ambushes, Roll perception,
    > less than X is surprised.

-   If running theater of the mind, provide some additional elements in
    > the room that players can use.

-   Add minions for boss fights to enable the focus to be on the big bad
    > and provide minions for action economy challenges.
