# Swarm of Stinging Insects

## Swarm of Stinging Insects
*Medium swarm of tiny beasts, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 22 (5d8)  
**Speed** 20 ft., fly 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 3(-4) | 15(+2) | 10(+0) | 1(-5) | 7(-2) | 1(-5) |

**Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned  
**Senses** blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 8  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Swarm.** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny insect. The swarm can't regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.

### Actions

**Stings.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 0 ft., one target in the swarm's space. *Hit:* 10 (4d4) piercing damage, or 5 (2d4) piercing damage if the swarm has half of its hit points or fewer. The target must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 5 (2d4) poison damage and is poisoned until the end of its next turn.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1-2 swarms  
**Environment:** Humid jungle, stagnant water, disturbed hives  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random wildlife encounter (#4 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Aggressive swarm that attacks anything that disturbs their territory
- Becomes more agitated in hot, humid weather or during breeding season

**Mannerisms**

- Creates loud buzzing drone that can be heard from 100+ feet away
- Forms dark clouds that shift and pulse as the swarm moves
- Attracted to bright colors, strong scents, and rapid movement

**Tactics**

- Swarms engulf isolated targets, occupying their space
- Focuses on one target until they fall unconscious or flee
- Retreats if reduced to 5 hit points or less
- May pursue fleeing targets for 1d4 rounds before giving up
- Particularly aggressive if their nest or breeding ground is threatened

**Environment Notes**

- Often found near rotting vegetation, stagnant pools, or flowering plants
- Nests built in hollow trees, caves, or abandoned structures
- Leaves victims with painful, swollen welts that take days to heal
- May carry diseases: Constitution save DC 11 or contract jungle fever

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine encounter location: disturbed hive, camp, or territory intrusion
- Roll for number of swarms: 1 during day, 2 at dawn/dusk when most active
- Consider weather: heat and humidity make insects more aggressive (+2 damage)
- Check for party precautions: smoke, repellent, or covering exposed skin

**Opening Scene Options**

**Disturbed Hive:**
> *Your machete strikes what you thought was solid wood, but the hollow tree trunk erupts in an explosion of angry buzzing. Thousands of stinging insects pour out in a writhing black cloud, their drone rising to a painful intensity as they swirl around your group.*

**Camp Attack:**
> *The evening meal is interrupted by a low, ominous buzzing from the jungle darkness. The sound grows louder, and suddenly your torchlight illuminates a seething mass of insects descending toward your camp like a living storm cloud.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Swarming Attack**
- Swarm moves to engulf the most exposed target
- Uses full movement to reach and occupy target's space
- Prioritizes characters without armor or those carrying torches/lanterns
- **Perception Check (DC 13):** Hear approaching swarm before attack

**Phase 2: Persistent Assault**
- Continues attacking same target until they fall or flee
- Additional swarms (if present) target other party members
- Poison damage begins to accumulate on repeated stings
- **Constitution Check (DC 12):** Resist disease from infected stings

**Phase 3: Retreat or Dispersal**
- Swarms retreat when reduced to 5 HP or less
- May pursue fleeing targets for short distance
- Fire, smoke, or area damage can drive off healthy swarms
- **Survival Check (DC 11):** Create smoke or repellent to deter insects

**Environmental Tactics**

**Swarm Advantages**
- Can move through any opening, making cover less effective
- Resistance to physical damage from weapons
- Difficult to target with single-target spells and abilities
- Can occupy multiple creatures' spaces simultaneously

**Party Countermeasures**
- Area effect spells and abilities are highly effective
- Fire damage causes swarms to retreat immediately
- Smoke from torches or campfires provides partial concealment
- Full body coverage reduces sting attacks by half

**Skill Integration**
- **Medicine (DC 10):** Treat poison stings and reduce swelling
- **Nature (DC 12):** Identify aggressive insect behavior and predict movement
- **Survival (DC 13):** Create natural repellents or find shelter from swarms
- **Sleight of Hand (DC 14):** Apply salve or medicine while being attacked

**Disease: Jungle Fever**
- **Incubation Period:** 1d4 days
- **Symptoms:** Fever, weakness, hallucinations
- **Effect:** Disadvantage on Constitution saves, -2 to maximum hit points per day
- **Recovery:** Constitution save DC 12 each day, two successes removes disease

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Swarms Defeated**
- Sting welts cause discomfort for 2d4 days (no mechanical effect)
- Honey or larvae from destroyed hive worth 1d4 × 5 gp
- Local reputation as skilled jungle survivors
- Other insects may be attracted to the scent of dead swarms

**If Swarms Driven Off**
- Area remains dangerous for insects, potential future encounters
- Swarms may return in larger numbers if territory threatened
- Party gains knowledge of effective countermeasures
- May discover the nest location for potential treasure or resources

**Story Integration**
- Disturbed hive indicates recent passage of other travelers
- Unusual insect aggression might signal environmental changes
- Infected party members provide ongoing challenge
- Local guides may know traditional remedies and treatments

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Single swarm, reduce poison damage
- **Harder:** 3+ swarms, or add disease transmission
- **Environmental:** During rainstorm (swarms more aggressive) or at night (harder to see)
