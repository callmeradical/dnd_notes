# Mercenary Salvage Crew

## Mercenary Captain
*Medium humanoid (human), neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (Chain Shirt)  
**Hit Points** 52 (8d8+16)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15(+2) | 16(+3) | 14(+2) | 14(+2) | 12(+1) | 15(+2) |

**Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +5, Wis +3  
**Skills** Athletics +4, Deception +4, Insight +3, Persuasion +4  
**Senses** passive Perception 11  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The captain makes two melee attacks or two ranged attacks.

**Rapier.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) piercing damage.

**Dagger.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4+3) piercing damage.

**Light Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) piercing damage.

### Reactions

**Parry.** The captain adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the captain must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 Mercenary Captain + 1d6+2 Mercenaries  
**Environment:** Salvage sites, ruins, abandoned settlements, trade routes  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#7 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- **Captain:** Pragmatic businessman who views everything through profit/loss lens
- **Mercenaries:** Professional soldiers who work for steady pay and fair treatment
- All value practical results over idealistic principles

**Ideals**

- Profit: Every situation should generate income for the crew
- Professionalism: Complete contracts honestly and deliver promised services
- Efficiency: Accomplish objectives with minimum risk and maximum gain

**Bonds**

- **Captain:** Responsible for crew's financial security and physical safety
- **Mercenaries:** Loyal to captain who provides consistent work and fair shares
- Crew operates as business partnership with clear profit-sharing agreements

**Flaws**

- **Greedy:** Sometimes takes excessive risks for valuable salvage
- **Amoral:** Willing to work for questionable employers if price is right
- **Competitive:** Views other salvage crews as rivals to be outmaneuvered

**Mannerisms**

- **Captain:** Constantly calculates costs, values, and profit margins
- **Mercenaries:** Treat dangerous work as routine job requiring professional competence
- Crew maintains business-like efficiency even in casual conversations

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Contact:**
> "Well, hello there! Captain Voss of the Iron Hawks Salvage Company. You wouldn't happen to be here about the contract, would you? No? Well, perhaps we can find some mutually profitable arrangement."

**When Negotiating:**
> "Look, we're professionals—we do dangerous work for fair pay. You need something recovered, cleared out, or 'relocated,' we can discuss terms. Our rates are competitive and our discretion is absolute."

**When Competing:**
> "Nothing personal, friends, but business is business. This site's already claimed by prior contract. You're welcome to bid on the next one, assuming there is a next one."

### Tactics

**Initial Approach**
- Captain approaches with friendly but cautious business demeanor
- Assesses party's potential as clients, competitors, or threats
- Offers professional services or proposes collaborative arrangements

**Combat Approach**
- Captain coordinates crew positioning and target selection
- Uses crossbows from advantageous positions before closing to melee
- Focuses on disabling rather than killing potential business partners
- Retreats strategically if combat becomes unprofitable

**Retreat Conditions**
- Withdraws if casualties exceed potential profit from engagement
- May offer negotiated settlement to avoid further losses
- Captain sacrifices equipment but not crew lives for salvage

### Knowledge

**What They Know**
- Location of valuable salvage sites and ruins throughout region
- Current market prices for recovered goods and services
- Other salvage crews, their capabilities, and recent activities
- Potential employers and their typical contract requirements

**What They Offer**
- Professional salvage and recovery services
- Escort missions for valuable cargo or dangerous routes
- Information about regional dangers and opportunities
- Equipment and supplies at competitive prices

**What They Seek**
- Lucrative salvage contracts and recovery missions
- Information about valuable sites or lost cargo
- Business partnerships with reliable professional groups
- Access to markets and buyers for recovered goods

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 1d6+2 for crew size (3-8 mercenaries plus captain)
- Determine crew's current mission: active salvage, scouting, or between contracts
- Note any valuable salvage or equipment party might be carrying
- Consider crew's financial situation: flush with recent success vs. desperate for work

**Opening Scene Options**

**Active Salvage Operation:**
> *The ruins ahead buzz with organized activity. A professional salvage crew works systematically through the debris, their captain coordinating operations with practiced efficiency. When they notice your approach, hands move casually toward weapons, but their leader steps forward with a business smile. "Captain Voss, Iron Hawks Salvage. You folks wouldn't be here to contest our claim, would you?"*

**Mobile Patrol:**
> *A well-equipped group approaches on the jungle path—mercenaries by their bearing, but cleaner and better organized than typical sellswords. Their captain raises a hand in greeting. "Good day, travelers! Captain Voss of the Iron Hawks. We're surveying potential salvage sites in this area. Perhaps we can be of mutual assistance?"*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Business Assessment (Social Primary)**
- Captain evaluates party as potential clients, partners, or competition
- Offers professional services and discusses current market conditions
- Assesses party's equipment and capabilities for future reference
- **Insight Check (DC 12):** Recognize captain is appraising party's wealth and potential
- **Investigation Check (DC 13):** Notice crew's professional equipment and organization
- **Persuasion Check (DC 12):** Establish friendly business relationship

**Phase 2: Commercial Negotiation**
- If party seems profitable, captain proposes various business arrangements
- May offer information, services, or partnership opportunities
- Becomes competitive if party interferes with crew's current operations
- **Deception Check (DC 14):** Conceal valuable items from captain's assessment
- **Intimidation Check (DC 14):** Attempt to drive crew away from salvage site
- **History Check (DC 11):** Know regional salvage markets and typical contract terms

**Phase 3: Partnership, Competition, or Avoidance**
- Successful negotiations lead to profitable business relationships
- Failed diplomacy may result in competitive rivalry or territorial disputes
- Combat occurs only if direct business interests are threatened

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Offering legitimate business opportunities or contracts
- Demonstrating professional competence and reliable payment history
- Sharing information about valuable salvage sites or market opportunities
- Showing respect for crew's expertise and professional reputation

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Attempting to claim crew's established salvage sites
- Criticizing mercenary profession or questioning crew's competence
- Making threats without ability to back them up
- Wasting captain's time with unprofitable discussions

**Business Relationship Dynamics**
- Captain prioritizes crew safety and long-term profitability
- Professional reputation more important than short-term gains
- Willing to collaborate with reliable partners for mutual benefit
- Competitive but not hostile toward other professional groups

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Profitable long-term business partnership with crew
- Access to salvage network and market intelligence
- Preferred customer status for future services and contracts
- Possible joint ventures for large-scale operations

**Success**
- Fair business arrangements for specific services or information
- Professional courtesy and non-interference agreements
- Market rate pricing for crew services

**Failure**
- Higher prices for any services requested
- Competitive rivalry for salvage sites and contracts
- Limited cooperation despite professional courtesy

**Critical Failure**
- Active business competition and potential territorial conflicts
- Crew spreads word about party's unreliability
- Exclusion from professional salvage networks

**Combat Considerations**

**Professional Tactics**
- Captain coordinates crew using efficient military-style commands
- Uses terrain and cover to minimize casualties while maintaining objectives
- Focuses on disabling opponents to preserve potential future business
- Organized retreat if situation becomes unprofitable

**Equipment Advantages**
- Well-maintained weapons and armor from successful contracts
- Professional-grade tools and equipment for specialized operations
- Superior organization and coordination compared to typical bandits
- Financial resources to replace lost equipment quickly

**Business-First Mentality**
- Avoids unnecessary combat that reduces crew effectiveness
- Negotiates surrender or withdrawal when combat proves costly
- Prioritizes crew survival over salvage or contract completion
- May offer compensation to avoid prolonged conflict

**Aftermath Scenarios**

**If Combat Occurred**
- Professional salvage community learns about dangerous party
- Other crews become more cautious or aggressive in future encounters
- Captain's crew may seek revenge or compensation for losses
- Party gains reputation as unreliable business partners

**If Resolved Peacefully**
- Access to professional salvage services and market information
- Potential ongoing business relationship with reliable crew
- Introduction to other professional mercenary and salvage groups
- Reputation as reasonable people to work with in dangerous situations

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Crew has information about specific salvage relevant to party's quest
- Joint salvage mission requires party's specialized skills
- Competing crews create ongoing territorial conflict
- Captain offers long-term employment for dangerous but profitable work

**Services Available**
- **Salvage Operations:** 100-300 gp per day depending on danger and complexity
- **Escort Services:** 50-150 gp per day based on route and cargo value
- **Site Clearing:** Professional removal of monsters or hazards from valuable locations
- **Equipment Sales:** Professional-grade tools and weapons at 10% markup

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce crew size, make captain more cooperative
- **Harder:** Add specialized crew members or rival salvage competition
- **Environmental:** Encounter at contested salvage site with time pressure
- **Economic:** Complex business negotiation with multiple competing interests
