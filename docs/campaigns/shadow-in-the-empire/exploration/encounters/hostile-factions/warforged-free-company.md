# Warforged Free Company

## Warforged Soldier
*Medium construct, lawful neutral*

**Armor Class** 16 (Natural Armor, Shield)  
**Hit Points** 30 (4d8+12)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16(+3) | 11(+0) | 16(+3) | 10(+0) | 10(+0) | 10(+0) |

**Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +2  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Condition Immunities** disease, exhaustion, poisoned  
**Senses** passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Constructed Resilience.** The warforged has advantage on saving throws against being poisoned, and it has resistance to poison damage.

**Integrated Protection.** The warforged's AC includes its proficiency bonus.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The warforged makes two weapon attacks.

**Longsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10+3) slashing damage if used with two hands.

**Javelin.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing damage.

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 2d4+1 Warforged Soldiers  
**Environment:** Patrol routes, abandoned outposts, strategic locations  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#5 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Disciplined military units operating with precise coordination
- Question authority of organic beings over construct autonomy
- Value freedom and self-determination above traditional loyalties

**Ideals**

- Liberty: All constructed beings deserve freedom from organic oppression
- Order: Military discipline and organization provide structure and purpose
- Equality: Constructs are equal to organic life, not property or tools

**Bonds**

- Sworn brotherhood with fellow warforged who served in same unit
- Loyalty to the concept of construct independence and dignity
- Commitment to protecting other freed warforged from re-enslavement

**Flaws**

- Rigid: Difficulty adapting to situations outside military experience
- Suspicious: Distrust organics who might want to reclaim them as property
- Inflexible: Black-and-white thinking about freedom and slavery issues

**Mannerisms**

- Speak in clipped, military terminology and formal address
- Move with precise, coordinated formations even during casual interaction
- Unconsciously polish and maintain weapons during conversations

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Challenge:**
> "Halt and identify yourselves. State your position on construct autonomy and your relationship to House Cannith or other creation forges."

**When Testing Loyalty:**
> "Many organics claim to support our freedom while secretly planning our return to bondage. Prove your intentions are genuine."

**In Combat:**
> "For the liberation of our people! No warforged shall know chains again!"

### Tactics

**Initial Approach**
- Form military formation to display discipline and readiness
- Question party about their stance on warforged freedom and rights
- Test party's knowledge of recent Treaty of Thronehold provisions

**Combat Approach**
- Fight as coordinated unit with established roles and formations
- Use shield walls and javelin volleys before closing to melee
- Protect wounded comrades and maintain battle lines
- Focus fire on obvious leaders or spellcasters

**Retreat Conditions**
- Withdraw in good order if facing overwhelming force
- Never abandon wounded comrades if rescue is possible
- Retreat to alert other warforged cells of hostile organics

### Knowledge

**What They Know**
- Locations of other warforged communities and safe houses
- House Cannith activities and attempts to reclaim warforged
- Recent political developments regarding construct rights
- Military intelligence from their service in the Last War

**What They Seek**
- Information about warforged persecution or liberation efforts
- Safe passage for construct refugees fleeing oppressive situations
- Allies willing to support warforged independence movement
- Resources to establish permanent free construct settlements

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 2d4+1 for unit size (3-9 warforged soldiers)
- Determine unit's current mission: patrol, escort, or reconnaissance
- Note any party members with warforged connections or House Cannith ties
- Consider recent news about construct rights and freedom issues

**Opening Scene Options**

**Military Checkpoint:**
> *The sound of synchronized marching echoes through the jungle before you see them—a patrol of warforged in formation, their metal bodies scarred from countless battles. Their leader raises a hand, bringing the unit to an immediate halt. "Halt and identify yourselves," comes the mechanical voice. "State your position on construct autonomy."*

**Liberation Patrol:**
> *Around a bend in the overgrown road, you encounter an unusual sight—a group of warforged escorting what appears to be recently freed construct laborers. The soldiers' bearing is proud but watchful, and their leader steps forward with hand on weapon. "These constructs travel under our protection. Do you challenge their right to freedom?"*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Ideological Assessment (Social Primary)**
- Warforged question party's stance on construct rights and freedom
- Test party's knowledge of recent political developments
- Assess threat level based on House affiliations and stated intentions
- **History Check (DC 12):** Know details of Treaty of Thronehold construct provisions
- **Insight Check (DC 13):** Recognize warforged are testing party loyalty, not seeking conflict
- **Persuasion Check (DC 14):** Demonstrate genuine support for construct independence

**Phase 2: Trust Building or Confrontation**
- If party shows support, warforged become cautiously cooperative
- If party appears hostile to construct rights, tension escalates
- May request party assistance with liberation or protection missions
- **Deception Check (DC 16):** Lie about supporting construct freedom (difficult due to warforged perception)
- **Intimidation Check (DC 15):** Threaten warforged (likely to provoke combat response)

**Phase 3: Alliance or Combat**
- Successful diplomacy may lead to information sharing and mutual aid
- Failed negotiations or hostile action results in disciplined military assault
- Combat is last resort but executed with military precision if necessary

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Expressing genuine support for warforged freedom and rights
- Sharing information about House Cannith activities or construct persecution
- Offering assistance with liberation efforts or safe passage
- Demonstrating knowledge of warforged culture and military history

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Any connection to House Cannith or other creation forges
- Referring to warforged as property, tools, or artificial beings
- Expressing support for return to pre-war construct slavery
- Threatening to report warforged location to authorities

**Key Cultural Notes**
- Warforged value directness and military courtesy in communication
- Freedom and self-determination are core values, not negotiable
- View themselves as soldiers fighting for civil rights, not criminals
- Deeply suspicious of organics but willing to work with proven allies

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Full cooperation including safe passage and area intelligence
- Invitation to visit warforged settlement or meet with leadership
- Possible recruitment for construct liberation missions
- Access to warforged network for information and assistance

**Success**
- Neutral relations, no hostility but limited cooperation
- Basic information about area dangers and safe travel routes
- Respect for party's stated position on construct rights

**Failure**
- Continued suspicion and surveillance by warforged patrols
- No assistance offered, must rely on own resources
- May face additional scrutiny at future encounters

**Critical Failure**
- Combat with disciplined military unit
- Word spreads through warforged network about hostile organics
- Future construct encounters become significantly more difficult

**Combat Tactics Deep Dive**

**Military Formation Fighting**
- Establish shield wall with javelin support from rear ranks
- Coordinate attacks to focus fire on single targets
- Use tactical movement to maintain formation integrity
- Protect wounded and maintain unit cohesion

**Constructed Advantages**
- No need for food, water, or rest during extended operations
- Immunity to poison, disease, and exhaustion effects
- Natural armor provides consistent AC without equipment dependency
- Resistance to environmental effects that might hinder organics

**Unit Coordination**
- Clear chain of command with designated leader
- Established battle signals and tactical procedures
- Willingness to sacrifice for unit survival and mission completion
- Organized retreat and regrouping if combat goes poorly

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Combat Occurred**
- Other warforged units learn of hostile organics in area
- Construct liberation network becomes less trusting of party
- Possible revenge attacks or increased patrols in region
- Party gains reputation as construct enemies among certain factions

**If Resolved Peacefully**
- Warforged remember party as potential allies or neutral parties
- Access to construct underground railroad for information
- Possible future missions involving construct liberation
- Positive reputation among freed construct communities

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Warforged unit guards access to construct settlement party needs to visit
- Liberation mission requires party assistance to succeed
- Information about party's quest held by warforged leadership
- House Cannith operations in area create conflict requiring mediation

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce numbers, make them more willing to trust party
- **Harder:** Add warforged officer with additional abilities
- **Environmental:** Encounter at strategic chokepoint or fortified position
- **Social:** Complex political situation requiring careful navigation
