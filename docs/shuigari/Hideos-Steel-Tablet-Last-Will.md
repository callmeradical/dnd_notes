# Master Hideo's Last Will and Testament - The Steel Tablet

**Item:** Hideo's Steel Tablet of Final Instructions  
**Bearer:** Kazuki Takemura (The Twice-Cursed Samurai)  
**Significance:** Last will, mission directive, and list of trusted allies  
**Revelation:** The party is named on it - Hideo knew about them before he died

---

## THE TABLET - PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

### Appearance

**Material:** 
- Folded steel (tamahagane) - same material used for soulsteel blades
- Six layers forged together, creating subtle wave pattern
- 8 inches tall, 5 inches wide, half-inch thick
- Surprisingly heavy for its size (3 pounds)

**Surface:**
- Polished to mirror finish on both sides
- Hideo's maker's mark stamped into top center (mountain and anvil)
- Text inscribed with acid etching - permanent, cannot be altered
- Characters filled with silver inlay for contrast
- Edges beveled, smooth - no sharp corners

**Condition:**
- Immaculate despite 3 years of carrying
- Kazuki cleans it daily (ritual, remembrance)
- No rust, no tarnish - soulsteel never degrades
- Warm to the touch when held (resonates with living steel)

**Carrying:**
- Wrapped in silk cloth (indigo with Hideo's mark)
- Secured to Kazuki's obi with leather cord
- He touches it frequently (unconscious gesture, comfort)
- Never lets it out of his sight

## THE INSCRIPTION - FRONT SIDE

### Master Hideo's Final Words (Read Aloud)

> The front of the tablet bears Hideo's maker's mark at the top - a mountain with an anvil at its base, struck through with a single vertical line. Below it, in elegant characters etched deep and filled with silver:
>
> **"The Last Instructions of Master Hideo Nakata, Smith of the Mountain, Guardian of the First Forge"**
>
> *Written on the Night of the Broken Moon, in my final hours of clarity*
>
> *To whoever finds this: My time ends not by age but by necessity. The Obsidian Circle seeks what I know, and I will not let my knowledge serve their unmaking. I have asked Kazuki Takemura to grant me the mercy of a swift end, and he has agreed. Judge him not - he acts on my plea, not his desire.*
>
> *What lies beneath Takashima must remain sealed. The Circle seeks the First Chain - the binding that holds Tsurogami in its prison. They will use the boy heir's blood as the key. They will corrupt what should remain pure. They will unmake what should remain whole.*
>
> *I have seen this in steel and flame. The forge never lies.*
>
> *To those who would stand against this darkness, I leave my final work: a list of names. These souls will arrive when needed. Trust them. Guide them. Give them what they need to succeed where I have failed.*
>
> *The furnace remembers the steel it forged. Let them remember me.*
>
> **- Hideo Nakata, Smith**
> **Year 2302, Autumn Moon**

## THE REVELATION SCENE

### Kazuki's Dramatic Reveal (After his story, before the proposal)

**Modified Scene Flow:**

After Kazuki explains his two curses and Hideo's death, add this moment:

> Kazuki's hand moves to his obi. From within the folds of cloth, he produces something wrapped in indigo silk bearing Hideo's mark.
>
> "Before Master Hideo asked me to end his life, he gave me this. His final work. Not a blade - those days were past him. This."
>
> He unwraps the silk carefully, almost reverently. Lamplight catches on polished steel.
>
> A tablet. Perfectly rectangular. Mirror-bright. Inscribed with silver-filled characters that seem to glow in the tea house's warm light.
>
> "His last will and testament. Written the night he died. The night I..." (His voice catches) "...honored his final request."
>
> He places it on the table between you. You see Hideo's maker's mark. You see the title. You see the date.
>
> Three years ago. The night of Master Hideo's death.
>
> "Read it," Kazuki says quietly. "The front first. Then the back. And then... then you'll understand why I came to find you specifically."

**Allow Players to Read/Study:**

Let them actually read the inscriptions above (or paraphrase/summarize as you read aloud). The key moment is when they turn to the back and see the list of names.

### The Impact Moment (Read Aloud)

> You turn the tablet over. At the top: Kazuki's mission, Hideo's final instructions to the Twice-Cursed.
>
> And below that...
>
> Names.
>
> Twenty names. Twenty souls Hideo deemed critical to protecting what lies beneath Takashima.
>
> And at the very top, listed first, designated "The Six Flames"...

>

## PLAYER REACTIONS & RESPONSES

### Common Questions & Kazuki's Answers

**"How did Hideo know about us?"**
> "He never explained fully. Said the forge showed him visions. Said steel speaks to those who listen deeply enough. Said he saw 'six flames approaching a mountain of iron.' Whether it was prophecy, divine insight, or simply the wisdom of a dying master... I don't know. But the tablet doesn't lie. Steel cannot lie."

**"Why are WE on this list?"**
> "You tell me. You're the ones who came to Takashima despite the warnings. You're the ones who rescued Kazuo when others would have left him. You're the ones sitting in a cursed tea house after meeting a twice-cursed ronin, still asking questions instead of running. Perhaps Master Hideo saw in you what I'm beginning to see: people who don't abandon what's right just because it's hard."

**"Who are the other names?"**
> "Allies. Some you've already met." (Gestures to Kadei, Minari, Kazuo on list) "Others you'll need to find. Some..." (Touches Lady Hana and Tenka's names) "...are warnings. And some..." (Touches Master Chen and Yuki) "...are ghosts who deserve justice."

**"Can we see the tablet / hold it?"**
> "Yes." (He offers it) "Master Hideo meant for you to see it. To know he trusted you before you even knew he existed. To understand this isn't random chance - you were called here. By a dead smith's final vision. By steel and flame. By whatever force moves heroes to where they're needed most."

**"Why didn't you show us this immediately?"**
> "Would you have believed me? A cursed ronin appears with a steel tablet claiming a dead master prophesied your arrival three years ago? You would have thought me mad. No. First, I needed to tell you my story. Show you my burden. Give you context. Now... now you understand what this means. What HE meant for you to do."

## MECHANICAL & NARRATIVE IMPLICATIONS

### What This Changes

**Trust Level:**
- This isn't just a job offer from a desperate ronin
- This is a DESTINY given by Master Hideo himself
- The party was specifically chosen, foreseen, trusted
- Makes the mission deeply personal

**Connections:**
- Every name on the list is a potential ally
- Some they've already met (validation of their path)
- Some they'll need to find (quest hooks)
- Some are warnings (creates tension)

**Player Agency:**
- Despite prophecy, they still choose
- Hideo trusted them to make the right choice
- But they're not forced - free will matters
- Refusal is still possible (but harder emotionally)

**Fox's Connection:**
- Master Hideo knew about the implanted memories
- Mentions "lost master" - validates Fox's grief
- Makes Hideo's death even more meaningful
- Fox was NAMED by the smith they mourn

**Campaign Weight:**
- Raises stakes significantly
- Not just helping a ronin - fulfilling a dead master's vision
- Every other name becomes important
- The list provides structure for Arc 2

## THE TABLET AS QUEST TRACKER

### How to Use This in Play

**Visual Aid:**
- Print or display the list for players
- Let them cross off names as they meet people
- Add notes about each person's status
- Track who they can trust vs. who's compromised

**Investigation Hook:**
- "Why did Hideo warn against Lady Hana specifically?"
- "Who is Tenka and why is he dangerous?"
- "What happened to Master Chen and Yuki?"

**Ally Recruitment:**
- Each name is a potential quest to recruit
- Some are easy (Kaoru, Mama Tora)
- Some require proving themselves (General Kenta)
- Some are complicated (High Priestess Amaya)

**Tragedy Tracker:**
- Names crossed out if allies fall
- Names moved to "Lost" if they're corrupted
- Each loss hurts more because Hideo CHOSE them

## GM NOTES: HANDLING THE REVEAL

### Pacing Tips

1. **Build Up Slowly:** Don't rush the revelation
2. **Let Players Process:** Give them time to react to each detail
3. **Individual Moments:** Address each PC's entry separately
4. **Emotional Weight:** This should feel HUGE
5. **Kazuki's Reverence:** He treats the tablet as holy relic

### Player Agency Reminder

Despite prophecy:
- They can still refuse
- Destiny isn't compulsion
- Choice matters MORE because they were chosen
- Refusal would be valid (but narratively costly)

### If Party is Skeptical

> "I understand doubt. I doubted too. But answer me this: How did a man three years dead know your names? Your burdens? Your specific struggles? How did he know you'd arrive in Takashima at this exact time? Coincidence? Perhaps. But steel doesn't forge coincidences. Steel forges truth."
