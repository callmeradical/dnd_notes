# The Iron Rest Inn - Cursed Street Scene

**Location:** Middle Terrace, Takashima City  
**District:** East of Smiths' Quarter, near the abandoned warehouse district  
**Reputation:** "The Cursed Street" - locals avoid after dark  
**Current State:** Nearly empty, struggling to survive, haunted by yokai  
**Scene Type:** Eerie downtime, celebration in isolation, yokai investigation hook

---

## THE STREET: WHISPERS AND WARNINGS

### Approaching the Inn (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> The streets grow quieter as you walk east from the bustling Smiths' Quarter. The forge-song fades behind you. The lamps grow fewer. And then... you're here.
>
> This street feels wrong.
>
> It's not abandoned - there are buildings, shops, homes. But they're... closed. Shuttered. Silent. Even though the sun hasn't fully set, every window is dark, every door barred. You see chalk marks on doorframes (Religion DC 12: protective wards against spirits). Prayer strips flutter from eaves, their ink faded by weather and time.
>
> The cobblestones are clean - too clean. No refuse, no debris, as if even the wind won't linger here. Your footsteps echo strangely, carrying farther than they should.
>
> And at the end of this street, standing alone: The Iron Rest Inn.
>
> It's the only building with lights burning. Three stories of dark wood and stone, it looks solid enough, but... worn. Paint peeling. Roof tiles cracked. One shutter hangs at an angle, creaking in the breeze that shouldn't exist here. The sign above the door shows an iron bed frame, but someone has scratched marks across it - claw marks? Or desperate attempts to warn travelers away?
>
> Through the windows, you see lamplight. Movement. Someone is inside.
>
> As you approach, a voice calls from a nearby house - an old woman's face appears briefly in a second-story window: "Don't stay there after dark! The tea house weeps!" Then her shutters slam closed.

### Entering the Inn (Read Aloud - 3 minutes)

> The door opens with a groan that sounds almost relieved, as if grateful someone finally entered.
>
> The common room stretches before you, and your first thought is: this place was built for crowds. Twenty tables at least, enough to seat sixty people easily. A massive hearth dominates one wall, fire crackling within - far too large a fire for so few guests. The ceiling is high, supported by thick beams, and hanging from these beams are... things.
>
> (Perception DC 10: Iron horseshoes, bells, mirrors, protective charms - all facing the door and windows. Someone has warded this place heavily.)
>
> But the room is empty.
>
> Not completely - in the far corner, an elderly merchant sits alone, eating soup in silence. He doesn't look up when you enter. Near the hearth, two younger travelers huddle together, speaking in whispers, their packs at their feet as if ready to flee. That's it. Three guests in a room built for sixty.
>
> The tables are clean but dust has settled in the corners. The floor is swept but the rushes are old. Everything is maintained, but barely, desperately, by someone who refuses to let this place die.
>
> And then she emerges from the kitchen.

## THE INNKEEPER: MAMA TORA

### First Appearance (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> She's a mountain of a woman - not fat, but solid, built like the dwarven smiths in the quarter you just left. Perhaps sixty years old, her hair iron-gray and pulled back in a severe bun. She wears a stained apron over practical clothes, and her forearms are scarred with old burns - forge marks, or kitchen accidents, it's hard to say.
>
> But it's her face that catches you. Weathered, lined, set in an expression of stubborn defiance. Her eyes are dark and sharp, and when they fix on you, you feel assessed, measured, and judged in the space of a heartbeat.
>
> She's carrying a wooden tray with sake cups, and she moves with the efficiency of someone who once served dozens of patrons and refuses to admit those days are gone.
>
> "Welcome to the Iron Rest," she says, her voice rough as old leather but warm underneath. "You'll be wanting rooms, I expect. I have plenty." A bitter smile touches her lips. "Twenty-three rooms. All empty except for the three you see here, and they'll be gone by morning like everyone else."
>
> She sets the tray down with a solid thunk. "But you're here now, and you look thirsty. So sit. Drink. Eat if you're hungry. And if you're smart, you'll be gone before the temple bells ring midnight."
>
> She doesn't say why. She doesn't have to.

### Physical Description - Detail Reference

**Build:** Stocky, muscular, broad-shouldered - former blacksmith's daughter  
**Height:** 5'4" but seems larger due to presence  
**Age:** 58 years old  
**Hair:** Iron-gray, pulled back tight, a few escaped strands  
**Face:** Weathered, lined with hard years, prominent nose (broken once, healed crooked), strong jaw  
**Eyes:** Dark brown, almost black, deeply set, always watching  
**Hands:** Large, calloused, scarred with old burns, strong grip  
**Voice:** Rough, deep for a woman, carries across rooms, no-nonsense  
**Clothing:** Practical work kimono (faded indigo), stained apron, sturdy sandals, rolled sleeves  
**Notable:** Small hammer pendant around her neck (her father's, she never removes it)

### Personality & Mannerisms

**Surface:**
- Gruff, direct, no tolerance for nonsense
- Efficient movements, wastes no motion
- Speaks plainly, sometimes bluntly
- Treats customers like they're her responsibility whether they like it or not

**Underneath:**
- Fiercely stubborn (won't abandon her inn despite the curse)
- Protective of the few guests she has
- Lonely but would never admit it
- Deeply angry at the yokai/Circle/whatever caused this
- Misses her husband desperately (he died 3 years ago, just before the hauntings began)

**Mannerisms:**
- Wipes hands on apron when thinking
- Stands with arms crossed when listening
- Points with her chin rather than her hand
- Pours drinks with aggressive precision
- Hums old forge-songs under her breath when working
- Checks door locks obsessively as midnight approaches

**Speech Patterns:**
- Short sentences, gets to the point
- Uses forge/smith metaphors ("That's dross and you know it")
- Calls people by what they do, not their names ("You, warrior" or "Masked one")
- Swears colorfully when provoked
- Never apologizes, even when she should

## MAMA TORA - CHARACTER PROFILE

### Background

**Full Name:** Tora Kaneshiro (鐘城 虎 - "Tiger of the Iron Castle")

**History:**
- Born in Takashima, daughter of Master Smith Genji Kaneshiro
- Learned smithing but was better at business than metalwork
- Married Kuro, a Mountain Guard soldier, 40 years ago
- They bought the Iron Rest together 35 years ago
- Built it into the most popular inn in the Middle Terrace
- Three children, all moved away to other prefectures (she's bitter about this)
- Husband Kuro died 3 years ago defending a caravan from bandits
- Six months after his death, the hauntings began
- Business collapsed over 2 years as the "curse" spread
- Now operates at massive loss, refuses to close or move

**Why She Stays:**
- This inn is her husband's legacy
- Her children want her to sell and move in with them - she refuses out of spite and pride
- Believes the yokai/curse can be defeated
- Has nowhere else to go even if she wanted to (which she doesn't)
- Stubborn as iron (her father's daughter)

### What She Knows

**About the Curse:**
- Started 2.5 years ago, six months after Kuro died
- Guests hear weeping at night - a woman's voice, heartbroken
- Some see a figure in white wandering the halls
- Objects move on their own - cups fall, doors open
- No one has been hurt, but everyone is terrified
- Hauntings worst between midnight and dawn
- Locals now call this "The Weeping Street"
- Several priests have attempted exorcisms - all failed

**About the Yokai (What she suspects but won't say unless pressed):**
- She thinks it's connected to the tea house two doors down
- That tea house closed suddenly 3 years ago (same time Kuro died)
- The owner's daughter vanished the same night
- Official story: she ran away with a lover
- Tora suspects: she was murdered, possibly by Circle agents
- The weeping sounds like a young woman calling for help

**About the Circle:**
- Knows they operate in Takashima
- Suspects they're involved in the disappearance
- Has seen suspicious figures on this street at night
- Doesn't know specifics but knows something evil happened here
- Refuses to leave because running away feels like admitting defeat

**About the Neighborhood:**
- This was once a prosperous street
- Mix of craftsmen, merchants, small tea houses
- Everything collapsed after the hauntings began
- Most buildings now abandoned or barely occupied
- City guard won't patrol here at night anymore
- Even Circle agents avoid this street (the curse is that strong)

### What She Wants

**Immediate:**
- Customers who won't flee at the first creak
- To serve a full house again
- To prove she can still run this inn

**Deep:**
- The hauntings to stop
- To understand what happened to the tea house girl
- Revenge against whoever caused this
- For her husband to be proud of her
- To not die alone in an empty building

**Will Pay For:**
- Investigation of the curse (can offer 100 gold, it's all she has)
- Exorcism if the party has the skills
- Information about what really happened 3 years ago
- Protection during the worst of the hauntings (midnight-dawn)

### Relationships

**The Elderly Merchant (Old Hiroshi):**
- Stays here out of loyalty to Tora
- Was friends with her husband Kuro
- Too poor to afford better lodging anyway
- Knows he might die here but doesn't care

**The Two Young Travelers:**
- Arrived today, didn't know about the curse
- Plan to leave at first light
- Tora's bitter about this but understands

**The Neighborhood:**
- Most have abandoned the street
- Those who remain pity Tora but won't help
- Some blame her for not closing (as if she's keeping the curse alive)
- Old Woman Next Door occasionally brings her food

**Her Children:**
- Three children, all living in other prefectures
- They beg her to close the inn and move
- She refuses, sees it as betrayal of Kuro's memory
- They've stopped visiting (too painful to argue)
- She misses them but is too proud to admit it

## THE SAKE SCENE

### Setting Up (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> Mama Tora returns from the kitchen carrying a wooden tray laden with sake and food. She moves with practiced efficiency, setting down:
>
> - A large ceramic sake bottle, steam still rising from its mouth (it's been warmed)
> - Six small ceramic cups, each slightly different (survivors from once-matching sets)
> - A plate of grilled fish, charred at the edges, still sizzling
> - Pickled vegetables in a small wooden bowl
> - Rice balls wrapped in seaweed, perfectly formed
> - A dish of miso paste for dipping
>
> "This is the good sake," she says, pouring the first cup with steady hands. "Not the swill I serve drunks. You look like you've earned something decent." 
>
> The liquid is clear with a faint golden tinge, and when she pours, the scent rises - rice wine, slightly sweet, with hints of fruit and warmth. (DC 12 Intelligence/History: This is Mountain Peak sake, expensive, from the northern distilleries. She's serving you her personal stock.)
>
> She pours for each of you, filling cups to precisely the same level, then sits down uninvited at your table with her own cup. "Three years I've been serving empty rooms. Forgive me if I join you. These days, conversation is rarer than coin."

### If Party Engages Her

**Initial Conversation (Warm-Up):**

*"Where are you from?"*
- "Born here. Raised here. Married here. Will die here, probably soon if business doesn't pick up. Which it won't. Not with the weeping."
- (Takes a drink, refills her cup)

*"The weeping?"*
- (Her face hardens) "You'll hear it tonight if you stay. Most don't make it to midnight before they're packing their things. A woman. Crying like her heart's been torn out. Every night for two and a half years."
- (Pause) "My husband died three years ago. Six months later, she started."

*"Do you think it's connected?"*
- (Sharp look) "Don't know. Don't care to guess. What I know is this street was alive when he was. Now it's dead, and I'm the fool still pretending otherwise."

**As Sake Flows (She Opens Up - Persuasion DC 12 or just genuine interest):**

*About the Tea House:*
> "Two doors down. The Jade Blossom it was called. Ran by Old Master Chen and his daughter Yuki. Pretty girl, eighteen maybe, always smiling. The kind of smile that made you feel like the sun came out."
>
> (Refills cups)
>
> "Three years ago, the same week Kuro died, she vanished. Just... gone. Master Chen said she ran off with some merchant boy. Closed up shop and moved away within the month. Never saw him again."
>
> (Her hand tightens on her cup)
>
> "I don't believe it. Yuki wasn't the running type. And the timing... too convenient. Something happened to that girl."

*About the Curse:*
> "Started six months after. Like whatever happened was... festering. Building. Then it erupted."
>
> (Another drink)
>
> "Objects fall. Doors open. Cold spots in rooms that have no right being cold. But mostly... the weeping. Starts at midnight. Stops at dawn. Every. Single. Night."
>
> "Priests tried. Salt. Prayers. Bells. Nothing works. Whatever's here, it's stubborn. Like me, I guess." (Bitter laugh)

*About Kuro:*
> (If asked gently, Insight DC 14 to know this is painful)
>
> "Mountain Guard. Twenty years of service. Good man. Fair. Strong as an ox and twice as stubborn."
>
> (Long pause, refills everyone's cups)
>
> "Died protecting a merchant caravan. Bandits. He killed four before they got him. They say it was quick. I hope they're lying. I hope he had time to think of me."
>
> (She doesn't cry, but her voice gets rougher)
>
> "This inn was his dream. Built it with his own hands, the foundation anyway. Can't let it die. Won't."

**If Party Offers to Help (She's Skeptical):**

> (Studies them carefully)
>
> "You're not the first to offer. Priests failed. Exorcists failed. Even had some fancy shrine maiden from the capital. Nothing."
>
> (But there's hope in her eyes despite her words)
>
> "But... you're different. Can see it. You've got iron in you. The kind that doesn't bend."
>
> (Pours another round)
>
> "I've got a hundred gold pieces. It's all I have left. It's yours if you can stop the weeping. Make this street safe again. Bring back my customers."
>
> (Pause)
>
> "And... if you can find out what really happened to Yuki... I'll add another hundred. That's my word. Tora Kaneshiro doesn't break her word."

## THE SAKE ITSELF

### Description for Atmosphere

**Appearance:** 
- Clear with slight golden tinge
- Slightly viscous, clings to the cup
- Steams gently when poured

**Aroma:**
- Rice wine base (clean, grain-like)
- Hints of pear and apple
- Slight floral note (mountain flowers)
- Warmth that rises to the nose

**Taste:**
- Smooth, warming from throat to chest
- Sweet at first, then dry finish
- Slight umami undertone
- Leaves clean palate, no harsh burn
- Encourages conversation (loosens tongues)

**Effects (Roleplay):**
- First cup: Warmth spreads, edges soften
- Second cup: Tongue loosens, truths come easier
- Third cup: The world seems simpler, clearer
- Fourth cup: CON save DC 12 or become tipsy (advantage on Persuasion, disadvantage on Deception)
- Fifth cup: CON save DC 14 or become drunk (disadvantage on DEX/INT checks)

### Sake Drinking Customs (Add Authenticity)

**Mama Tora will:**
- Always pour for others first, never herself
- Hold the bottle with both hands when pouring
- Pour until told to stop (generous in grief)
- Refuse to let cups stay empty
- Drink only after everyone else has drunk
- Become progressively more honest with each cup

**Cultural Notes (If players want to be respectful):**
- Let the eldest/host pour first
- Hold cup with both hands when receiving
- Never pour your own (wait for others to pour for you)
- Say "Kanpai!" before first drink
- Small sips, not gulping (unless celebrating)
- Accepting a pour is accepting the relationship

## THE HAUNTINGS (If Party Stays)

### Midnight Arrives

> The temple bells ring twelve times, the sound carrying across the Middle Terrace. 
>
> And the weeping begins.
>
> It starts softly - so softly you almost think you imagined it. A woman's voice, distant, muffled by walls or distance or something worse. She's crying. Deep, wrenching sobs that speak of loss so profound it has no bottom.
>
> The two young travelers bolt upright, grab their packs, and flee. The door slams behind them.
>
> Old Hiroshi just sighs and pulls his blanket tighter.
>
> Mama Tora sits perfectly still, sake cup halfway to her lips. "There she is. Right on time. She's never late. Never early. Always midnight."
>
> The weeping grows louder.

### Investigation Options

**Follow the Sound (Perception/Investigation checks):**
- DC 12: It's coming from outside, toward the closed tea house
- DC 15: No, it's inside the inn, upstairs
- DC 18: It's both places at once, or moving, or... wrong somehow

**Check the Tea House:**
- Door is barred, windows shuttered
- Chalk wards all around it (protective symbols)
- The weeping is loudest here
- DC 16 Arcana/Religion: These wards aren't keeping something out - they're keeping something IN

**Spiritual Investigation:**
- DC 14 Religion: This isn't a normal haunting
- DC 16 Arcana: The spirit is bound somehow, can't move on
- DC 18 Insight: The crying isn't random - there's a pattern, almost like words

**If They Try to Communicate:**
- The weeping stops briefly
- Then starts again, louder, more desperate
- DC 18 Perception: You can almost make out words: "Help me... find me... remember me..."

## MAMA TORA IN COMBAT/DANGER

**If Yokai Manifests:**
- She grabs a heavy iron poker from the hearth
- "Not in my inn, spirit. Not while I'm breathing."
- Will fight to defend her guests and property
- Knows she'll probably die but doesn't care
- Better to die fighting than live in fear

**Stats (If Needed):**
- **AC:** 12 (no armor, just tough)
- **HP:** 45 (she's survived a hard life)
- **Attack:** Iron Poker +4 (1d8+3 bludgeoning)
- **Special:** Stubborn Will - Advantage on saves against fear
- **Roleplay:** Protects guests over herself

## AFTERMATH & HOOKS

### If Party Solves the Haunting:

> Mama Tora stands in the doorway as the first morning light breaks across the street. The weeping has stopped. Truly stopped. The silence is... peaceful.
>
> She doesn't say anything for a long moment. Then:
>
> "I'll get your gold."
>
> But when she returns, she's carrying more than money. A small wooden box, battered but well-kept.
>
> "This was Kuro's. His seal, his papers, his guard token. You did what priests couldn't. What I couldn't. You gave this street back its life. You gave me back my purpose."
>
> She presses the box into your hands.
>
> "The Iron Rest is yours any time you need it. Rooms are free. Food's on the house. And if you ever need an angry old woman with a poker to watch your back... well. You know where to find me."

### If Party Fails or Leaves:

> Mama Tora watches them go, her face unreadable. Old Hiroshi approaches.
>
> "They tried, at least. More than most."
>
> Tora just refills her sake cup.
>
> "Everyone tries. No one stays. Story of this damn inn."

### Future Role:

**Mama Tora can become:**
- A safe house in the Middle Terrace
- Information broker (she hears things)
- Emergency backup in combat
- Emotional anchor showing cost of Circle's actions
- Quest giver for street-level adventures
- Mother figure who feeds them and scolds them

## QUICK REFERENCE ROLEPLAYING MAMA TORA

**Voice:** Rough, direct, warm underneath  
**Posture:** Arms crossed or hands busy, always moving  
**Favorite Phrases:**
- "That's dross and you know it"
- "Iron doesn't bend. Neither do I."
- "In my day..." (followed by grumbling)
- "Kuro would have..."
- "Empty rooms won't pay themselves"

**Mood Indicators:**
- Happy: Hums forge-songs
- Angry: Slams things down harder
- Sad: Goes very still, very quiet
- Worried: Obsessively cleans already-clean surfaces
- Drunk: More honest, less bitter, laughs more

**Bonds Quickly Over:**
- Shared drinks
- Stories of lost loved ones
- Standing up to bullies
- Craftsmanship and quality
- Keeping one's word

**Triggered By:**
- Mention of abandoning responsibilities
- Cowardice
- Disrespect of the dead
- Waste (food, drink, money)
- Pity (she hates being pitied)
