# Adventure: The Twice Cursed – Act I, Master Hideo’s Funeral

Act I opens *The Last Whisper of Steel* with a solemn rite in the Whispering Glade. The scene establishes relationships, tensions, and mysteries that propel the campaign.

## Setting the Stage
- **Location:** Whispering Glade outside [Nagano](Nagano.md), ringed by spirit-touched cherry trees that shed glowing petals.
- **Occasion:** Funeral of [Master Hideo Nakata](Master%20Hideo%20Nakata.md) during the Moonlit Reverence festival (21 Tsukinami).
- **Atmosphere:** Cold breeze, distant temple bells, and an undercurrent of unease. The glade doubles as a spirit nexus.

## Key Beats
1. **Arrival & Procession:** Mourners assemble—Nagano villagers, Takashima retainers, Lady Hana Arashi’s delegation, and wandering adventurers. Each arrival offers introductions and first impressions.
2. **Remembrance Rites:** The Temple of the Three Moons leads prayers; attendees may offer eulogies or tokens. Insight checks reveal conflicting emotions among nobles and apprentices.
3. **Disruptions & Omens:**  
   - A **raven yokai** circles overhead leaving a single black feather charged with moonlight.  
   - A tremor ripples through the glade as cherry petals drift against the wind.  
   - Whispers hint that artifacts from Hideo’s shrine have vanished.
4. **Political Undercurrents:**  
   - **Lady Hana Arashi** probes for allies while weighing Nagano’s vulnerability.  
   - **Lord Ichiro Kagemura** deploys retainers (Akane Hiroshi, Riku Matsunaga) to monitor rivals.  
   - **Kana Yoshiro** and **Mayor Hiroshi Takeda** juggle logistics, worried about bandits and Obsidian Circle activity.
5. **The Warning:** A raven yokai manifests briefly, intoning that “steel remembers” before vanishing. Arcana/Religion checks reveal ties to Tsukuyomi and impending imbalance.

## Roleplay & Skill Opportunities
- **Social Scenes:** Offer condolences, trade secrets, or challenge a noble’s statement. Persuasion or Insight can secure future favors.
- **Ritual Participation:** Players can add offerings; successful Religion checks reinforce bonds with the kami (granting inspiration or minor boons).
- **Investigation:** Searching the glade uncovers disturbed earth, missing relics, and traces leading toward the Frostwood.

## Clues & Leads
- **Missing Relics:** Notes point to the temple storeroom, the market quarter, and possible Obsidian Circle interference.
- **Raven Feather:** Functions as a focus; Divination or Identify reveals it resonates with Tsukuyomi’s domain and the Twice-Cursed Samurai.
- **Contradictory Testimonies:** NPCs provide partial truths—use these to seed side quests (e.g., tracking a bandit infiltrator or escorting Lady Hana’s envoy).

## Featured NPCs
- **Lady Hana Arashi:** Diplomat seeking leverage; offers sponsorship but expects discretion.
- **Akane Hiroshi & Hiroto Masanori:** Rival retainers whose shared history can spark duels or alliances.
- **Renji “Driftwood” Sato:** Observes quietly; may leave a driftwood carving at Hideo’s altar.
- **Reiko Matsuda:** Priestess who senses the shrine’s wards weakening.

## Outcomes
- Players choose how they honor Hideo, whom they trust, and which lead to follow.  
- The act concludes once the funeral rites end and immediate investigations or escort missions are assigned.

Use Act I to cement motivations, reveal stakes, and place each character on the board before the journey into the Frostwood begins.
