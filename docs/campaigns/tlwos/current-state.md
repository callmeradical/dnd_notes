# TLWoS Current State (Live - Middle Terrace)

**Last Updated:** 2026-04-08

## Canon Snapshot

- The party returned to Takashima after rescuing the blacksmith's daughter; her dragonborn father died.
- The party killed a Bheur Hag.
- The party regrouped with Kaoru and the Hidden Lotus Society, took payment, and resumed Obsidian Circle investigations.
- The lower city was besieged by goblins, ogres, and ettins.
- An oni infiltrator attacked civilians during the siege and was dealt with by the party.
- Takemura's condition is confirmed in play: bound to the twice-cursed blade; demon-slayer role active.
- A city-scale bubble field expanded from the forest and enclosed Takashima.
- A lightning / falling-star event struck or settled in the palace.
- Forest hostiles reversed course and fled from the city.
- Party assessment: the prince is in danger and palace pressure is now the core threat.
- The undercity tree restoration sequence is complete.
- The sage scene and kitsune village/brewery sequence (Kokuei Shuzo) are complete.
- The party has returned aboveground to Takashima's **middle terrace**.
- The party has just fought a **basilisk** in the streets.
- Additional hostile creatures are visibly roaming the middle terrace.
- Pressure on the upper city remains active: the prince is still under oni-linked threat.
- The prince is currently enthralled.
- Krixarion remains a live personal-pressure thread via the snowglobe, with Yasei as a primary target.
- Treat the snowglobe as a cross-world cursed artifact first, not a guaranteed transport portal to Eryssyl.

## Active Objectives

- Survive and navigate escalating creature pressure in the middle terrace.
- Determine whether the roaming creatures are spillover, directed releases, or part of a broader influence pattern.
- Survive and gain entrance to the citadel (top tier/palace approach).
- Continue Obsidian Circle pursuit while city command-and-control is unstable.
- Define Krixarion's exact bargain/lure terms so consequences can be tracked cleanly in canon.

## GM Truth (Not Yet Revealed In-World)

- The sky/lightning event was an oni traveling to the prince (an anomaly of fate).
- The oni is a pact-holder warlock of Tsurogami and had been sealed in a great tree in the forest.
- The current Daimyo imprisoned/enslaved goliaths and forced clear-cutting in the northern mountain forest for boatbuilding.
- That coerced clear-cut breach enabled the oni mage's return/escalation.
