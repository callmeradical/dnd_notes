# The Frostwood

The Frostwood is the liminal frontier north of [Nagano](Nagano.md)—a twilight forest where ever-falling snow mingles with spectral mists. Yokai, corrupted shrines, and forgotten Soulsteel relics lie hidden beneath its towering pines. Every step feels like a test issued by kami unsure whether mortals deserve entry.

## Travel Conditions
- **Perpetual Chill:** Temperatures drop abruptly once the Frostwood canopy closes overhead. Exhaustion checks appear swiftly for unprepared parties.
- **Shifting Paths:** Trails realign after heavy fogs or during moonlit nights, often redirecting travelers toward shrines or ambushes.
- **Kami Presence:** Whispers, floating lights, or sudden gusts may be signs of unseen guardians—or warnings to turn back.

## Notable Encounters
- **Frostwood Guardian:** A slumbering stone sentinel that animates to test intruders who disturb sacred groves.
- **Spirit Fog Ambush:** Dense fog hides kappa yokai and disorients navigation checks.
- **Corrupted Shrine:** Desecrated altars to Tsuchi-no-Megumi spawn shadow elementals until purified.
- **Hungry Jorogumo:** Spider yokai mimicking the pleas of trapped travelers; webs stretch between ancient cedars.
- **Bandit Hideaways:** Camps belonging to Jiro the Thorn and other smugglers exploiting the forest’s cover.
- **Phantom Stag:** Ethereal stag leads worthy seekers to an ancient dryad or a long-forgotten shrine.
- **Shadow Wolf Pack:** Sleek predators that coordinate hit-and-run attacks at dusk.
- **Whispering Tree:** Sentient tree issuing riddles; wrong answers awaken treant guardians.
- **Raven Yokai & Flock:** Mischievous spirit accompanied by giant ravens—messengers of omens tied to Hideo’s death.
- **Planar Rift:** Slivers of astral energy spill into the woods, releasing chaotic entities and warping nearby flora.
- **Soulsteel Forge Ruins:** An abandoned forge whose spectral caretaker guards a fragment of unfinished Soulsteel.

## Themes & Usage
- **Spiritual Balance:** Each encounter underscores the tug-of-war between kami guardians and corrupting forces.
- **Exploration Pressure:** Limited visibility, winter storms, and shifting trails create tension for overland travel scenes.
- **Adventure Gateway:** Serve as the staging area for Act II and Act III of *The Twice Cursed*—clues, allies, and dangers all converge here.
