# The Brass Anvil

**Location:** Fringe of Newthrone  
**Type:** Ruined smithy rebuilt into barracks and trading post  
**Proprietor:** Tikka Ashwright

---

## Outpost Description

### **Physical Layout**
Built into the remains of a colonial-era smithy, the Brass Anvil combines functional workspace with fortified trading post. The original forge still operates, now fueled by charcoal and scavenged materials. Sleeping quarters occupy the former storage areas, while the main trading floor showcases weapons and equipment hung from iron hooks and displayed on rough wooden tables.

### **Atmosphere**
The constant sound of hammering metal mingles with the acrid smell of forge-ash that Tikka uses as insect repellent. Smoke stains the ceiling black, and the heat from the forge creates a perpetual haze. Despite the rough conditions, everything is organized with military precision - weapons clean, supplies inventoried, sleeping areas neat.

### **Defensive Features**
- Reinforced wooden palisade with firing positions
- Multiple escape routes through connected buildings
- Weapon caches hidden throughout the compound
- Clear sightlines to approaching roads

## Proprietor

### **Tikka Ashwright**
*Small humanoid (rock gnome), lawful neutral*

**Appearance:** Grizzled gnome with arms like corded steel and hands permanently stained by forge work. Her gray hair is kept in practical braids to avoid forge sparks. Wears a leather apron embedded with metal studs and tools. Always has ash smudged on her cheeks and forehead.

**Personality:** Direct and no-nonsense, Tikka values competence over conversation. She's generous with advice about weapon maintenance but expects payment upfront for everything else. Has little patience for haggling but respects those who prove their worth through actions.

**Background:** Former military smith who stayed behind when Brelish forces withdrew. Converted her smithy into a survival post serving mercenaries, refugees, and anyone else willing to pay fair prices for honest work.

**Services Offered:**
- Weapon and armor repair (expert quality)
- Custom metalwork and modifications
- Military contacts and mercenary recruitment
- Equipment evaluation and authentication

## Patrons & Rumors

### **Sergeant Kal Branth**
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

**Appearance:** Middle-aged human with the bearing of a professional soldier. Wears pieces of Brelish uniform mixed with practical mercenary gear. Has a network of scars from multiple campaigns and carries himself with quiet authority.

**Background:** Former Brelish sergeant who remained in Q'barra after the withdrawal. Now works as a mercenary recruiter and military advisor, maintaining contacts throughout the region's various armed groups.

**Key Information:**
- **"Newthrone's council is three factions pretending to work together. One spark and the whole city burns."**
- **"The collaborators have Brelish equipment but no Brelish discipline. Dangerous amateurs."**
- **"Mercenary bands are hiring, but half of them won't live to see winter. Choose your employers carefully."**

**Services:**
- **Mercenary Recruitment:** Can arrange contracts with various groups (10% finder's fee)
- **Military Intelligence:** Current troop movements and political tensions (25 gp)
- **Equipment Sourcing:** Access to military-grade gear through contacts (20% markup)
- **Tactical Consultation:** Battle planning and strategic advice (50 gp per day)

---

### **Lira Stonesong**
*Small humanoid (halfling), chaotic good*

**Appearance:** Young halfling with callused fingers from constant lute playing. Wears traveling clothes patched with fabric from a dozen different regions. Her instrument case is covered with political slogans and protest songs written in multiple languages.

**Background:** Wandering bard who spreads seditious songs and subversive messages throughout the region. Uses music to encode information and rally resistance against wyrm cult influence.

**Key Information:**
- **"The guard captain in Newthrone wears a dragon scale pendant - that's no coincidence."**
- **"My songs about the old days make people remember what they've lost to the cults."**
- **"There's a network of resisters, but they're careful. Trust has to be earned one song at a time."**

**Services:**
- **Information Network:** Messages passed through musical code (5 gp per message)
- **Entertainment:** Morale-boosting performances (reduces exhaustion recovery time)
- **Cultural Intelligence:** Local customs and political undercurrents (15 gp)
- **Resistance Contacts:** Introduction to anti-cult networks (if trust is earned)

## Roleplay Opportunities

### **The Forge as Gathering Place**
Tikka's forge serves as a natural meeting point for various characters:
- **Equipment Demonstrations:** Tikka shows off new modifications or repairs
- **Information Exchange:** Patrons share news while waiting for metalwork
- **Trust Building:** Working alongside Tikka at the forge builds respect and rapport

### **Military Culture**
The outpost's military atmosphere creates specific interaction dynamics:
- **Chain of Command:** Respect shown to military experience and competence
- **Professional Standards:** Shoddy work or unreliable behavior not tolerated
- **Unit Loyalty:** Strong bonds between those who've served together

### **Political Tensions**
Proximity to Newthrone brings political complexity:
- **Faction Loyalties:** Patrons may represent different Newthrone factions
- **Information Warfare:** Rumors and counter-rumors about city politics
- **Recruitment Pressure:** Various groups trying to recruit capable individuals
