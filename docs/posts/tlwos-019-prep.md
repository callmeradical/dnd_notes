# Session 019: The Steel Tablet's Summons

**Campaign:** [[Shuigari - The Last Whisper of Steel]]  
**Arc:** Arc 2 - The Iron Court  
**Date Played:** [To be filled]  
**Session Type:** Investigation & Introduction

---

## Session Summary

The party receives a mysterious summons bearing Master Hideo's maker's mark to meet at the Whispering Willow Tea House in Takashima's Middle Terrace. There, they encounter [[Twice-Cursed Samurai|Kazuki Takemura, the Twice-Cursed Samurai]] - a ronin carrying the burden of two curses and Master Hideo's final instructions. He offers employment: infiltrate the Upper Citadel, identify Circle agents, protect the boy heir, and discover what lies in the hidden vault beneath the fortress. The meeting is interrupted by Shadeblade Cultists hunting Kazuki, forcing a violent confrontation that reveals the true danger of his cursed blade, Yomi-no-Kiba.

---

## Key Events

### Opening: The Summons
- Party finds a wooden token bearing Master Hideo's maker's mark and the word "Debt"
- Directed to the Whispering Willow Tea House at sunset
- Three days have passed since arriving in Takashima

### Meeting the Twice-Cursed
- [[Twice-Cursed Samurai|Kazuki Takemura]] enters, scarred and carrying a cursed blade
- Bears Master Hideo's steel tablet of admission - proof of unpaid debt
- Explains his two curses:
  1. **First Curse**: Cast out from House Arashi for choosing duty over innocents (37 souls lost)
  2. **Second Curse**: Blade Yomi-no-Kiba binds him to the souls of those he failed

### Kazuki's Revelation
- Admits to killing Master Hideo at the smith's final request
- Hideo knew the Circle would torture him for knowledge
- Final instructions: "Guard what they seek. Trust those who grieve true."
- Hideo showed Kazuki something about the vault the night he died

### The Proposal
Kazuki offers 500 gold upfront (500 more on completion) for the party to:
1. Identify Circle agents within the Upper Citadel
2. Protect the boy heir from assassination/kidnapping  
3. Discover what lies in the vault beneath the citadel
4. Complete the mission before the Festival of Ascending Iron (3 days)

**Key Intelligence Provided:**
- A sealed vault lies beneath the Upper Citadel
- The boy heir's blood is connected to the vault's seal
- At least three Circle agents operate within the citadel
- Lady Hana Arashi is likely Circle (unproven)
- The vault contains "the first chain" (cryptic)

### Combat: Shadeblade Ambush
- Three [[The Obsidian Circle|Shadeblade Cultists]] attack the tea house
- Hunting Kazuki specifically
- Combat reveals the true horror of Yomi-no-Kiba:
  - Spectral faces of the failed souls swirl around the blade
  - Kazuki whispers "forgive me" with each strike
  - Souls of the slain are visibly torn away into the sword
  - The curse grows stronger with each death

### The Choice
Party must decide whether to:
- Accept Kazuki's employment (investigate the Upper Citadel)
- Decline but offer alternative help
- Walk away entirely

### Aftermath
- City guard investigates the disturbance
- Tea house owner (Mama Hoshiko) vouches for the party
- Cultist bodies reveal hidden Circle tattoos
- Crumpled note found: "The Twice-Cursed hunts us. Silence him before the festival. The boy's ascension cannot be delayed."

## Key Quotes

**Kazuki on his first curse:**
> "Twenty years ago, I was samurai of House Arashi - sworn to protect a mining village on the southern border. Bandits came. My lord ordered me to hold the pass while he secured the iron shipment. I... obeyed. The village burned. Thirty-seven souls. I kept my oath to my lord and broke my oath to the innocent. House Arashi cast me out. That was my first curse - to live masterless, knowing I chose wrong."

**Kazuki on killing Hideo:**
> "Hideo made me a final request. He knew his time was ending - not by age, but by design. He asked me to end his suffering before the Circle could take him, torture him, use his knowledge. I... fulfilled that request. I killed the man who tried to save me."

**Kazuki's warning about his curse:**
> "I am cursed. Those who work alongside me find that violence follows. The blade calls to blood. I resist, but... the curse is stronger each day. If you accept this work, you accept that risk."

**After combat:**
> "They're... louder now. Fresh death feeds them. This is what I meant. This is why I need you. Without those who can pull me back... I fear I'll become what they need me to be. A monster."

**Kazuki on the mission:**
> "Master Hideo used to say: 'Every blade is forged in fire and quenched in water. The fire is our suffering, the water is our choice.' You've chosen to walk into the fire with me. I pray we emerge as steel, not ash."
