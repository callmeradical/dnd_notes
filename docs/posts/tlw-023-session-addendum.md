## Decision Point

Rather than continue through the middle terrace creature surge, the party opted to brave the vault complex beneath Takashima. This bypasses the planned street-level encounters and moves directly into the vault arc established in the Arc 2 Master Plot Framework.

## Vault Navigation

The vault is a massive underground complex containing the Well of Life, the One Tree seedling, and the fading Hikari's Light portal. Eight sealed entrances exist, aligned to wind directions. The Oniwaban clan guarded it before their massacre by the Obsidian Circle. The Deep Mines (Fukai Kozan) were sealed 50 years ago after an incident — something rose from below that desired the silver-thread stream.

### Cavern Encounters (Available / Used)

1. **The Shattered Register** — Shadow Register bleeds downward; skill challenge (DC 14, 3 successes before 3 failures) to navigate doubled geography. Fox's false memories contain a mapped route. Using them grants advantage but makes the memories feel more real.

2. **The Oniwaban Seal-Lock** — Soulsteel gate with eight wind-sigils, seven broken by the Obsidian Circle. The north wind sigil still responds to Oniwaban lineage. Aoshi can activate with DC 12 Con save. DC 15 Investigation reveals the breaker used Oniwaban technique — the traitor trained with Aoshi's clan.

3. **The Silver Thread Guardian** — A Jorogumo nested where the silver-thread stream feeds the One Tree roots. Absorbing soulsteel residue for decades. Can be fought (CR 9) or bargained with (DC 16 Persuasion). Her charmed voice echoes Akemi's voice to Kanji: *"I went down to find what was beneath the water. I never came back up."*

4. **The Kagemi Crossing** — Obsidian bridge over a lightless chasm. A Kagemi no Oni watches from below. Spirit-sight users must save (DC 15 Wis) or see their phantom-self plummeting. Yasei's spirit-attunement triggers it passively — save at disadvantage unless a party member physically guides them across.

5. **The Hollow Archive** — Hidden Oniwaban cache. Vault schematic (confirms Circle had inside knowledge), soulsteel tempering compound (one use), Ryo-Seki's clan seal, and a sealed document addressed to *"the last of us, whenever you arrive"* — contents TBD for Aoshi.

6. **The Exhaustion Gate** — Three Spectral Samurai (last Oniwaban patrols, still holding). They target the most rested character. The path opens when at least one PC is below 25% HP and still standing. The samurai stop, step aside, one bows to Aoshi and fades. Beyond: Hikari's Grove — the One Tree seedling, rest in 4 hours, Thornbringer and Yasei connection beats.

## Major NPC Encounter: Kazan-Tetsu (火山鉄)

**Kazan-Tetsu, Ward-Tender of the Deep Forge** — A fire giant forge-warden from the Ember Crucible order, sealed underground for 50 years by accident during the incident. He has been single-handedly maintaining the vault's ward-seals, tending forge-channels, and keeping the silver-thread stream clear. He is effectively the reason the One Tree seedling is still alive.

**Key details:**
- Twelve feet tall, bent from decades in low spaces. Skin of cooling lava, ember-glow fissures. Wears stone-fiber haori hung with hundreds of iron markers — one per blade forged underground.
- Speaks slowly, uses forge-craft as metaphor. *"This conversation needs more tempering."*
- First words: *"You took long enough. Which of you is a smith?"*
- Spent four days talking metalwork with Master Hideo years before the sealing. Considers it among the best days of his life.
- Knows all seven vault sections, the three failing ward-seals, Obsidian Circle incursions (drove three away, let one grief-stricken figure pass — regrets it), and that something is wrong in the palace above (*"The stone up there is frightened."*).
- Knows the incident was caused by something that wanted to *absorb* the silver-thread stream — Shindarasu-adjacent corruption.
- An Oniwaban named **Chiyo** apologized through the sealed door and promised to send someone in the spring. She never did.
- **Wants:** To see the sky. Afraid the vault will fail without him. The party's arrival creates the possibility of someone taking over.
- **CR 8** if combat. Fights to stop, not kill. If forced into sustained combat, two ward-seals fail immediately from his absence.

## Treasure Room: Oniwaban Vault-Cache

Three items on separate pedestals in a sealed guardian cache. Placed deliberately, not hoarded.

### The Lantern of Hikari's Last Breath (光最後の息灯) — Given to Yasei

A folded paper lantern that unfolds on touch and emits true sunlight — a captured fragment of the Radiant Realm portal sealed by the Oniwaban before Hikari began fading.

**Properties:**
- Bonus action to light: 30 ft bright sunlight / 30 ft dim for 1 hour. Undead and shadow creatures in bright light have disadvantage on attacks and saves.
- Once per long rest, flare as action: 30 ft radius, DC 15 Con save, 4d8 radiant (half on success). Yasei immune.
- **Krixarion hungers for it.** The snowglobe curse operates through preservation and freezing — this is living light, the one thing Krixarion cannot replicate. Every time Yasei lights the lantern, the snowglobe grows hungry-cold. The dragon watches. Every use is a beacon.

### The Songs of the Unbroken Road (唄の不折道) — Given to Shigeo

A leather-bound songbook by Jubei One-String, a bard who traveled Shuigari two centuries ago. Twelve battle chants, drinking songs, a devastating lullaby, and one unfinished song about a father whose son doesn't recognize him.

**Properties:**
- Once per day, open to a random page. Roll d6 for spell level, then randomly select a bard spell of that level.
- Shigeo makes a Charisma (Performance) check. DC = 10 + spell level.
- **Success:** Spell fires using Shigeo's Charisma (spell save DC = 8 + prof + Cha mod). No slot expended.
- **Failure by 1-2 (Sour Note):** Spell fires but targets Shigeo or a random ally (beneficial spells hit a random enemy).
- **Failure by 3-4 (Broken Verse):** Spell doesn't fire. Shigeo takes [spell level]d6 psychic damage. Jubei's voice mutters: *"No, no, no — from the CHEST."*
- **Failure by 5+ (The Audience Turns):** Spell doesn't fire. All creatures within 30 ft make DC 13 Wis save or are hostile toward Shigeo for 1 round. Disadvantage on Performance checks until short rest.
- **Natural 20:** Spell fires at maximum effect. The unfinished song gains one new line of text.

### The Oaken Gauntlets of the Last Warden (最後の番人の樫手甲) — Given to [Recipient TBD]

Sentient Gauntlets of Ogre Power grown from heartwood of the original One Tree's canopy. The voice inside is **Chiyo** — the Oniwaban warden who stayed on the inside of the seal and held the lower passage for eleven days before dying at her post. Not a ghost; an impression recorded by the heartwood.

**Properties:**
- Gauntlets of Ogre Power (Strength score becomes 19).
- **Warden's Rooting:** Once per long rest, bonus action. Cannot be moved against will until start of next turn. Advantage on Strength saves. Wood grain extends visibly into the ground.
- **Sentience:** Int 10, Wis 16, Cha 12. Hearing and tremorsense 30 ft. Senses when wards/seals/barriers within 60 ft are damaged (gauntlets grow warm).
- **Chiyo's personality:** Patient, steady, tired in the way of someone who finished their work. Respects endurance. Dislikes waste of strength on pride. Goes cold and silent if the wearer strikes in anger rather than purpose.
- **Conflict:** If the wearer abandons a downed ally or flees while someone behind them can't escape, the gauntlets lock — wearer is incapacitated for 1 round.
- **The question she eventually asks:** *"When the door closes, which side will you be on?"*

**Connection to Kazan-Tetsu:** Chiyo is the Oniwaban who apologized through the sealed door and promised to send someone. She never could — she was already dying on the other side. Kazan-Tetsu does not know this yet.

## GM Notes

- The vault descent skips the planned middle-terrace street encounters but accelerates the core Arc 2 plot. All PC backstory threads converge here.
- Kazan-Tetsu knowing a direct passage to the citadel's lower armory provides an alternative palace entry that bypasses the lift gate / shrine path / waterworks routes from the original Session 023 prep.
- The Chiyo/Kazan-Tetsu connection is a loaded emotional beat. If the gauntlet wearer is in the same room as Kazan-Tetsu and Chiyo's impression senses his presence through tremorsense, something will happen. Save it for the right moment.
- The silver-thread stream connects encounters 3, 5, and 6. Players who follow it can navigate to Hikari's Grove directly.
- Shadow Register pressure decreases as the party descends. Deeper = earth-smell instead of iron, torches burn gold instead of blue. They are moving toward something older.
