# Towns of Shugane

The various towns of Shugane each have their own unique character, role, and strategic importance. Spread across the main island, these settlements serve as vital hubs of commerce, defense, and culture, connecting the larger cities with the rural villages. Below is an overview of the twelve major towns of Shugane, each contributing to the prosperity and stability of the Shogunate in its own way.

## Takayama (River Town, Central Plains)

- **Population:** 7,500
- **Defenses:** Takayama is surrounded by stone walls and guarded by a well-trained militia of around 300 soldiers. The town's position along the river makes it vulnerable to waterborne assaults, but watchtowers along the riverbanks provide early warnings of any incursions.
- **Commerce:** The town is a major trade hub for goods transported via the river. Local industries include rice milling, textiles, and the production of fine ceramics, which are exported to nearby cities.
- **Organizations:** The **Takayama Trade Guild** regulates the flow of goods and ensures fair trade practices, while the **Brotherhood of the Silver Eel** manages the town's river transport operations.

## Yokosuka (Fishing Town, Eastern Coast)

- **Population:** 4,200
- **Defenses:** Yokosuka is fortified with wooden palisades and a small naval garrison of 100 soldiers. The town also maintains a fleet of 20 fishing boats that can be converted into makeshift patrol vessels if needed.
- **Commerce:** The town's primary industry is fishing, known for its high-quality seafood, including salt-cured fish and dried seaweed. Yokosuka also supplies fish oil, used both for cooking and as a base component in alchemical mixtures.
- **Organizations:** The **Yokosuka Fishermen's Union** wields significant influence, overseeing both the town's economic and political affairs. A small shrine dedicated to the Sea Kami, **Kuraokami**, protects sailors and fishermen.

## Koganei (Castle Town, Southern Plains)

- **Population:** 9,800
- **Defenses:** Dominated by a large castle perched atop a hill, Koganei is heavily defended with stone walls, reinforced gates, and a standing garrison of 500 samurai. The castle serves as a key military outpost for the southern plains.
- **Commerce:** The town's economy revolves around providing arms and armor for the Shogunate's forces. Blacksmiths, fletchers, and armorers are abundant, and Koganei is known for producing high-quality weapons.
- **Organizations:** The **Order of the Iron Hawk**, a local samurai order, protects the town and its lord. The **Koganei Armorer's Guild** controls the production of weapons and armor.

## Miyako (Port Town, Southwestern Coast)

- **Population:** 6,300
- **Defenses:** Surrounded by a high wooden palisade and guarded by a small naval detachment, Miyako has 150 soldiers stationed at its docks. A watchtower on the cliffs provides a vantage point for spotting approaching ships.
- **Commerce:** As a port town, Miyako thrives on trade and fishing. It exports seafood, pearls, and coral, while importing luxury goods from other parts of Shuigari.
- **Organizations:** The **Miyako Merchant League** governs all trade passing through the port, while the **Mariners' Brotherhood** protects sailors and ensures that Miyako's ships are well maintained.

## Tsubasa (Mountain Town, Celestial Spine)

- **Population:** 3,200
- **Defenses:** Tsubasa is strategically located at a mountain pass, making it easily defensible with a small garrison of 200 warriors. The town is protected by natural rock formations and fortified barricades.
- **Commerce:** Known for its production of rare minerals and precious gems, Tsubasa's miners extract valuable resources from the surrounding mountains. The town also produces enchanted jewelry and ceremonial weapons.
- **Organizations:** The **Tsubasa Miners' Guild** controls the gem trade, while the **Society of the Celestial Anvil** manages the production of enchanted items.

## Futaba (Fortified Town, Northern Hills)

- **Population:** 8,400
- **Defenses:** Futaba is a heavily fortified town, encircled by high stone walls and guarded by 400 soldiers. Its elevated position in the hills makes it a strong defensive position against potential incursions from the northern border.
- **Commerce:** The town's primary industries include horse breeding and leatherworking. Futaba's horses are highly prized by the samurai for their endurance and speed.
- **Organizations:** The **House of the Golden Spur** oversees the breeding and training of horses, while the **Futaba Leatherworkers' Guild** supplies armor and saddles to the Shogunate's armies.

## Kameno (Market Town, Central Plains)

- **Population:** 5,700
- **Defenses:** Kameno is lightly defended, with only 100 town guards, relying on its strong trade connections for protection. The town has a modest stone wall and a central watchtower.
- **Commerce:** Known as the "Merchant's Crossroads," Kameno is a bustling market town where goods from all over Shugane are traded. The town hosts a monthly fair that draws traders from across the island.
- **Organizations:** The **Kameno Trade Consortium** controls the town's markets, and the **Wayfarer's Lodge** provides services and accommodations to traveling merchants.

## Shinami (Fishing Town, Northeastern Coast)

- **Population:** 3,900
- **Defenses:** Shinami has basic fortifications, with wooden palisades and 80 town guards. A small fleet of fast coastal skiffs patrols the waters, protecting the town from raiders.
- **Commerce:** Primarily a fishing community, Shinami is known for its smoked fish and shellfish. It also supplies fine seaweed and kelp used in various culinary and alchemical recipes.
- **Organizations:** The **Shinami Fishermen's Guild** manages the town's fishing rights, and the **Tidecaller's Circle** performs rituals to honor the sea spirits.

## Kawagata (River Town, Southern Plains)

- **Population:** 4,800
- **Defenses:** Kawagata has modest defenses, with a small wooden palisade and 150 town guards. Its position along the river makes it an important checkpoint for waterborne trade.
- **Commerce:** The town is known for its rice production and river trade. It serves as a collection point for goods transported downstream to the larger cities.
- **Organizations:** The **Kawagata Rice Merchants' Guild** controls the flow of grain, while the **River Wardens** ensure safe passage for trade barges.

## Oda (Forest Town, Northern Edge)

- **Population:** 3,500
- **Defenses:** Oda is lightly fortified with wooden barricades and a small militia of 100 archers. The dense forests around the town provide natural protection.
- **Commerce:** The town specializes in logging and the production of high-quality lumber. It also harvests rare herbs from the forest, used in various healing salves and potions.
- **Organizations:** The **Oda Foresters' Guild** manages the logging operations, while the **Circle of Verdant Spirits** ensures sustainable harvesting practices.

## Izanami (Monastery Town, Central Hills)

- **Population:** 2,800
- **Defenses:** Izanami is protected by a monastery militia of 150 warrior monks. The town is built around a central temple complex and is surrounded by stone walls adorned with spiritual wards.
- **Commerce:** The town serves as a center for spiritual learning and pilgrimage. Its main exports include spiritual charms, herbal medicines, and sacred texts.
- **Organizations:** The **Brotherhood of the Sacred Flame** manages the temple, while the **Order of Tranquil Waters** trains monks in both martial and spiritual arts.

## Torihana (Border Town, Southern Coastline)

- **Population:** 6,100
- **Defenses:** Torihana is heavily fortified, with thick stone walls and a garrison of 300 soldiers. Its position as a border town makes it a critical defensive outpost against incursions from rival territories.
- **Commerce:** The town is known for its production of salt and dried fish. Torihana also serves as a trade hub for goods entering Shugane from the southern isles.
- **Organizations:** The **Torihana Guard** maintains the town's defenses, while the **Saltmaster's Guild** oversees the salt production and trade.
