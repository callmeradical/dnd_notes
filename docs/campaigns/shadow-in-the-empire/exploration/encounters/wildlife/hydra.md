# Hydra

## Hydra
*Huge monstrosity, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 15 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 172 (15d12+75)  
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 20(+5) | 12(+1) | 20(+5) | 2(-4) | 10(+0) | 7(-2) |

**Skills** Perception +6  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Features

**Hold Breath.** The hydra can hold its breath for 1 hour.

**Multiple Heads.** The hydra has five heads. While it has more than one head, the hydra has advantage on saving throws against being blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, stunned, and knocked unconscious.

Whenever the hydra takes 25 or more damage in a single turn, one of its heads dies. If all its heads die, the hydra dies.

At the end of its turn, it grows two heads for each of its heads that died since its last turn, unless it has taken fire damage since its last turn. The hydra regains 10 hit points for each head regrown in this way.

**Reactive Heads.** For each head the hydra has beyond one, it gets an extra reaction that can be used only for opportunity attacks.

**Wakeful.** While the hydra sleeps, at least one of its heads is awake.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The hydra makes as many bite attacks as it has heads.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d10+5) piercing damage.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 hydra (always solitary)  
**Environment:** Deep swamps, large pools, cave systems with water  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random wildlife encounter (#6 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Ancient guardian of swamp territories, extremely territorial
- Each head may show slightly different behavioral patterns
- Patient ambush predator that can wait motionless for hours

**Mannerisms**

- Multiple heads move independently, creating unsettling coordination
- Heads may appear to "argue" with each other through body language
- Creates massive ripples and splashes when moving through water

**Tactics**

- Positions itself in deep water where it has swimming advantage
- Uses multiple bite attacks to focus fire on single targets
- Attempts to prevent enemies from using fire by staying in water
- May submerge most of its body, leaving only heads visible above water
- Regenerates heads unless opponents use fire damage consistently
- Never retreats from its territory, fights to the death

**Environment Notes**

- Lair consists of deep pools connected by underwater tunnels
- Territory marked by massive claw marks on trees and bones of prey
- Water in area may be stagnant and brackish due to hydra's presence
- Fish and smaller aquatic life flee the area when hydra is active

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine encounter location: deep swamp pool, flooded cave, or river confluence
- Note water depth: hydra prefers areas 10+ feet deep for tactical advantage
- Consider visibility: murky swamp water provides concealment for hydra
- Check party's access to fire damage sources for head regeneration mechanic

**Opening Scene Options**

**Swamp Guardian:**
> *The ancient cypress trees form a cathedral around the dark pool ahead. As you approach the water's edge, massive ripples disturb the surface. Then they emerge—five serpentine heads rising from the depths, each as long as a man is tall. The hydra's multiple sets of eyes fix on your group with predatory intelligence.*

**Submerged Ambush:**
> *What you mistook for floating logs suddenly explodes into motion. The swamp erupts as a massive creature unfolds from the murky depths. Five heads weave and twist through the air like deadly snakes, each mouth filled with razor-sharp teeth. The hydra has been watching you approach for some time.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Territorial Display**
- Hydra emerges from water to establish dominance
- Uses all five heads for intimidation display
- Positions itself to control access to water
- **Perception Check (DC 16):** Notice hydra before it fully emerges
- **Nature Check (DC 15):** Recognize hydra and understand head regeneration

**Phase 2: Multi-Head Assault**
- Makes bite attacks equal to current number of heads
- Focuses attacks on most dangerous or isolated targets
- Uses reach advantage to attack from water
- **Initiative Order:** Hydra heads act on same initiative but can make opportunity attacks with extra reactions

**Phase 3: Regeneration Challenge**
- Regrows heads unless fire damage has been dealt since last turn
- Becomes more dangerous as heads multiply if fire isn't used consistently
- May have 8-10 heads if party doesn't understand regeneration mechanic
- **Fire Damage Requirement:** Must be dealt between hydra's turns to prevent regeneration

**Combat Mechanics Deep Dive**

**Head Regeneration System**
- Heads die when 25+ damage dealt in single turn
- Two heads regrow for each head lost (unless fire damage applied)
- Hydra regains 10 HP per head regrown
- Maximum of 10 heads possible before becoming unmanageable

**Multiple Head Benefits**
- Number of bite attacks equals number of heads
- Extra reactions for opportunity attacks (heads beyond first)
- Advantage on mental saves while multiple heads remain
- Cannot be flanked or surprised easily

**Aquatic Combat Advantages**
- Swimming speed equals land speed
- Can submerge partially for cover
- Difficult terrain for non-swimming party members
- Natural resistance to fire due to water environment

**Skill Integration**
- **Arcana (DC 14):** Understand magical nature of regeneration
- **Medicine (DC 16):** Recognize how to prevent head regrowth
- **Nature (DC 13):** Know hydra behavioral patterns and weaknesses
- **Survival (DC 15):** Navigate swamp terrain safely during combat

**Environmental Hazards**
- **Difficult Terrain:** Muddy swamp bottom slows movement
- **Visibility:** Murky water provides partial concealment
- **Deep Water:** Swimming required in hydra's preferred areas
- **Quicksand:** Possible hazards at swamp edges (DC 12 Perception to avoid)

**Fire Damage Strategy**
- **Alchemist's Fire:** Thrown weapons for non-spellcasters
- **Torches:** Improvised weapons but require close combat
- **Spells:** Fire Bolt, Burning Hands, Fireball most effective
- **Magic Items:** Flaming weapons or fire-based magic items

**Scaling the Challenge**

**Head Management**
- Start with 5 heads as written
- Allow regeneration to demonstrate mechanic
- Hint at fire solution through environmental clues
- Maximum drama around 8-9 heads before overwhelming

**Tactical Advice for Players**
- **Coordinate Fire Attacks:** Time fire damage between hydra turns
- **Control Positioning:** Use ranged attacks to avoid opportunity attacks
- **Environmental Usage:** Force hydra onto land where fire is more effective
- **Resource Management:** Don't waste all fire damage early in encounter

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Hydra Defeated**
- Hydra hide valuable for crafting armor (AC 15, resistance to acid)
- Teeth and claws worth 200-500 gp to right buyers
- Lair may contain treasure from previous victims
- Local ecosystem shifts without apex predator

**If Party Retreats**
- Hydra remains in territory as ongoing threat
- May pursue party briefly but returns to defend lair
- Reputation as hydra-slayers spreads if they return successful
- Knowledge of regeneration mechanic helps future encounters

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Hydra guards access to underwater cave system
- Local village plagued by hydra attacks on water sources
- Ancient treasure hoard in hydra's deep pool lair
- Hydra is result of magical experiment gone wrong

**Treasure Possibilities**
- 2d4 × 100 gp in coins and gems from previous victims
- 1d3 magic items that survived hydra's digestive process
- Rare alchemical components from hydra's body parts
- Maps or documents in waterproof containers

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Start with 3 heads, reduce regeneration to 1-for-1 ratio
- **Harder:** Poisonous bite attacks or breath weapon variant
- **Environmental:** Combat during storm or in treacherous swamp conditions
