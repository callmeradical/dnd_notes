#### Who are his/her parents?

Brauga's father, Togrr, was an orc farmer and sheep herder in a settlement in the Roughs near the foothills of the Monte Di Madre.  One day, a human soldier, Vivian, wandered through the village, bruised and battered.  Togrr, seeing her, took her in and tended to her wounds and during the course of her care, discovered she was pregnant.  Wishing to protect her, he took her in and raised the son, Brauga, as his own.

When Brauga was 12, a plague passed through their village and with little warning, wiped out over half of the village, including his his parents, Togrr and Vivian.

Left alone, Brauga ventured into the wilderness to hunt and find his way of surviving in the world on his own.  One day, wandering through the woods scouting for a target, he was ambushed by an aether-mutated dire wolf.  As he lost consciousness, he saw an Orc.

- Orc saved him
- Became mentor
- Coached him into what he is now
- Built reputation for his ability to sneakily stalk and hunt a specific target.
