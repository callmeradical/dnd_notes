# Shinro Takeda

*“The Last Apprentice” of [[Master Hideo Nakata]]*

- **Age:** 24  
- **Origin:** Nagano, Takashima Prefecture  
- **Occupation:** Blacksmith apprentice and keeper of Hideo’s forge  
- **Role:** Emotional anchor for Hideo’s legacy; wrestles with doubt while inheriting unfinished promises.

## Appearance
- Slim, wiry build with forge-scarred hands.
- Dark, singed hair and warm hazel eyes that drift between confidence and insecurity.
- Wears a patched smithing tunic and apron; keeps a whetstone pendant Hideo gifted him at eighteen.

## Personality
- Earnest yet uncertain, channeling anxieties into relentless work.
- Compassionate and people-first, mirroring Hideo’s teachings.
- Overwhelmed by expectations and highly susceptible to guidance—benevolent or manipulative.

## Background & Key Memories
1. **Chosen Apprentice:** Recruited at age 12 after Hideo noticed his knack for repairs, elevating his farming family’s status.  
2. **The Failed Dagger:** Early ceremonial blade shattered during quenching; Hideo counseled patience: *“The forge tempers all in time.”*  
3. **Gifted Whetstone Pendant:** Symbolizes his role as the one who sharpens others.  
4. **Final Weeks with Hideo:** Noticed the master’s fatigue and cryptic mutterings about sacrifice and guardianship but never pressed for answers.

## Skills & Limitations
- Competent at forging and exceptional at repairing weapons; artistry still developing.
- Latent talent for imbuing spiritual energy into steel, currently untrained.
- Minimal combat training beyond wielding a smith’s hammer defensively.

## Motivations & Struggles
1. **Living Up to Hideo:** Fears tarnishing the master’s reputation through failure.  
2. **Unanswered Mysteries:** Needs closure on Hideo’s death and the missing Soulsteel relics.  
3. **Seeking Validation:** Desperate for someone to believe in him, making him easy to sway.

## Adventure Role
### Act I – Funeral Vigil
- Grieving apprentice placing the whetstone pendant at the shrine.
- Shares stories of Hideo’s cryptic final sayings and can confirm the last known location of missing artifacts.

### Act II – Into the Frostwood
- Serves as a guide to the hidden forge and interprets kami omens despite an admitted fear of yokai.
- Helps decipher Soulsteel schematics and identify forged-forgery attempts.

### Act III – Facing the Twice-Cursed Samurai
- Assists in reforging *Yomi-no-Kiba* or conducting a restorative ritual.
- His self-belief crystallizes; success here marks the first step toward mastering Soulsteel craft.

## Roleplaying Notes
- **Voice:** Quiet, self-questioning cadence that steadies when discussing smithing.  
- **Mannerisms:** Fidgets with the whetstone pendant when anxious; moves with practiced precision when focused at the forge.  
- **Growth Trigger:** Genuine encouragement or witnessing noble sacrifice pushes him toward confidence.
