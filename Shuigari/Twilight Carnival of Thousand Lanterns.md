# Twilight Carnival of Thousand Lanterns

## Overview
- **Name:** *Senrō no Tasogare* — the Twilight Carnival of Thousand Lanterns.
- **Nature:** A wandering festival that appears during solstices, equinoxes, or rare celestial events.
- **Reputation:** Equal parts wonder and warning. Patrons speak of breathtaking performances, uncanny fortunes, and prizes that feel “touched” by the spirit world. Rumors persist that yokai host the revels behind smiling masks.

## Physical Layout
- **Torii Gate Entrance:** A vermillion gate draped in gauze that chills the air as guests pass through, marking the liminal boundary.
- **Lantern Boulevard:** The central thoroughfare hung with a canopy of paper lanterns in crescent-moon hues. Lantern light never flickers, even in rain.
- **The Sakura Stage:** Built around a cherry tree that blooms regardless of season. Hosts nightly dances, kabuki morality plays, and shadow-puppet retellings of ancient bargains.
- **Twilight Pavilion:** A curtained tent of indigo silk where Mistress Tsukiyo receives supplicants, answers questions in riddles, and offers “opportunities” that linger after dawn.

## Notable Figures
- **Mistress Tsukiyo:** Enigmatic impresario. Her porcelain mask shifts with the moon’s phase. She delights in bargains that balance mortal ambition against spiritual consequence.
- **Hajime the Flame Dancer:** Performs on the Sakura Stage with ribboned blades of living fire. His choreographies double as coded messages about brewing troubles within the carnival.
- **Kohana the Fox-Masked Girl:** Oversees the game stalls. Her laughter rings like chimes, and attentive visitors notice her shadow occasionally splits into nine tails.

## Attractions & Challenges
- **Performance Ring:** Flute and shamisen ensembles, acrobat troupes, and storytelling contests where the winner glimpses the future in rippling silk.
- **Carnival Games:** Ring tosses, goldfish scooping, and archery ranges. Winning yields charms, moonstones, or cryptic clues. Accepting a “double-or-nothing” challenge may bind the victor to a favor owed.
- **Hidden Booths:** Behind certain lanterns lie secret stalls—fortune-weaving spider yokai, talisman etchers, and a masked bookmaker exchanging memories for answers.

## Adventure Hooks
- **Lanterns Gone Dark:** Lantern Boulevard flickers for the first time, signaling an imbalance. Characters must trace the missing light to a stolen kami ember.
- **Wager of Shadows:** Hajime seeks protection after discovering an Obsidian Circle agent manipulating wagers to siphon festival blessings.
- **A Child’s Promise:** A villager’s child accepted a prize that binds them to Mistress Tsukiyo for “one lantern cycle.” The party must negotiate a loophole before the carnival vanishes.

The carnival is ideal for downtime sequences, social intrigue, and forging pacts with the uncanny. Treat each visit as a rare alignment—opportunities earned beneath the lanterns tend to follow characters long after the festival fades.
