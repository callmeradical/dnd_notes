# Social Orders of Shuigari

Every prefecture observes the Shogunate’s fourfold hierarchy, though the balance shifts as magic fades and war encroaches.

## Nobility & Court
- **Emperor & Imperial Family:** Symbolic authority centred in Hikosan. Their blessing legitimises daimyo rule, but decisions are filtered through the Shogunate.
- **Daimyo:** Regional lords controlling prefectures. Their courts host samurai retainers, artisans, and diplomats seeking favour.
- **Hatamoto & High Officials:** Rank-and-file court officers, scribes, and fiscal stewards who manage logistics. Circle infiltrators target this layer to redirect resources.

## Samurai Class
- **Retainers:** Sworn to daimyo or influential noble houses. Expected to provide military service, enforce law, and mediate peasant disputes.
- **Ronin:** Masterless warriors. The Twice-Cursed Samurai and his followers represent how easily ronin can become either heroes or threats.

## Artisans & Merchants
- **Guilds:** Blacksmiths, weavers, shipwrights, and scribes. Guild charters grant limited autonomy; conflict arises when the Emerald Lotus Syndicate undermines them.
- **Traveling Merchants:** Connect prefab markets, often acting as information brokers. PCs can tap this network for rumours or supply smugglers.

## Commoners & Farmers
- **Village Hōjin:** Rice farmers, fishers, and labourers. Provide shrine upkeep and levy troops. Their morale indicates regional stability.
- **Shrine Families:** Some communities dedicate families to caretaker roles. They guard lore that the PCs may need to restore seals.

## Outcasts & Hidden Communities
- **Okugōri Clans:** Deep Province inhabitants separate from the main hierarchy, obeying their own spiritual councils.
- **Yokai-Blessed:** Individuals marked by kami—respected in some regions, ostracised in others. A fertile ground for character backstories.

### Social Pressure in Play
- **Tax Levies:** Daimyo demand higher tribute to fund war. PCs might intervene to protect villages or collect taxes under duress.
- **Class Conflict:** Artisans resent Soulsteel monopolies; peasants question samurai who fail to repel Circle attacks.
- **Marriage & Alliances:** Political marriages, adoption, or hostage traditions can entangle characters in negotiation arcs.

Use these structures to shape stakes beyond combat—negotiations, honour tests, and community support can determine whether regions stand with or against the party.
