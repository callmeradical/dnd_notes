The Cavern of Unspoken Truths
=============================

On the ground near the remains of a few of the skeletons you find a book
of poetry with a few notes scribbled on the inside of the cover, the
book has no title.

Outline
-------

The long road

The whispers of the dead

Treasure room

Boss fight (Bard of Lolth, spiders and webs)

1.The long road
---------------

**Sights**. *A long hallway 15 feet wide and 120 feet long made of
finely hewn stone. Spiderwebs and dust are thick on the walls and
floors, faint light seems to be flowing in from the end of the hallway.*

**Sounds.** *You hear the sound of wind howling in the corridor, a howl
reminiscent of a windy winters night. There is another sound beneath
that sounds constant, perhaps moving water.*

**Smells.** *The air is surprisingly fresh.*

As you walk along the long hallway you see traces of light flowing into
the hall.

**Wisdom (Perception) Check DC10** to spot the traps that litter this
hallway. Each failure results in a more damaging trap.

The path before you gives way to a large gorge 20 feet across with no
discernible way around.

A spirit appears from the wall to the rear. He communicates in common.
He informs that there are trials ahead to determine your worthiness.

When role playing the spirit… He is the spirit of Arcos’ mentor, the
last person to receive the gifts that this place will give. He is old,
and talks in a gravelly weakened voice. He wears a fine suit with no
obvious tears or rips. Allister.

He disappears into the ether, presenting a puzzle on the wall. A bronze
plate is engraved with the words of someone named Allister, and it reads
the following passage. The book they previously recovered has the primer
to find the passphrase.

**Handout 1a.**

> “On with the show!
>
> I implore you to perform wherever you go.
>
> Read between the lines,
>
> If it is knowledge you seek.
>
> We are but wolves among sheep.
>
> Everyone has their secrets to keep.
>
> Power can be gained from but a chat.
>
> One needs but to listen. ”

**Handout 1b.**

> 5:1:1, 1:2:4, 3:4:2, 8:5:3, 7:1:1, 2:6:7, 6:4:4, 4:6:1

2.Whispers of the Dead
----------------------

After reading the word out loud the spirit shows them the path, a door
behind the plate opens and descends further downward.

You come to the bottom of the stairs. A large metal door bars your path.
As the door opens you see another room 30x30 ft, and along the walls are
words written in all manner of languages, once all party members are in
the room the door shuts and dancing lights appear, the lights emerge
from clay pots that have been placed in alcoves around the room. 6
will-o-wisps appear.

A singular light approaches Amiel and begins telling him a story he knew
in his life, about a great creature of the sea. You hear the story
echoed by each of the lights. Each party member makes their initial
saving throw. In order to complete this challenge.

The party can…

> Destroy all the pots,
>
> Kill all the wisps,
>
> All succeed wisdom saving throws of DC13.

The wisps act on initiative 20.

You hear intense whispering between the dancing lights, disembodied The
wisps cause everyone to make the wisdom save, on a failure they take 1d6
psychic damage and are frightened of a single wisp, every time they down
one, the wisp they are afraid of changes.

After they are finish, the door opens to the treasure room.

3.Treasure Room
---------------

The large door at the end of the room opens and the wisps disappear,
their words no longer audible as they fade away. A bright light fills
the room, mountains of gold and gems, armor, and weapons fill the room
beyond. You can see braziers illuminating the room beyond.

As the characters walk in, the floor is hard to walk on. Coins slide and
move beneath their feet. (Disadvantage on stealth if moving, and
difficult terrain), they eventually find the items that Fernando has,
give high-end magical items to other party members, with as much gold as
they can carry.

They lift some of the items up and there is a sticky silk like substance
attached to some of the weapons, this summons the spider queen.

“Defeat my champion and you can leave with whatever you can carry.
Refuse and you die.”
