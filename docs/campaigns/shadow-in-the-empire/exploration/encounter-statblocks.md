# Q'barra Random Encounter Index

Detailed encounter pages for all random encounters during Q'barra exploration. Each encounter includes full statblocks using the NPC template format, tactics, roleplay information, and environmental details.

## Wildlife Encounters (d8)

### 1. [Velociraptor Pack](encounters/wildlife/velociraptor-pack.md)
**Pack hunters (2d4+1), CR 1/4 each**  
Intelligent pack hunters that coordinate attacks and use hit-and-run tactics.

### 2. [Jungle Cat](encounters/wildlife/jungle-cat.md)  
**Solitary predator, CR 1**  
Stalks party for hours before ambushing during rest periods.

### 3. [Giant Constrictor Snake](encounters/wildlife/giant-constrictor-snake.md)

### 5. [Spinosaurus](encounters/wildlife/spinosaurus.md)  
**Apex predator, CR 13**  
Massive territorial dinosaur that drives off intruders.

### 6. [Hydra](encounters/wildlife/hydra.md)  
**Multi-headed guardian, CR 8**  
Guards swamp pools and waterways.

### 7. [Pteranodon Flock](encounters/wildlife/pteranodon-flock.md)

**Petrifying predator, CR 3**  
Ambushes from vine-covered dens using petrifying gaze.

## Usage Notes

**For GMs:**
- Each encounter link provides complete statblocks, tactics, and roleplay guidance
- Encounters are designed for a level 13 party
- Mix social, combat, and environmental challenges
- Use encounter triggers from the main exploration rules

**Encounter Frequency:**
- Roll 1d4 each travel period (morning, afternoon, night)
- On a 1, roll for encounter type, then specific encounter
- Roughly 2 encounters every 3 days of travel

**Customization:**
- Adjust numbers and challenge ratings for your party size
- Use environmental details to enhance immersion
- Incorporate encounters into larger narrative beats
