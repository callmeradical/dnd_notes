# Frostwood Outpost

Perched on Nagano’s northern edge, the Frostwood Outpost marks the last palisade before the haunted woods. Militia patrols, traveling merchants, and nascent adventurers all cycle through its gates, making it a natural staging ground for missions tied to *The Last Whisper of Steel*.

## Layout
- **Watchtower:** Wooden tower with signal lanterns and a clear view of the Frostwood treeline.
- **Barracks:** Bunks for up to twenty militia members, plus an armory stocked with polearms, bows, and emergency Soulsteel shards.
- **Palisade & Gatehouse:** Sharpened log walls and a permanently manned gate.
- **Supply Depot:** Stores dry rations, healing kits, and cold-weather gear; doubles as a rescue cache.
- **Shrine of Tsuchi-no-Megumi:** Militia offer prayers to the Earth Kami before each patrol; shrine doubles as a ritual site when spiritual imbalance is detected.

## Command Staff
- **Captain Shiro Tanabe (half-orc):** Tactical, even-tempered, and fiercely protective of Nagano. Values clear intel and decisive action.
- **Lieutenant Akari Fujita (elf):** Lead scout with encyclopedic knowledge of Frostwood trails; brings dry humor and detailed field reports.
- **Militia Rotations:** Twelve to twenty guards on staggered shifts covering sentry duty, supply management, and quick-response patrols.

## Core Duties
- **Defensive Screen:** Patrols the Nagano road and keeps watch for yokai, bandits, or Obsidian Circle agents testing the boundary.
- **Traveler Aid:** Issues warnings, weather reports, and temporary shelter to caravans or pilgrim groups.
- **Intelligence Hub:** Consolidates sightings of yokai movement, shrine desecrations, and unusual celestial events for the mayor and temple leadership.
- **Emergency Response:** Acts as launch point for rescue missions when villagers or scouts vanish within the Frostwood.

## Ongoing Problems (Adventure Hooks)
- **Missing Scouts:** Two rangers vanished after reporting strange chittering near a sealed shrine. Captain Tanabe offers a sizable reward for confirmation of their fate.
- **Jiro the Thorn’s Bandits:** A clever highwayman uses the forest canopy for ambushes. Kana Yoshiro seeks allies to smoke him out.
- **Spiritual Disruption:** The shrine’s wards flicker during Tsukuyomi’s brightest nights. Reiko Matsuda suspects corruption spreading beneath the roots.
- **Raven Yokai Sign:** A giant raven yokai circled the outpost during Hideo’s funeral. Interpreting its omen could change how Nagano prepares for the coming conflict.

Use the outpost as a repeatable stopover: characters can resupply, gather rumors, and feel the weight of duty as the Frostwood presses ever closer.
