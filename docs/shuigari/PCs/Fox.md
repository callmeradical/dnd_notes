## Background

Fox is a masked assassin whose identity remains shrouded in mystery. The mask serves both practical and symbolic purposes - concealing their face while representing the distance they maintain from their past and their emotions.

## Current Situation

### Master Hideo's Death
Fox must confront and mourn the death of Master Hideo, though their relationship to the forgemaster remains unclear. The loss weighs heavily, suggesting a connection deeper than Fox has revealed.

### The Kenshin Mystery
Fox grapples with memories of a "brother" named Kenshin - but these memories are false, implanted by Shindarasu through the Hollowed Breath. The realization that entire relationships in their mind never existed creates a profound identity crisis.

**The Implanted Memory:**
- Kenshin presented as Fox's brother
- Memories feel real and emotionally resonant
- Planted by Shindarasu (the Cracked Shell)
- Grants 4 temporary HP but causes narrative corruption
- May spread false memories to other party members

### Memory Vulnerability
Fox's experiences with false memories make them particularly vulnerable to further manipulation. Every memory must now be questioned - which are real? Which are planted? Can they trust their own mind?
