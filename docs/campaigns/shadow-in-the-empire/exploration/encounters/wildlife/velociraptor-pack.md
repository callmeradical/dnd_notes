# Velociraptor Pack

## Velociraptor
*Small beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 13 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 10 (3d6)  
**Speed** 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 6(-2) | 16(+3) | 11(+0) | 4(-3) | 12(+1) | 6(-2) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Pack Tactics.** The velociraptor has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the velociraptor's allies is within 5 feet of the target and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Actions

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) slashing damage.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 2d4+1 velociraptors  
**Environment:** Dense jungle, hunting grounds  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random wildlife encounter (#1 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Intelligent pack hunters that coordinate attacks
- Curious about new prey and will stalk before attacking

**Mannerisms**

- Click and chirp to communicate with pack members
- Test defenses with feints before committing to attack
- Circle prey to find weaknesses

**Tactics**

- Hunt in coordinated packs, using pack tactics advantage
- Use hit-and-run strategies, darting in to attack isolated targets
- Focus on spellcasters or wounded party members first
- Attempt to separate the party by driving some members into difficult terrain
- Retreat if more than half the pack is killed

**Environment Notes**

- Often encountered in areas with thick undergrowth for cover
- May use fallen logs or rocky outcroppings for elevated attack positions
- Leave distinctive three-toed tracks in mud or soft ground

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 2d4+1 for pack size (3-9 velociraptors)
- Consider party positioning - are they in single file on a jungle path?
- Note environmental features: thick underbrush, fallen logs, muddy patches
- Have the pack been tracking the party? Roll 1d4: 1-2 they've been following for hours

**Opening Scene**
> *The jungle falls unnaturally quiet. Even the ever-present buzz of insects seems muted. Then you hear it—a series of quick, sharp clicks echoing from the dense foliage around you, followed by answering calls from different directions.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Stalking (Optional)**
- If tracked, allow Perception checks (DC 14) to notice they're being followed
- Velociraptors maintain 60-80 feet distance, using stealth
- Party may try to prepare or choose better ground for the fight
- Build tension with occasional glimpses and sounds

**Phase 2: The Hunt Begins**
- Raptors attempt to surround the party before attacking
- Use pack tactics: 2-3 raptors attack the most isolated PC first
- Others wait for opportunity attacks or to exploit positioning
- Click and chirp in Draconic-like communication during combat

**Phase 3: Tactical Escalation**
- If initial attack fails, raptors use hit-and-run tactics
- Attempt to drive PCs into difficult terrain or separate them
- Focus fire on wounded targets or spellcasters
- Use their superior speed (50 ft.) to control engagement range

**Key Mechanics**
- **Pack Tactics:** Emphasize how they coordinate for advantage
- **Speed:** They can easily outrun most PCs if needed
- **Terrain:** Use jungle features - thick brush provides cover but slows movement
- **Morale:** Retreat if half the pack dies or if alpha is killed

**Skill Challenges During Combat**
- **Animal Handling (DC 15):** Might intimidate 1-2 raptors to hesitate
- **Intimidation (DC 13):** Frightening display could cause pack to pause
- **Nature (DC 12):** Recognize pack behavior patterns for tactical advantage
- **Survival (DC 10):** Identify escape routes or defensive positions

**Environmental Complications**
- **Thick Undergrowth:** Difficult terrain in some areas
- **Fallen Logs:** Provide elevation and cover for both sides
- **Muddy Ground:** May cause slipping (Dex save DC 10) during movement
- **Limited Visibility:** Heavy canopy creates shadowed areas

**Aftermath Considerations**
- Velociraptors make excellent mounts if tamed (very difficult)
- Pack behavior may attract larger predators to the noise of combat
- Fresh kills may lure scavengers or other dangers
- Tracks can be followed back to nesting areas (potential treasure/danger)

**Scaling for Difficulty**
- **Easier:** Reduce pack size to 2d4, or have some flee early
- **Harder:** Add 1-2 raptors, or introduce alpha with veteran stats
- **Environmental:** Add hazards like quicksand or unstable ground

**Story Integration**
- Raptors may be fleeing from larger predator (hooks future encounter)
- Pack behavior suggests intelligence - are they being directed?
- Successful navigation of encounter builds party confidence in jungle survival
