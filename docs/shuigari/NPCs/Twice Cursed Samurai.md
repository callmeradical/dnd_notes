# Twice Cursed Samurai

## Overview
A mysterious figure in samurai armor haunting an old tea house in Takashima. Reported as a vengeful spirit by locals, but the truth is far more complex.

## Current Situation
**Location:** Old tea house in Takashima  
**Status:** Mistaken for a vengeful spirit  
**Reputation:** Feared by locals

### Reported Manifestations
- Figure appearing in full samurai armor
- Objects moving on their own
- Sudden temperature drops
- Injuries to visitors (though no deaths)
- Recent onset - something triggered the haunting

## The Truth

The Twice Cursed Samurai is **not evil** and **did not murder Master Hideo**.

**Key Facts:**
- Not actually a vengeful spirit (or not only that)
- Innocent of Master Hideo's death
- Reasons for haunting the tea house unknown
- True nature and purpose unclear

## Master Hideo Connection

Master Hideo's murder has been attributed to the Twice Cursed Samurai, but this is false. The real circumstances of Hideo's death remain a mystery.

**Questions:**
- What actually happened to Master Hideo?
- Why is the Twice Cursed Samurai being blamed?
- Who benefits from this false accusation?
- Does the Twice Cursed Samurai know the truth about Hideo's death?

## The "Twice Cursed" Title

The name suggests dual curses or afflictions. 

**Possible interpretations:**
- Cursed in life and death
- Two separate curses affecting him
- Cursed by two different sources
- Metaphorical - cursed by fate twice over
