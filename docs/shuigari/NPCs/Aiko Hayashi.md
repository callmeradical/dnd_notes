# Aiko Hayashi

## Overview
A quiet, intense member of [[The Starcall Table]] gambling group, notable for being the only member who appears to be an active warrior. Her travel-worn armor suggests she's seen real combat, making her an unusual addition to a group of gamblers and merchants.

## Appearance
- Wears travel-worn armor that shows signs of use
- Carries herself with the bearing of someone trained in combat
- Quiet and observant demeanor
- Intense presence despite her silence

## Personality
- Quiet and reserved, especially compared to her companions
- Intense and focused
- Observant - watches more than she speaks
- Professional and composed
- Knows her limits and when to fold

## Notable Moments
During the storytelling wager between [[Shigeo]] and [[Kaoru Sugimoto]], Aiko simply shook her head and folded without fanfare. Her quiet assessment of the situation showed the same tactical awareness she likely brings to combat - know when you're outmatched and conserve your resources.

## Mysteries
- What brings a warrior to travel with a gambling circle?
- Is she providing security for the group, or is gambling simply another challenge she enjoys?
- Her combat experience seems significant - what battles has she fought?
- Why does someone who appears to be a capable fighter spend her time at card tables on the Silver Road?

## Connections
**The Starcall Table:**
- [[Kaoru Sugimoto]] - The elegant merchant and skilled storyteller
- [[Daisuke "Silver Pockets" Inoue]] - The charming younger gambler
- [[Haruki "The Boar" Takeda]] - The boisterous drinker

## Campaign Notes
- First encountered: Along the Silver Road during the caravan journey to Toriyama
- Witnessed the famous storytelling wager
- Continues to travel with the Starcall Table
- Her presence suggests the group might face dangers that require armed protection
