# Ancient Gatekeeper Menhir Circle

## Guardian Spirit
*Medium undead, neutral good*

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 45 (10d8)  
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 7(-2) | 15(+2) | 10(+0) | 10(+0) | 12(+1) | 17(+3) |

**Damage Resistances** acid, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** cold, necrotic, poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11  
**Languages** Common, Druidic  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Incorporeal Movement.** The spirit can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

### Actions

**Withering Touch.** *Melee Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (4d6+3) necrotic damage.

**Gatekeeper's Warning (1/Day).** All creatures within 30 feet of the spirit must make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is frightened for 1 minute and experiences horrifying visions of ancient aberrant threats. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

---

### Encounter Information

**Environment:** Ancient stone circle hidden in jungle overgrowth  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random lost world discovery (#4 on d8)  
**Discovery Method:** DC 15 Investigation or Survival to notice worked stones among natural formations

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Ancient guardian bound to protect the menhir circle from desecration
- Speaks in archaic Common mixed with Druidic phrases
- Deeply sorrowful about the world's current state but still dutiful

**Ideals**

- Sacred Trust: Must protect the seals that keep ancient evils at bay
- Balance Restored: Hopes to see the natural order returned to the world
- Knowledge Preserved: Ancient wisdom must not be lost to time

**Bonds**

- Eternally bound to guard the Gatekeeper circle until properly relieved
- Connected to the spirits of other Gatekeepers across the continent
- Remembers the druids who first created these wards millennia ago

**Flaws**

- Obsessive: Cannot focus on anything other than its guardian duty
- Suspicious: Assumes all mortals are potential corruptors of the circle
- Melancholic: Overwhelmed by sorrow at the world's current state

**Mannerisms**

- Hovers protectively near the central menhir when speaking
- Voice echoes with the sound of wind through ancient forests
- Occasionally lapses into Druidic when emotional or agitated

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Contact:**
> "Hold, travelers. These stones are sacred to the old ways, older than your kingdoms, older than your wars. Why do you disturb this place of binding?"

**When Questioned About Purpose:**
> "Long ago, when the world was younger and darkness crept from beyond the stars, we raised these stones. They hold... things... that should not walk beneath sun or moon."

**If Threatened:**
> "I have guarded this circle for a thousand turnings of the seasons. I will not fail in my duty now, though the world crumbles around me."

### Tactics

**Non-Combat Approach**
- Prefers to warn intruders away rather than fight
- Will negotiate and share knowledge with respectful visitors
- Offers blessings to those who help restore or protect the circle

**Combat Approach**
- Only fights if the circle is threatened or if attacked first
- Uses Gatekeeper's Warning to show visions of what the seals contain
- Focuses on driving off intruders rather than killing them
- Retreats into the central stone if reduced to 10 hit points

**Retreat Conditions**
- Flees only if the circle itself is in immediate danger of destruction
- Will not abandon post under any other circumstances
- Death releases the spirit to finally find rest

### Knowledge

**What It Knows**
- The location and purpose of other Gatekeeper sites across Khorvaire
- Ancient lore about aberrant threats and how to combat them
- The proper rituals to maintain or strengthen the binding circle
- Recent changes to the planar boundaries due to wyrm activity

**What It Can Offer**
- **Gatekeeper's Blessing:** Protection from aberrant creatures for 7 days
- **Ancient Knowledge:** Information about pre-human civilizations
- **Ward Maintenance:** Instructions on how to strengthen the circle
- **Safe Rest:** The circle provides sanctuary from random encounters for one night

### Circle Description

**Physical Appearance**
- Seven weathered stone menhirs arranged in a perfect circle, 30 feet in diameter
- Central altar stone covered in worn Druidic runes and nature symbols
- Faint magical aura visible to detect magic (abjuration school)
- Vines and moss partially conceal the stones, but the circle is still intact

**Magical Properties**
- **Detect Evil and Good** reveals strong aberrant auras contained within the stones
- **Dispel Magic** cast at the circle releases horrible screaming sounds but fails
- **Desecration** of the altar weakens the seals and may release bound horrors

**Potential Rewards**
- **Gatekeeper Shard:** Fragment of warding stone that provides +1 AC vs. aberrations
- **Druidic Scroll:** Contains *Protection from Evil and Good* and *Detect Magic*
- **Nature's Blessing:** Advantage on saves against disease and poison for 7 days

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine discovery method: party notices worked stones among natural formations
- Roll for circle's current state: intact, partially damaged, or weakening
- Note party composition: druids, clerics, and nature-focused characters react differently
- Consider time of day: dawn and dusk are when the guardian is most active

**Opening Scene Options**

**Discovery Through Investigation:**
> *Among the twisted vines and moss-covered logs, you notice something unusual—stones too regularly placed to be natural. As you clear away centuries of growth, ancient menhirs emerge, weathered but still standing in a perfect circle. At the center, an altar stone bears carved symbols that seem to shift in the dappled sunlight.*

**Guardian's Greeting:**
> *A translucent figure materializes above the central stone—an ancient human in simple robes, face marked with ritual scars. When it speaks, its voice carries the whisper of wind through long-dead forests: "Hold, travelers. These stones are sacred to the old ways, older than your kingdoms, older than your wars. Why do you disturb this place of binding?"*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Initial Contact (Social - Primary)**
- Guardian appears to assess party's intentions and threat level
- Speaks in archaic Common mixed with Druidic phrases
- Tests party's knowledge of ancient lore and respect for sacred sites
- **Religion Check (DC 13):** Recognize Gatekeeper symbols and purpose
- **History Check (DC 16):** Recall legends of the druid orders who built these circles
- **Nature Check (DC 12):** Understand the natural balance implications

**Phase 2: Negotiation and Knowledge Exchange**
- Guardian becomes more helpful if party shows respect and genuine curiosity
- Shares warnings about regional threats and bound dangers
- May offer blessings or aid if party helps maintain the circle
- **Insight Check (DC 11):** Recognize the guardian's deep sadness and burden
- **Persuasion Check (DC 14):** Gain guardian's trust and detailed information
- **Arcana Check (DC 15):** Understand the magical bindings and their current state

**Phase 3: Conflict Resolution (If Needed)**
- Only occurs if party threatens the circle or attacks the guardian
- Guardian fights defensively to drive away threats, not to kill
- Uses fear effects to demonstrate the horrors the circle contains
- **Intimidation Check (DC 16):** Frighten guardian with superior force (negative outcome)
- **Deception Check (DC 18):** Lie about intentions (guardian sees through easily)

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Speaking Druidic or showing druid/ranger class features
- Offering to help maintain or protect the circle
- Demonstrating knowledge of aberrant threats or planar lore
- Showing respect for ancient traditions and natural balance

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Threatening the circle or attempting to damage stones
- Dismissing the guardian's warnings as superstition
- Showing symbols of aberrant powers or carrying tainted items
- Acting with disrespect toward sacred sites or nature

**Key Cultural Notes**
- Guardian has been alone for centuries and craves meaningful conversation
- Values duty and sacrifice above personal desires
- Views modern civilization as chaotic and shortsighted
- Considers aberrant creatures the greatest threat to all life

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- **Gatekeeper's Blessing:** Party gains resistance to psychic damage for 7 days
- **Ancient Wisdom:** Detailed map showing locations of other Gatekeeper sites
- **Sacred Knowledge:** Instructions for ritual to strengthen planar barriers
- **Safe Sanctuary:** Circle provides magical rest benefits and protection

**Success**
- **Guardian's Warning:** Information about regional aberrant activity
- **Basic Blessing:** Advantage on saves against fear effects for 24 hours
- **Historical Lore:** Knowledge about pre-human civilizations and their threats

**Failure**
- Guardian remains suspicious but doesn't attack
- Limited information sharing, only basic warnings
- No benefits or blessings offered

**Critical Failure**
- Guardian views party as potential corruptors
- Immediate use of Gatekeeper's Warning ability
- Combat if party doesn't immediately retreat

**Combat Tactics Deep Dive**

**Defensive Strategy**
- Guardian never initiates combat unless circle is directly threatened
- Uses incorporeal movement to stay near the central altar stone
- Employs Gatekeeper's Warning to show visions of what seals contain
- Focuses on fear and intimidation rather than direct damage

**Environmental Advantages**
- Can move through stone menhirs for cover and positioning
- Circle provides spiritual anchor, increasing guardian's resolve
- Sacred ground may impose disadvantage on attacks by evil creatures
- Disturbing the altar stones weakens the guardian but may release bound horrors

**Spell-Like Abilities in Combat**
- **Turn 1:** Gatekeeper's Warning to frighten and disorient attackers
- **Subsequent Turns:** Withering Touch against persistent threats
- **Defensive:** Uses incorporeal movement to avoid direct confrontation
- **Desperate:** May threaten to break seals if pushed to near-death

**Non-Combat Resolutions**

**Mutual Benefit Scenarios**
- Party helps repair damaged menhirs in exchange for blessings
- Information exchange about aberrant threats in the region
- Guardian provides sanctuary in exchange for news of the outside world
- Cooperation in strengthening the magical bindings

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Combat Occurred**
- Other Gatekeeper spirits become aware of the conflict
- Circle's protective wards may be weakened by violence
- Bound entities might sense the disturbance and test their prisons
- Local druids and rangers may investigate reports of supernatural conflict

**If Resolved Peacefully**
- Guardian remembers party favorably for future encounters
- Other Gatekeeper sites may offer better reception
- Knowledge gained helps party recognize and counter aberrant threats
- Possible recurring ally for battles against extraplanar invaders

**Circle Interaction Options**

**Restoration Activities**
- **Stone Repair:** Requires mason's tools and Constitution (Athletics) DC 15
- **Rune Renewal:** Needs druid or cleric with paint/chalk, Religion DC 14
- **Cleansing Ritual:** Remove corruption, requires holy water and incense
- **Ward Strengthening:** Arcana DC 16 with rare material components

**Research Opportunities**
- **Historical Investigation:** 4 hours study reveals circle's creation date and purpose
- **Magical Analysis:** Detect Magic and Identify reveal binding spells and their targets
- **Planar Studies:** Commune or similar divination shows what entities are sealed
- **Druidic Translation:** Reading ancient Druidic reveals maintenance instructions

**Blessing Effects**

**Gatekeeper's Blessing (7 days)**
- Resistance to psychic damage and advantage on Wisdom saves
- Can sense aberrant creatures within 120 feet as a vague unease
- Dreams provide cryptic warnings about impending aberrant threats
- Animals are naturally drawn to blessed characters, offering minor aid

**Sacred Rest (One Night)**
- Long rest provides maximum hit points instead of rolling
- Spell slots recover as if taking a long rest plus short rest
- Natural healing removes one level of exhaustion
- Prophetic dreams may provide insight into current quest

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Guardian reveals that other circles are failing, creating campaign arc
- Sealed entity begins communicating through dreams, tempting party members
- Circle's location becomes strategic point in conflict with aberrant cult
- Guardian's relief of duty requires party to find replacement or successor

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Guardian is more trusting, offers blessings more readily
- **Harder:** Circle is partially breached, bound entities influence the encounter
- **Environmental:** Encounter during eclipse when seals are weakest
- **Social:** Multiple guardian spirits appear, each with different personality and demands
