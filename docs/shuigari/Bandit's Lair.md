# Bandit's Lair

## Important NPCs
1. **Raulf (he/him)** – Captured merchant eager for rescue; offers intel on lair layout and recent bandit activity. Middle-aged, greying hair, thick beard.
2. **Jorik (he/him)** – Disillusioned guard once loyal to the Jarl; willing to aid the party for treasure and reveals secret entrances and patrol patterns. Scruffy, early twenties, nervous energy.
3. **Lyra (she/her)** – Cultist spy observing the bandits for Death's Shadow. Striking, cautious, seeks safe passage in exchange for cult intel but may sabotage the party.

## Relevant Scenes
1. **Entrance Hall:** Two heavily armed sentries challenge intruders; one carries a lair map and shipment notes.
2. **Ashen Cavern:** Ash-coated remains of half-dragon cultists rise as zombies, immune to fire and exploding on defeat.
3. **Treasure Room:** Hoard of valuables plus a shrine and prophecies honoring Death's Shadow.
4. **Ritual Chamber:** Cultists mid-ritual to summon Death's Shadow; arcane symbols and traps saturate the room.
5. **Dragon's Lair:** Climactic confrontation with Death's Shadow, a devastating dragon demanding every resource the party has.

## Locations
1. **Entrance Chamber:** Narrow tunnel to a torchlit guard post; heavy door seals behind intruders.
2. **Prisoner's Chamber:** Cage-lined room of captives held for ransom; freeing them triggers alarms.
3. **Trapped Hallway:** Pressure plates launch wall spikes while archers snipe from raised platforms.
4. **Armory:** Stockpile of gear protected by a near-invulnerable golem requiring clever disabling.
5. **Final Chamber:** Vast pit chamber housing cultists, half-dragons, treasure, and the summoning ritual.

## Hints & Clues
1. Rumors of bandit raids accompanied by dragonlike creatures.
2. Villagers burdened by unexplained taxes from the Jarl of Vindheim.
3. Burned village survivors describe ash-marked attackers.
4. Evidence of underground passages near the lair.
5. Strange chanting and green light emanate from within the hideout.
6. Intercepted note detailing the cult's ritual to summon Death's Shadow.
7. Half-dragon mercenaries bolster lair defenses.
8. Regional map marking the lair as critical with notes about an impending event.
9. Proof the Jarl funds the bandits for protection in secret.
10. Mysterious artifact tied to the ritual awaits deciphering.

## Magical Items
1. **Dragon's Fang Sword (Major):** Bonus damage versus dragons; slices through scales.
2. **Ashen Fire Shield (Major):** Fire resistance and a triggered fiery burst.
3. **Potion of Dragon Breath (Minor):** Grants temporary cone breath weapon.
4. **Scroll of Ashen Wings (Minor):** Bestows short-lived flight on wings of ash.
5. **Flask of Dragon's Blood (Minor):** Temporary fire resistance and enhanced physicality.

## Relevant Creatures
1. **Bandits:** Skilled fighters with solid equipment.
2. **Half-Dragons:** Cultists transformed for superior strength and fire breath.
3. **Dragon Wyrmlings:** Trained young dragons bolstering lair defense.
4. **Fire Elementals:** Summoned allies of the cult.
5. **Ash Zombies:** Undead husks erupting in fiery bursts upon defeat.
