# Draconic Stone Glyphs

## Glyph Warden Construct
*Large construct, neutral*

**Armor Class** 16 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 85 (10d10+30)  
**Speed** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18(+4) | 6(-2) | 16(+3) | 3(-4) | 10(+0) | 1(-5) |

**Damage Immunities** poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** understands Draconic but can't speak  
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Immutable Form.** The warden is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

**Magic Resistance.** The warden has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Glyph Resonance.** While within 30 feet of the draconic glyphs, the warden regains 5 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The warden makes two slam attacks.

**Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6+4) bludgeoning damage.

**Glyph Pulse (Recharge 5-6).** The warden activates the stone glyphs, causing them to emit a pulse of draconic energy. All creatures within 20 feet of the glyphs must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Command Word Response.** If a creature speaks a command word in Draconic within 30 feet, the warden must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it becomes inactive for 1 minute or until attacked.

### Encounter Information

**Environment:** Ancient standing stones carved with draconic script and symbols  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random lost world discovery (#7 on d8)  
**Discovery Method:** DC 12 Perception to notice worked stones, DC 16 Arcana to recognize draconic magical significance

### Glyph Site Description

**Physical Structure**
- Circle of seven standing stones, each 12 feet tall and covered in intricate carvings
- Central platform with complex geometric patterns and draconic script
- Glyphs glow faintly with magical energy, intensity varying with time and conditions
- Surrounding area cleared of vegetation in perfect circle extending 50 feet from center

**Draconic Inscriptions**
- Combination of historical records, magical formulae, and territorial claims
- Written in Ancient Draconic with layers of meaning and magical significance
- Some glyphs function as active magical wards and detection systems
- Certain passages contain prophecies or warnings about regional threats

**Magical Properties**
- **Detect Magic** reveals strong abjuration and divination auras
- Glyphs respond to draconic speech and certain magical triggers
- Site functions as permanent *alarm* spell detecting intruders
- Contains stored magical energy that can be accessed with proper knowledge

### Roleplay Information

**Warden Construct Behavior**
- Activates only when someone approaches within 20 feet of central platform
- Follows ancient programming to protect glyphs from damage or unauthorized access
- Responds to specific draconic command words if spoken correctly
- Cannot communicate but understands complex draconic instructions

**Site Atmosphere**
- Ancient power thrums through the carved stones
- Air shimmers with barely visible magical energy
- Draconic script seems to shift and move when viewed peripherally
- Sense of being observed by ancient, powerful intelligences

**Historical Significance**
- Marker stone for ancient draconic territorial boundaries
- Record of pre-human draconic civilization in the region
- Magical research notes from powerful dragon mages
- Warnings about sealed dangers and forbidden knowledge

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine glyphs' current magical activity: fully active vs. partially dormant
- Note any recent disturbances or attempts to study the site
- Consider whether local dragons know about this location
- Check party's knowledge of draconic language and magical research

**Opening Scene Options**

**Ancient Monument Discovery:**
> *Seven towering stones rise from a perfectly circular clearing, their surfaces covered in intricate carvings that seem to shimmer in the filtered sunlight. The draconic script covers every inch of the monoliths, and magical energy hums in the air around them. As you approach, the glyphs begin to glow more brightly, as if responding to your presence.*

**Guardian Activation:**
> *The moment you step within twenty feet of the central platform, stone grinds against stone. What you had taken for decorative elements on the standing stones unfolds into a large construct. Its form incorporates elements of the glyph designs, and its eyes glow with the same energy as the carved symbols. It moves to block your access to the ancient writings.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Site Investigation (Research/Puzzle Primary)**
- Party can study glyphs and attempt to understand their meaning
- Warden activates if central platform is approached too closely
- Draconic language skills essential for full comprehension
- **Arcana Check (DC 16):** Understand magical nature of glyph system
- **History Check (DC 18):** Recognize historical significance of draconic writings
- **Investigation Check (DC 14):** Notice patterns and recurring themes in script

**Phase 2: Guardian Challenge**
- Warden attempts to prevent unauthorized access to most sacred glyphs
- Can be deactivated with proper draconic command words
- Combat occurs if party persists without showing proper respect
- **Languages (Draconic) Check (DC 15):** Understand command word requirements
- **Religion Check (DC 14):** Recognize draconic ritual protocols for approaching sacred sites

**Phase 3: Knowledge Acquisition**
- Successful navigation allows detailed study of glyph contents
- Contains multiple layers of information requiring time and expertise to decode
- Some information may trigger additional magical effects or revelations
- **Multiple skill checks over extended time:** Full translation requires days or weeks

**Glyph Content Analysis**

**Historical Records**
- **History Check (DC 16):** Ancient draconic territorial claims and boundaries
- **Investigation Check (DC 14):** Genealogies of powerful dragon families
- **Arcana Check (DC 15):** Records of magical research and experimental results
- Information spans thousands of years of pre-human civilization

**Magical Formulae**
- **Arcana Check (DC 18):** Spell research notes and magical theory
- **Religion Check (DC 16):** Draconic religious and philosophical texts
- **Nature Check (DC 14):** Knowledge about regional magical phenomena
- Some formulae still functional and could be learned or activated

**Prophecies and Warnings**
- **Insight Check (DC 15):** Cryptic warnings about future threats
- **History Check (DC 17):** References to cyclical events and returning dangers
- **Arcana Check (DC 16):** Magical conditions that trigger prophetic events
- Some prophecies may be relevant to current regional or campaign events

**Command Word Discovery**

**Research Process**
- Requires fluent Draconic and multiple successful skill checks
- **Investigation Check (DC 16):** Identify recurring ritual phrases
- **Arcana Check (DC 17):** Understand magical activation requirements
- **Religion Check (DC 15):** Recognize proper ceremonial pronunciation

**Command Word Effects**
- **"Dorash Thrae" (Guardian Rest):** Deactivates warden for 1 hour
- **"Molik Shar" (Knowledge Seeker):** Allows safe approach to central platform
- **"Thurkear Daardendrian" (Ancient Memory):** Activates historical projection magic
- **"Vethara Usv" (Sealed Warning):** Reveals hidden prophecy texts

**Extended Research Opportunities**

**Daily Study Requirements**
- 8 hours per day of careful translation work
- Requires proficiency in Arcana and fluency in Draconic
- **Daily skill checks:** Arcana DC 15, Investigation DC 14, History DC 16
- Three successes in each skill reveals major information category

**Research Rewards**
- **Week 1:** Basic historical understanding and territorial knowledge
- **Week 2:** Advanced magical theory and spell research notes
- **Week 3:** Prophetic warnings and their interpretation keys
- **Week 4:** Complete understanding including hidden command words

**Knowledge Categories**

**Territorial Information**
- Ancient dragon territories and their modern implications
- Sacred sites and forbidden areas throughout the region
- Traditional dragon travel routes and seasonal patterns
- Neutral zones and ancient treaty boundaries

**Magical Research**
- Experimental spell designs and theoretical magical frameworks
- Dragonshard cultivation and enhancement techniques
- Planar research and dimensional manipulation studies
- Weather control and environmental modification projects

**Historical Events**
- Major draconic wars and their resolutions
- Interactions with pre-human civilizations
- Catastrophic events and their magical aftereffects
- Patterns of conflict and cooperation between dragon types

**Prophetic Content**
- Warnings about recurring threats and their timing
- Conditions that trigger ancient magical safeguards
- References to "the time of shadow and flame"
- Cryptic references to current campaign events

**Long-term Consequences**

**If Site Respected and Studied**
- Valuable repository of ancient knowledge for ongoing research
- Potential magical benefits from understanding glyph systems
- Positive attention from scholarly dragons interested in preserved lore
- Base of operations for magical research with unique resources

**If Site Damaged or Desecrated**
- Loss of irreplaceable ancient knowledge
- Possible hostile response from dragons who value the historical record
- Magical backlash from disrupted ward systems
- Reputation damage among scholars and draconic communities

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Prophecies contain specific warnings relevant to party's quest
- Historical records reveal locations of other important sites
- Magical research provides keys to understanding current mystical phenomena
- Dragon lords may seek party's knowledge about glyph contents

**Research Applications**
- Spell scrolls copied from ancient formulae
- Maps to other draconic sites and treasure locations
- Understanding of regional magical phenomena and their control
- Diplomatic intelligence for negotiating with draconic powers

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Some glyphs already translated by previous scholars
- **Harder:** Multiple wardens or active magical defenses
- **Environmental:** Site actively monitored by interested dragons
- **Political:** Knowledge becomes valuable in current draconic territorial disputes

**Treasure and Rewards**
- Ancient spell scrolls worth 500-2,000 gp each
- Maps showing locations of other significant sites
- Magical research providing permanent Intelligence bonus (rare)
- Political knowledge worth substantial amounts to appropriate buyers
