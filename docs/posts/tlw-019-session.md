**Arc:** The Iron Court (Arc 2)

## Session Summary

The party received Master Hideo's summons to the Whispering Willow Tea House, where they met Kazuki Takemura, the Twice-Cursed Samurai. He revealed the truth about Master Hideo's death and presented an ancient steel tablet inscribed with a prophecy - naming the party specifically as those who would come to save Takashima. After accepting employment to infiltrate the Upper Citadel, the party retired to the Iron Rest Inn for sake, where they reunited with the Starcall Table and learned Kaoru Sugimoto leads the Hidden Lotus Society. Kaoru enlisted their aid in finding a missing blacksmith's daughter, leading them to investigate the corrupt Master of the Watch Saan. Through clever sleight of hand, they stole one of Saan's coded ledgers, discovering a conspiracy hidden within guard records. The session ended with the party planning how to retrieve the cipher key and decode the message before the theft is discovered.

## Key Events

### I. The Whispering Willow Tea House - Meeting the Twice-Cursed

**The Summons:**
- Wooden token bearing Master Hideo's maker's mark + word "Debt"
- Directed to Whispering Willow Tea House at sunset
- Private alcove prepared, expensive tea service waiting

**Kazuki Takemura Arrives:**
- Scarred ronin enters (clouded white eye, cursed blade Yomi-no-Kiba)
- Immediately recognizable as the "Twice-Cursed Samurai" from legend
- Commands both fear and respect from tea house patrons

**The Story of Two Curses:**

*First Curse:*
- Twenty years ago, samurai of House Arashi
- Ordered to protect iron shipment instead of village
- Obeyed lord, 37 innocent souls burned
- Cast out from House Arashi - became masterless ronin
- Lives with guilt of choosing duty over innocents

*Second Curse:*
- Dueled yokai to protect a village
- Soul fractured in battle, bound to cursed blade
- Yomi-no-Kiba binds failed souls to it
- Half-living, half-dead existence
- Blade hungers, curse strengthens with each kill
- Whispers "forgive me" when forced to fight

**Master Hideo's Death - The Truth:**
- Kazuki killed Master Hideo at the smith's request
- Hideo dying from illness, knew Circle was coming
- Chose honorable death over torture for his secrets
- Final words: "Guard what they seek. Trust those who grieve true."
- Kazuki has carried guilt for three years

### II. Master Hideo's Steel Tablet - The Prophecy Revealed

**The Artifact:**
- Folded steel (tamahagane/soulsteel) tablet
- 8" tall, 5" wide, mirror-polished
- Acid-etched with silver inlay
- Inscribed night of Hideo's death (3 years ago)
- **Steel cannot lie** - this is absolute truth

**Front Side - Hideo's Final Words:**
> "The Last Instructions of Master Hideo Nakata, Smith of the Mountain, Guardian of the First Forge"
>
> *Written on the Night of the Broken Moon, in my final hours of clarity*
>
> "My time ends not by age but by necessity. The Obsidian Circle seeks what I know, and I will not let my knowledge serve their unmaking. I have asked Kazuki Takemura to grant me the mercy of a swift end, and he has agreed. Judge him not - he acts on my plea, not his desire.
>
> What lies beneath Takashima must remain sealed. The Circle seeks the First Chain - the binding that holds Tsurogami in its prison. They will use the boy heir's blood as the key.
>
> To those who would stand against this darkness, I leave my final work: a list of names. These souls will arrive when needed. Trust them. Guide them. Give them what they need to succeed where I have failed.
>
> The furnace remembers the steel it forged. Let them remember me."

**Back Side - The Mission & The Names:**

*Kazuki's Mission:*
> "To Kazuki Takemura, the Twice-Cursed, who carries my final burden:
>
> Guard what they seek until those who can truly protect it arrive. You cannot enter the citadel, but you can guide those who can. When the names below arrive in Takashima, find them. Test them. If they are worthy, employ them.
>
> This is my final request, and my final gift: permission to trust again."

### IV. The Iron Rest Inn - Sake and Reunions

**The Cursed Street:**
- Traveled to nearby street locals avoid
- Shuttered buildings, protective wards everywhere
- Prayer strips, chalk symbols, atmosphere of fear
- Only the Iron Rest Inn has lights burning

**Mama Tora Kaneshiro:**
- Mountain of a woman (60), former blacksmith's daughter
- Husband Kuro died 3 years ago (same week as Hideo)
- Inn once thrived, now nearly empty due to "curse"
- Stubborn, refuses to abandon inn despite losses
- Named on Hideo's tablet: "Daughter of iron, widow of courage, keeper of cursed street"

**Her Reaction to the Tablet:**
> "Kuro died three years ago. Same night Hideo did. Same night Hideo wrote this. He knew. Somehow he knew I'd end up here, alone, holding this place together with spite and sake. If Hideo trusted you enough to put you on that tablet... then I trust you too. The dead don't lie. Neither does steel."

**Sake Evening:**
- Mama Tora served Mountain Peak sake (her personal finest)
- Party bonded, processed prophecy revelation
- Discussed implications of being "chosen" by dead master

**The Starcall Table Arrives:**

Familiar faces from the caravan journey:

- **Daisuke "Silver Pockets" Inoue**
- **Haruki "The Boar" Takeda**
- **Aiko Hayashi**

**Kaoru's True Identity Revealed:**
- Leader of the Starcall Table (gambling/merchant front)

- Coin-splitting trick was REAL supernatural ability
- Works outside official channels to help common folk
- Named on Hideo's tablet: "Weaver of stories, seeker of truth"

**Her Reaction to the Tablet:**
> "He knew about the Hidden Lotus Society? He trusted us? We've been investigating the Circle for three years, as if our whole purpose was to prepare for THIS moment. If you're on this list... if HE saw you coming... then perhaps we actually have a chance."

### V. A New Quest - The Missing Blacksmith's Daughter

**Kaoru's Request:**

Missing Person Case:
- Young girl, daughter of **Kaito** (half-dragon blacksmith)
- Works in the Iron Market district
- Disappeared several days ago
- No trace, no witnesses, no body
- Father desperate for help

**The Problem:**
City guard refuses to investigate:
- **Master of the Watch Saan** (Middle Terrace commander) ignores it
- Claims "lack of resources" but really just corruption
- Won't help commoners with "minor" crimes
- Guard culture is rotten from top down

**Why Hidden Lotus Exists:**
> "When the guard won't protect the people, someone must. We fill the gaps - investigating crimes, hunting supernatural threats, bringing justice where the system refuses to. This is what we do."

**Party Agreed:** Will investigate both guard corruption and missing girl

### VI. Guard Tower Investigation - Confronting Corruption

**Journey to Middle Terrace Guard Tower:**
- Next morning after resting at Iron Rest Inn
- Imposing structure, headquarters for local Mountain Guard
- Professional appearance but lax atmosphere inside

**Kento Discovery:**
- **Kento** - Shigeo's eldest son from first marriage - works here!
- Guard under Master of the Watch Saan
- Reunion was complicated, emotional, tense
- Kento professional but wary
- Caught between family loyalty and duty

**Meeting Master of the Watch Saan:**

Atmosphere:
- Dismissive, arrogant, clearly corrupt
- Office lavishly furnished (beyond guard captain's salary)
- Five leather-bound ledgers on desk (guard records)
- Particular disdain for Shigeo

Saan's Position:
> "I've heard of you. The braggart of Nagano. Tell me - do your tall tales hold water here in Takashima, or will you drown in reality?"

Response to Missing Girl:
- Refused to help investigate
- Claimed resources "better spent elsewhere"
- Made clear he doesn't care about commoner problems
- Implied they were wasting his time

**The Heist:**
During conversation, party noticed the five ledgers. Through clever distraction and expert sleight of hand (Legerdemain), they managed to steal **one ledger** without Saan noticing.

### VII. The Coded Message Discovery

**Ledger Contents:**
- Standard guard duty rosters
- Patrol schedules and routes
- Incident reports
- Personnel assignments

**BUT - Last 5 Pages:**
- Sophisticated coded message
- Numbers, symbols, arrangements
- Not simple substitution cipher
- Appears to be **book cipher** (requires key text)

**The Cipher Key Realization:**
Party members recalled seeing a specific book in Saan's office:
- Unusual volume among military records
- Out of place, seemed important
- Likely the **primer/key** needed to decode message

**The Dilemma:**
- Stolen ledger will eventually be discovered missing
- Need the cipher key book from Saan's office
- Can't return openly without raising suspicion
- Time pressure before theft noticed

**Session Ended:** Party debating options for retrieving key book and returning stolen ledger
