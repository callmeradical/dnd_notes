Notes for The Last Whisper of Steel
Campaign: Shuigari, The Last Whisper of Steel
A blacksmith gives up his life to save his grandson and more importantly stall the return of the Woven Tyrant. Master Hideo Nakata, the last known forgemaster, has made swords and weapons for the most dangerous of samurai. He has forged soulsteel blades and with these amazing weapons many lives have been taken. Each wielder of a soulsteel blade must give a portion of their lives to create the blade. There is one blade for each of the shrines of Daichi No Kame. The forging of these blades required the temple sites.
￼
Here are seven legendary samurai for whom Master Hideo forged Soulsteel blades. Each carries a unique burden, philosophy, or fate, with their weapons reflecting their souls.

⸻

1. The Twice-Cursed Samurai (Sōma no Kenshiro)
	•	Blade: Jisei no Kiba (Fang of the Final Verse) – A soulsteel katana that hums a sorrowful lament when unsheathed.
	•	Fate: Sōma no Kenshiro was a principled man turned revenant, driven by vengeance against those who slaughtered his clan. He hunted them to extinction, but in doing so, he lost everything. The first curse was his oath of vengeance, and the second was that it left him nothing once fulfilled.
	•	Final Fate: He vanished into the northern wastes, sword in hand, whispering to ghosts that only he could see. Some say his soul is bound to his blade, forever awaiting an opponent worthy of breaking his curses.

⸻

2. The Laughing Demon (Tenkai of the Crimson Gale)
	•	Blade: Shōgetsumei (Moonlit Laughter) – A wavy, flame-like soulsteel blade that leaves afterimages, as if the wielder moves faster than the eye can track.
	•	Fate: Once a brilliant swordsman of the Imperial Guard, Tenkai turned rogue when he saw the hypocrisy of the court. He became a duelist and wanderer, laughing as he cut down the corrupt. Many sought him, none defeated him.
	•	Final Fate: Tenkai was ambushed by Hikari paladins who deemed him too dangerous. Even as he bled out, he laughed, saying, So the righteous need assassins, too?” before vanishing into the night. No body was ever found.

⸻

3. The One-Armed Crane (Lady Hoshiko, Keeper of the Winds)
	•	Blade: Kazemaru (The Wind’s Perfect Path) – A curved soulsteel nodachi, impossibly light, able to cut even falling leaves without disturbing them.
	•	Fate: Once a legendary swordmaster, Lady Hoshiko lost her sword arm in battle yet never stopped fighting. With her remaining hand, she mastered a one-handed style that made her strikes as fluid as the wind.
	•	Final Fate: She vanished at sea, defending a sacred vessel against a monstrous storm yokai. Some say she rides the wind itself, appearing only when a warrior fights with perfect grace.

⸻

4. The Wolf in the Iron Mask (Daigorō the Silent)
	•	Blade: Shizukamaru (Silent Thunder) – A heavy soulsteel greatsword that absorbs sound, making its swings deathly quiet.
	•	Fate: A warrior who was never seen without his iron mask, Daigorō took a vow of silence after being betrayed by his own daimyo. His only words before donning the mask: “I will never speak for liars again.”
	•	Final Fate: He was last seen guarding a remote bridge, refusing to let anyone pass without proving their honor in a duel. Eventually, he and his blade disappeared, leaving only the mask, embedded in the wood.

⸻

5. The Ashen Widow (Tsukiko of the Last Ember)
	•	Blade: Akebono (Dawn’s Ember) – A soulsteel tanto, its edge always warm to the touch, glowing in darkness.
	•	Fate: A fire-wielding onna-bugeisha, Tsukiko was once a noble’s wife, but her home was burned, and she alone survived. She took up the short blade and became an assassin, hunting those responsible. Each death she delivered was like a funeral pyre.
	•	Final Fate: When her vengeance was done, she set herself aflame, walking into the mountains, saying, “A blade that knows only sorrow should never know peace.” Her dagger remains, untouched by time or flame.

⸻

6. The Shadow’s Ghost (Hibana no Arashi, the Night Lotus)
	•	Blade: Yoru-no-Hibana (Sparks of the Night) – A twin-bladed ninjatō, with a mirrored edge that vanishes in shadow.
	•	Fate: A shinobi commander of the Oni Waban, Hibana was a master of both deception and justice. She refused to betray her comrades, even when threatened with death.
	•	Final Fate: She vanished on the night of the Oni Waban purge. Some say she walks the Hidden Provinces, protecting the last shinobi enclaves from the shadows. Her blade is said to return only to the true heir of her lost clan.

⸻

7. The Stone Guardian (Rokurō of the Mountain’s Root)
	•	Blade: Daichi-no-Ishi (Earth’s Will) – A broad, chipped soulsteel blade, forged with stone dust, making it resonate with the land itself.
	•	Fate: A warrior-monk, Rokurō swore to protect the Forge of Daichi-no-Kame from those who would abuse soulsteel. He was said to be stronger than ten men, his blade capable of splitting boulders in half.
	•	Final Fate: When the forge was attacked, he stood alone, giving the smiths time to flee. When reinforcements arrived, all they found was his sword, buried upright in the earth, untouched by rust. Some believe his spirit became part of the land itself.

⸻

The Legacy of Hideo’s Soulsteel
	•	Each of these seven warriors carried a piece of Hideo’s craft, and their blades remain scattered across Shuigari.
	•	Some weapons still hold their spirits, awaiting the right wielder to claim them.
	•	Others are lost, their resting places whispered in myths and rumors.

Here are the legendary Soulsteel weapons forged by Master Hideo, each tied to its former wielder. These weapons hold powerful abilities, reflecting their wielders’ souls and legacies, and are balanced for Dungeons & Dragons 5e.

⸻

1. Jisei no Kiba (Fang of the Final Verse)

Weapon (katana, longsword), legendary (requires attunement by a creature who has lost something dear to them)
Forged for Sōma no Kenshiro, the Twice-Cursed Samurai, this blade hums softly, its sorrowful whispers felt rather than heard.

Properties
	•	Soulsteel Edge – The sword deals +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
	•	Echo of Vengeance – When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you can mark another creature within 30 feet. Your next attack against that creature has advantage, and the damage ignores resistances.
	•	Cursed Reflection – Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you can choose to remain at 1 HP instead. However, the blade brands you with a dark sigil—if you fall in battle again before your next long rest, you cannot be revived by any means.
	•	Final Verse – If the wielder willingly sacrifices themselves, the sword releases a wave of sorrow, dealing 8d10 psychic damage to all enemies within 60 feet (DC 18 Wis save for half).

⸻

2. Shōgetsumei (Moonlit Laughter)

Weapon (katana, scimitar), legendary (requires attunement by a Dexterity-based martial class)
A graceful, unpredictable blade, once wielded by Tenkai the Laughing Demon. The edge flickers like an illusion.

Properties
	•	+3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
	•	Phantom Strikes – When you hit an enemy, you can teleport up to 15 feet in any direction (no opportunity attacks).
	•	Duelist’s Wit – If you miss an attack, your next attack against the same creature is made with advantage.
	•	Moonlit Phantom – Once per long rest, you can cast Mirror Image as a bonus action.
	•	Crimson Gale (Ultimate) – If you land three attacks in one turn, you unleash a flurry of slashes that deals 6d8 slashing damage to all enemies within 10 feet.

⸻

3. Kazemaru (The Wind’s Perfect Path)

Weapon (nodachi, greatsword), legendary (requires attunement by a Samurai, Fighter, or Monk)
This blade is weightless in hand, as if the wind itself guides its wielder. It belonged to Lady Hoshiko, the One-Armed Crane.

Properties
	•	+3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
	•	Unburdened Grace – Your movement speed increases by 10 feet.
	•	Deflect the Storm – If you are targeted by a ranged attack, you can use your reaction to make a Dex save (DC 17). On a success, you redirect the attack to another target within 30 feet.
	•	Whisper of the Wind – If you move at least 20 feet before making an attack, your strike deals extra 2d8 force damage.
	•	Skyborne Cut (Ultimate) – Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you become weightless for 1 minute, gaining flight (60 feet speed) and can make one extra attack per turn.

⸻

4. Shizukamaru (Silent Thunder)

Weapon (greatsword), legendary (requires attunement by a Strength-based martial class)
The heavy, soundless blade of Daigorō the Silent, it muffles the world around it.

Properties
	•	+3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
	•	Silent Blade – Attacks with this weapon make no sound and do not break Stealth.
	•	Endless Vigil – You gain Darkvision (120 feet) and advantage on Insight checks.
	•	Immovable Warrior – If you plant this blade in the ground as a reaction, you gain resistance to all damage until the start of your next turn but cannot move.
	•	Silent Execution (Ultimate) – Once per long rest, you can make a single attack that, if it hits, deals 10d10 force damage and paralyzes the target for 1 round (DC 18 Con save to resist).

⸻

5. Akebono (Dawn’s Ember)

Weapon (tanto, dagger), legendary (requires attunement by a Monk, Rogue, or Warlock)
A blade of fire and sorrow, once wielded by Tsukiko, the Ashen Widow.

Properties
	•	+3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
	•	Everburning Blade – The dagger sheds dim light (30 feet) and deals extra 2d6 fire damage.
	•	Ashen Strike – If you reduce a creature to 0 HP, their body burns to ash, leaving no corpse.
	•	Phoenix Resilience – Once per long rest, if you drop to 0 HP, you instead heal 20 HP and release a pulse of fire (4d6 fire damage, 10-foot radius, DC 17 Dex save for half).
	•	Funeral Pyre (Ultimate) – Once per long rest, you can plunge this dagger into the ground, creating a 30-foot radius of flames for 1 minute. Enemies inside take 4d10 fire damage per round.

⸻

6. Yoru-no-Hibana (Sparks of the Night)

Weapon (twin ninjatō), legendary (requires attunement by a Rogue or Monk)
These twin blades, belonging to Hibana no Arashi, the Night Lotus, flicker like dying embers.

Properties
	•	+3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
	•	Shadowstep – As a bonus action, you can teleport up to 30 feet in dim light or darkness.
	•	Ghost Slash – Once per turn, if you hit while hidden, the target takes an extra 4d6 psychic damage.
	•	Blade of the Vanished – You can turn Invisible for 1 round after making a successful attack (1/short rest).
	•	Flicker Strike (Ultimate) – Once per long rest, you can make three attacks in a single action, each ignoring resistances and dealing 3d10 necrotic damage.

⸻

7. Daichi-no-Ishi (Earth’s Will)

Weapon (colossal blade, greataxe), legendary (requires attunement by a Strength-based class)
This mighty blade was wielded by Rokurō, the Stone Guardian, its weight nearly impossible to bear.

Properties
	•	+3 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
	•	Earthen Stance – While wielding this weapon, you cannot be moved against your will.
	•	Mountain’s Strength – You gain +2 Strength (max 22) and can wield this weapon one-handed.
	•	Shockwave Strike – As an action, slam the blade into the ground, creating a 15-foot shockwave. Creatures in range must make a DC 18 Strength save or be knocked prone and take 5d8 bludgeoning damage.
	•	Unyielding Titan (Ultimate) – Once per long rest, for 1 minute, you gain resistance to all damage, advantage on Strength checks, and your melee attacks deal double damage against objects and structures.

⸻
