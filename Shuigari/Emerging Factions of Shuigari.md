# Emerging Factions of Shuigari

Several movements exist outside the Empire’s official registry. Some oppose the Obsidian Circle; others risk tipping the balance in their favour. These notes expand the hooks referenced in earlier planning documents.

## The Blades of the Phoenix
- **Base:** Nomadic, currently active along the southern trade roads.
- **Goal:** Reignite the “phoenix cycle” by destroying old orders and guiding rebirth through controlled burns—both literal and political.
- **Methods:** Guerilla strikes on granaries and bureaucratic archives they deem corrupt. Seek Soulsteel embers to forge a symbolic phoenix blade.
- **Campaign Use:** Potential uneasy allies if convinced the Circle represents the true corruption. Their penchant for collateral damage can cause civilian backlash.

## The Ghostwalkers
- **Base:** Hidden monasteries within the Rei no Mori forest.
- **Goal:** Maintain the silence between worlds. They believe the dwindling of magic is a *correction* and try to prevent mortals from meddling with seals.
- **Methods:** Astral projection, dream infiltration, sabotage of rituals they deem reckless.
- **Campaign Use:** Their information on Shindarasu’s prison is unmatched, but they may sabotage shrine repairs if players fail to earn trust.

## Emerald Lotus Syndicate
- **Base:** Harado Prefecture ports; smugglers with polite etiquette.
- **Goal:** Profit from the scarcity of ritual reagents while claiming to “stabilise markets.”
- **Methods:** Trade monopolies, counterfeit shrine incense, covert alliances with Circle quartermasters.
- **Campaign Use:** Offer black-market resources or intel in exchange for access to ley nodes. Unmasking their Circle ties can sway public opinion in Harado.

## The Way of the Flame
- **Base:** Volcano monasteries near Mount Hoshigami.
- **Goal:** Preserve martial traditions blessed by Izanami no Kagutsuchi. They fear Soulsteel misuse and train warriors to enforce responsible forging.
- **Methods:** Trial-by-fire duels, confiscation of cursed blades, forging oaths.
- **Campaign Use:** Provide advanced smithing techniques or blessings if the party dedicates a weapon to their order. Oppose the Obsidian Circle’s desecration of furnaces.

## The Crimson Maw
- **Base:** River delta slums coined “Bloody Wharf.”
- **Goal:** Control river transport by wielding fear. They view Circle chaos as opportunity, not ideology.
- **Methods:** River piracy, hostage-taking, intimidation of shrine caretakers for “protection fees.”
- **Campaign Use:** As antagonists in travel arcs or unwilling informants who can be flipped with leverage over their leadership.

## Seekers of the Wheel
- **Base:** Traveling philosophers charting the “Wheel of Seasons.”
- **Goal:** Categorise each spiritual phenomenon into an eight-spoke wheel model, proving every event repeats. Believe they can predict when the seal fails.
- **Methods:** Archive exchanges, prophecy lectures, infiltration of temple libraries.
- **Campaign Use:** Their research can forecast Circle offensives or reveal unknown shrines. If corrupted, they may rationalise Shindarasu’s release as “the next turn of the wheel.”

Integrate these groups as quest patrons, rivals, or complications when the party seeks allies against the Circle. Each can anchor side arcs or introduce moral dilemmas aligned with Shuigari’s themes.
