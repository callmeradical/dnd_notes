### Background Feature
Your accent, mannerisms, figures of speech, and perhaps even your appearance
all mark you as foreign. Curious glances are directed your way wherever you go,
which can be a nuisance, but you also gain the friendly interest of scholars
and others intrigued by far-off lands, to say nothing of everyday folk who are
eager to hear stories of your homeland.

You can parley this attention into access to people and places you might not
otherwise have, for you and your traveling companions. Noble lords, scholars,
and merchant princes, to name a few, might be interested in hearing about your
distant homeland and people.

### Backstory
Monte Di Madre  (Berronar's Hearth) Mountain Mama, Berronar Truesilver (mother
of the dwarves) Giver of life... Oloric is a advocate for the Dwarves of "The
Mothers Mountain" or Berronar's Hearth... He has made many trips out of the
mountain. Unlike most of the Dwarves of his clan he has adventured and mingled
with many other peoples of the realm.

Clan Rumnaheim's gates have been closed to the outside world for millennia.
Even their closest allies have only been welcomed into the The Hearth's outer
halls.

Oloric's clan has a relationship with the "children of the mountain" A small
community of Goliath that live on the mountain and help the dwarves to fend off
intruders... They readily barter and trade services with them.
The council has paired me with a Goliath Warrior/druid from the "children of
the mountain" they have tasked us with finding a way to stop house alunno, and end their
continued invasion on our homeland... we are to complete this mission at all
cost!

#### My early years...
I was orphaned at an early age, and never new my birth parents. I was taken in
by my uncle and his wife as they were childless. I was brought up in a tough,
but loving environment. When I became of age I was expected to follow in my
uncles shoes and I became an apprentice Blacksmith.  Working long days in the
heat of the forge along side of the only father I ever knew "Rurik Rumnaheim" I
learned many Valuable lessons. Not only did I learn how to shape steel I also
learned of the deep and rich History of my people and the Mountain home in
which we live!

Even as I became quite skilled in the arts of fire and steel, there was always
something there making me feel incomplete... Something burning deep in my soul.
A feeling of restlessness that I dare not share with anyone. for the Dwarves of
my clan were expected to follow in their fathers footsteps and become
upstanding members of society!

One day while working on a shield for a priestly Cleric of our clan I struck My
hammer down upon the holy symbol that we were adorning to the center of the
shield, and it shattered into a thousand pieces. One of these pieces lodged
itself in my left eye. For sure they thought I was going to lose that eye, but
by some miracle my vision returned! The clerics of my clan saw this as an omen.
I was taken from my Uncle and brought to the monastery. The Clerics shield
which I was working on took an interest in me and showed me the ways of our
reverend mother... Even as I became a devout follower of the reverend Mother
and received the holy blessing of Clerichood something still yearned in me.
Something That I could no longer hold back... A need that could only be filled
by leaving my beloved home and hearth. I never felt at peace like I do when I'm
on the road in a foreign land!

We are known as the "Hermit Dwarves" to most outsiders. Our Mountain home gives
us all we need to prosper, but with the approaching intrusion of House Alunno
our way of life is threatened. The elders of my clan along with King Uraim have
called upon a few select individuals to Champion their interests...

#### The Traveler Dwarf prophecy...
An unlikely hero leads the Dwarves of Mont de Madre out of their mountain home
from which they will never return! Many Dwarves will perish in an Apocalyptic

This story is told as a children's Fairytale it the flavor of Pied Piper meets Ragnarok!
