# Ruins of Q'barra

Q’barra is layered with history — dragonborn, lizardfolk, goblinoid, colonial, and even the scars of the Age of Demons. These ruins provide exploration hooks, survival challenges, and thematic ties to wyrm-dominion.

---

## Types of Ruins

### 1. Dragonborn Clan Ruins
- Abandoned fortresses, shrines, and ceremonial halls from older generations.  
- Carved draconic runes, sealed weapon vaults, spectral guardians.  

### 2. Lizardfolk Sacred Sites
- Ziggurats, swamp temples, and canals honoring Progenitors or land spirits.  
- Murals of dragons, cursed pools, elemental guardians.  

### 3. Dhakaani Outposts
- Fortified keeps, tunnels, and bridges from goblinoid expansion.  
- Still-functioning traps, rust monsters, revenant hobgoblins.  

### 4. Sahuagin & Coastal Ruins
- Half-sunken shrines and drowned palaces.  
- Coral-encrusted stairs, sea hag covens, drowned undead.  

### 5. Age of Demons Remnants
- Cyclopean ruins, colossal bones, scorched glass plains.  
- Obsidian monoliths, rakshasa-bound wards, dragon-ghost echoes.  

### 6. Colonial Brelish & Cannith Ruins
- Mining towns, forts, extraction facilities abandoned after wyrm rise.  
- Cannith forgeworks, dragonshard caches, scavenger squatters.

## d12 Ruins Table
Roll 1d12 to generate a ruin site during travel or exploration.

1. **Dragonborn Watchtower** — Cracked and vine-choked, still guarded by a spectral warrior bound to duty.  
2. **Shrine of the Serpent Moon** — Lizardfolk ziggurat, its pool infested with venomous swarms.  
3. **Collapsed Dhakaani Tunnel** — Echoes of goblin drums resound; revenant hobgoblins rise at trespass.  
4. **Sahuagin Tide Temple** — Half-flooded ruin where sahuagin zealots conduct nightly rites.  
5. **Obsidian Monolith** — A shard of the Age of Demons, humming with Khyber energy; attracts fiendish spawn.  
6. **Cannith Extraction Station** — Dragonshard drills left half-buried; arcane guardians still patrol.  
7. **Dragonborn Ancestral Hall** — Murals glow faintly; unlocking the vault requires passing a draconic trial.  
8. **Lizardfolk Stone Circle** — Overgrown with moss; druids once bound elementals here, some remain hostile.  
9. **Dhakaani Fortress Gate** — Rusted iron still holds; inside waits a chamber of perfectly preserved arms.  
10. **Drowned Palace** — Coral and barnacles cover statues of sahuagin royalty; a sea hag lairs here.  
11. **Dragon’s Gravefield** — Petrified bones from the Age of Demons; infused with necrotic echoes.  
12. **Colonial Outpost Ruins** — Collapsed barracks and a shard vault beneath; occupied by scavenger raiders.  

---

## GM Guidance
- Each ruin should tie into **themes of survival, prophecy, or wyrm dominion**.  
- Use ruins as opportunities for **exploration challenges**, not just combat.  
- Sprinkle **hooks** (relics, wards, dragonshards) that foreshadow larger arcs.  
- Encourage player choices: plunder the ruins, respect them, or exploit them as bases.
