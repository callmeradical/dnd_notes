# Shuigari Civilization - Gaps Addressed

## Summary

Five comprehensive documents created to complete the "Checklist for Civilizations" framework, filling critical gaps in Shuigari's worldbuilding:

## Documents Created

### 1. Currency and Trade Systems (14,000+ words)
**What it covers:**
- Complete monetary system (Ryo/Bu/Shu/Mon)
- Rice-based koku measurement system
- Regional currency variations (Takashima Iron Mon, Imperial Ryo, etc.)
- Foreign currency handling
- Tax systems (rice tax, merchant taxes, mining taxes, port duties)
- Economic classes and typical wealth by social tier
- Comprehensive price lists (food, lodging, weapons, services, animals, tools)
- Black market economics
- Debt culture and money-lending
- Economic warfare and inflation

**Key Features:**
- Exchange rates clearly defined (1 Ryo = 4 Bu = 16 Shu = 16,000 Mon)
- Prices make sense relative to income levels
- Campaign integration with treasure guidelines
- Plot hooks for economic intrigue

### 2. Education and Literacy (12,000+ words)
**What it covers:**
- Literacy rates by class (35-40% overall, 95%+ samurai)
- Writing system types (Kaisho/Gyosho/Sosho/Kana)
- Educational institutions:
  - Domain academies for samurai
  - Temple schools (terakoya) for commoners
  - Artisan apprenticeships
  - Ninja clan training
  - Onmyoji magical training
  - Women's education
- Libraries and archives
- Book production and costs
- Specialized knowledge (smithing, martial arts, magic)
- Examination system for civil service
- Current educational trends and tensions

**Key Features:**
- Explains who knows what and why
- Provides social mobility through education
- Creates knowledge hierarchies
- Campaign hooks for lost texts and secret knowledge

### 3. Legal System and Courts (14,000+ words)
**What it covers:**
- Four pillars of justice (Loyalty, Hierarchy, Collective Responsibility, Confession)
- Sacred vs. Secular law (Imperial vs. Shogunate)
- Complete crime classifications (capital/major/minor)
- Court system (village/domain/Shogunate/Imperial)
- Criminal investigation procedures
- Law enforcement structure
- Civil law (property, contracts, debt)
- Punishments (execution methods, corporal punishment, exile, fines)
- Samurai legal privileges
- Temple sanctuary rights
- Corruption and jurisdictional conflicts

**Key Features:**
- Class determines justice received
- Detailed court procedures for dramatic trials
- Investigation methods for mystery plots
- Punishment as story consequences

### 4. Writing Systems and Record-Keeping (11,000+ words)
**What it covers:**
- Three script systems (Kaisho/Gyosho/Sosho) with usage rules
- Supplementary scripts (Kana, Runic, Ciphers)
- Calligraphy as art form
- Number systems and mathematics
- Abacus usage
- Record-keeping systems:
  - Government (tax, land, population, military)
  - Commercial (ledgers, credit, inventory, contracts)
  - Temple (rituals, congregations, donations)
  - Personal (genealogy, accounts, diaries)
- Document authentication (seals, witnesses, dating)
- Archival practices
- Regional variations

**Key Features:**
- Explains forgery difficulty
- Different scripts for different purposes
- Creates value for rare texts
- Authentication as plot device

### 5. Scientific and Technical Knowledge (10,000+ words)
**What it covers:**
- Metallurgy and materials science
  - Theoretical understanding (Five Metal Essences)
  - Practical techniques (temperature control, quenching, pattern welding)
  - Quality testing and standardization
- Medicine and anatomy
  - Medical theory (Four Humors)
  - Diagnostic techniques
  - Treatment methods (herbal, acupuncture, surgery)
  - Pharmaceutical knowledge
  - Public health
- Architecture and engineering
  - Earthquake-resistant design
  - Structural principles
  - Surveying and tools
- Astronomy and timekeeping
  - Calendar calculations
  - Eclipse prediction
  - Time measurement systems
- Agriculture and animal husbandry
- Alchemy and proto-chemistry
- Navigation and cartography
- Knowledge organization (guild secrets vs. public)

**Key Features:**
- Blends empirical knowledge with spiritual theory
- Explains why technology is where it is
- Creates valuable knowledge as treasure
- Guild secrets as power
