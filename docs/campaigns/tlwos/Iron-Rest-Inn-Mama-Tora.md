# The Iron Rest Inn - Cursed Street Scene

**Location:** Middle Terrace, Takashima City  
**District:** East of Smiths' Quarter, near the abandoned warehouse district  
**Reputation:** "The Cursed Street" - locals avoid after dark  
**Current State:** Nearly empty, struggling to survive, haunted by yokai  
**Scene Type:** Eerie downtime, celebration in isolation, yokai investigation hook

---

## THE STREET: WHISPERS AND WARNINGS

### Approaching the Inn (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> The streets grow quieter as you walk east from the bustling Smiths' Quarter. The forge-song fades behind you. The lamps grow fewer. And then... you're here.
>
> This street feels wrong.
>
> It's not abandoned - there are buildings, shops, homes. But they're... closed. Shuttered. Silent. Even though the sun hasn't fully set, every window is dark, every door barred. You see chalk marks on doorframes (Religion DC 12: protective wards against spirits). Prayer strips flutter from eaves, their ink faded by weather and time.
>
> The cobblestones are clean - too clean. No refuse, no debris, as if even the wind won't linger here. Your footsteps echo strangely, carrying farther than they should.
>
> And at the end of this street, standing alone: The Iron Rest Inn.
>
> It's the only building with lights burning. Three stories of dark wood and stone, it looks solid enough, but... worn. Paint peeling. Roof tiles cracked. One shutter hangs at an angle, creaking in the breeze that shouldn't exist here. The sign above the door shows an iron bed frame, but someone has scratched marks across it - claw marks? Or desperate attempts to warn travelers away?
>
> Through the windows, you see lamplight. Movement. Someone is inside.
>
> As you approach, a voice calls from a nearby house - an old woman's face appears briefly in a second-story window: "Don't stay there after dark! The tea house weeps!" Then her shutters slam closed.
