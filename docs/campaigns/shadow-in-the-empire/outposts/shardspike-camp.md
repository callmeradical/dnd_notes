# Shardspike Camp

**Location:** Approach to Pitchwall  
**Type:** Ramshackle mining camp turned smuggler outpost  
**Proprietor:** Overseer Vorn Blackshard

---

## Outpost Description

### **Physical Layout**
Built around the skeletal remains of old mining equipment, Shardspike Camp sprawls across several terraced levels cut into a hillside. Wooden walkways connect various shacks and storage areas, while the central area features a large tent that serves as both trading post and meeting hall. Mining machinery rusts in place, repurposed as decorative intimidation.

### **Atmosphere**
The air glimmers with dragonshard dust that catches the light, creating an otherworldly shimmer. The camp has a barely controlled chaos - valuable goods mixed with dangerous equipment, legitimate miners working alongside obvious criminals. An undercurrent of barely suppressed violence runs through all interactions.

### **Notable Features**
- Chained spinosaurus hatchling (Vorn's "pet") in reinforced pen
- Hidden storage caves carved into hillside  
- Elevated watch platforms with clear sightlines to approaches
- Emergency escape tunnels known only to inner circle

---

## Proprietor

### **Overseer Vorn Blackshard**
*Medium humanoid (half-elf), neutral evil*

**Appearance:** Slick half-elf with gold teeth and dragonshard-studded jewelry. Wears expensive clothes that clash with the rough camp environment. His hands are soft but his eyes are hard - clearly someone who lets others do dangerous work while reaping profits.

**Personality:** Charming and dangerous in equal measure. Claims to have "exclusive rights" to various mining sites through forged documents and intimidation. Treats the chained spinosaurus as both pet and threat, feeding it scraps while making veiled threats about what happens to those who cross him.

**Background:** Former legitimate mine supervisor who went underground when the colonial administration collapsed. Built a network of smugglers and claim-jumpers throughout the region.

**Services Offered:**
- Access to restricted mining sites (for hefty fees)
- Dragonshard authentication and valuation
- "Problem solving" services (muscle for hire)
- Black market connections throughout Q'barra

## Patrons & Rumors

### **Marros "Three-Finger" Kett**
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic neutral*

**Appearance:** Weathered human miner missing two fingers on his left hand from dragonshard accidents. His remaining digits are stained permanent blue from shard exposure. Wears patched mining clothes and has a nervous habit of flexing his damaged hand.

**Background:** Veteran miner who's worked Q'barra's deposits for over a decade. Survived multiple cave-ins and shard explosions through skill and luck. His missing fingers serve as constant reminder of the dangers below ground.

**Key Information:**
- **"The mines hum at night - not machinery, something else. Like the earth itself is restless."**
- **"Found shards that were warm to the touch in the deep tunnels. That's not normal."**
- **"Whatever's down there in Pitchwall, it's been getting more active. Equipment fails, tunnels shift."**

**Services:**
- **Mining Guidance:** Knows safest routes through dangerous areas (25 gp per day)
- **Shard Identification:** Can distinguish valuable from dangerous specimens (5 gp per assessment)
- **Hazard Detection:** Experience recognizing cave-ins, gas pockets, unstable ground (invaluable)

---

### **Zhalir Cannith-Bastard**
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

**Appearance:** Thin human with prematurely gray hair and ink-stained fingers. Wears a modified Cannith uniform stripped of house insignia. Carries a leather satchel full of broken tools and mechanical components that he constantly tinkers with.

**Background:** Disowned Cannith heir who lost family standing through political scandal. Maintains technical expertise but lacks house resources and protection. Trades in salvaged equipment and technical consultation.

**Key Information:**
- **"House Cannith sealed the deep chambers before the evacuation. Standard protocol."**
- **"Those extraction engines are still functional - I designed some of them myself."**
- **"The sealed chamber isn't just locked, it's warded. They were containing something dangerous."**

**Services:**
- **Technical Consultation:** Understanding Cannith equipment and procedures (50 gp per session)
- **Equipment Modification:** Improving mining tools and protective gear (25-100 gp)
- **Magical Item Repair:** Restoration of Cannith-made magical equipment (expertise rare)
- **Restricted Access:** Knowledge of house security measures and bypasses

## Roleplay Opportunities

### **The Spinosaurus Threat**
Vorn's chained hatchling creates constant tension:
- **Intimidation Display:** Vorn feeds the creature while making threats
- **Accidental Release:** Combat encounter if restraints fail
- **Moral Dilemma:** Party may want to free the obviously mistreated creature

### **Black Market Dynamics**
The camp's illegal nature affects all interactions:
- **Code Words:** Certain goods and services referenced indirectly
- **Trust Networks:** Establishing credibility with criminal contacts
- **Price Negotiations:** Bartering often more valuable than coin

### **Mining Dangers**
Technical expertise becomes crucial for survival:
- **Equipment Failure:** Poorly maintained gear creates hazards
- **Shard Exposure:** Handling raw dragonshards without proper protection
- **Tunnel Navigation:** Getting lost in unstable underground areas

## Story Integration

### **Connection to Pitchwall**
- Marros provides advance warning about supernatural activity in mines
- Zhalir's technical knowledge essential for accessing sealed areas
- Vorn's "exclusive rights" may conflict with party's exploration plans

- Insight into post-war illegal trade networks
- Understanding of shard properties and applications
- Connections to buyers throughout region

### **House Cannith Legacy**
- Zhalir provides crucial intelligence about house operations
- Technical solutions for accessing abandoned facilities
- Political complications from house exile and family connections

## GM Notes

### **Encounter Opportunities**
- **Claim Disputes:** Conflict over mining rights and territory
- **Equipment Sabotage:** Rival groups attempt to eliminate competition
- **Creature Escape:** Spinosaurus breaks free during tense negotiations

### **Economic Dynamics**
- Dragonshard prices fluctuate based on supply and demand
- Information about productive sites worth as much as physical goods
- Payment plans available for expensive purchases (with interest and collateral)

### **Criminal Network**
- Camp serves as hub for illegal activities throughout region
- Information flows to other smuggling operations and criminal contacts
- Law enforcement presence minimal but house security forces remain active

### **Environmental Hazards**
- Dragonshard exposure causes long-term health effects
- Mining equipment requires constant maintenance in jungle environment
- Unstable geological conditions from past extraction activities

### **Social Hierarchy**
- Vorn at top, with inner circle of trusted lieutenants
- Working miners at bottom, exploited but necessary
- Visiting traders and specialists occupy middle ground
- Party's position depends on demonstrated value and reliability
