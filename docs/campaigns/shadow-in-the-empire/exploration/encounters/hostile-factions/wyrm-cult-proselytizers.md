# Wyrm Cult Proselytizers

## Cult Fanatic (Wyrm Cultist Leader)
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic evil*

**Armor Class** 13 (Leather Armor)  
**Hit Points** 33 (6d8+6)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 11(+0) | 14(+2) | 12(+1) | 10(+0) | 13(+1) | 14(+2) |

**Skills** Deception +4, Persuasion +4, Religion +2  
**Senses** passive Perception 11  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Dark Devotion.** The fanatic has advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

**Spellcasting.** The fanatic is a 4th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 11, +3 to hit with spell attacks). The fanatic has the following cleric spells prepared:

- **Cantrips (at will):** light, sacred flame, thaumaturgy  
- **1st level (4 slots):** command, inflict wounds, shield of faith  
- **2nd level (3 slots):** hold person, spiritual weapon  

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The fanatic makes two melee attacks.

**Dagger.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 4 (1d4+2) piercing damage.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 Cult Fanatic + 2d4 Cultists  
**Environment:** Roads, settlements, anywhere people gather  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#4 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Fanatically devoted to their chosen wyrm master
- Speaks with fervent conviction about the "new age of dragons"
- Views non-believers as either future converts or necessary sacrifices

**Ideals**

- Dragon Supremacy: Dragons are the rightful rulers of the world
- Divine Purpose: Spreading the wyrm's influence is a sacred calling
- Transformation: The weak must be purged to make way for the strong

**Bonds**

- Sworn to serve their draconic patron until death
- Considers fellow cultists as true family
- Believes their wyrm speaks directly to them in visions

**Flaws**

- Blind Faith: Cannot accept that their wyrm might be wrong or evil
- Obsessive: Becomes fixated on converting or destroying those who resist
- Paranoid: Sees enemies and heretics everywhere

**Mannerisms**

- Constantly touches a crude dragon scale pendant while speaking
- Eyes have an unsettling fervor when discussing their dragon master
- Speaks in religious metaphors about "scales," "fire," and "submission"

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Opening Conversion Attempt:**
> "Brothers! Sisters! You travel dangerous roads in a world reborn. The Great [Color] has shown us the path to survival. Join us, and know the protection of true power!"

**When Refused:**
> "Blind fools! You cling to the old ways while the world burns around you. Our master's mercy is finite, but their wrath is eternal!"

**In Combat:**
> "For the glory of the [Color] Wyrm! May your deaths feed their ascension!"

### Tactics

**Initial Approach**
- Attempts peaceful conversion first, using Persuasion and religious rhetoric
- Offers protection, food, or shelter in exchange for loyalty
- Uses Thaumaturgy to create dramatic effects while preaching

**Combat Approach**
- Uses Command to force enemies to grovel or drop weapons
- Casts Shield of Faith on self, then uses Spiritual Weapon
- Directs cultists to focus fire on apparent leaders
- Uses Hold Person on spellcasters or dangerous fighters

**Retreat Conditions**
- Fights to the death unless explicitly ordered to retreat by their wyrm
- May flee only to warn their master of powerful opposition
- Views death in service as martyrdom and pathway to their dragon's side

### Knowledge

**What They Know**
- Location of their wyrm master's lair (but won't reveal willingly)
- Other cult cells operating in the region
- Which settlements have submitted to dragon rule
- Recent movements and moods of their draconic patron

**Conversion Offers**
- Safe passage through dragon-controlled territory
- Share of treasure hoards for valuable service
- Protection from other predators and hostile forces
- "Blessed transformation" for the most devoted (usually death)

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 2d4 for cultist numbers plus the fanatic leader
- Choose dragon type: affects cultist appearance and spells (fire, cold, acid, etc.)
- Determine cultist mood: confident after recent success vs. desperate after losses
- Note if any PCs have dragonmarked houses affiliations (affects reactions)

**Opening Scene Options**

**Zealous Conversion Attempt:**
> *A group approaches on the muddy road ahead—humans in crude robes bearing painted dragon scales as pendants. Their leader, eyes bright with fervor, raises both hands in greeting. "Brothers! Sisters! You travel treacherous paths in an age of transformation. The Great Red has shown us salvation through submission. Join us, and know true protection!"*

**Desperate Plea:**
> *Ragged figures stumble from the jungle, their dragon-scale vestments torn and stained. "Please," gasps their leader, clutching a crude holy symbol. "You must hear the truth! The old ways are dead. Only through serving the Crimson Lord can any of us hope to survive what's coming!"*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: The Pitch (Social - 2-3 rounds)**
- Fanatic attempts conversion using Persuasion and religious rhetoric
- Uses Thaumaturgy for dramatic effects (flame flickers, voice echoes)
- Cultists nod and murmur agreement, creating social pressure
- **Religion Check (DC 12):** Recognize twisted draconic theology
- **Insight Check (DC 14):** Notice fanatic's underlying desperation despite confident words
- **Persuasion Check (DC 15):** Engage in theological debate, might delay combat

**Phase 2: Growing Tension**
- If party shows resistance, fanatic becomes more aggressive
- Begins using veiled threats about dragon's displeasure
- Cultists move to flanking positions "innocently"
- **Deception Check (DC 13):** Pretend interest to gather information
- **Intimidation Check (DC 16):** Cause some cultists to hesitate
- **History Check (DC 11):** Recall similar cults in pre-war period

**Phase 3: Violence Erupts**
- If conversion fails, fanatic declares party "heretics" and "enemies of the new order"
- Uses Command to force party members to grovel or drop weapons
- Casts Shield of Faith on self, then Spiritual Weapon
- Cultists coordinate attacks based on fanatic's shouted orders

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Conversion Resistance Tactics**
- **Challenge Their Faith:** "If your dragon is so powerful, where are they now?"
- **Demand Proof:** "Show us this protection you promise"  
- **Theological Arguments:** Point out contradictions in their beliefs
- **Appeal to Humanity:** "What about your families? Your old lives?"

**Playing Along (Deception)**
- **Show Interest:** Ask about joining, gathering intel
- **Fake Conversion:** Pretend to be swayed by their arguments
- **Information Gathering:** Learn about dragon's lair, other cult cells
- **Escape Planning:** Buy time while positioning for advantage

**Key Roleplay Elements**

**Fanatic's Behavior Patterns**
- Quotes dragon "scripture" frequently
- Touches dragon scale pendant when nervous
- Eyes take on glazed look when discussing their patron
- Becomes enraged at mockery of their dragon master
- Genuinely believes they're offering salvation

**Cultist Psychology**
- Mix of true believers and those who joined for protection
- Some show doubt when challenged directly
- Fear their dragon master more than death
- May break and flee if fanatic is killed early

**Combat Tactics Deep Dive**

**Opening Round**
- Fanatic casts Command on most dangerous-looking PC
- Uses Thaumaturgy to create distracting effects
- Cultists spread out to avoid area effects
- Focus fire on obvious spellcasters or holy symbols

**Sustained Combat**
- Spiritual Weapon provides consistent damage
- Hold Person to disable key threats
- Cultists protect fanatic while he casts
- Use terrain and numbers advantage

**Desperation Tactics**
- At low health, fanatic calls for dragon to witness their sacrifice
- May attempt ritual suicide to "please" their master
- Surviving cultists fight with renewed fervor if fanatic dies dramatically
- Could flee to warn dragon of "heretical" party

**Spell Usage Strategy**
- **Command:** "Grovel," "Drop," "Halt," "Approach"
- **Hold Person:** Target fighters or those in heavy armor
- **Sacred Flame:** Ignores armor, fits dragon theme
- **Inflict Wounds:** For dramatic finishing attempts
- **Shield of Faith:** Self-preservation while coordinating

**Environmental Considerations**
- **Open Ground:** Cultists try to surround party
- **Dense Cover:** Fanatic uses range advantage with spells
- **Elevation:** High ground for better Sacred Flame targeting
- **Escape Routes:** Cultists know local terrain better

**Information Gathering Opportunities**

**If Interrogated (Alive)**
- Location of dragon's lair (general direction, 1-3 days travel)
- Number and disposition of other cult cells
- Recent dragon activities and moods
- Cult hierarchy and important personalities
- Tribute schedules and collection points

**Documents/Evidence**
- Crude map marking cult meeting points
- Letters from other cells or dragon representatives
- Tribute lists showing what dragon demands
- Religious texts mixing draconic lore with fever dreams

**Aftermath Scenarios**

**If Cultists Win**
- PCs may wake up bound, being prepared for dragon sacrifice
- Could lead to direct dragon encounter
- Other cult cells learn of "worthy sacrifices"

**If PCs Win (Combat)**
- Noise may attract dragon's attention eventually
- Other cults become more aggressive toward party
- Reputation as cult-killers spreads (good or bad depending on audience)

**If PCs Win (Social)**
- Converted cultists provide information willingly
- May gain local allies who were coerced into cult
- Dragon may view party as potential rivals worth investigating

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Cult leader carries message to/from dragon
- PCs discover cult has infiltrated known settlement
- Cult's dragon patron is rival to party's ally
- Former cult member seeks party's protection
- Cult guards important location party needs to access

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce cultist numbers, fanatic has fewer spell slots
- **Harder:** Add second fanatic or draconic sorcerer ally
- **Environmental:** Encounter at cult shrine with protective wards
- **Social:** Encounter in settlement where cult has support
