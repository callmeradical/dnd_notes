# Ladrian Hawkhurst

*I am the instrument, played to perfection, in the symphony of his revenge.*

My patron has given me the tools to survive, by any means necessary. I can be
anyone, sound like anything. I can shed my skin as a snake, and speak with such
grace in my tongue.

**Occupation:** Sailor and Merchant (Former slaver)

 **Family:** Born to a drow mother and a human father. I have many half brothers
and sisters who are from another of my mother's marriages. Drow society being
what it is, this is not uncommon. My mother and my brethren exist in the
under-dark. My father was killed by my mother. I have 5 siblings that I know of
but don't know any of them other than that they exist.

 **Hobbies:** He enjoys gossip and stories. He loves to hear tales, and loves to
 tell them. He is also very fond of birds, and enjoys mimicking the sounds of
 birds.

 **Religous, Political, and Philosophical Leanings:** He believes in a free
 society,
 where a man or woman can make their own destiny. He is completely unimpressed
 by so-called gods based on his experience with his patron. He is very adamantly
 against oppression and slavery. He believes that he will always manage to
 surive, because that is who he is, a survivor.

 **Affiliations:** He once worked for the Seven Suns Trading Coster.
