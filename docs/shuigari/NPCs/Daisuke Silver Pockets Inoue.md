# Daisuke "Silver Pockets" Inoue

## Overview
A younger, charming member of [[The Starcall Table]] gambling group, known for the distinctive silver bangles he wears that jingle when he moves. He's part of the traveling gambling circle that follows trade routes throughout the land.

## Appearance
- Younger than the other Starcall Table members
- Wears multiple silver bangles that jingle when he moves (hence the nickname "Silver Pockets")
- Likely well-dressed in a way that suggests both style and practicality for travel

## Personality
- Charming and personable
- Knows when to fold - recognizes when he's outmatched
- Comfortable in his role within the group dynamics
- Professional gambler with good instincts

## Notable Moments
During the storytelling wager between [[Shigeo]] and [[Kaoru Sugimoto]], Daisuke immediately folded when he saw the stakes and recognized that this contest was beyond his expertise. This shows wisdom and self-awareness - qualities important in a successful gambler.

## Connections
**The Starcall Table:**
- [[Kaoru Sugimoto]] - The elegant merchant and skilled storyteller
- [[Haruki "The Boar" Takeda]] - The boisterous drinker
- [[Aiko Hayashi]] - The quiet warrior

## Campaign Notes
- First encountered: Along the Silver Road during the caravan journey to Toriyama
- Witnessed the famous storytelling wager
- Continues to travel with the Starcall Table
