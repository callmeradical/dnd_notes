## Background

Shigeo is defined by his boisterous personality and love of a good fight. A brawler who isn't afraid to talk himself up, he carries himself with the confidence of someone who's backed up his boasts more times than not. But beneath the swagger lies a man learning hard lessons about the cost of greed.

## Current Situation

### Greed's Embrace
Shigeo bears a curse called **Greed's Embrace**, which has reduced his maximum hit points by 5. This curse serves as a constant reminder of choices made in pursuit of wealth or power.

**The Curse:**
- -5 to maximum HP
- Acquired through greedy choices or hoarding (500+ gp triggers corruption saves)
- Represents spiritual corruption manifesting physically
- Can potentially be cleansed through purification rituals or Mirror-of-Memory truth-telling

### The Great Tortoise Charm
Shigeo possesses (or possessed) the Great Tortoise charm, which he once wagered in a storytelling contest against [[Kaoru Sugimoto]] at [[The Starcall Table]]. His victory in that contest with the tale of Atsuko of Kagemura showed that beneath the bravado, Shigeo understands the power of humble, believable stories.

## Personality Traits
- Boastful and loud
- Confident in combat ability
- Willing to take risks (gambling, fighting)
- Has learned (or is learning) about consequences of greed
- Capable of surprising depth in storytelling

## The Storytelling Contest
During the journey along the Silver Road, Shigeo wagered his Great Tortoise charm against Kaoru's 100 gold kobans in a storytelling contest. Rather than matching Kaoru's theatrical tale of supernatural warriors, Shigeo told the grounded story of Atsuko - a woman who opened trade routes through persistence and connection rather than magic. His choice of authenticity over spectacle won the day.

This victory revealed a side of Shigeo beyond the braggart - someone who understands that not every story needs to be about legendary feats.
