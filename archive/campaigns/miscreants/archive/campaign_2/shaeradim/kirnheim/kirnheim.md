# Kirnheim

*The Last Bastion of Gnomes and Dwarves and The Last Cogs Works of Shaeradim*

## History

In the Shaeradim, on the slopes of Illaerothil exists a city shrouded in mystery, far from civilization, and yet a beacon of industrial marvel. 

Kirnheim has stood for tens of thousands of years. Built on the slopes of Ilaerothil (The Sentinel), the city was originally founded as an outpost to trade with the elves of Evereska. As time grew on and the mythal corrupted, the city became a fortress to protect from the wilds and beasts of Shaeradim. The Cogs Works supplied automatons and clockworks to the city of Evereska who would then enchant them to stand watch as eternal guardians. Each race provided a single part of the inventions: The gnomes, the know-how; The dwarves, the raw material; and the elves, the magic.

## Important Background Questions 

* Where is this city in the world? 

    Kirnheim exists within the Shaeradim Mountains on the slope of “The Sentinel”, the tallest mountain in Shaeradim.

* Why is it there?

    The city has been there for tens of thousands of years, it started out as a means to trade with the Elves of Evereska but eventually became a stronghold during later wars.

* How many people live there?
    There are upwards of 400 to 500.

* What is the racial makeup?
    Gnomes and Dwarves

* Is the city walled? Or protected by any natural features?

    The city has one pass of Ilaerothil that passes through it. The exterior is a sheer cliff face, while the rest of the city, the more dwarven population lives inside the mountain.

* What is the government?
    Two councils, one of the gnomes, and the other of the dwarves.

* What kind of legal system, if any, is there?
    Civil law, codified.

* What are the main Temples/Shrines/Cults/Whatever?

* What is the city's means of income? Do they produce raw goods, or finished, or luxury, or all three?

    The city is barred off from the rest of the world. The currency is primarily in rare and exotic gems. Some of the merchants may accept the characters’ currency.

* How does the city get its food?
    Hunting parties and foraging from inside the mountains.

* What is the economy like? Is the city rich? Or poor? Or somewhere in-between?

    The economy is stable but stagnant. With no one to trade the artificers and artisans have not been practicing their skills.

* Are there any Guilds?

    Arms, Armorers, and Artificers make up the primary guilds but there are relatively small guilds present too.
