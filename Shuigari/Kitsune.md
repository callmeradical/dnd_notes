# Kitsune

*Medium fey, chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 27 (5d8 + 5)
**Speed:** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) |

**Saving Throws:** Dex +5, Cha +5
**Skills:** Deception +5, Insight +3, Stealth +5, Perception +3
**Damage Resistances:** Psychic, Radiant
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
**Languages:** Common, Sylvan
**Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

## Traits

**Shapechanger (2/Day).**
The kitsune can transform into a humanoid form or revert to its fox form as a bonus action. While in humanoid form, the kitsune retains its game statistics, except for its speed, which becomes 30 ft. It cannot make Bite or Tail Swipe attacks in humanoid form.

**Fey Ancestry.**
The kitsune has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put it to sleep.

**Illusory Double (Recharge 6).**
As a bonus action, the kitsune creates an illusory duplicate of itself in a space within 20 feet. The duplicate mimics the kitsune's movements and can move up to 20 feet as part of the kitsune's movement. Attacks targeting the kitsune have disadvantage until the duplicate is destroyed or dispelled. The duplicate has AC 10 and 1 hit point.

**Foxfire (Recharge 6).**
The kitsune conjures a mystical flame in its tails, targeting one creature within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or take 10 (3d6) psychic damage and become charmed for 1 minute. A creature charmed in this way views the kitsune as a trusted friend. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Magic Resistance.**
The kitsune has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

**Multiattack.**
The kitsune makes two attacks: one with its Bite and one with its Tail Swipe.

**Bite.**
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.

**Tail Swipe.**
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

**Mischief Pulse (1/Day).**
The kitsune emits a wave of chaotic energy in a 15-foot radius centered on itself. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or be affected by one of the following effects (roll 1d4):
- *Laughter:* The target falls prone, laughing uncontrollably until the end of its next turn.
- *Confusion:* The target cannot take reactions and must roll a d8 at the start of its turn to determine its actions (per the *confusion* spell).
- *Blindness:* The target is blinded until the end of its next turn.
- *Euphoria:* The target gains temporary hit points equal to 5% of its maximum hit points but cannot attack until the end of its next turn.

## Reactions

**Elusive Dash.**
When the kitsune is hit by an attack, it can use its reaction to move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

## Tactics

- The kitsune starts combat by using **Foxfire** to charm the most dangerous enemy, buying time or sowing confusion.
- It uses **Illusory Double** when threatened, creating space to escape or reposition.
- If overwhelmed, the kitsune uses **Mischief Pulse** to disrupt foes and retreat.
