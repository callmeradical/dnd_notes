# New Magic Items

## Blade of the Wastes (Major Item, Very Rare)
* *Short Sword +3 (Requires Attunement)*
* *Increased Crit Range (19-20)*
* *As a bonus action you may grant yourself advantage on your next attack. You may use this ability a number of times equal to your constitution modifier (minimum of 1) per long rest.*

**Description:** A finely crafted short sword, or at least at one point it was. The blade is rusty and dry, you can see bits of it crumbling away.

**Cursed:** Once this blade draws blood (first critical after attuned), the thirst is unquenchable. The blade never becomes wet, all moisture is drawn into the blade. Every day that goes by without scoring a critical hit you take 1 point of exhaustion. Every critical hit removes all exhaustion levels.

Lifting the curse requires the user to dip the sword into a small body of fresh water. The sword will absorb all the water leaving a small riverbed/pond/stream/lakebed.

## Potion of the Great Old One (Minor Item, Uncommon)
When you drink this bitter potion, your arms are transformed into tentacles, which deal 1d8 lightning damage with a successful melee attack. It lasts for 1 hour.

## Chaospiercer & Nightlance (Major Item, Rare)
* *Daggers +1 (Requires Attunement)*
* *Range is doubled*
* *As long as one dagger is in your hand the other is immediately drawn back to you.*

**Description:** These ornate daggers are mirror images of each other, a set, designed to do one thing with incredible efficiency. The pommel of Nightlance has a pearl, and Chaospiercer has an onyx. The handles are silver, though the blades are of an unknown material.

## Draketooth (Major Item, Very Rare)
* *+1 Light Crossbow*
* *When you roll a 20 with this Magic Weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 piercing damage.


**Description:** It is ornate with gold and silver trimming. It is reported to have belonged to a vampire named Sansige. The wooden elements are actually aged drake bones so weathered from the years that the ivory has taken on a wooden appearance, like petrified wood.

## Durendal (Major Item, Legendary)
* *+1 Greatsword, (Requires Attunement)* *Gives off 15 feet of light* *If you
* are of a good alignment you can cast the spell healing word at level 1, 3
* times daily and recharges at dawn, strength is your spell casting ability for this.*

**Description:** A roughly made greatsword with a razor sharp edge. It's previous owner is unkown. The blade glows like a hot ember.

## Book of Ziri's Dimensional door
* *A book detailing the ritual of summoning Ziri’s Dimensional Door.*
* *The ritual requires expending a spell slot and 25 gp of silver powder.*

**How It Works:** To activate the door is a 1 hour ritual and the door stays open for 30 minutes, must be done in the view of the sun or moon, and requires a unique praise to Ziri (cannot be repeated).
