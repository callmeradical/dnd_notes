# Takashima Prefecture

The rugged northern mountains of [Shugane](Shugane.md) belong to Takashima Prefecture, a land defined by its harsh terrain and its role as the island's shield against external threats. The daimyo of Takashima, a stern and uncompromising warrior, commands the prefecture from the imposing fortress city of Takashima, a place as unyielding as the mountains that surround it. Takashima's defenses are legendary, its walls and watchtowers guarding the mountain passes against both human invaders and the yokai that are said to haunt the high peaks.

The prefecture's settlements reflect the martial nature of its society. Shimizu, a secluded mountain village, is known for its tradition of producing master swordsmen and its hidden swordsmiths who forge the finest blades in the archipelago. Futaba, a fortified town at the foot of the mountains, serves as a key military outpost, its garrison always ready to respond to threats from beyond the prefecture's borders. Nagano, a small forest village, stands as a lone sentinel amidst the dense woods, its inhabitants maintaining a delicate balance between respecting the spirits of the forest and harnessing its resources. The mountain villages of Shirogane and Hagane, isolated and austere, produce the iron and timber that sustain the prefecture's war machine.

Takashima's daimyo is fiercely loyal to the Shogunate, but his rigid adherence to tradition and martial values sets him apart from the more politically savvy rulers to the south. In a land where strength is respected above all else, the daimyo's authority is unchallenged—within the borders of his own prefecture, at least.

## Towns and Villages

- **Shimizu**
  - **Type**: Mountain village.
  - **Known for**: Its skilled blacksmiths and connection to legendary swordsmith lore.

- **Nagano**
  - **Type**: Forest village.
  - **Known for**: The masterful archers and their spirit-blessed archery techniques.

- **Shirogane**
  - **Type**: Mining village.
  - **Known for**: Its rich deposits of precious ores in the Celestial Spine.

- **Hagane**
  - **Type**: Logging village.
  - **Known for**: Supplying critical timber for Shugane's military and spiritual constructions.

- **Futaba**
  - **Type**: Fortified town.
  - **Known for**: Its strategic location guarding northern passes.
