# Twice-Cursed Samurai

**True Name:** Kazuki Takemura  
**Campaign Tie:** [Shuigari - The Last Whisper of Steel](Shuigari%20-%20The%20Last%20Whisper%20of%20Steel.md)  
**Related Notes:** [The Twice Cursed Samurai](The%20Twice%20Cursed%20Samurai.md), [Master Hideo Nakata](Master%20Hideo%20Nakata.md), [Soulsteel Blades - Legendary Weapons of Shuigari](Soulsteel%20Blades%20-%20Legendary%20Weapons%20of%20Shuigari.md), [Shindarasu](Shindarasu.md)

## Quick Lore
- Once a loyal retainer of the Arashi clan, Kazuki broke his oath to protect an innocent village and was cast out as a ronin—his first curse.
- [Master Hideo Nakata](Master%20Hideo%20Nakata.md) forged the Soulsteel katana *Yomi-no-Kiba* for him, binding Kazuki to the souls of those he failed, creating the second curse that keeps him half-anchored to the spirit world.
- Kazuki carried out Hideo's request to end the smith’s life and guard his legacy, but the act shattered him emotionally and opened the door for [Shindarasu](Shindarasu.md)’s influence.
- See [The Twice Cursed Samurai](The%20Twice%20Cursed%20Samurai.md) for full narrative history, adventure beats, and thematic analysis.

## Encounter Guidance
- **Setting:** Best introduced during Act II or III of *The Twice Cursed* arc, often near Hideo’s hidden forge or within yokai-tainted Frostwood ruins.
- **Motivation:** He tests intruders to judge whether they are worthy heirs to Hideo’s ideals. His default stance is hostile but conflicted.
- **Resolution Hooks:** Winning his respect, purifying *Yomi-no-Kiba*, or honoring a pact made with Hideo can shift the encounter from lethal duel to uneasy alliance.

**Damage Resistances** necrotic, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Languages** Common, Spirit Tongue  
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

### Traits

**Aura of Dread (Frightening Presence).** Creatures within 10 ft. that start their turn there must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. They can repeat the save at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on a success. On a success, the creature is immune to the aura for 24 hours.

**Ethereal Stride.** Kazuki can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain. He takes 5 (1d10) force damage if he ends his turn inside an object.

**Soulbound Katana — Yomi-no-Kiba.** On a hit, the target rolls a d6 to determine which ability score suffers an additional 1d4 reduction (1 Strength, 2 Dexterity, 3 Constitution, 4 Intelligence, 5 Wisdom, 6 Charisma). The reduction lasts until the creature finishes a long rest.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Kazuki makes two attacks with *Yomi-no-Kiba*.

**Yomi-no-Kiba (Soulsteel Katana).** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d8 + 3) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) necrotic damage and triggers **Soulbound Katana**.

**Phantom Strike (Recharge 5–6).** Kazuki teleports up to 20 ft. to an unoccupied space he can see, leaving a cloud of black mist. Each creature within 10 ft. of the destination must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 13 (3d8) necrotic damage.

### Legendary Actions (Optional)

Kazuki can take up to 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

1. **Parry (Costs 1 Action).** Kazuki adds +2 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him.
2. **Shadow Step (Costs 1 Action).** Kazuki teleports up to 15 ft. to an unoccupied space he can see, optionally passing through creatures as per **Ethereal Stride**.

## Tactics & Roleplay
- **Opening Move:** Begins with **Phantom Strike** to scatter foes, then focuses on characters who appear bound to Hideo’s legacy.
- **Battle Rhythm:** Alternates between hit-and-fade strikes and aura pressure, forcing the party to choose between repositioning or enduring fear.
- **Turning Point:** If reduced below half hit points, he questions the party’s resolve: *“Even now, the curse binds me… can you sever it?”*
- **Defeat or Surrender:** On defeat, he kneels and offers *Yomi-no-Kiba*, asking: *“Honor demands a sacrifice. Will you bear Hideo’s burden when I no longer can?”* Granting him peace may dispel one of his curses and earn a powerful, albeit haunted, ally.
