## Strong Start

**Cinematic opening** (improvised), 1 Zarantyr 998 YK - Hot and Wet in Moonwatch

Almost 6 months ago, you all answered a job from a mysterious figure through a variety of contacts. The job was to retrieve a valuable artifact that had been stolen from the Brelish government and was in the process of being trafficked to Xen'drik. Another interested party saw this as the perfect opportunity to make the heist. The reward for the successful retrieval of the artifact was substantial, but there was a catch—legend has said that the artifact is cursed, and whoever comes in contact with it will be hunted by powerful forces.

You have somewhat successfully liberated the goods of the ship and are coming back from Xen'drik. You managed to swap the goods and collect your reward. The previous captain also parted ways a few weeks ago since you made sight of Breland. Your ship had sustained heavy damage while sailing through Shargan's teeth almost a month ago. You and your party had evaluated all options and took a chance on the town of [[Moonwatch]]. When you arrived you were surprised to see the small but bustling town also outfitted with a standing army of about 1,200 soldiers from the Brelish army, and that is where we begin this tale.

You have secured lodging at [[The Graymoon Tavern]] and are just sitting down to a warm meal when a heavy hand falls on the table followed by a hearty laugh. A large man with a few associates has surrounded your table.

*"You all seem to be flush. Why not spread the wealth around a little bit? You know times are hard and people gotta eat."*

## Location Details

### The Graymoon Tavern

The Graymoon Tavern is one of the most popular inns in Moonwatch, known for its warm atmosphere, hearty meals, and cozy rooms. The tavern is located near the river and is a popular spot for merchants, travelers, and sailors. The innkeeper, a friendly half-elf named [[Elara]], takes great pride in making sure her guests are comfortable and well-fed. The tavern features a large common room with a roaring fireplace, several private dining rooms, and a large outdoor patio with a stunning view of the Graymoon River.

The rooms at the Graymoon Tavern are clean, comfortable, and well-appointed, with soft beds and fresh linens. The tavern is also famous for its delicious food, with a menu that features hearty soups, stews, and roasted meats. The inn is known for its hearty breakfasts, which are a popular way for travelers to start their day before setting out on their journey.

The Graymoon Tavern is also a hub of local gossip and news, and travelers can often hear interesting stories and rumors from the locals and other guests. With its warm atmosphere, delicious food, and comfortable rooms, the Graymoon Tavern is a great place to rest and relax after a long day of adventure.

### About Moonwatch

Moonwatch is a town located in the nation of Breland. It is known for its strategic location as a key port town on the southwestern coast of Khorvaire, and its position on the banks of the Graymoon River. Moonwatch is a hub of commerce and trade, with many merchants and travelers passing through on their way to and from the nearby city of [[Sharn]].

Moonwatch is also known for its large, well-equipped and highly trained standing army. The town is able to maintain this level of military readiness due to its position on the frontier between Breland and the neighboring nation of Droaam. Moonwatch is considered an important bulwark against any potential invasion from the tribes of Droaam.

In addition to its military presence, Moonwatch is also home to a large community of skilled craftsmen, who produce goods ranging from weapons and armor to art and jewelry. The town is also known for its rich cultural heritage, with many historic sites and festivals that celebrate the traditions of its people. Overall, Moonwatch is a thriving and important town in the nation of Breland.

**Population:** ~1,300 civilians + 1,200 Brelish soldiers

## NPC Details

### Elara of Moonwatch and The Graymoon Inn

Elara is a friendly half-elf in her mid-30s. She has long, curly hair that is the color of chestnuts, and sparkling green eyes that twinkle with good humor. Elara is known for her warm smile and welcoming demeanor, and she takes great pride in making sure her guests are comfortable and well-fed. She has a quick wit and a sharp tongue, and she is not afraid to speak her mind when necessary.

Elara has lived in Moonwatch for most of her life and has deep roots in the community. She inherited the Graymoon Tavern from her parents, who were well-respected merchants in the town. Elara has a keen business sense and a talent for cooking, and she has turned the tavern into one of the most popular inns in Moonwatch.

Despite her busy schedule, Elara always takes time to chat with her guests and make them feel at home. She is a good listener and has a warm, motherly demeanor, which makes her a popular figure in the community. When she is not working at the tavern, Elara enjoys fishing on the Graymoon River, and she is known for her excellent cooking skills.

### Capt. Kaine Colworn

Captain Kaine Colworn is a human soldier who serves in the army of Breland. He is a tall, broad-shouldered man in his late 30s, with a clean-shaven face and short-cropped hair. He has piercing blue eyes that seem to take in everything around him, and his no-nonsense demeanor can be both reassuring and intimidating.

Captain Colworn is an experienced and capable warrior, who has served in the Brelish army for many years. He is fiercely patriotic and deeply committed to the defense of his nation. He is an uncompromising and dedicated leader, who demands the best from himself and those under his command.

However, Captain Colworn's zeal for the defense of Breland sometimes causes him to act in a rash or impulsive manner. He has a tendency to see potential threats everywhere, and he is not afraid to use force to neutralize them. He is well-respected by his troops, but many in the community view him as a hothead who is more interested in demonstrating his power than in preserving peace.

Despite his sometimes overzealous behavior, Captain Colworn is a capable and dedicated soldier who is highly regarded by the Brelish army.

### Kor ir'Wynarn

Kor ir'Wynarn, younger brother to King Boranel, serves as the king's advisor and commander of the King's Citadel. He cherishes his older brother and is extremely loyal to both crown and country. As commander of the Citadel, Kor is far from the best agent the group has to offer. He has learned leadership and commitment from Boranel, however, and he has developed a keen ability to administer the duties of the office.

Kor splits his time between the Citadel headquarters and the king's chambers in Brokenblade Castle. As commander of the Citadel, Kor directs a team of captains who each command a division of the elite agency. As the royal advisor, Kor brings important information to Boranel, listens to the king, and offers advice on matters of state. King Boranel often follows his own heart, but not without first listening to the advice of his trusted brother.

It has been suggested that Kor might step in to lead the nation after Boranel's reign ends, but the royal advisor doesn't want the office or the responsibility. Besides, he is only a few years younger than Boranel—not the strong and vigorous leader the nation will need when the time comes. He is content to watch for threats to crown and country, offer opinions and intelligence as warranted, and be a friend and brother to the king.
