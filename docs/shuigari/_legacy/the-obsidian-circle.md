The Obsidian Circle

Shindarasu | Lord Hideo Takatsume: The Smiling Shadow

The Obsidian Circle: Weavers of Shadow and Destiny

Motto:
“In shadows, we weave destiny’s threads. In darkness, we unbind the light.”

Overview

The Obsidian Circle is a clandestine sect that operates across Takashima and the surrounding regions. Fanatically devoted to Shindarasu, the Primordial Shadow, the Circle believes it is their sacred duty to shatter the seals binding Shindarasu and unleash its power. They envision a new world order, one forged through chaos and bound under their control.

Led by the enigmatic and charismatic Hideo Takatsume, the Circle operates through secrecy, manipulation, and calculated violence. They infiltrate political, spiritual, and social hierarchies, spreading discord while advancing their ultimate goal: the Ritual of Unbinding, which will free Shindarasu from its prison and reshape the world.

Symbol
A black circle interwoven with faint, shimmering threads, representing the threads of fate they seek to sever and reweave. Members often wear this symbol discreetly, as jewelry, embroidery, or tattoos.

Leadership
Hideo Takatsume, Lord of Subtle Chains
A noble of impeccable reputation, Hideo uses his wealth and influence to conceal his role as the Circle’s leader.
Publicly, he is a benevolent mediator, known for his philanthropy and diplomacy. Privately, he is a dark sorcerer who draws power from Shindarasu’s essence.
Hideo believes himself destined to become Shindarasu’s chosen vessel, transcending mortality to usher in a new age of unity under his rule.

Key Traits of Hideo’s Leadership:
* Strategic Genius: Hideo’s plans span years, leveraging espionage, politics, and spiritual corruption to achieve his goals.
* Master Manipulator: He uses charm and persuasion to turn allies into pawns, often hiding his hand until it is too late to stop him.
* Devotion to Shindarasu: Hideo views himself as Shindarasu’s herald, tasked with freeing the Primordial Shadow and reshaping the world.

Structure and Members
The Obsidian Circle is hierarchical, with Hideo at the top and several layers of operatives beneath him:
The Shadowed Council:
A group of elite operatives overseeing the Circle’s activities. Each member specializes in a key area:
Lady Mitsume: A former priestess turned yokai sorceress who binds spirits to the Circle’s will.
Renshiro Akaza: A disgraced samurai who leads the Circle’s enforcers and yokai warriors.
The Umbrawraiths: Aberrant shadow creatures used for assassination and espionage.
Inner Circle Operatives:
Skilled agents who manage infiltration, relic recovery, and shrine desecration across Shuigari.
Cultists:
Rank-and-file members drawn from all walks of life, including disillusioned samurai, ambitious nobles, and yokai sympathizers.

History
Age of Sealing (Second Age):
During the Second Age, a coalition of celestial kami and mortal heroes sealed Shindarasu in the Veil of Night, a prison anchored by the celestial moons Dendaro, Intara, and Tsukuyomi.
Remnants of Shindarasu’s cult survived the war, preserving its teachings and rituals in secrecy.

Early Third Age:
Following the Sundering of Ydrafor, the Circle saw an opportunity to restore Shindarasu. Their initial efforts were thwarted by a coalition of daimyo and kami, forcing the Circle to retreat into obscurity.

Rebirth Under Hideo Takatsume:
Decades ago, Hideo discovered ancient texts tied to the Circle and restructured the sect into a sophisticated network.
His leadership transformed the Circle from a fragmented cult into a powerful organization with influence in courts, temples, and mercantile guilds.

Goals
Unbinding Shindarasu:
* The Circle’s ultimate goal is to perform the Ritual of Unbinding, which requires:
    * The Boy’s Blood: A child born under a celestial alignment, prophesied to be the key to breaking the seals.
    * Sacrifices of Yokai: Captured yokai provide the spiritual energy needed to weaken the seals.
    * Celestial Relics: Artifacts tied to the moons amplify the ritual’s power.
* Desecrating Shrines: The Circle targets sacred sites, corrupting their energies to sever the spiritual ties binding Shindarasu.
* Consolidating Power: By infiltrating Shuigari’s political and spiritual systems, the Circle ensures no unified resistance can stand against them.
* Destabilizing Shuigari: Through assassinations, sabotage, and subterfuge, the Circle sows discord to weaken the region’s defenses.

Philosophy
* The Primordial Shadow as a Force of Renewal: The Circle views Shindarasu not as a destroyer but as a necessary force of change. They believe the current world is stagnant and flawed, requiring Shindarasu’s touch to evolve.
* Balance as Weakness: The Circle rejects Shuigari’s traditional philosophy of balance, seeing it as a hindrance to progress.
* Unity Through Fear: By unleashing Shindarasu, the Circle believes they can forge a world united under their rule, free from division and strife.

Modern Activities
* Infiltration: Members hold positions in courts, guilds, and temples, subtly steering policies and decisions in their favor.
* Relic Hunting: The Circle searches for artifacts tied to Shindarasu’s sealing, such as fragments of celestial wards or ancient Soulsteel weapons.
* Yokai Manipulation: The Circle binds or corrupts yokai to spread chaos, draw attention away from their true goals, or provide sacrifices for rituals.
* Political Machinations: Hideo uses his reputation to pit factions against each other, ensuring the Circle’s enemies remain divided.

Notable Locations
* The Hall of Shattered Light: A desecrated temple hidden deep in the Frostwood, serving as a central hub for the Circle’s rituals.
* The Moonlit Chamber: A secret chamber beneath Takashima where the Ritual of Unbinding is prepared.
* Desecrated Shrines:Sacred sites like the Shrine of Daichi-no-Kame are targeted to sever their connections to the celestial wards.

Adventure Seeds
1. A Stolen Relic: The party discovers a sacred artifact has been stolen, leading them to uncover the Circle’s plans.
2. A Corrupted Shrine: The players investigate a shrine where yokai activity has increased, revealing the Circle’s desecration efforts.
3. The Captured Boy: The Circle kidnaps the child key to the Ritual of Unbinding, forcing the party into a race against time to save him.
4. Confrontation with Hideo: A final showdown with Hideo and the Shadowed Council could decide the fate of Shindarasu’s prison—and the world.

Overview
The Obsidian Circle is a clandestine sect that operates within Takashima and the surrounding region. Members are fanatical devotees of the primordial Tsurogami, the Woven Tyrant, believing it to be the catalyst for a new age of perfection. The sect works in secrecy, infiltrating political, spiritual, and social hierarchies to ensure their ultimate goal: the unbinding of Tsurogami from its ancient prison beneath Takashima.

Key Features
* Symbol: A black circle interwoven with faint, shimmering threads, often worn as a brooch or embroidered discreetly into clothing.
* Leadership: The charismatic and enigmatic Lord Daizen Kuroda heads the sect, serving as its public face and spiritual guide.
* Recruitment: The sect draws disillusioned samurai, ambitious nobles, and those seduced by the promise of immortality or power under Tsurogami’s new order.
* Operations: They use diplomacy, espionage, and summoning yokai or aberrations to sow chaos and weaken the spiritual barriers holding Tsurogami.

History of The Obsidian Circle

“In shadows, we thrive; in darkness, we ascend.”

The Obsidian Circle is a centuries-old sect that emerged from Takashima’s deepest recesses of lore, ambition, and fear. While its public presence has been erased through careful manipulation, its dark influence has lingered in Shuigari, steadily growing more ambitious and insidious.

Origins of the Obsidian Circle

The Age of Sealing (Second Age)
The War Against Tsurogami:
During the Second Age, a coalition of celestial kami, mortal heroes, and divine powers united to confront Tsurogami, an ancient primordial being that sought to dominate existence.
Tsurogami was defeated and imprisoned beneath Takashima, its essence bound by celestial wards tied to the Three Moons (Dendaro, Intara, Tsukuyomi).
The Cult of the Primordial:
* After Tsurogami’s sealing, remnants of its followers—a mix of aberrations, yokai, and mortals seduced by its promises—went into hiding.
* They began worshipping Tsurogami as the “Woven Tyrant,” preserving rituals and knowledge for a future time when the seals would weaken, and the primordial could return.
* These remnants evolved into the first iteration of the Obsidian Circle, taking their name from the black, web-like threads said to form Tsurogami’s prison.

Early Influence (Late Second Age)
* The Circle operated as a secretive cult, gathering relics tied to Tsurogami and researching forbidden texts to uncover the conditions for its return.
* They infiltrated temples, noble courts, and yokai clans, sowing seeds of chaos and mistrust to weaken the celestial wards.

A Temporary Defeat (Third Age, Early Years):

The Obsidian Circle’s first major resurgence was during the early Third Age, shortly after the Sundering of Ydrafor, when spiritual and physical boundaries across Atellas were destabilized.
Their efforts were thwarted by a coalition of Shuigari daimyo and kami, who executed a campaign to expose and root out the sect.
Many believed the Circle to be destroyed, but its true leaders and core operatives survived, retreating into the shadows.

Rebirth Under Lord Daizen Kuroda

Daizen’s Rise:

In the past few decades, Lord Daizen Kuroda, a shrewd and charismatic nobleman, discovered remnants of the Obsidian Circle while researching his family’s history.
Drawn to its philosophies of “creative destruction” and disillusioned by the stagnation of Shuigari’s political systems, Daizen restructured the sect with a focus on calculated influence rather than chaotic sabotage.
Under Daizen, the Circle transformed into a sophisticated network of spies, mystics, and assassins, consolidating power in Takashima’s courts and temples.

Daizen’s Vision:

Daizen believes Tsurogami’s return is not an act of destruction but one of rebirth. He sees himself as the rightful herald of this new age, destined to guide Shuigari (and Atellas) into a perfected existence.
His leadership has pushed the Obsidian Circle closer to their ultimate goal than ever before.

Modern Activities of the Obsidian Circle

Methods:

Infiltration:
Members of the Circle hold positions in Takashima’s courts, guilds, and temples, working to destabilize key institutions.
Relic Hunting:
They search for artifacts tied to Tsurogami’s sealing, such as fragments of the celestial wards or Soulsteel blades forged during the Second Age.
Yokai Manipulation:
The Circle binds yokai to their service, using their influence to spread chaos in rural areas and draw attention away from their true objectives.
Political Machinations:
Daizen’s reputation as a mediator allows him to subtly steer political factions into conflict, weakening alliances and strengthening the Circle’s reach.

Philosophy of the Obsidian Circle

Tsurogami as a Necessary Evil:
The Circle views Tsurogami not as a destroyer but as a force of change, capable of reshaping the world into something greater.
They see themselves as “weavers of destiny,” believing that mortals must embrace chaos to achieve true perfection.
The Weakness of Balance:
The sect opposes the traditional Shuigari philosophy of balance and harmony, arguing that such ideals stifle progress and innovation.
Unity Through Fear:
The Circle believes that fear of Tsurogami’s power will unify Shuigari under their leadership, creating a society free of conflict and division.

Current Goals

Unsealing Tsurogami:
The sect is focused on completing the Ritual of Unbinding, which requires the following:
The Boy’s Blood: Born under a specific celestial alignment, the boy’s essence is the key to shattering the celestial wards.
Ancient Relics: Artifacts tied to Tsurogami’s original sealing are needed to amplify the ritual’s power.
Yokai Sacrifices: The sect captures and sacrifices powerful yokai, channeling their spiritual energy to weaken the seals.
Undermining Shuigari:
The Circle continues to destabilize Shuigari’s spiritual and political systems, ensuring that no unified opposition can stand against them.
Securing Allies:
Daizen seeks to recruit disillusioned daimyo, corrupted monks, and ambitious samurai to bolster the Circle’s ranks.

Legacy and Impact

The Obsidian Circle’s influence has quietly shaped the course of Shuigari’s history. Though often thwarted, their philosophy of destruction and rebirth has left scars on the land, inspiring both fear and fascination. Under Daizen’s leadership, they are closer than ever to achieving their ultimate goal: unleashing Tsurogami and remaking the world in their image.
