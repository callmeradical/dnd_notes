# Campaign Planner

## Overarching Quest

Stop the Cult of the Dragon from resurrecting or releasing Tiamat, the goddess
of dragons, from the Nine Hells.

## Quest Lines

|Levels | Book | Objective |
|-------|-----------|------|
| 1-9 | Hoard of The Dragon Queen | Prevent the Hoard of the Dragon Queen from Reaching the Well of Dragons. |
| 9-16 | Rise of Tiamat | The Cult and the Red Wizards are using souls to open the portal to the Nine Hells. |

## Hoard of the Dragon Queen Complications

### SkyReach Castle

The party must gain access to SkyReach Castle.

#### Negotiating With Talis

Talis is willing to help them but wants the Black Dragon Mask in return. She is
willing to team up with the Dragons in the Mere of Dead Men to hunt Varram the
White.

A very high Insight check might reveal she has other motives as well, but she is
sincere in her plan.

If the characters refuse she stalls for a few moments, and opens with an attack.

This also gives Sandesyl an opening to attack as well and will join in after
the first round.

**Vampire Sandesyl:** A spy in the midst!
The vampire is spying on Talis. The vampire will attempt to go unnoticed. The
area around the lodge becomes obscured by a low fog and a stillness in the
forest. If confronted the vampire will fight until it drops to 0 HP, then
retreating to SkyReach Castle.

If the vampire is not dealt with The castle will begin to make preparations to
depart, and informing their agents in Parnast.

#### The City of Parnast

The party must gain access to SkyReach Castle, but the City of Parnast is
completely occupied by the Cult of the Dragon.

**The Golden Tankard:**
If the party decides to get to the Castle by way of Parnast and stops in the
Inn. The cult will make preparations and Raggnar Redtooth and his patrons
distract the party until such a time the castle can make way.

A messenger is sent to retrieve Captain Othelstan, three dragonclaws, a veteran
and six guards. All of which are determined to give their lives for the cause.

**Wyvern Riding:**
The wyverns in the stable are trained to carry up to two Medium or six
Small riders at a time. The difficult part is getting their harnesses attached
so that riders don’t fall off, and giving the proper commands once airborne.
Any character can put the harness on a wyvern with a successful DC 10 Wisdom
(Animal Handling) check.

If the check fails by 5 or more, the wyvern strenuously objects and attacks the
character once with its tail stinger as a reaction. The wyverns aren't choosy
about who rides them, but convincing a wyvern to fly or move in a specific
direction requires an action to make a DC 15 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check; if
the check fails, the wyvern ignores the rider’s commands until the rider’s next
turn.

After a rider succeeds on two such checks, the wyvern goes where the rider
wants for the next hour, or until it takes damage or the rider dismounts.
The castle is easily spotted, day or night, out to a range of five miles. The
wyverns are faster than the castle in flight, and they can catch up to it.
