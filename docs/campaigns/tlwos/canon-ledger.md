# TLWoS Canon Ledger

Purpose: maintain one continuity authority for Arc 2 so session notes, prep docs, and world lore stay synchronized.

## Canon Authority Order

Use this order when two files conflict:

1. Live canon pages:
   - `campaigns/tlwos/current-state.md`
   - Latest `posts/tlw-0xx-session.md` entries
2. Active module/session prep in `campaigns/tlwos/planning/sessions/`
3. Reference summaries in `campaigns/tlwos/planning/reference/`
4. Global lore in `shuigari/`
5. `_legacy/` material

If conflicts remain, live canon pages win.

## Naming Standards

- City: **Takashima**
- District state: **middle terrace** (current live play)
- Brewery: **Kokuei Shuzo** (`九映酒造`)

- Samurai: **Kazuki Takemura, the Twice-Cursed Samurai**

## Entity Mapping (Working Canon)

- **Tsurogami** and **the Woven Tyrant** are the same referent.
- **Shindarasu** (also table-spoken as "Shindarsu") is **distinct** from Tsurogami.
- **Shindarasu** is the **Broken Shell**, a yokai of greed.
- **Tsurogami** is a separate fate-linked power represented by the Woven Tyrant frame.
- Legacy pages that collapse these entities should be treated as in-world confusion, cult distortion, or outdated notes.

## Current Arc Causality (GM Truth)

- The sky-strike/lightning event is an oni traveling to the prince, who is an anomaly of fate.
- The oni is a **pact-holder warlock of Tsurogami**.
- The oni has forced Takashima into a **Shadow Register** state (partial Shadowfell overlay, not full transfer).
- The enthralled prince is the anomaly-anchor stabilizing this overlap.
- The oni mage was previously sealed in a great tree in the forest.
- The current Daimyo has imprisoned/enslaved goliaths and is forcing them to clear-cut northern mountain forest for boatbuilding.
- That forced clear-cut violation is the release/breach condition connected to the oni mage's return.

## Takemura Continuity Ruling

- Takemura wields a cursed soulsteel blade.
- First curse: loss of family.
- Second curse: undead condition; he cannot die while the blade remains whole.
- Each demon he kills strengthens the blade.
- Current play confirms he functions as a demon slayer.

## Antagonist Separation Ruling

- **Krixarion** is a separate antagonist thread tied to the cursed snowglobe.
- Yasei is the current holder of the snowglobe.
- Krixarion's home setting is **Eryssyl**, a Demiplane of Dread.
- The snowglobe is a cross-world cursed artifact that can surface outside Eryssyl.
- Default handling: the globe imports curse-pressure and influence, not automatic full setting transfer.
- Do not collapse Krixarion into the Tsurogami/oni-palace chain unless explicitly changed in future canon.
