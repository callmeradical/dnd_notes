# Colonial Bandits

## Bandit Captain
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic evil*

**Armor Class** 15 (Studded Leather)  
**Hit Points** 65 (10d8+20)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15(+2) | 16(+3) | 14(+2) | 14(+2) | 11(+0) | 14(+2) |

**Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +5, Wis +2  
**Skills** Athletics +4, Deception +4, Intimidation +4  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The captain makes three melee attacks: two with its scimitar and one with its dagger. Or the captain makes two ranged attacks with its daggers.

**Scimitar.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) slashing damage.

**Dagger.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4+3) piercing damage.

### Reactions

**Parry.** The captain adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the captain must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 Bandit Captain + 2d6 Bandits  
**Environment:** Roads, bridges, abandoned settlements  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#3 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- **Captain:** Desperate former colonial soldier turned to banditry for survival
- **Bandits:** Mix of deserters, refugees, and criminals seeking easy targets

**Ideals**

- Survival: Do whatever it takes to stay alive in this hostile land
- Profit: Every encounter is an opportunity for gain
- Strength: The strong take from the weak, that's the natural order

**Bonds**

- **Captain:** Responsible for the lives of his desperate followers
- **Bandits:** Loyalty bought through shared hardship and promise of loot

**Flaws**

- **Desperate:** Will take dangerous risks for even small rewards
- **Paranoid:** Suspicious of anyone claiming friendship or offering aid
- **Cowardly:** Quick to abandon fight if it turns against them

**Mannerisms**

- **Captain:** Wears faded colonial uniform pieces as badges of former honor
- **Bandits:** Nervous tics from constant stress and poor living conditions
- Constantly look over shoulders, expecting pursuit or ambush

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Opening Demand:**
> "Hold there, travelers! These roads aren't safe for honest folk. Lucky for you, we provide... protection services. A small toll ensures your safe passage through our territory."

**When Negotiating:**
> "Look, we're not monsters. We're just trying to survive same as you. Help us out with some coin or supplies, and we all walk away happy."

**In Combat:**
> "Take them down! Remember, dead men carry lighter purses, but they don't fight back!"

### Tactics

**Initial Approach**
- Position themselves to block road or path
- Captain tries negotiation first, bandits remain ready for violence
- Use intimidation and displays of numbers to avoid actual combat

**Combat Approach**
- Bandits use crossbows from cover while captain engages in melee
- Focus fire on obvious spellcasters or heavily armored targets
- Captain uses Parry and positioning to stay alive while bandits provide support
- Attempt to capture rather than kill valuable-looking targets

**Retreat Conditions**
- Flee if captain falls or more than half the bandits are defeated
- May offer surrender if completely outmatched
- Know local terrain and escape routes well

### Knowledge

**What They Know**
- Safe routes through local area and dangerous locations to avoid
- Recent traveler movements and potential wealthy targets
- Location of abandoned settlements and supply caches
- Gossip and information from captured or intimidated travelers

**What They Want**
- Coins, jewelry, and easily portable valuables
- Food, medicine, and basic survival supplies
- Weapons and armor in good condition
- Information about wealthy caravans or settlements

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 2d6 for bandit numbers (3-12 bandits plus captain)
- Determine encounter location: roadblock, bridge, or ambush site
- Consider bandit desperation level: desperate (aggressive) vs. cautious (negotiable)
- Note any environmental advantages bandits might use

**Opening Scene Options**

**Road Toll Setup:**
> *A makeshift barrier of fallen logs blocks the muddy path ahead. Behind it, a mix of desperate-looking humans in tattered clothing and improvised armor. Their leader, wearing pieces of what might once have been a colonial uniform, steps forward with hands raised but ready. "Hold there, travelers! These roads aren't safe for honest folk..."*

**Ambush from Ruins:**
> *Movement in the vine-covered ruins catches your eye just as crossbow bolts whistle past. Bandits emerge from concealed positions among the crumbling walls, their leader shouting orders: "Drop your weapons and bags! We don't want to hurt anyone, but we will if we have to!"*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Intimidation and Negotiation**
- Captain attempts to extract toll through intimidation
- Bandits remain partially concealed but visible as threat
- Party can negotiate, pay toll, or attempt to intimidate bandits
- **Intimidation Check (DC 13):** Frighten bandits into letting party pass
- **Persuasion Check (DC 15):** Negotiate reduced toll or alternative arrangement
- **Deception Check (DC 12):** Convince bandits party has little of value

**Phase 2: Combat Escalation**
- If negotiation fails, bandits attack with crossbow volley
- Captain moves to engage strongest-looking melee fighter
- Bandits attempt to use cover and superior numbers
- **Initiative Advantage:** Bandits act first if they initiated combat

**Phase 3: Morale Breaking**
- Bandits begin fleeing when captain falls or casualties mount
- May attempt to surrender if completely outmatched
- Captain fights desperately to protect his followers
- **Morale Check:** When half bandits fall, remainder must make Wisdom saves

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Offering food or medicine instead of coin
- Speaking about shared hardships of frontier life
- Showing respect for captain's military background
- Demonstrating superior force without immediate violence

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Displaying obvious wealth while claiming poverty
- Threatening to report them to authorities
- Insulting their desperation or circumstances
- Making sudden movements or reaching for weapons

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Bandits provide safe passage and local information
- May offer services as guides or guards for fair payment
- Captain shares knowledge of area dangers and opportunities
- Possible future alliance or repeat encounters

**Success**
- Reduced toll accepted (25-50% of original demand)
- Safe passage through bandit territory
- Basic information about road conditions ahead

**Failure**
- Full toll demanded or combat ensues
- Bandits remember party as difficult targets
- May set up ambush later if combat avoided

**Critical Failure**
- Immediate combat with bandits using prepared positions
- Word spreads to other bandit groups about wealthy targets
- Captain takes personal offense, becomes recurring antagonist

**Combat Considerations**

**Environmental Usage**
- Bandits know local terrain and use it for cover
- May have prepared positions with improvised fortifications
- Use fallen logs, ruins, or natural obstacles for protection
- Crossbows allow engagement from relatively safe distance

**Tactical Coordination**
- Captain directs bandit positioning and targets
- Bandits focus fire on single targets
- Use numerical advantage to surround isolated enemies
- Retreat to prepared positions if initial assault fails

**Aftermath Scenarios**

**If Bandits Defeated**
- 2d6 × 10 gp in mixed coins and small valuables
- Information about other bandit groups or dangerous areas
- Possible maps or letters revealing larger criminal network
- Local authorities may offer reward for bandit leader's death

**If Negotiated Settlement**
- Bandits may become recurring friendly NPCs
- Information network about regional travelers and threats
- Possible employment opportunities for later adventures
- Reputation among local criminals as reasonable people

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Captain is former soldier from party member's unit
- Bandits have information about missing persons or lost caravans
- Part of larger criminal organization with higher-level leadership
- Desperate circumstances create opportunity for rehabilitation subplot

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce numbers, make bandits more willing to negotiate
- **Harder:** Add veteran soldiers or magical support
- **Environmental:** Encounter during storm, at night, or in difficult terrain
