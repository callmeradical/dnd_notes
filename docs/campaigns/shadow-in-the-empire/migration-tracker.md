## Migration Guidelines

When migrating notes:

1. **Preserve formatting** - Maintain headers, lists, and emphasis
2. **Add frontmatter** - Include title, tags, and relevant metadata
3. **Create wiki links** - Convert references to other notes into `[[wiki links]]`
4. **Organize by type** - Place in appropriate subdirectory (sessions/, npcs/, locations/, etc.)
5. **Update this tracker** - Check off items as they're completed
