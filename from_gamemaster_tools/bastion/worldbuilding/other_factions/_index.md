## The Bloodreavers

A gang of goblinoif slavers that have primarily been feeding Kalarel his
subjects. They prey on the denizens of the ruins and outside the city centers.

## The Wayfinder's Foundation

The primary authority over the lower ruins and governing body that grants
possession or regulates the trade of Enlightenment War artifacts. The
foundation is made up of representatives of all the guilds (chartered). All
excursions must be approved by the foundation and granted written papers.
Adventuring parties that go forth without the permission of the foundation are
hunted by the black cloaks.

### The Black Cloaks

The Black Cloaks are the arm of justice for the enforcement of the Wayfinder's
Foundation. They regulate the trade, exploration, and security of
pre-enlightenment war technology and relics. They are also the primary
bounty-hunters in legal matters.

Bounty Hunters, unaffiliated with the guilds, and work as independents.
Need a cast of characters with various quirks. Adventurer’s that brave the
ruins without permission may exact the attention of a “black cloak"

## Media

The media is waging a constant war of propaganda all over the city-states, the
unified front is the only one that is concerned with the actual city itself,
rather than commerce, they sense something larger is going on.

There are two primary publications that exists in the city of Bastion
There is a secondary publication for each precinct/city-state with the
exception of precinct four, they highly regard L’Unita Fronte.

### L'Unione di Bastion

Printmaster of L’Unione di Bastion is Pietro Serrenissima

#### Affiliation and Alliances

LUDB is the publication of the Government/University/Royal Family (Marivaldi, Alunno)

### Giornale di Commercio

Printmaster of Giornale Di Commercio is Agnello Dalmatia

#### Affiliation and Alliances

GDC is the publication of the merchant houses (excluding Marivaldi Mercantile)

### L'Unita Fronte
There is a very old scribe/sage that works on studying the history of Bastion
and its workings. Loremaestrae Obelerio Seccolo. He lives in the fourth
precinct and is near-untouchable because of the goodness and kindness he shows
all. He is beloved by all.			They are very much concerned with Bastion
itself and the problems that are facing, they are the only ones trying to do
anything about the problems of Bastion itself, rather than society or commerce.
Attempting to warn the people they are commonly brushed off as doomsayers,
fanatics, and children that believe in the things of nightmares. Paranoid.			

#### Affiliation and Alliances

They are aligned with an upstart merchant house known as Selvaggio.
