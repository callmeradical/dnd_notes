## Highlights

* This guy is super badnews.

* They run the mafia or underground syndicate that traffics all manner of
 illicit materials, and people. 
* They also run the bloodreavers and the goblin trade.

## About

Sico is a person of high-tastes and exquisite hobbies. They run multiple different
bars and taverns within Cagliari, most notably in the Seven Hills, Fortune's
Wheel and The Blue Moon. They are paranoid and for good reason, everyone is trying
to get their own riches off of their business.

## Description
Sico does not prescribe to a gender, as most aetherborn but refers to itself as
a collective, such as a collective of memories and thoughts. Sico is pale white
with multiple cracks in the stony skin that almost looks like white polished
marble, with intricate filigree of gold spanning the cracks and joints (almost
like tattoos)

Sico's eyes are hollow and ghostly with an blue glow that hides behind lenses of crystal.

## Quirks
