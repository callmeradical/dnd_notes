# Lesser Tengu

*Medium humanoid (yokai), chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 33 (6d8 + 6)
**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills:** Acrobatics +5, Perception +4, Stealth +5
**Damage Resistances:** Necrotic, Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
**Languages:** Common, Spirit Tongue, Auran
**Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

## Traits

**Aerial Agility.**
The Lesser Tengu can take the Dash or Disengage action as a bonus action while flying.

**Mimicry.**
The Lesser Tengu can mimic sounds it has heard, including voices. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful **DC 13 Insight check.**

**Magic Resistance.**
The Lesser Tengu has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

**Multiattack.**
The Lesser Tengu makes two attacks: one with its katana and one with its talons.

**Katana (Shortsword).**
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage.

**Talons.**
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.

**Wind Slash (Recharge 5–6).**
The Lesser Tengu creates a blade of slicing wind in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking 14 (4d6) slashing damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a success.

## Reactions

**Feather Dodge.**
When a creature the Lesser Tengu can see makes a ranged weapon attack against it, the Tengu can use its reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll.

## Tactics

- **Ambush Predator:** The Lesser Tengu prefers to attack from above, using its flight and **Wind Slash** to weaken the party before diving into melee combat.
- **Hit-and-Run:** It uses **Aerial Agility** to attack, then disengage and reposition, avoiding prolonged engagements.
- **Mimicry to Deceive:** Outside combat, the Lesser Tengu uses its **Mimicry** ability to lure the party into traps or ambushes.
