# Jorogumo (Spider Spirit)

*Large monstrosity, chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 84 (13d10 + 13)  
**Speed:** 30 ft., climb 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 8 (-1) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills:** Deception +7, Insight +3, Perception +4, Stealth +8  
**Damage Resistances:** Poison, Psychic  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., Passive Perception 14  
**Languages:** Common, Sylvan, Spirit Tongue  
**Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

**Shapechanger.**  
The Jorogumo can transform into a humanoid form or revert to its spider form as a bonus action. While in humanoid form, it cannot use its Web or Venomous Bite.

**Spider Climb.**  
The Jorogumo can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Web Sense.**  
While in contact with a web, the Jorogumo knows the exact location of any other creature in contact with the same web.

**Web Walker.**  
The Jorogumo ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.

## Actions

**Multiattack.**  
The Jorogumo makes two attacks: one with its Venomous Bite and one with its Web Strike.

**Venomous Bite (Spider Form Only).**  
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature.  
*Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) poison damage and be poisoned for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Web Strike.**  
*Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 30 ft./60 ft., one creature.  
*Hit:* The target is restrained by webbing. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 14 Strength check, escaping from the webbing on a success. The webbing can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 10; hp 5; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to bludgeoning, poison, and psychic damage).

**Enthrall (Recharge 5–6).**  
The Jorogumo uses its beguiling voice to charm up to three creatures within 30 feet of it. Each target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed for 1 minute. While charmed in this way, the creature regards the Jorogumo as a trusted ally and will not attack it. The effect ends if the Jorogumo harms the charmed creature.

**Web Lair (1/Day).**  
The Jorogumo creates a 20-foot-radius area of sticky webbing centered on itself. The area is considered difficult terrain, and creatures other than the Jorogumo that enter the web must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained.

## Tactics

- The Jorogumo uses **Enthrall** to lure victims and **Web Lair** to control the battlefield, isolating weaker targets.
- It prefers guerrilla tactics, climbing and striking from the shadows with **Venomous Bite** and **Web Strike**.
- In humanoid form, it uses charm and deception to disarm opponents before revealing its true nature.
