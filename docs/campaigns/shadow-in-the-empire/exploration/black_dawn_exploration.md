# Submersible Exploration: The Black Dawn

This framework mirrors the jungle exploration system, adapted for travel by submarine along Q'barra's coast and beneath the waves.

---

## Travel & Pace
- **Normal Pace:** 4 miles/hour, ~40 miles/day if uninterrupted. 
- **Reduced Pace:** If conserving air/fuel or evading detection, ~20 miles/day.
- **Extended Pace:** Risky; can push to 60 miles/day, but crew fatigue and mishaps roll with disadvantage. *Extended pace is unavailable if the party cannot pay upkeep costs.*

---

## Operating Costs
- The *Black Dawn* is a **prototype submersible** with unstable arcane engineering. Its elemental containment ring and structural seals require constant upkeep.
- **Operating Cost:** 100 gp/day in specialized supplies (spare parts, dragonshard fuel, arcane stabilizers).  
- Failure to meet this cost increases the chance of mishaps and limits travel speed.

---

## Mishap System
- Mishaps can occur **even when the upkeep cost is met**. Each travel shift, roll a die to determine if a mishap occurs:
  - **Normal Operation:** Roll **1d8** → Mishap on a **1**.  
  - **Extended Pace:** Roll **1d6** → Mishap on a **1**.  
  - **Without Upkeep (100 gp not paid):** Roll **1d4** → Mishap on a **1**.  
  - If upkeep is not met, **Extended Pace cannot be attempted.**

### Mishap Table (d6)
1. **Containment Flicker:** Elemental ring falters, speed halved until repaired.  
2. **Arcane Surge:** Internal sparks; all crew make DC 14 Dex saves or take 2d6 lightning damage.  
3. **Leak:** Compartments begin flooding; requires DC 15 Strength or Tools check to seal.  
4. **Navigation Drift:** Vessel veers 1d6 miles off course.  
5. **System Failure:** Life support compromised, reducing air supply by 1d4 days.  
6. **Critical Strain:** Hull creaks; next depth hazard save made at disadvantage.

---

## Daily Exploration Procedure
For each day of travel, roll **3 checks** to represent hazards and encounters. 
- Roll **1d4** three times a day (morning, afternoon, evening). On a **1**, roll on the appropriate encounter table.

### Encounter Types
1. **Wildlife Encounters** (Sea Beasts, Reef Predators, Swarms)
2. **Environmental Hazards** (Storms, Pressure Depths, Hull Damage)
3. **Hostile Factions** (Cultists, Pirates, Wyrm Servants)
4. **Lost World Mysteries** (Ruins, Ancient Wyrm Relics, Sunken Temples)

---

## Hazards of Submersible Travel
- **Pressure Depths:** Descending too far risks crushing damage unless reinforced (Con saves DC 15 per crew shift).  
- **Hull Breaches:** On a natural 1 during navigation, the *Black Dawn* suffers a leak (crew must repair with tools or magic).  
- **Fuel & Air:** Supplies last 14 days (extended with rationing). Running out forces surfacing into hostile territory.  
- **Visibility:** Murky waters limit sight; Perception checks at disadvantage unless using magical or arcane instruments.  
- **Maintenance:** Even when upkeep is met, mishaps may still occur. Without upkeep, mishap odds rise dramatically.

## Crew Roles & Checks
Each day, assign PCs or NPCs to ship roles:
- **Navigator:** Int (Investigation) or Wis (Survival) for course.
- **Helmsman:** Dex (Vehicles: Water) for piloting. 
- **Lookout:** Wis (Perception) for hazards.
- **Engineer:** Int (Arcana/Tools) for repairs and efficiency.

---

## Layout of the Black Dawn (Player Map Summary)
- **Nose Entry Hatch:** PCs and crew enter through the reinforced forward hatch. Tight ladders lead down into the control deck.  
- **Control Deck:** Dreyden’s helm and arcane navigation systems. Space is cramped, with periscopes and binding ring monitors.  
- **Crew Quarters:** Narrow bunks, hammocks, and storage niches line the midsection. Air is stale and always humming with arcane resonance.  
- **Galley & Stores:** Holds supplies, food, and water for up to 14 days. Repair kits and 100 gp/day worth of prototype parts are stored here.  
- **Infernal Bay (Aft Hold):** A massive cargo chamber at the rear, large enough to contain the chained infernal war machine. Workbenches, salvage space, and tethering rings dominate the room.  
- **Escape Hatch:** Emergency exit in the aft chamber, opening directly to the sea. Used rarely due to pressure risk.

---

## GM Guidance
- Encounters should feel claustrophobic: darkness, pressure, limited escape routes.  
- Emphasize resource management (air, food, repair tools, gold for supplies) the way jungle play emphasizes disease and exhaustion.  
- Each hazard/encounter should remind the party: **they are deep in a world not meant for them.**
