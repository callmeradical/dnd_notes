# History of Shugane

## The Founding of the Imperial Line - Emperor Hosikage

[h1]The Founding of the Imperial Line[/h1]<br />**Year 150 CP.** The event that marks the beginning of Shuigari’s modern history, the founding of the Imperial Line by Emperor Hosikage is considered both a turning point and a sacred moment in the archipelago’s past. Prior to the Imperial Line, Shugane was a land torn apart by war, with countless warlords vying for supremacy in the aftermath of the Sundering. Amidst the chaos, a great figure emerged—Hosikage, a warrior and mystic who claimed to have been chosen by the celestial kami themselves.<br />[h2]Emperor Hosikage’s Ascent[/h2]<br />Emperor Hosikage was said to be blessed by the kami at birth, marked by a mysterious star-shaped birthmark on his brow that shone faintly under the light of the two moons, Dendaro and Intara. Hosikage rose from humble origins, gathering followers with his message of unity and spiritual renewal. His mastery of martial arts was only surpassed by his ability to wield the **Tetsu no Kagayaki**—a divine blade gifted by the fire kami Izanami, capable of cleaving through both mortal and spiritual foes. Armed with this legendary weapon, he challenged the most powerful warlords of Shugane, defeating them one by one and binding their oaths to the newly formed **Empire of Shuigari**.<br />[h2]The Rite of the Imperial Bloodline[/h2]<br />To cement his rule, Emperor Hosikage performed the **Rite of the Celestial Pact**, a powerful ritual said to bind his bloodline to the heavens. The pact was conducted atop the sacred peak of **Mt. Hoshigami**, where the Emperor communed with the kami and yokai alike. Legends tell that he struck a bargain with both benevolent and malevolent spirits, promising to uphold the balance between light and shadow. In exchange, the kami swore to watch over his descendants, making the Imperial Line a living symbol of the divine order in Shuigari.<br />[h2]The Legacy of Emperor Hosikage[/h2]<br />Emperor Hosikage reigned for fifty years, his wisdom and strength guiding the early empire through its most turbulent period. He established the framework for the Imperial Court, built the first temples of the **Three Moons**, and forged alliances with the nascent Shogunate. Upon his death, his son, Emperor Raiken, inherited the mantle, beginning a dynasty that would endure for over a millennium. The line of Hosikage has faced many trials, but its continued existence is seen as proof that the celestial pact holds strong.<br />[h2]Modern Impact[/h2]<br />Emperor Hosikage’s name is still revered throughout Shuigari, and the current ruler,** Emperor Tenno II**, bears his name as a sign of hope and continuity. Born under auspicious omens, Tenno II is expected to live up to the legacy of Hosikage—an impossible standard that burdens him greatly. As political tensions rise and the Shogunate weakens, many wonder if Tenno II will be able to reclaim the glory of the past, or if the Imperial Line will finally break under the weight of its own history.

## The Rise of the Shogunate

[h2]The Rise of the Shogunate[/h2]<br />**The Founding of the Shogunate.** The establishment of the Shogunate marked a turning point in the history of Shugane and the wider Shuigari archipelago. Following centuries of turmoil and near-constant warfare between the rival clans of Shugane, the warlord **Kazuhito Tetsu** emerged as a unifying force, carving a path to dominance through military brilliance, political savvy, and strategic alliances. Kazuhito’s rise began in the aftermath of the brutal Unification Wars, a conflict that devastated much of Shugane as the clans fought for control of the island’s heartland. The wars left the land scarred and the people broken, setting the stage for a leader who could restore order and establish lasting peace.<br />[h2]The Unification of Shugane[/h2]<br />After years of bloodshed and shifting alliances, Kazuhito Tetsu’s army, the **Crane Legion**, stood victorious over the last of his rivals. In **Year 275 CP**, with the backing of the most powerful clans and the blessing of the reigning Emperor, Kazuhito declared himself the first **Shogun** of Shugane. His rule marked the beginning of the Shogunate Era, where the Shogun wielded supreme military and political authority over the daimyos, while the Emperor remained the spiritual figurehead. The title of Shogun, initially seen as a temporary wartime position, quickly evolved into a hereditary office, cementing the Tetsu line as the rulers of the newly unified Shugane.<br />[h2]Consolidation and Expansion[/h2]<br />In the following decades, the Shogunate expanded its control over the rest of the Shuigari Archipelago, bringing the smaller islands under its sway through a combination of military campaigns and diplomatic marriages. During this period, the Shogunate established the capital in **Hikosan**, building the grand fortress of **Kuroyama Keep** as a symbol of the Shogun’s power. Kazuhito’s reign is remembered as one of strict enforcement of order and discipline, laying the foundations for the **Bushido Code**, which would later become the guiding principle for all samurai.<br />[h2]Founding of the Bushido Code[/h2]<br />The establishment of the Shogunate not only unified the fractured lands but also formalized the social and martial structures that would define the samurai class for centuries to come. In Year 450 CP, during the reign of Kazuhito’s grandson, the third Shogun, the **Bushido Code** was codified. This code emphasized absolute loyalty to one’s lord, personal honor, and mastery of martial skills. Under the strict enforcement of the Shogunate, the samurai were transformed from mere warriors into paragons of virtue and discipline, bound by a code that placed duty and honor above all else. This period also saw the creation of many of the great martial academies, where young samurai would train in the arts of war and philosophy.<br />[h2]The Emperor’s Blessing and the Silver Peace[/h2]<br />The **Silver Peace**, which began in **Year 800 CP**, was a time of relative stability, marked by a flourishing of the arts, literature, and culture. The Shogunate’s power was solidified when Emperor **Tenno I** blessed the Shogun’s family line, granting them the right to rule in perpetuity as guardians of the Emperor’s realm. Tenno I’s reign is remembered as a golden age of cooperation between the Shogunate and the Imperial Court, a stark contrast to the tensions that would arise in later centuries.<br />[h2]The Waning Shogunate and the Current Shogun[/h2]<br />Over time, however, the strength of the Shogunate began to fade. By the current era, the Shogunate’s authority is challenged on all sides. The current Shogun, **Mitsuhide Takara**, ascended to power in **Year 1171 CP** after the mysterious death of his predecessor. Though his early reign was marked by a fierce consolidation of power, his health has since deteriorated, and with it, the Shogunate’s control over the daimyos has begun to wane. This instability has led to renewed conflict, as rival factions within the Shogunate and the daimyo councils vie for dominance.<br />[h2]The Legacy of Emperor Tenno II[/h2]<br />Meanwhile, the birth of **Emperor Tenno II** in **Year 1170 CP** has rekindled hope in the Imperial Court. Named after his legendary ancestor, Tenno I, the young Emperor carries the burden of great expectations. His birth was heralded by celestial signs, and many believe that he is destined to restore the glory of the Imperial Line. However, Tenno II has yet to prove himself as a leader, and his influence over the Shogunate is limited. With the Shogun’s health failing, the Emperor’s supporters within the court are quietly gathering strength, seeking to position him as a true ruler in the turbulent times ahead.<br />[h2]Current Year: 1200 CP[/h2]<br />With 1200 years of history behind it, the Shogunate now stands at a crossroads. The legacy of the first Shogun, Kazuhito Tetsu, is one of unity and martial strength, but that unity is cracking. As the power of the Shogunate wanes and the daimyos grow bolder, the people of Shuigari look to both the Shogun and the Emperor for leadership. Whether the Shogunate will endure or collapse, only time will tell.

## The Sundering

**The Sundering of Ydrafor.** The Sundering was a cataclysmic event that reshaped the entire world of Atellas. It marked the end of the Common Period and the beginning of a new era, defined by fragmented realities and untamed magic. The Sundering was the culmination of the Convergence, a violent clash between the primordial forces of Order and Chaos, which had been unleashed during the shattering of the celestial Wheel of Ascension. The divine powers receded from the material plane, and as their influence waned, the world was torn apart. The supercontinent of Ydrafor splintered into four distinct landmasses: Shuigari, Lokai, Slauduin, and Ydra, each reflecting the energies of the realms that once coexisted in harmony.<br />The Impact of the Sundering<br />The Sundering was more than just a physical disaster—it was a cosmic upheaval that ripped the fabric of reality itself. Entire cities crumbled into the sea, mountain ranges split and reformed, and once-peaceful seas roared with new tempests. The remnants of the **Jarldenholmian Empire**, which had been the dominant power in Ydrafor, were obliterated, its mighty armies and great cities lost to the devastation. The survivors were scattered, and a new age of conflict and uncertainty dawned across Atellas.<br />The Creation of the Fractured Lands<br />As the dust settled, new landmasses emerged from the wreckage of Ydrafor, each taking on unique attributes influenced by the energies that tore them apart. **Shuigari**, the most spiritually resonant land, became a center of divine and mystical power, where the lines between the mortal and spirit worlds blur. **Lokai** is a land of relentless storms and unstable terrain, embodying the chaotic forces that reshaped it. **Slauduin**, the desert continent, is characterized by its ever-shifting dunes and the rocky shores that threaten the most adept of sailors with death, while **Ydra**, the remnant core of the supercontinent, bears the deepest scars of both divine and infernal forces, its shattered plains haunted by the lingering echoes of the Sundering.<br />The Role of Quelantheril Nev&#39;ash<br />During the chaos of the Sundering, the ancient lich **Quelantheril Nev&#39;ash** emerged as the ultimate arbiter of balance. Seeking to stabilize the forces of Order and Chaos, he bound these primordial energies within his undying form, becoming a living anchor for the fractured reality of Atellas. This act prevented the world from unraveling completely, but it came at a terrible cost. Quelantheril’s presence now influences the entire world, creating a delicate equilibrium that keeps the forces of Order and Chaos in check. The effects of his power can still be felt today, as the landmasses themselves subtly reflect the ebb and flow of this balance.<br />The Fragmented Realms<br />With the Wheel of Ascension shattered and Quelantheril acting as the stabilizing force, the borders between the material world and the divine realms became thin and permeable. Shuigari, Lokai, Slauduin, and Ydra are all touched by this instability. Travelers speak of places where the veil between worlds tears, allowing glimpses into realms of fire, shadow, or celestial light. The balance that Quelantheril maintains is fragile, and the ever-present tension between Order and Chaos shapes the fate of these lands, creating pockets of wild magic and zones of law where the nature of reality itself bends.<br />The Legacy of the Sundering<br />Twelve centuries have passed since the Sundering, but its effects still reverberate throughout Atellas. Shuigari’s spiritual resonance, Lokai’s tempestuous skies, Slauduin’s turbulent seas, and Ydra’s broken landscapes all bear witness to the cataclysm that gave them form. The people of these lands have learned to navigate the dangers and harness the powers that define their homes, yet the world remains in a state of flux, ever vulnerable to the forces that Quelantheril struggles to contain.<br />The Shattered Pantheon<br />The Sundering also marked the decline of the gods. Many were banished or destroyed, their essence scattered and absorbed into the land itself. Some of the surviving deities withdrew from the world, retreating into seclusion, while others remain as mere shadows of their former selves. The diminished presence of the gods left a void, giving rise to new beliefs and faiths. Mortals must now navigate a world where divine guidance is scarce, and the influence of yokai and kami is far more pronounced. This absence has allowed the rise of new powers, both mortal and spiritual, each seeking to fill the vacuum left by the shattered pantheon.<br />Current Era (1200 CP)<br />Today, the fractured lands are still shaped by the aftermath of the Sundering. The tenuous balance maintained by Quelantheril keeps the world from collapsing, but the equilibrium is far from perfect. With the decline of the Shogunate, the rise of new factions, and the growing influence of spiritual forces, the world of Atellas stands at a crossroads. The choices made in the coming years will determine whether Shuigari and its sister continents find stability or plunge once more into chaos.
