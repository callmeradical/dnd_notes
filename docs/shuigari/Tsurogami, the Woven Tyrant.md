# Tsurogami, the Woven Tyrant

*Primordial Deity of Destruction and Domination*

## Nature and Origins

- **Identity:** Tsurogami is an ancient entity of chaos, a being that predates the gods and represents the unraveling of mortal will and the reweaving of existence according to its alien designs.
- **Sealed Prison:** Tsurogami's essence is bound beneath [Takashima](Takashima%20Prefecture.md) in a complex web of celestial wards maintained by the balance of the Three Moons (Dendaro, Intara, Tsukuyomi).

## Manifestation

- **Appearance:** A vast, shapeless entity resembling a monstrous spider woven from threads of ichor and shadow. Its shifting form sprouts grotesque eyes, limbs, and mouths at random.
- **Voice:** Speaks in overlapping tones, each word laced with hypnotic power, compelling mortals to submit.
