# Weapon Crafting and Smithing Traditions of Shuigari

Master Hideo Nakata was celebrated, but he stood within a broader culture of smiths and forge orders. These notes provide hooks for crafters, rituals, and the politics of steel.

## Forge Orders
- **Forge of Reflection (Nagano):** Hideo’s workshop. Soulsteel attunement rituals require meditative dialogues between smith and wielder. Currently dormant; reactivating it is a key objective for Shinro Takeda.
- **The Ember Crucible (Way of the Flame):** Volcano monastery workshops specialising in fire-tempered blades. They demand oaths binding any new Soulsteel to responsible use.
- **Riverglass Foundries (Mizukawa):** Blend water rituals with metallurgic precision to create flexible naginata and ceremonial armour.
- **Iron Bell Collective (Kurogane-mura):** Renowned for bell metal and heavy armour. Resentful of Soulsteel because it bypasses their traditional mastery.

## Materials & Techniques
- **Soulsteel:** Requires kami blessing, wielder resonance, and rare ores. See [Soulsteel Blades – Legendary Weapons of Shuigari](Soulsteel%20Blades%20-%20Legendary%20Weapons%20of%20Shuigari.md).
- **Storm-Forging:** Harnesses captured lightning from Tatsuhara ritualists to temper blades that cut through elemental wards.
- **Moon-Quenching:** Uses Moonvine-infused water during Tsukuyomi festivals to create weapons that glow softly under moonlight, ideal for shrine guardians.
- **Ash-Tempering:** Incorporates purified ash from the Breathless Hollow—dangerous but creates blades that resist corruption.

## Crafting Ritual Hooks
- **Threefold Oath Ceremony:** Wielder swears loyalty to their cause, the smith vows craftsmanship, and a kami witness seals the pact. Breaking the oath risks the blade shattering or turning on its master.
- **Kami Ledger:** Ancestral smiths record every major blade in a ledger kept at the Temple of the Three Moons. The Circle seeks to steal entries to hide stolen Soulsteel.
- **Festival Commissions:** During the Moonlit Reverence festival, artisans compete to forge symbolic offerings. Winners gain political favour and access to rare materials.

## Adventure Seeds
- Recover a stolen Soulsteel ledger page before the Circle erases its trail.
- Help Shinro relight the Forge of Reflection by combining Way of the Flame techniques with elemental beast components.
- Broker peace between the Iron Bell Collective and Shinro to prevent a smithing feud from sabotaging weapon supplies for Takashima’s defence.
- Exorcise a cursed blade forged without proper vows, now haunting its owner.

Use these traditions to enrich downtime crafting, reward players with bespoke equipment, or escalate stakes when the Obsidian Circle corrupts the island’s industrial heart.
