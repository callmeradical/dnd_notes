# Kaoru Sugimoto's Arrival - The Starcall Table Scene

**Timing:** After sake scene with Mama Tora, before midnight (or after investigating the haunting)  
**Purpose:** Introduce Hidden Petal Society, provide Plot C mission options, connect to larger narrative  
**Key NPC:** Kaoru Sugimoto (Third Bloom)  
**Key Item:** The Starcall Table

---

## KAORU'S ARRIVAL

### The Knock (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> You're on your second (third? fourth?) cup of sake when Mama Tora's head snaps toward the door. Her hand moves to the iron poker by instinct.
>
> Someone is knocking.
>
> Not pounding. Not urgent. Just... persistent. Three measured knocks. Pause. Three more.
>
> "Nobody knocks on that door after dark," Mama Tora says quietly. She stands, poker in hand. "Not for two years."
>
> The knock comes again. Three. Pause. Three.
>
> Old Hiroshi looks up from his soup. "That's... that's the old merchant code. 'Friend seeking shelter.'"
>
> Mama Tora doesn't lower the poker, but she moves to the door. Slides the viewing panel aside. Her expression shifts - surprise, then something like respect.
>
> She opens the door.

### Kaoru Enters (Read Aloud - 3 minutes)

> The woman who enters is perhaps forty, though it's hard to say - she has one of those faces that could be younger with hard years or older with good care. She wears a traveling cloak of deep indigo over a practical kimono of gray silk with subtle purple patterns. Her hair is bound up with simple wooden pins, and she carries a leather satchel slung across her shoulder.
>
> But it's her eyes that catch you. Dark, intelligent, and utterly fearless. She surveys the room - notes each of you, Old Hiroshi, Mama Tora with her poker, the empty tables, everything - in a single sweeping glance. Missing nothing.
>
> "Mama Tora," she says, her voice cultured but warm, like a noble who actually speaks to commoners. "It's been too long. I heard you were still serving sake to ghosts and shadows."
>
> Tora lowers the poker slightly. "Kaoru. Didn't expect to see you on this street. Most people with sense stay away."
>
> The woman - Kaoru - smiles. "Then it's fortunate I've never been accused of having sense." She turns to you, and that smile deepens. "And you must be the ones who rescued Kazuo Himura from the Circle's warcamp. Word travels fast in the Middle Terrace. Especially when heroes arrive."
>
> She removes her cloak with a graceful gesture, revealing something strapped across her back - a flat wooden case, perhaps three feet wide and two feet tall, beautifully lacquered in dark wood with silver inlays.
>
> "I apologize for the intrusion, but I have a proposition. One that benefits us both." She glances at Mama Tora. "May I sit? I promise the sake will flow both ways."

### Mama Tora's Reaction

> Tora studies her for a long moment, then grunts. "You're paying customer, and you're braver than most. Sit. But you're pouring your own sake - I'm not your servant."
>
> Kaoru laughs - a genuine sound. "Wouldn't dream of it, old friend."
>
> (To the party) "Kaoru Sugimoto. Some call me a fixer. Others, a meddler. I prefer 'story-weaver' - I find threads that want to be connected and I... help them along."
>
> She sets the wooden case on the table with reverence.
>
> "But tonight, I'm simply someone who needs help. And who can offer help in return."

## THE STARCALL TABLE

### Unveiling (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> Kaoru's fingers move to the case's silver clasps. They open with soft clicks.
>
> She lifts the lid, revealing what lies within.
>
> It's a board - no, a map. No, something more. The interior of the case has been converted into an intricate display, and as lamplight falls across it, you see:
>
> A miniature representation of Takashima. Three tiers carved in relief - the Upper Citadel rising high, the Middle Terrace spreading wide, the Lower Yards sinking deep. Buildings are marked with tiny carved symbols. Streets wind between them like veins.
>
> But it's alive with markers. Small wooden tokens rest at various points across the map, each carved with different symbols and painted in different colors:
>
> - White tokens with cherry blossoms (completed matters)
> - Red tokens with warning symbols (urgent dangers)
> - Blue tokens with question marks (mysteries to solve)
> - Black tokens with tears (the troubled and suffering)
> - Green tokens with hands clasped (opportunities for alliance)
>
> And connecting some of these tokens: thin silver threads, barely visible, showing connections between seemingly unrelated events.
>
> "This," Kaoru says softly, "is the Starcall Table. Every token is a voice crying out in Takashima. Every thread is a connection waiting to be revealed. Some we can handle ourselves. Others..." She looks at you. "...require those with steel in their spine and fire in their hearts."

### Kaoru's Explanation (If Asked)

**"What is this?"**
> "A tool. A map. A... conscience, perhaps. The Hidden Petal Society - you may not have heard of us, and that's by design - maintains this. Every token represents someone who needs help. Someone whose problem falls through the cracks. Too small for the samurai, too shameful for the nobles, too supernatural for the guard."
>
> "We track them. We help when we can. We connect those who need help with those who can give it."

**"The Hidden Petal Society?"**
> "We bloom where no eye watches. We're messengers, fixers, investigators. We help commoners navigate threats no one else will touch. Missing persons. Spirit troubles. Stolen honor. Whispers of treachery. The small problems that become large tragedies if ignored."

**"Why are you showing us this?"**
> "Because you're different. You fought the Circle and won. You rescued Kazuo Himura. You're sitting in a cursed inn drinking sake instead of fleeing like sensible people." (A slight smile) "You're either exactly what this city needs, or you're going to get yourselves killed spectacularly. Either way, I'd rather have you working with us than against us."

**"What do you want?"**
> "Help. There are too many tokens, too few hands. And some of these..." (She touches a black token) "...are connected to something darker. The Obsidian Circle. The succession crisis. The very things you're already tangled in. Help the small people, and you'll uncover the big secrets. That's how this city works."

## THE TOKENS - MISSION OPTIONS

### Current Starcall Table Status

Kaoru gestures to the map. Various tokens are scattered across it:

#### BLACK TOKENS (The Suffering)

**1. The Iron Rest Haunting** (Current Location)
- Token placed directly on the inn
- "The weeping woman. Two and a half years. Mama Tora refuses to leave, and I refuse to let her die alone in an empty building."
- Silver thread connects to: closed tea house two doors down
- **Reward:** 100 gold (from Mama Tora), another 100 if they discover what happened to Yuki
- **Circle Connection:** Possible - the disappeared girl

**2. The Deep Mines Disappearances** (Lower Yards)
- Token in the mining district
- "Seven miners in three weeks. Gone without trace. No bodies. No blood. Just... empty tunnels."
- Silver thread connects to: Upper Citadel, warehouse district
- **Reward:** 50 gold, gratitude of miners' families
- **Circle Connection:** Definite - ritual sacrifices suspected

**3. The Widow's Vigil** (Middle Terrace, Temple District)
- Token at small shrine
- "Old Widow Masako sits at her husband's grave every night. She says she's waiting for him to come back. But something IS coming back, and it's not her husband."
- Silver thread connects to: Breathless Hollow (where party has been)
- **Reward:** No gold, but shrine blessing (mechanical benefit)
- **Circle Connection:** Possible - corrupted spirits

#### RED TOKENS (Urgent Danger)

**4. The Boy's Miracle** (Upper Citadel)
- Token at the citadel's highest point
- "Tomorrow. Noon. The boy heir performs another 'miracle' before the court. Each one grows stranger. Each one feels... wronger."
- Silver threads connect to: multiple locations, Circle safe houses
- **Reward:** Critical intelligence about the vault
- **Circle Connection:** CONFIRMED - This IS Kazuki's mission
- **Note:** "You're already involved in this, whether you know it or not."

**5. The Poison Wind** (Middle Terrace, Market)
- Token at the Iron Market
- "Someone is spreading sleeping powder at the market. Twenty collapsed yesterday. Some aren't waking. Circle agents preparing something? Or testing something?"
- Silver thread connects to: Lower Yards warehouse, medicine shop
- **Reward:** 75 gold, market guild favor
- **Circle Connection:** Likely - distribution network test

#### BLUE TOKENS (Mysteries)

**6. The Painted Walls** (Multiple Districts)
- Multiple tokens scattered
- "Symbols appearing on walls throughout the city. Always at night. Always in places no one should be able to reach. What do they mean?"
- Silver threads connect many locations in a pattern
- **Reward:** 50 gold, unlock fast-travel network
- **Circle Connection:** Unknown - possibly anti-Circle resistance?

**7. The Stolen Faces** (Middle Terrace)
- Token at theater district
- "Three performers. Their masks were stolen. Not expensive masks - personal ones. Family heirlooms. Now their actual faces are... changing. Becoming the masks they lost."
- No silver threads (isolated incident?)
- **Reward:** 60 gold, theater troupe performs for party
- **Circle Connection:** Possible - face-stealing yokai or curse

#### GREEN TOKENS (Alliances)

**8. The Ronin's Gathering** (Middle Terrace)
- Token at eastern district
- "Masterless samurai are gathering. Quietly. Purposefully. They're organizing. For what? Against what? Could be allies. Could be problem."
- Silver thread connects to: Upper Citadel, multiple exit roads
- **Reward:** Potential allies for combat missions
- **Circle Connection:** Unknown - investigating Circle or joining it?

## KAORU'S PITCH (Read Aloud - 3 minutes)

> Kaoru pours sake for everyone - herself included - with practiced grace. Then she leans back, studying each of you.
>
> "I won't insult your intelligence with pretty words. You know the Obsidian Circle is here. You know they're moving toward something. And you know that whatever it is, it's going to hurt people."
>
> She gestures to the black and red tokens.
>
> "These are the people who get hurt first. The miners who vanish. The widow waiting for a corrupted ghost. The boy who speaks with a god's voice that isn't divine. The small problems that connect to large catastrophes."
>
> Her finger traces the silver threads.
>
> "Help them, and you'll uncover threads leading to the Circle's heart. Ignore them, and you'll face the Circle blind. The choice is yours."
>
> (She drinks)
>
> "I can offer small payment - the Society pools resources. I can offer information - we see and hear everything in this city. I can offer shelter - even on cursed streets." (A nod to Mama Tora, who snorts but looks pleased) "And I can offer purpose - the knowledge that you're protecting those who can't protect themselves."
>
> "What I cannot offer is safety. The Circle knows we exist. They hunt us when they can. Working with us paints a target on your back."
>
> (Another drink, refills everyone's cups)
>
> "But I think you already have that target. I think you came to Takashima knowing you'd make enemies. So. Will you help us help them? Or will you walk your own path and hope our paths don't cross at the worst possible moment?"

## KAORU SUGIMOTO - FULL CHARACTER PROFILE

### Basic Information

**Full Name:** Kaoru Sugimoto (杉本 薫 - "Fragrance of the Cedar Grove")  
**Age:** 38 years old  
**Role:** Third Bloom of the Hidden Petal Society  
**Reputation:** "The Story-Weaver," "The Thread-Finder," "The Elegant Mediator"

### Physical Description

**Build:** Slender but strong, moves with dancer's grace  
**Height:** 5'6" (tall for Shuigari woman)  
**Face:** Elegant features, high cheekbones, kind eyes with sharp intelligence  
**Hair:** Black with hints of early gray, usually bound in practical but elegant style  
**Eyes:** Dark brown, observant, miss nothing  
**Clothing:** Quality but practical - favors indigo and gray, subtle purple accents (Hidden Petal colors)  
**Notable:** Wooden hair pins (actually weapons - +1 daggers), always carries the Starcall Table, wears no jewelry except small flower pin (cherry blossom - her identifier)

### Personality

**Public Face:**
- Warm, diplomatic, disarming
- Speaks in stories and metaphors
- Makes people feel heard and valued
- Never threatens, only suggests
- Smiles easily, laughs genuinely

**Private Truth:**
- Absolutely ruthless when protecting innocent
- Carries immense guilt about those she couldn't save
- Exhausted from balancing too many tragedies
- Deeply afraid the Society will fail when needed most
- Believes in small acts of kindness as revolution

**Core Traits:**
- Empathetic to fault
- Strategic thinker
- Patient (will wait years for right moment)
- Loyal to commoners over nobles
- Sees connections others miss

### Background

**Origin:** Born to minor merchant family in Takashima  
**Tragedy:** Parents killed during riot 20 years ago when she was 18  
**Recruitment:** Hidden Petal found her, trained her, gave her purpose  
**Rise:** Became Third Bloom through talent, dedication, and results  
**Current:** One of Society's most trusted operators

**Personal Mission:** 
- Prevent others from losing family like she did
- Expose corruption before it destroys lives
- Believes every person matters, even when system says they don't

### Skills & Abilities

**Social:**
- Master negotiator (advantage on Persuasion)
- Reads people instantly (advantage on Insight)
- Knows everyone and their secrets
- Can blend into any social class

**Investigation:**
- Pattern recognition genius
- Information synthesis
- Contact network throughout city
- Access to Society's archives

**Combat:**
- Not a warrior, but not helpless
- Hair pin daggers (+1, poisoned if needed)
- Will fight to protect clients/allies
- Prefers escape to combat

**Mechanical Suggestion:**
- Level 7 Mastermind Rogue / Level 3 Eloquence Bard
- Expertise in Insight, Persuasion, Investigation, Deception
- Spells focused on information gathering and social manipulation
- Not built for combat but can hold her own

### Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech:**
- Measured, thoughtful, uses silence effectively
- Speaks in complete sentences
- Uses metaphors and stories
- Voice is warm alto, carries authority without raising volume

**Mannerisms:**
- Touches the Starcall Table when thinking
- Pours tea/sake for others before herself
- Eye contact is intense but not threatening
- Gestures gracefully, economically
- Smiles with eyes first, mouth second

**Tells:**
- Touches flower pin when worried
- Goes very still when calculating
- Drinks more when stressed
- Speaks faster when passionate about something

### Relationships

**With Mama Tora:**
- Old friends (10+ years)
- Mutual respect
- Kaoru helped Tora when Kuro died
- Tora feeds Kaoru when she's running herself ragged
- Professional respect softened by genuine affection

**With Hidden Petal Society:**
- Respected but not revered
- Some members think she cares too much
- Others think she doesn't act fast enough
- All trust her judgment ultimately
- Carries weight of their expectations

**With Obsidian Circle:**
- They know she exists and hunts them
- She knows they know
- Cat and mouse game
- Several attempts on her life (all failed)
- Personal vendetta developing

**With Party (Potential):**
- Sees them as wildcards
- Valuable but unpredictable
- Will recruit if they prove trustworthy
- Won't hesitate to cut ties if they betray Society
- Hopes they'll be the allies she desperately needs

### What She Knows

**About the Party:**
- Rescued Kazuo Himura (impressive)
- Traveling with Twice-Cursed Kazuki (concerning)
- Investigating Upper Citadel (dangerous)
- Connected to Master Hideo's legacy (significant)
- Not sure if they're heroes or liabilities yet

**About the Circle:**
- Operating throughout Takashima
- At least 15 identified agents
- Plans involve the boy heir
- Something beneath Upper Citadel they want
- Timeline accelerating (Festival of Ascending Iron)

**About the City:**
- Every rumor, scandal, secret
- Which nobles are corrupt
- Where bodies are buried (literally)
- Faction tensions ready to explode
- Common folk's breaking point approaching

**What She Doesn't Know:**
- Full scope of Circle's ritual
- Location of vault
- Who the boy heir really is
- How deep Circle infiltration goes in Upper Citadel
- If Society can win this fight

### What She Wants

**From the Party:**
- Help with missions she can't handle
- Eyes and ears in places she can't go
- Someone to share the burden
- Proof they won't betray commoners for power

**For Takashima:**
- Expose Circle before they complete their plan
- Prevent civil war
- Protect common folk from noble schemes
- Restore faith that good people can make difference

**Personally:**
- Sleep without nightmares
- Save everyone (impossible, but she'll try)
- Honor her parents' memory
- Not die alone surrounded by empty tokens

## THE STARCALL TABLE - MECHANICAL DETAILS

### Physical Description

**Construction:**
- Lacquered wooden case (dark cherry wood)
- Silver inlay depicting Hidden Petal Society symbol
- Interior: 3D relief map of Takashima
- Dimensions: 3 feet wide, 2 feet tall, 4 inches deep when closed
- Weight: 15 pounds
- Quality: Masterwork, clearly expensive and maintained

**The Map:**
- Three-tiered city carved in relief
- Buildings marked with tiny symbols
- Streets carved as channels
- Can be updated with chalk or pins
- Beautiful AND functional

**The Tokens:**
- Hand-carved wood
- Painted with natural dyes
- Each symbol represents mission type
- Size: 1 inch diameter
- Stored in velvet-lined compartment

**The Threads:**
- Actual silver wire (thin as hair)
- Connect related tokens
- Can be added/removed as patterns emerge
- Sometimes form constellations (thus "Starcall")

### How It Works

**Updating:**
- Society members report issues
- Kaoru categorizes by urgency and type
- Places token on map location
- Connects to related issues with threads
- Updates as situations evolve

**Pattern Recognition:**
- Threads reveal hidden connections
- Multiple threads = major operation
- Isolated tokens = random or decoy
- Color-coded for priority

**Mission Selection:**
- Party can choose which tokens to address
- Some are time-sensitive
- Some connect to others
- Completing one may unlock others

### Mechanical Benefits (If Party Earns Access)

**Short Rest at Safe House:**
- Study Starcall Table
- Gain insight into one active mission
- Advantage on first Investigation check related to that mission

**Information Gathering:**
- Society has eyes everywhere
- Can ask about specific locations/people
- DC 15 Persuasion/shared mission history: Kaoru shares relevant intelligence

**Fast Travel Unlocked:**
- As party completes missions, unlock Society safe houses
- Can travel between districts faster
- Avoid random encounters in areas where Society operates

**Favor Currency:**
- Missions earn Society favor
- Spend favor for:
  - Information (1 favor)
  - Safe house access (2 favors)
  - Supply procurement (3 favors)
  - Emergency extraction (5 favors)
  - Society combat support (10 favors)

## SCENE RESOLUTION OPTIONS

### If Party Accepts Working with Society

> Kaoru's relief is visible for just a moment before her professional mask returns.
>
> "Thank you. Truly." She touches the flower pin at her collar. "You don't know how rare it is to find people who... care. Who see the small people and don't look away."
>
> She gestures to the Starcall Table.
>
> "Choose. One, two, all of them if you're ambitious. I'll provide what intelligence we have. When you solve a problem, send word through..." (She produces a small carved flower - cherry blossom) "...leave this at any shrine with a message. We'll find you."
>
> (Stands, preparing to leave)
>
> "And if you need shelter, the Iron Rest is under Society protection now." (A nod to Mama Tora) "The weeping won't touch you while we're watching. That's my word."
>
> Mama Tora harrumphs but looks touched.
>
> Kaoru pulls her cloak back on. "Choose wisely. Some of these tokens... if we don't help them, no one will. And the darkness is patient."

### If Party Declines

> Kaoru nods slowly, closing the Starcall Table with careful precision.
>
> "I understand. You have your own path. Your own mission. I won't pretend to know what drives you."
>
> (Stands, gathering her things)
>
> "But if you change your mind, the Society remembers. And if your path and ours cross..." (A slight smile) "...I hope we cross as allies, not obstacles."
>
> To Mama Tora: "Keep the good sake flowing, old friend. Better days will come."
>
> Tora: "Better days, or the end of days. Either way, I'll be here."
>
> Kaoru leaves as quietly as she came, and the inn feels somehow emptier for her absence.

### If Party Wants to Negotiate/Ask Questions

**Allow 10-15 minutes of conversation. Kaoru will:**
- Answer honestly within bounds of Society secrecy
- Share intelligence about missions they're interested in
- Provide context about Takashima's situation
- Gauge their trustworthiness
- Not pressure them to decide immediately
- Offer to return tomorrow if they need time

**Key Information She Can Share:**
- Details about any token mission they ask about
- How Society operates (general overview)
- Why she specifically came to them
- What's at stake if Circle succeeds
- How missions connect to larger plot
