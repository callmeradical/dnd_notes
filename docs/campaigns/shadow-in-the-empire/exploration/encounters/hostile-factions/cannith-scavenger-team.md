# Cannith Scavenger Team

## House Cannith Artificer
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (Studded Leather + Shield)  
**Hit Points** 44 (8d8+8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10(+0) | 14(+2) | 13(+1) | 16(+3) | 12(+1) | 11(+0) |

**Saving Throws** Con +4, Int +6  
**Skills** Arcana +6, Investigation +6, Perception +4  
**Senses** passive Perception 14  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Spellcasting.** The artificer is a 4th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following artificer spells prepared:

- **Cantrips (at will):** guidance, mage hand, mending  
- **1st level (4 slots):** detect magic, identify, shield  
- **2nd level (3 slots):** heat metal, magic missile, web  

**Magical Tinkering.** The artificer can imbue tiny objects with minor magical properties.

### Actions

**Light Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8+2) piercing damage.

**Dagger.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4+2) piercing damage.

**Infused Weapon (1/day).** The artificer can activate a magical enhancement on one weapon, giving it +1 to attack and damage rolls for 1 hour.

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 House Cannith Artificer + 2d4 Guards  
**Environment:** Ruins, mining sites, dragonshard deposits, crashed airships  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#8 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- **Artificer:** Brilliant inventor focused on recovering lost Cannith technology
- **Guards:** Professional soldiers loyal to House Cannith interests and contracts

**Ideals**

- Innovation: Lost knowledge and technology must be preserved and advanced
- House Glory: Everything done reflects on House Cannith's reputation
- Profit: Valuable discoveries fund future research and development

**Bonds**

- **Artificer:** Sworn to recover technology lost during the Mourning
- **Guards:** Contracted to protect Cannith assets and personnel
- Both bound by house loyalty and professional obligations

**Flaws**

- **Obsessive:** Will take dangerous risks to secure valuable magical items
- **Arrogant:** Believes Cannith innovations are superior to all others
- **Territorial:** Views all magical discoveries as rightfully belonging to their House

**Mannerisms**

- **Artificer:** Constantly examines objects with magnifying devices and detection spells
- **Guards:** Maintain professional bearing but show deference to artificer's expertise
- Team works with efficient, business-like coordination

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Contact:**
> "Greetings, travelers. I am [Name], representing House Cannith's Salvage and Recovery Division. Are you aware of any magical artifacts, construct remains, or dragonshard deposits in this area?"

**When Negotiating:**
> "House Cannith is always willing to trade fairly for information or artifacts. Our gold spends as well as anyone's, and our magical services are unmatched."

**If Threatened:**
> "Threatening House Cannith operations is unwise. We have resources and connections that extend far beyond this frontier."

### Tactics

**Initial Approach**
- Present credentials and explain legitimate salvage mission
- Offer trade opportunities and information exchange
- Assess party's magical items and potential value

**Combat Approach**
- Artificer uses spells for battlefield control and protection
- Guards form protective screen around artificer
- Focus on disabling rather than killing potential customers
- Use magical advantages and superior equipment

**Retreat Conditions**
- Withdraw if artificer is seriously threatened
- Guards sacrifice themselves to protect valuable artificer
- May flee to return with larger force or house support

### Knowledge

**What They Know**
- Locations of magical ruins, construct graveyards, and dragonshard sites
- Recent house activities and competing faction movements  
- Value and properties of magical items and arcane materials
- Political developments affecting house interests in Q'barra

**What They Seek**
- Lost Cannith technology, especially construct-related items
- Dragonshard deposits and mining rights
- Information about rival house activities
- Specimens of unique magical creatures or phenomena

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 2d4 for guard numbers (3-8 guards plus artificer)
- Determine team's current mission: salvage operation, site survey, or recovery mission
- Note party's visible magical items that might interest Cannith
- Consider whether team has found anything valuable recently

**Opening Scene Options**

**Professional Salvage Operation:**
> *A well-organized camp sits among the ruins ahead, with several humans in House Cannith colors carefully excavating magical artifacts. Their leader, a woman with artificer's tools and detection devices, approaches with a professional smile. "Greetings, travelers. I am Artificer d'Cannith. Perhaps we can be of mutual assistance?"*

**Competitive Recovery:**
> *The sound of magical analysis emanates from the crashed airship wreckage. A House Cannith team has clearly beaten you to this location, their artificer using detection spells while guards stand watch. When they notice your approach, hands move to weapons, though their leader raises a hand for parley.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Business Assessment (Social Primary)**
- Artificer evaluates party's potential as customers, competitors, or threats
- Offers trade opportunities and information exchange
- Assesses party's magical resources and knowledge
- **Insight Check (DC 12):** Recognize artificer is appraising party's value and threat level
- **Arcana Check (DC 14):** Identify specific magical items or effects team is seeking
- **Persuasion Check (DC 13):** Establish friendly business relationship

**Phase 2: Negotiation and Trade**
- If party appears valuable, artificer proposes mutually beneficial arrangements
- May offer magical services, information, or gold for party assistance
- Becomes territorial if party claims rights to same magical discoveries
- **Investigation Check (DC 15):** Assess true value of items artificer offers
- **Deception Check (DC 14):** Conceal valuable magical items from artificer's notice
- **History Check (DC 12):** Know house politics and potential complications

**Phase 3: Alliance or Competition**
- Successful negotiations lead to temporary partnership or information exchange
- Failed diplomacy may result in competing claims or resource conflicts
- Open hostility only if party directly threatens house interests

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Demonstrating magical knowledge or artificer skills
- Offering valuable information about magical sites or artifacts
- Showing respect for House Cannith's expertise and reputation
- Proposing mutually profitable business arrangements

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Claiming exclusive rights to magical discoveries in area
- Criticizing House Cannith methods or magical innovations
- Associating with rival dragonmarked houses
- Attempting to conceal obviously valuable magical items

**Business Relationship Considerations**
- Cannith values profit and innovation over personal relationships
- Professional courtesy expected but warm friendship not necessary
- Contracts and agreements taken very seriously
- House reputation more important than individual team members

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Profitable partnership with shared information and resources
- Access to House Cannith services and magical expertise
- Possible ongoing business relationship beyond current encounter
- Invitations to house facilities or introduction to other teams

**Success**
- Fair trade agreements for information or services
- Neutral coexistence with no territorial conflicts
- Basic magical services available for reasonable payment

**Failure**
- Competitive relationship with potential resource conflicts
- Higher prices for any house services needed
- Suspicion about party's true intentions and capabilities

**Critical Failure**
- Open hostility and competing claims to magical resources
- House Cannith networks warned about untrustworthy party
- Possible theft attempts or sabotage of party operations

**Combat Considerations**

**Magical Advantages**
- Artificer uses spells for battlefield control and team protection
- Detection spells reveal hidden party members and magical items
- Shield spell and magical armor provide superior defense
- Heat metal and web spells control enemy positioning

**Professional Team Tactics**
- Guards form protective formation around valuable artificer
- Crossbow volleys before closing to melee combat
- Coordinated retreat if artificer's safety is threatened
- Use superior equipment and magical enhancements

**Aftermath Scenarios**

**If Combat Occurred**
- House Cannith marks party as threats to house interests
- Other Cannith teams in region become hostile
- Potential escalation to house vendetta or bounty situation
- Loss of access to house services and magical expertise

**If Resolved Peacefully**
- Ongoing business relationship provides future opportunities
- Access to house information networks and magical services
- Possible recruitment for house-sponsored expeditions
- Reputation among dragonmarked houses as reliable partners

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Artificer has information about party's quest but requires payment or service
- Competing salvage claims create ongoing subplot
- House needs party's help with dangerous recovery mission
- Artificer's discoveries lead to larger adventure opportunities

**Treasure and Services**
- 3d6 × 50 gp in house currency and letters of credit
- 1d3 minor magical items or alchemical supplies
- Spell services available for appropriate payment
- Maps and documentation of regional magical sites

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce numbers, make artificer more willing to cooperate
- **Harder:** Add second artificer or magical construct assistant
- **Environmental:** Encounter at contested magical site with time pressure
- **Political:** Complex house politics require careful diplomatic navigation
