# Geography of Shugane

## Plains of Kisaragi

The **Plains of Kisaragi**, often called the *“Breadbasket of Shugane”*, are a vast expanse of fertile farmland stretching across the heart of the island. This region is known for its sprawling **rice paddies**, golden fields of **barley**, and tranquil **tea plantations**. The plains are crisscrossed by numerous rivers and canals that branch off from the mighty **River Torawa**, providing essential irrigation to the fields and linking the many small villages and market towns that dot the landscape. Though the plains are a place of relative peace and prosperity, they are not without their dangers. Bands of marauding **yokai**, and rival daimyo seeking control of the fertile lands, often clash here, threatening to disrupt the delicate balance of the region.<br />[h2]Mythology[/h2]<br />According to local legend, the Plains of Kisaragi were once a barren wasteland until the kami of the land, **Kisara-no-Kami**, blessed the earth with her tears, turning the soil fertile and giving rise to the lush fields that now stretch as far as the eye can see. The people of Kisaragi believe that Kisara-no-Kami’s spirit still watches over the plains, ensuring the land’s bounty as long as proper respect is shown. It is said that every few decades, a sacred tree, the *“Kisara Sakura”*, blooms somewhere in the fields, and those who find it and offer prayers are granted a season of unparalleled harvest.<br />[h2]Special Locations[/h2]<br />**Silverwind Market:** Located at the center of the plains, Silverwind Market is the largest and most vibrant trading hub in the region. Farmers and merchants from across Shugane gather here to trade goods, from rice and tea to silks and rare herbs. The market is known for its colorful festivals and bustling atmosphere, but it is also a place of intrigue, where political agents and rival factions vie for influence over the local economy.<br /><br />**The Fields of Gold:** An endless stretch of barley fields that glow a brilliant gold under the sun. Local farmers believe the fields are blessed by the sun kami, and it is considered good fortune to harvest the first crop at sunrise. However, strange figures have been seen wandering the fields at dusk—phantom farmers believed to be the spirits of those who perished defending the land from invaders.<br /><br />**Tea Gardens of Jisaku:** A series of terraced tea plantations on the eastern edge of the plains, renowned for producing the finest tea in all of Shuigari. The gardens are meticulously maintained by a reclusive order of monks known as the *“Jisaku Brotherhood”*, who claim the plants are tended under the guidance of the kami of tranquility. The tea from these gardens is said to have subtle mystical properties, providing clarity of thought and vision to those who drink it.<br /><br />**The Hollow Mound:** A mysterious burial site located in a secluded corner of the plains, the Hollow Mound is an ancient barrow believed to hold the remains of a long-forgotten warlord. It is said that his restless spirit haunts the mound, guarding a treasure of immense value. The Hollow Mound is avoided by locals, who whisper that only the bravest or most foolish dare disturb the spirit’s eternal watch.<br />[h2]Legends[/h2]<br />**The Curse of the Scorched Fields:** Local farmers tell of a curse that befalls those who disrespect the land. It is said that when someone tries to exploit the fields without honoring the kami of the plains, the crops will wither and the earth itself will crack and burn, leaving a desolate patch of scorched earth. This phenomenon is rare but serves as a grim reminder to always show respect to the spirits of the land.<br /><br />**The Phantom Harvesters:** During the autumn harvest, some claim to see ghostly figures working in the fields at night. Known as the Phantom Harvesters, these spirits are believed to be the souls of farmers who died defending their lands from bandits. It is said that if you leave an offering of rice at the edge of your field, the Phantom Harvesters will ensure a bountiful harvest—but failing to do so may invite disaster.<br /><br />**The Shrine of the Wandering Kami:** There is a legend that speaks of a wandering shrine that appears only during the full moon. Those who manage to find it are said to be able to commune directly with Kisara-no-Kami herself and ask for a single blessing. However, the shrine is guarded by a fierce guardian spirit that tests the resolve of all who seek it.<br />[h2]Creatures that Inhabit the Plains[/h2]<br />**Yokai of the Crops:** Mischievous spirits known as *Tanuki* and *Kodama* inhabit the fields and groves of the plains. While they are generally benign, they can become hostile if their homes are disturbed or if the fields are not properly maintained. They are known for playing tricks on unwary travelers and sometimes stealing crops during the night.<br /><br />**Gaki (Hungry Ghosts):** The Hollow Mound is home to *Gaki*, the spirits of those who died of starvation or neglect. These malevolent ghosts haunt the burial site, eternally hungry and vengeful. They are said to prey upon those who venture too close, feeding on the life force of the living.<br /><br />**Rice Field Serpents:** Long, sinuous snakes that are believed to be the guardians of the rice paddies. These serpents are seen as both protectors and omens, bringing good fortune when they appear in your fields but foreshadowing disaster if they are harmed or killed.<br /><br />**Kami&#39;s Deer:** Rare, snow-white deer said to be the messengers of Kisara-no-Kami. Seeing one is considered a great blessing, and it is believed that the deer guide the lost and protect those who show respect to the land.<br />[h2]Factions[/h2]<br />**The Keepers of the Soil:** A group of farmers and land stewards who maintain the spiritual and physical health of the Plains of Kisaragi. They are highly respected and work closely with the local priests to conduct rituals that ensure the land’s fertility.<br /><br />**The Jisaku Brotherhood:** A reclusive order of monks who tend to the Tea Gardens of Jisaku. The Brotherhood is dedicated to preserving the ancient techniques of tea cultivation, believing the gardens to be a direct gift from the kami of tranquility. They guard the secrets of their craft fiercely.<br /><br />**The Golden Crescent Society:** A powerful merchant guild that controls most of the trade flowing through the Silverwind Market. They are known for their wealth and influence, and are suspected of manipulating local politics to expand their control over the plains.<br />[h2]Rumors[/h2]<br />**The Whispering Earth:** Farmers have reported hearing strange whispers coming from beneath the soil at night. Some say these voices belong to ancient spirits trapped beneath the fields, while others believe they are the voices of the dead calling out for release.<br /><br />**The Harvest Moon Ritual:** It is said that every ten years, a secret ritual is performed during the Harvest Moon to summon Kisara-no-Kami to the fields. Those who partake in the ritual are granted a single wish, but the ritual’s details are known only to the Keepers of the Soil.<br /><br />**The Blood of the Warlord:** Some say that the treasure within the Hollow Mound is not gold or jewels, but the warlord’s own blood, transformed into a powerful elixir that can grant immortality to anyone who drinks it. Many have sought it, but none have survived the warlord’s wrath.

## Rei no Mori

The Rei no Mori, also known as the Forest of Souls, is a vast and mysterious woodland on the eastern side of Shugane, renowned for its ethereal beauty and profound spiritual significance. Constantly shrouded in a soft veil of mist and twilight, the forest is believed to be a place where the veil between the mortal realm and the spirit world is at its thinnest, allowing kami, yokai, and the souls of the dead to freely move between worlds.<br /><br />Adventurers, monks, and pilgrims are drawn to the Rei no Mori in search of spiritual enlightenment, lost knowledge, or forbidden power, but it is also a place fraught with peril, where yokai and vengeful spirits roam, and those who stray from the path often disappear, never to be seen again.<br />[h2]Mythology[/h2]<br />The Rei no Mori has been revered for centuries as a sacred place where the souls of the dead are said to find peace, guided by the kami that dwell within the forest. According to legend, the forest was created by the kami of death, Shinigami, who granted it to the spirits as a sanctuary. Over time, the forest has become a gateway between life and death, a place where the spirits of the departed linger before moving on to the afterlife.<br /><br />It is said that within the heart of the forest lies the Yūrei Oka (Hill of Spirits), where the boundary between the worlds is so thin that the living can hear the whispers of the dead. Those who venture here are believed to be able to commune with their ancestors or even receive visions of their future fate.<br />[h2]Special Locations[/h2]<br />**The Yūrei Oka (Hill of Spirits).** The most sacred location within the forest, this ancient hill is said to be a place where the spirits of the dead gather. It is here that monks and pilgrims come to commune with the departed, seeking guidance or closure. It is also a place of great spiritual danger, where the unwary might be lured into the spirit world by malevolent forces.<br /><br />**The Shrine of Whispering Leaves.** Hidden deep within the forest, this shrine is dedicated to the kami of wind and whispers. It is said that the trees around the shrine are inhabited by spirits who communicate with the living through the rustling of the leaves. Pilgrims come here seeking answers to riddles or guidance on difficult decisions, but the spirits are known to be fickle, sometimes offering misleading or dangerous counsel.<br /><br />**The Twilight Grove.** A small, serene clearing in the forest where twilight never fades, even during the day. This grove is often used by monks as a place of meditation and spiritual cleansing. However, it is also a known haunt of benevolent yokai, who sometimes test those who enter the grove with spiritual trials.<br /><br />**The Lost Path.** A shifting trail within the forest that only reveals itself under certain conditions, such as during foggy nights or under a full moon. It is said that the Lost Path leads to a forgotten temple where the yokai lords of old were once worshipped. However, finding the path is treacherous, and those who follow it may never return.<br />**The Mirror Pools.** A series of crystal-clear ponds scattered throughout the forest that reflect not only the physical world but also glimpses of the spirit realm. Those who gaze into the pools are said to see visions of the future or past lives, but the pools can also trap the unwary, leading them to become lost in phantom reflections.<br />[h2]Legends[/h2]<br />**The Spiritbound Sword.** According to legend, there is a sword hidden deep within Rei no Mori that was once wielded by a famous samurai who became bound to the spirit world after his death. It is said that the sword contains a fragment of his soul, and whoever wields it can summon his spirit to fight alongside them. However, the sword is also cursed, and its wielder may become trapped between life and death.<br /><br />**The Yokai Queen.** An ancient tale speaks of a yokai queen who ruled over the forest centuries ago. Her spirit is said to still dwell within the deepest parts of the forest, guarding a great treasure. Those who venture into her domain often find themselves caught in elaborate illusions, unable to distinguish between reality and the spirit world.<br /><br />**The Ghostly Procession.** It is believed that during the full moon, a procession of spirits walks through the forest, retracing the path they walked in life. It is said that those who encounter this procession will either be blessed with good fortune for the rest of their lives or cursed to join the spirits in their eternal march.<br />[h2]Creatures that Inhabit the Forest[/h2]<br />**Onryō (Vengeful Spirits).** These are the restless souls of those who died violently or with unresolved grievances. They roam the forest, seeking vengeance on the living. These spirits are dangerous and unpredictable, often attacking travelers or leading them into traps.<br /><br />**Kitsune (Fox Spirits).** The forest is home to several kitsune, mischievous fox spirits who enjoy playing tricks on the unwary. Though not inherently malevolent, they can be deceptive and manipulative, sometimes leading travelers astray or involving them in dangerous games.<br /><br />**Tengu (Crow Demons).** These bird-like yokai are said to inhabit the highest treetops of Rei no Mori, watching over the forest. The tengu are known for their martial prowess and are often depicted as skilled swordmasters. They occasionally challenge travelers to duels, and those who best them may earn their respect and protection.<br /><br />**Yurei (Ghosts). **The spirits of the dead, known as yurei, are a common sight in Rei no Mori. They are often seen floating through the trees or wandering the forest paths. While many are harmless, simply searching for peace, others can be vengeful and will attack those they perceive as a threat.<br /><br />**Spirit Wolves.** These ethereal wolves are guardians of the forest, believed to be the protectors of sacred shrines and kami within the Rei no Mori. They are both feared and revered by the locals, appearing only to those in need or those who disturb the balance of the forest.<br />[h2]Factions[/h2]<br />**The Whispering Monks.** A group of spiritual ascetics who have dedicated their lives to maintaining the balance of souls within the forest. They believe that the spirits of the dead should not be disturbed and spend their days performing rituals of exorcism and meditation to keep the spirits at peace. They also act as guides for those seeking the Yūrei Oka.<br /><br />**The Yokai Pact.** A secretive group of shrine guardians who worship the yokai of the forest. They believe that the yokai are part of the natural order and that appeasing them through rituals and offerings is the best way to keep the forest in balance. They are viewed with suspicion by the monks, who see them as heretics.<br /><br />**The Lost Seekers.** A faction of adventurers and scholars who seek to uncover the lost knowledge and artifacts hidden within the forest. They are known for taking great risks in their explorations, and many of their number have disappeared in the Rei no Mori, never to be seen again. However, those who return often bring with them valuable information or rare treasures.<br />[h2]Rumors[/h2]<br />**The Soul Collector.** A terrifying rumor speaks of a yokai known as the Soul Collector, who roams the forest at night, stealing the souls of those who venture too far into the Rei no Mori. It is said that the Soul Collector can trap souls in objects, using them to increase its own power.<br /><br />**The Hidden Library.** There are whispers of a hidden library within the forest, said to contain forbidden knowledge of the spirit world and the secrets of the afterlife. However, the Lost Path leading to the library is treacherous, and few who seek it have returned.<br /><br />**The Whispering Blade.** A rumor persists of a sword forged in the forest, whose blade is imbued with the voices of the dead. Those who wield it are said to hear the whispers of spirits, guiding them in battle but also driving them mad over time.<br /><br />Rei no Mori is a place of mystical beauty and deep spiritual significance, but it is also a land filled with danger and dark forces. The forest’s spiritual power and otherworldly atmosphere offer adventurers the chance to uncover hidden knowledge, commune with the spirits, or claim forbidden treasures—if they are willing to risk the wrath of the yokai and the souls of the dead that haunt its mist-covered paths.

## River Torawa

The **River Torawa** is the largest and most vital river on the island of **Shugane**, flowing from the **Ryuseki Peaks** in the north, through the central plains, and emptying into the **Sea of Mirrors** along the eastern coast. Revered as the *lifeblood of the island*, the river nourishes the vast rice fields and supplies fresh water to many of the island’s towns and villages. But the River Torawa is much more than a simple waterway—it&#39;s regarded as a sacred entity, home to powerful **kami**, and steeped in legends of ancient beings and forgotten magic.<br />Mythology<br />The people of Shugane believe the River Torawa was created by the **kami of water**, *Ryujin*, after he descended to cool the burning earth left by the eruptions of **Mt. Hoshigami**. Legend says Ryujin guided the river’s waters to flow through the land, blessing it with life and fertility. It&#39;s said that Ryujin still dwells in the deepest parts of the river, protecting it from harm and punishing those who disrespect its sacred waters.<br /><br />The river is also known as the *Pathway of Spirits*, a sacred route believed to guide the souls of the dead from the mountains to the sea, where they journey to the afterlife. Rituals are performed along its banks during festivals to ensure safe passage for these souls.<br />Special Locations<br /><br />**Shrine of Ryujin:** Located near the river’s source in the *Ryuseki Peaks*, this shrine is dedicated to the water kami, Ryujin. Pilgrims come here to pray for rain, harvests, and protection. It&#39;s said that Ryujin appears as a giant serpent to bless the waters during certain seasons.<br /><br />**Wailing Falls:** A series of waterfalls cascading from the mountains into the river. Local legend claims the falls are haunted by spirits of those lost in floods, whose mournful cries can be heard on quiet nights.<br /><br />**Pool of Reflections:** A still pool in the lower plains, said to show visions of the past or future. It&#39;s believed that the kami communicate with those who seek guidance through the pool’s reflective surface, though impure hearts may see dark omens.<br /><br />**Lost Village of Nanami:** Once a prosperous fishing village, Nanami was abandoned after angering Ryujin through overfishing. The river rose and swallowed the village, and now the ruins lie submerged, haunted by *yokai of drowned souls*.<br /><br />**Bridge of Ten Thousand Prayers:** An ancient stone bridge adorned with thousands of prayer tags and charms tied by those seeking protection from floods. It&#39;s considered sacred, and travelers often leave offerings for Ryujin before crossing.<br />Legends<br />**Serpent of Silver Waters:** A legend speaks of a silver serpent that swims the length of the river, ensuring its purity. This serpent, believed to be an avatar of Ryujin, is said to bless fishermen and boatmen who encounter it with good fortune.<br /><br />**River’s Heart:** Hidden deep beneath the river’s surface is a mythical pearl called the River’s Heart, which is said to contain the power of Ryujin. Those who possess it can command the waters, but the pearl is fiercely guarded by spirits.<br /><br />**Flood of Shadows:** Every century, the River Torawa risks being consumed by dark forces. During this time, the river is said to turn black as yokai of darkness overtake it. Only the *Ritual of the Silver Serpent* can prevent disaster.<br />Creatures that Inhabit the River<br />**Kappa:** Mischievous, turtle-like water spirits that play tricks on travelers near the river. They sometimes challenge people to games or duels, and are known to drown those they deem disrespectful.<br /><br />**Ryujin’s Serpents:** Large, serpentine creatures believed to be guardians of the river, manifestations of Ryujin’s power. They patrol the waters, maintaining the balance of nature.<br /><br />**Yurei of Nanami:** Restless spirits of those who perished when the village of Nanami was flooded. These vengeful spirits haunt the area, dragging the disrespectful into the river’s depths.<br /><br />**River Spirits (Mizuchi):** Small, benevolent spirits that live in sacred parts of the river. They help purify the water and protect travelers who offer proper respects. Playful by nature, they guide the lost to safety.<br />[h2]Factions[/h2]<br />**Keepers of the Torawa.** Priests and monks dedicated to protecting the river and maintaining its balance. They oversee the Shrine of Ryujin and conduct annual rituals to honor the water kami.<br /><br />**Ferrymen’s Guild.** A guild of boatmen who transport goods and people along the river. They are well-versed in the river’s currents and superstitions, and some are rumored to have made pacts with spirits for safe passage.<br /><br />**Order of Silver Waters.** A secretive order of warriors and spiritualists who seek the River’s Heart, the legendary pearl that can control the flow of the river. They are sworn to protect the river’s sacred sites.<br />[h2]Rumors[/h2]<br />**The River That Walks:** A persistent rumor claims that the River Torawa is a living entity that can shift its course at will, punishing those who disrespect its power by moving or flooding their lands overnight.<br /><br />**The Drowned King:** Some whisper of a yokai lord known as the Drowned King, said to slumber beneath the river. It is believed he will one day rise, bringing chaos and darkness to the riverbanks.<br /><br />**The River’s Blessing:** An old tale suggests that by diving into the river at midnight during a full moon and offering a silver mirror to Ryujin, one can gain the River’s blessing, ensuring good fortune and protection from floods.

## Ryuseki Peaks

The Ryuseki Peaks, also known as **the Dragonstone Mountains**, dominate the landscape of Shugane, their towering volcanic peaks a constant reminder of the island’s elemental power and spiritual significance. These mountains are a place of both natural beauty and mystical danger, home to ancient legends, sacred sites, and powerful spirits. Rising from the heart of the island, the Ryuseki Peaks are revered by the people of Shugane as the dwelling place of the kami and the source of the island’s life force. The Peaks are a place of danger, mystery, and spiritual significance, where the lines between mortal ambition and divine intervention blur. For adventurers, the peaks offer both great rewards and immense perils, as the secrets of the mountains and their ancient powers continue to shape the future of Shugane.<br />[h2]Mythology[/h2]<br />The Ryuseki Peaks are steeped in the mythology of the kami and yokai, with the towering Mt. Hoshigami at the center of it all. It is said that the mountains were formed by the clash between two great dragons, one representing Order and the other Chaos, during the creation of the world. The defeated dragon’s body became the stone and ash of the mountains, while its spirit was said to seep into the land, forever imbuing the mountains with immense spiritual energy.<br /><br />At the heart of the mythology surrounding the peaks is Izanami no Kagutsuchi, the fire kami who resides within the active volcano, Mt. Hoshigami. According to legend, the volcanic eruptions are manifestations of Kagutsuchi’s emotions—when he is angered, the mountain spews lava and ash; when he is pleased, the mountain remains calm, and the land thrives. The people of Shugane believe that maintaining the balance of the kami within the peaks is essential to keeping the island fertile and prosperous.<br />[h2]Special Locations[/h2]<br />**Mt. Hoshigami.** The tallest and most sacred peak in the Ryuseki range, Mt. Hoshigami is both a revered site and a dangerous place. Pilgrims travel to the Fire Shrines at the mountain’s base, where monks perform rituals to honor Kagutsuchi and seek his blessings. Few dare to ascend to the volcanic caldera, where the air is thick with ash and the spirits of fire roam freely.<br /><br />**Kiyomizu Lake.** Nestled in the foothills of the Ryuseki Peaks, this sacred lake is believed to be blessed by the water kami. The River Kiyomizu flows from the lake, bringing life-giving water to the plains of Shugane. The lake is a popular destination for pilgrims and priests, who come to perform rituals of purification.<br /><br />**The Hidden Shrine of Ryujin.** Deep within the mountain caves, there is a secret shrine dedicated to Ryujin, the dragon god of water. Legend says that Ryujin’s spirit was bound to the mountains after the clash of the great dragons. Those who manage to find the shrine are said to receive visions of the past and future, though the journey is perilous, with lava flows and rockslides blocking the way.<br /><br />**The Ashen Fields.** A desolate plain of blackened stone and hardened lava that stretches across the southern slope of the peaks. This barren land is said to be cursed by Kagutsuchi, where yokai roam freely and the dead are unable to rest. Many warriors come here to test their courage, but few return unscathed.<br /><br />**The Celestial Pagoda.** A sacred tower built high in the peaks, where monks meditate to commune with the kami of the wind and sky. It is said that the winds here carry whispers of the gods, and that those who can hear them will gain insight into the true nature of the world.<br />[h2]Legends[/h2]<br />**The Sleeping Dragon.** There is a long-held belief that beneath the Ryuseki Peaks lies the body of a great dragon, slumbering since the dawn of time. It is said that if the balance between the kami and yokai in the mountains is broken, the dragon will awaken, bringing cataclysmic destruction not just to Shugane, but to all of Shuigari.<br /><br />**The Sword of Eternal Flame.** According to legend, hidden deep within Mt. Hoshigami is a sword forged by Kagutsuchi himself, said to be a weapon of immense power. The sword is said to burn with an eternal flame that can cut through anything. Many have sought this weapon, but none have returned.<br /><br />**The Spirit of the Ryuseki.** Local legend speaks of a mysterious spirit that guards the Ryuseki Peaks. Described as a luminous figure that walks among the ash and stone, this spirit is believed to be a guardian of balance, ensuring that the powers of Order and Chaos do not overwhelm one another. Some say the spirit is a manifestation of the fallen dragon, watching over the land it once shaped.<br /><br />[h2]Creatures that Inhabit the Peaks[/h2]<br />**Fire Elementals.** Roaming the lava flows and volcanic craters, these beings are embodiments of Kagutsuchi’s fiery power. They are both feared and revered, often seen as messengers of the fire kami, carrying out his will.<br />Yokai of the Ashen Fields: The Ashen Fields are home to numerous yokai, from vengeful spirits of those who died in volcanic eruptions to shadowy creatures born from the ash. These spirits are drawn to the residual power left behind by ancient conflicts between the kami.<br /><br />**Oni.** Large, demonic creatures that stalk the mountain caves and prey upon travelers. Oni are said to be spawned from the hatred and fear of those who died in the mountains, cursed to wander the Ryuseki Peaks for eternity.<br /><br />**Ryuseki Serpents.** Gigantic serpent-like creatures that are rumored to dwell deep beneath the earth, occasionally emerging during volcanic eruptions. These creatures are believed to be remnants of the fallen dragon that helped form the mountains, and their appearance is seen as a bad omen.<br /><br />[h2]Factions[/h2]<br />**The Fire Monks of Hoshigami.** A group of devout monks who live at the base of Mt. Hoshigami. They are tasked with maintaining the Fire Shrines and performing rituals to appease Kagutsuchi. The Fire Monks are highly respected and feared, as they are believed to have direct access to the will of the fire kami.<br /><br />**The Guardians of the Peaks.** A secretive order of warriors dedicated to protecting the sacred sites of the Ryuseki Peaks. They are said to be the descendants of ancient dragon hunters, sworn to prevent the awakening of the Sleeping Dragon.<br /><br />**The Ashen Brotherhood.** A rogue group of adventurers and mercenaries who explore the Ashen Fields in search of ancient relics and lost treasures. Though they are notorious for their reckless behavior, they are also rumored to possess forbidden knowledge about the mountains.<br /><br />[h2]Rumors[/h2]<br /><br />**The Dragon’s Heart.** A rumor has spread through the towns surrounding the peaks that there is a magical gemstone called the Dragon’s Heart, hidden deep within the Ryuseki Peaks. This gemstone is said to contain the essence of the dragon that created the mountains and could grant immortality or unlimited power.<br /><br />**The Yokai King.** Whispers among the people tell of a Yokai King who has made his home in the Ashen Fields. Said to be the ruler of all the yokai that haunt the peaks, this figure is believed to be gathering dark forces to one day challenge the kami and plunge the island into chaos.<br /><br />**The Hidden Forge.** Adventurers speak of an ancient forge hidden somewhere in the depths of Mt. Hoshigami, where Kagutsuchi himself is said to have forged the weapons of the gods. Those who can find the forge are said to be able to create weapons of unimaginable power, but the forge is also guarded by fire spirits and lava beasts.
