# Akan’s Elixirs and Herbs

## About
A 323 years old male hill dwarf scribe. He has long, straight, black hair and blue eyes. He has veiny golden skin.
He stands a little over 4 foot and has a muscular build. He has an oblong, cute face with a medium beard. He is missing his right foot, which has been replaced with what looks to be an Iron made implement of some sort that resembles a chisel.


## Personality

Akan is a pious dwarf who quietly worships Tyr, God of justice. He is always very excited and very self-confident. He carries out a complicated religious ritual every morning and occasionally gives to the poor and needy. He has a habit of working hard but if only to play hard afterward. Akan practices gem-cutting as a hobby. 

## Available Wares
He has a map of Evereska that he is willing to part with.

In addition to the map Akan has a variety of potions available for sale.
