## Quick Generation Tips
- Combine a first name with a descriptive surname to hint at origin or role: *Takumi Shadowbrook* (scout), *Yuki Raincaller* (shrine maiden), *Shin Ironshell* (guard captain), *Nozomi Moonwatch* (oracle), *Riku Thornwatch* (ranger).
- Nobility often favour traditional surnames (Arashi, Kagamura). Commoners or guild members may adopt nature- or profession-linked surnames.
- Kami-touched or spiritual NPCs can carry evocative surnames (Sunwhisper, Ghostpine) to suggest connections beyond the mortal realm.

Keep this list handy during prep or at the table to maintain naming consistency across Shuigari.
