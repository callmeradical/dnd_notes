## Background

Aoshi carries the weight of being the last of the Oni Waban - a title that suggests they are the final survivor or practitioner of an ancient warrior tradition connected to oni heritage. This identity as "the last" creates both a sense of purpose and profound isolation.

## Current Situation

### The Vision of Shikigo
Aoshi seeks meaning behind a vision of **Shikigo**, whose nature and significance remain mysterious. This vision drives Aoshi's journey and suggests a spiritual connection to forces beyond the mortal realm.

### The Vision of Oni Babamon
Aoshi has also received visions of **Oni Babamon**, another entity whose relationship to Aoshi and purpose remains unclear. These visions may be:
- Ancestral communications
- Spiritual guidance
