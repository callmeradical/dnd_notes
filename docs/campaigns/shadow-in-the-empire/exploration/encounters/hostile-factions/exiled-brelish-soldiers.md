# Exiled Brelish Soldiers

## Brelish Veteran
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class** 17 (Splint)  
**Hit Points** 58 (9d8+18)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16(+3) | 13(+1) | 14(+2) | 10(+0) | 11(+0) | 10(+0) |

**Skills** Athletics +5, Intimidation +2, Perception +2, Survival +2  
**Senses** passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The veteran makes two longsword attacks. If it has a shortsword drawn, it can also make a shortsword attack.

**Longsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10+3) slashing damage if used with two hands.

**Shortsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing damage.

**Heavy Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d10+1) piercing damage.

### Reactions

**Parry.** The veteran adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the veteran must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

## Brelish Soldier
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

**Armor Class** 18 (Chain Mail, Shield)  
**Hit Points** 16 (3d8+3)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13(+1) | 12(+1) | 12(+1) | 10(+0) | 11(+0) | 10(+0) |

**Skills** Athletics +3, Perception +2  
**Senses** passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

**Spear.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6+1) piercing damage, or 5 (1d8+1) piercing damage if used with two hands to make a melee attack.

**Heavy Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d10+1) piercing damage.

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 Brelish Veteran + 1d4+1 Soldiers  
**Environment:** Abandoned outposts, defensible positions, river crossings  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#6 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- **Veteran:** Bitter war hero stripped of rank and exiled for political reasons
- **Soldiers:** Loyal followers who chose exile over abandoning their commander
- All carry deep resentment against Brelish nobility and political machinations

**Ideals**

- Brotherhood: Loyalty to comrades supersedes loyalty to corrupt nations
- Honor: True soldiers protect each other regardless of politics
- Survival: Adapt and endure until opportunities for redemption arise

**Bonds**

- **Veteran:** Responsible for soldiers who sacrificed careers to follow into exile
- **Soldiers:** Unshakeable faith in veteran's leadership and eventual vindication
- Shared memories of battles fought and comrades lost in the Last War

**Flaws**

- **Bitter:** Cannot discuss Breland or the war without anger
- **Paranoid:** Suspects political enemies have sent assassins or spies
- **Desperate:** Willing to take dangerous contracts to fund their survival

**Mannerisms**

- **Veteran:** Still wears tarnished officer's insignia despite official disgrace
- **Soldiers:** Maintain military discipline and address veteran by former rank
- All show visible signs of hard living but keep weapons and armor in perfect condition

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Challenge:**
> "That's far enough, strangers. State your business and your allegiances. We've had enough of Brelish 'diplomats' and their lies."

**When Discussing Exile:**
> "Fought for that cursed crown for twenty years, bled for Breland in a dozen battles. Our reward? Exile because we wouldn't lie about what really happened at [battle name]. Politics, not honor, rules that nation now."

**When Recruiting:**
> "You have the look of capable people. We're building something here—a company of soldiers who serve honor, not politicians. The pay's fair and the cause is just."

### Tactics

**Initial Approach**
- Form defensive positions with clear fields of fire
- Veteran questions party while soldiers maintain readiness
- Assess threat level and potential for recruitment or alliance

**Combat Approach**
- Veterans coordinate soldier positioning and target priority
- Use crossbows from cover before engaging in melee
- Soldiers protect veteran and maintain formation discipline
- Fight with tactical coordination learned in professional military

**Retreat Conditions**
- Withdraw in good order if facing superior force
- Never abandon wounded comrades if rescue is possible
- May offer surrender if completely outmatched but honorable opponent

### Knowledge

**What They Know**
- Detailed military intelligence from Last War campaigns
- Current Brelish political situation and internal conflicts
- Mercenary opportunities and potential employers in the region
- Safe routes and dangerous areas based on recent scouting

**What They Seek**
- Contracts for military services with honorable employers
- Information about Brelish agents or political enemies
- Resources to establish permanent military company
- Allies who understand the value of professional soldiers

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 1d4+1 for soldier numbers (2-5 soldiers plus veteran leader)
- Determine unit's current situation: recently arrived vs. established in area
- Note any party members with Brelish connections or military backgrounds
- Consider veteran's specific exile circumstances and remaining loyalties

**Opening Scene Options**

**Military Checkpoint:**
> *A makeshift outpost blocks the forest path ahead—professionally constructed earthworks and camouflaged positions that speak of military experience. Soldiers in faded Brelish uniforms emerge from concealment, crossbows trained on your group. Their leader, a grizzled veteran with torn insignia, steps forward. "That's far enough, strangers."*

**Mercenary Camp:**
> *Smoke rises from a disciplined military camp hidden among the ruins. As you approach, sentries challenge you with professional competence. At the camp's center, a scarred veteran in patched officer's gear looks up from studying regional maps. "Either you're here about the contract, or you're lost. Which is it?"*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Military Assessment (Social Primary)**
- Veteran evaluates party's threat level and potential usefulness
- Questions about political affiliations and military experience
- Tests party's honor and trustworthiness through conversation
- **History Check (DC 12):** Recognize specific battle references and military units
- **Insight Check (DC 13):** Understand veteran's deep bitterness toward Brelish politics
- **Persuasion Check (DC 14):** Gain veteran's respect through honest communication

**Phase 2: Negotiation or Recruitment**
- If party proves trustworthy, veteran may offer alliance or employment
- Professional military services available for appropriate compensation
- May share intelligence or resources in exchange for mutual assistance
- **Deception Check (DC 16):** Lie about Brelish connections (veteran is perceptive)
- **Intimidation Check (DC 15):** Threaten the unit (likely to provoke defensive response)

**Phase 3: Alliance, Employment, or Conflict**
- Successful negotiations lead to potential mercenary contracts or military aid
- Failed diplomacy may result in territorial conflict or mutual avoidance
- Combat only occurs if party poses direct threat to unit's security

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Demonstrating military knowledge or veteran status
- Showing respect for soldier's sacrifice and service
- Offering fair compensation for professional military services
- Criticizing corrupt politicians or acknowledging political injustice

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Any obvious connection to Brelish nobility or current government
- Dismissing veteran's grievances or defending political decisions
- Questioning unit's competence or loyalty to each other
- Attempting to recruit individual soldiers away from the group

**Military Culture Notes**
- Veterans value directness and professional competence
- Loyalty to comrades is absolute and non-negotiable
- Honor matters more than money, but money is still necessary
- Political discussions trigger deep anger and suspicion

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Mercenary contract for military services or escort missions
- Access to veteran military intelligence and tactical advice
- Possible permanent alliance with growing mercenary company
- Introduction to other military contractors and professional soldiers

**Success**
- Neutral relations with mutual respect
- Limited cooperation for specific tasks or information exchange
- Fair prices for any military services needed

**Failure**
- Continued suspicion and territorial behavior
- No military assistance available regardless of payment offered
- May actively oppose party if interests conflict

**Critical Failure**
- Open hostility and potential combat
- Warning sent to other military units about untrustworthy party
- Active recruitment of enemies and rivals against party

**Combat Tactics Deep Dive**

**Professional Military Coordination**
- Veteran directs soldiers with clear tactical commands
- Crossbow volleys from prepared positions before melee engagement
- Disciplined retreat and regrouping if initial positions compromised
- Soldiers willing to sacrifice for veteran's survival

**Battlefield Positioning**
- Use terrain and prepared positions for maximum advantage
- Establish overlapping fields of fire for crossbow support
- Maintain formation integrity even during tactical movement
- Protect wounded comrades while maintaining combat effectiveness

**Equipment and Training**
- Well-maintained weapons and armor despite exile circumstances
- Professional military tactics adapted to mercenary operations
- Veteran's parry ability represents superior combat training
- Soldiers follow orders without hesitation even under pressure

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Combat Occurred**
- Other mercenary units learn of party's hostile actions
- Professional military community becomes less trusting
- Potential ongoing conflict with established mercenary companies
- Loss of access to professional military services and intelligence

**If Resolved Peacefully**
- Access to growing network of military contractors and veterans
- Potential ongoing mercenary services for appropriate missions
- Military intelligence about regional threats and opportunities
- Reputation among professional soldiers as honorable employers

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Veteran has specific knowledge relevant to party's quest
- Mercenary contract leads to larger military campaign
- Political enemies in Breland create ongoing subplot
- Unit's establishment affects regional power balance

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce numbers, make veteran more willing to trust
- **Harder:** Add second veteran or specialized military roles
- **Environmental:** Encounter at strategically important location
- **Political:** Complex political situation requires careful navigation

**Service Opportunities**
- **Escort Missions:** 50-100 gp per day for military escort
- **Tactical Consultation:** Professional military advice for complex operations
- **Training Services:** Combat training for party members or allies
- **Intelligence Gathering:** Military reconnaissance and area assessment
