# Weakened Spectral Samurai

*Medium undead, lawful neutral*

**Armor Class:** 15 (spectral armor)
**Hit Points:** 44 (8d8 + 8)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

**Saving Throws:** Dex +4, Wis +3
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Insight +3, Perception +3
**Damage Resistances:** Necrotic, Psychic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13
**Languages:** Common, Spirit Tongue
**Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

## Traits

**Ethereal Stride.**
The Spectral Samurai can move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. If it ends its turn inside an object, it takes 5 (1d10) force damage.

**Soul Blade.**
The Spectral Samurai's katana is a spectral weapon that deals necrotic damage in addition to its normal damage type.

## Actions

**Multiattack.**
The Spectral Samurai makes two melee attacks with its spectral katana.

**Spectral Katana.**
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 2) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

**Phantom Strike (Recharge 5–6).**
The Spectral Samurai teleports up to 20 feet to an unoccupied space it can see and strikes with its katana. Each creature within 5 feet of the destination must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 9 (2d8) necrotic damage.

**Challenge of Honor (1/Day).**
The Spectral Samurai targets one creature within 30 feet and marks it for 1 minute. While marked, the Samurai has advantage on attack rolls against the creature, and its attacks deal an extra 2 (1d4) necrotic damage to the marked target. The effect ends if the Samurai is reduced to 0 hit points or the marked creature is incapacitated.

## Reactions

**Parry.** The Spectral Samurai adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the Samurai must see the attacker and be wielding its katana.

## Tactics

- **Opening Moves:** The Samurai uses **Challenge of Honor** against a perceived leader or strongest attacker, creating tension and a duel-like scenario.
- **Mobility:** It uses **Phantom Strike** sparingly, repositioning to target vulnerable backline characters or avoid being surrounded.
- **Defensive Maneuvers:** It relies on **Parry** to stay in melee combat longer, focusing on strong melee attackers to maximize its defenses.

## Adjustments for Difficulty

**Easier:** Reduce its hit points to 35 and remove the necrotic damage from its katana.
**Harder:** Increase the damage dice of **Phantom Strike** to 3d8 or allow it to mark two creatures with **Challenge of Honor**.
