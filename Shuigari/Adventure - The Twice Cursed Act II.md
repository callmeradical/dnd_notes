# Adventure: The Twice Cursed – Act II, Journey Through Hisui no Mori

Act II escorts the party from Nagano’s mourning into the living maze of the Jade Forest (Hisui no Mori). The goal: follow clues, earn the forest’s favor, and locate the gate into the Ryuseki Peaks.

## Objectives
1. **Track the Clues:** Follow the raven feather’s pull, missing shrine relics, or spectral footprints leading into the forest.
2. **Navigate the Living Maze:** Overcome shifting paths, spirit lantern riddles, and disorienting fogs.
3. **Face the Forest’s Trials:** Engage kami, yokai, and environmental tests through diplomacy, wits, or combat.
4. **Unlock the Ryuseki Gate:** Prove worth to the spectral guardian and secure passage toward the Twice-Cursed Samurai.

## Day 1 — Afternoon: Entering Hisui no Mori
- **First Checks:** Survival (DC 12) or Perception (DC 15) to stay on the true trail. Failure introduces kitsune trickery or detours toward yokai lairs.
- **Shrine Encounter:** A sorrowful Lesser Earth Kami warns “The forest remembers; tread lightly.” Offerings grant advantage on future navigation checks.
- **Environmental Tone:** The air hums with latent magic; characters feel watched even in silence.

## Day 1 — Evening: Shifting Paths
- **Skill Challenge:** Use the “Navigating the Living Maze of Hisui no Mori” challenge (Athletics, Survival, Insight, Arcana) to push through shifting routes.
- **Spirit Lantern Puzzle:** Lanterns marked with kanji for Hideo’s virtues lead onward; misreading the signs attracts shikigami or shadow wolves.
- **Ambush Risk:** Failing Stealth/Insight checks triggers a coordinated strike by yokai defenders testing the party’s readiness.

## Day 1 — Night: Trials of the Forest
- **Trial of Wits:** Reflections pose riddles tied to steel and honor. Success reveals a hidden causeway; failure inflicts exhaustion or psychic strain.
- **Trial of Honor:** A kami in samurai guise challenges the group to a controlled duel or a debate on duty. Victors gain inspiration-like boons; dishonorable conduct angers the forest.
- **Optional Trial:** Players can petition the forest for guidance, trading personal secrets or memories for safe passage.

## Day 2 — Dawn: Threshold of the Ryuseki Peaks
- **Ancient Gate:** Stone torii carved with Soulsteel motifs hum with moonlit energy.
- **Spectral Samurai:** Guardian demands proof of worth—either a duel (use the Weakened Spectral Samurai stat block) or solving the Gate of Master Hideo puzzle.
- **Consequences:** Victory opens the passage and awards a blessing; failure means retreating to recover or negotiating aid from forest spirits.

## Playstyle Guidance
- **Spiritual Route:** Emphasize diplomacy with kami, invoking rituals and offerings to gather allies or shortcuts.
- **Martial Route:** Treat encounters as a gauntlet of escalating battles culminating in the spectral duel.
- **Exploratory Route:** Focus on lore discoveries—cartography, hidden shrines, and clues pointing toward the Obsidian Circle’s manipulations.

By the end of Act II, the party should feel transformed—tempered by riddles, whispers, and the forest’s judgment—as they ascend toward the Ryuseki Peaks and the Twice-Cursed Samurai.
