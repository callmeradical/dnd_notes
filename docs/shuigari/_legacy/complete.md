## What We Are Aiming For

A blend of **Kurosawa-inspired drama** (complex characters, moral ambiguity, and stark beauty)

with the **fantastical awe of Studio Ghibli** (living spirits, magical nature, and ethereal

landscapes).

Think **Game of Thrones-like intrigue** , but tempered with a spiritual reverence that keeps even

the most ruthless characters bound by a higher order.

set in the fragmented world of Shuigari. The players are drawn into the Ryuseki Peaks, a

treacherous mountain range shrouded in myth, to uncover the truth behind Master Hideo’s

death and confront the enigmatic Twice-Cursed Samurai, who resides in a labyrinthine domain

death and the strange circumstances surrounding his funeral. As they uncover the truth, they

must journey into the treacherous Ryuseki Peaks to confront the enigmatic Twice-Cursed

Samurai, a tragic and dangerous figure whose actions hold the key to Hideo’s sacrifice. Along

the way, the party will explore an ancient forest alive with spiritual forces, navigate a labyrinthine

stronghold, and face trials of honor, resolve, and survival.

Samurai’s role in protecting Hideo’s hidden legacy.

#### Adventure Hooks for The Twice-Cursed Blade

Here are some adventure hooks for The Twice-Cursed Blade to draw players into the mystery

surrounding Master Hideo’s death and the journey to confront the Twice-Cursed Samurai. Each

hook presents a unique motivation for players to engage with the story, providing flexibility

based on their character backgrounds and the party dynamic.

##### Honoring a Master’s Legacy

Master Hideo was a revered smith and sage, known for his unmatched skill in forging Soulsteel

blades and his unwavering dedication to aiding others. Each player has a personal reason to

honor Hideo’s memory:

```
● Perhaps he crafted a weapon or tool for them in the past.
```
```
● Maybe he offered wisdom that changed the course of their life.
```
**Motivation.** Attending the funeral allows the players to pay their respects, but whispers of foul

play and a strange emissary at the ceremony compel them to investigate. What could have led

to Hideo’s death by the hand of the Twice-Cursed Samurai?

##### A Debt of Honor

One of the players (or someone close to them) owes a life-debt to Master Hideo. Perhaps Hideo

defended them from a threat, repaired an irreplaceable item for them, or helped their family in a

time of need.


**Motivation.** With his death, the players feel duty-bound to ensure his passing was honorable

and to protect his legacy. When the Twice-Cursed Samurai’s jackals deliver their cryptic

message at the funeral, the party’s oath to repay the debt drives them to investigate the

Samurai and their connection to Hideo.

##### Protecting the Boy

The players notice a child being protected by a veiled woman at the funeral, seemingly avoiding

attention. Rumors among the mourners suggest the boy is Hideo’s grandson and that he may

be in danger following the master’s death.

**Motivation.** When the players learn of jackals from the Ryuseki Peaks lurking near Nagano,

they suspect the child may be a target. Their investigation leads them to uncover Hideo’s true

connection to the boy and the role of the Twice-Cursed Samurai in his death.

##### The Call of the Cursed Blade

The Twice-Cursed Samurai’s blade is a weapon of legend, said to have been forged in part by

Master Hideo himself. Rumors claim the blade has the power to sever a soul and bind it in ways

few can comprehend.

**Motivation.** Hearing of Hideo’s death at the hands of the Samurai drives the players to seek the

truth. Was Hideo’s death a betrayal, or is there something more to this fateful act? Tracking the

blade and its wielder to the Ryuseki Peaks becomes their obsession.

##### A Rivalry Rekindled

One of the players has a personal rivalry with the Twice-Cursed Samurai, either due to a shared

history or a grudge involving their cursed blade. The Samurai’s appearance at Master Hideo’s

death reignites this rivalry, providing a chance to settle the score.

**Motivation.** The player must navigate their desire for revenge while uncovering the Samurai’s

connection to Hideo. Along the way, they discover a deeper purpose to Hideo’s death, forcing

them to choose between vengeance and understanding.

##### Seeking the Soulsteel Forge

Master Hideo’s forge, known for producing unparalleled Soulsteel blades, is rumored to hold

secrets about the craft. The players—whether smiths, warriors, or seekers of knowledge—are

drawn to Nagano to learn from the master’s legacy.

**Motivation.** At the forge, the players find clues pointing to a final, unfinished blade and cryptic

messages hinting at Hideo’s purpose. The path to completing the weapon leads to the Ryuseki

Peaks and the Twice-Cursed Samurai.


##### The Jackal’s Challenge

At the funeral, a jackal emissary of the Twice-Cursed Samurai appears and challenges the

players directly:

```
“Master Hideo’s fate is sealed, but his path is not yet finished. Seek the peaks if you wish
```
```
to know the truth.”
```
**Motivation.** The players are intrigued or insulted by the jackal’s message. Whether to uncover

the Samurai’s role in Hideo’s death, prove their strength, or learn more about the master’s

legacy, they set off toward the Ryuseki Peaks.

##### A Broken Spirit

A local shrine keeper or wandering priest tells the players of strange spiritual disturbances near

Nagano. The kami grow restless, and the yokai become bold, whispering of a fractured soul

lingering in the mortal world.

**Motivation.** The players are drawn to uncover the spiritual imbalance, which leads them to the

Twice-Cursed Samurai and the nature of Hideo’s fractured soul. Resolving this disturbance

becomes a test of their honor and spiritual strength.
