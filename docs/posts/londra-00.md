## Overview

The table came together with a shared hunger for something different—a story
shaped by character choice, built on grit, consequence, and shadow. Instead of
high fantasy heroics, this world leans into dirt under the fingernails: broken
systems, flickering hope, and people trying to survive in the cracks.

The campaign structure feels cinematic—episodic, focused, finite. Every scene
matters. Every session moves something forward. Rest is rare. Magic is unstable.
Reputation cuts deeper than gold.

The group embraced the idea that they’re not a band of allies, but a bundle of
frayed wires sparking in the dark—bound not by trust, but by necessity. Secrets
are few. Motivations are murky. But together, they form something that just
might hold the line.

The fog is coming. And this time, the light isn’t guaranteed.
