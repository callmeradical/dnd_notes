# Wyrmwatch Tower

**Location:** 200 miles deep in the Q'barran wilds  
**Type:** Ancient colonial watchtower complex  
**Proprietor:** Captain Dareth Ironward

---

## Outpost Description

### **Physical Layout**
A massive stone watchtower rises from a fortified compound, its upper levels providing commanding views of the surrounding jungle canopy. The original colonial architecture has been reinforced with newer defensive additions - iron spikes, murder holes, and reinforced gates. Multiple smaller towers and buildings cluster around the main structure, connected by covered walkways that allow movement without exposure to aerial threats.

### **Atmosphere**
The constant sound of wind through the tower's heights mingles with the distant calls of exotic jungle creatures. Weapon racks line the walls, and every surface bears scorch marks or claw gouges - evidence of draconic encounters. Despite the damage, the complex radiates defiance and determination, a fortress that refuses to fall.

### **Notable Features**
- Central stone tower standing 80 feet high with observation platforms
- Reinforced compound walls with anti-aerial defenses
- Underground storage vaults carved into bedrock
- Massive ballista emplacements designed for dragon-hunting
- Signal beacon capable of sending messages across vast distances

## Proprietor

### **Captain Dareth Ironward**
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful good*

**Appearance:** Weathered human veteran in his late fifties with graying hair and numerous scars. Wears modified plate armor bearing the insignia of an old Brelish ranger company. His left arm is a mechanical prosthetic of obvious Cannith make, ending in a hand that can lock around weapons with inhuman strength.

**Personality:** Grimly determined veteran who sees Wyrmwatch as the last bastion of organized resistance to draconic rule. Speaks with military precision but shows genuine care for his soldiers and travelers alike. Haunted by past failures but driven by the belief that someone must stand watch.

**Background:** Former Brelish frontier ranger who lost his arm and most of his company to a dragon attack three years ago. Gathered survivors and refugees to maintain Wyrmwatch as a monitoring post and refuge. Refuses to abandon his duty despite being cut off from any higher command.

**Services Offered:**
- Military intelligence about draconic movements and territories
- Training in anti-dragon tactics and equipment use
- Secure communication relay to other distant outposts
- Veteran's guidance on survival in wyrm-haunted lands

## Patrons & Rumors

### **Lieutenant Kara Stormwind**
*Medium humanoid (half-elf), lawful neutral*

**Appearance:** Athletic half-elf in her thirties with wind-tousled brown hair and keen amber eyes. Wears practical leather armor designed for mobility and carries an enchanted longbow. Her ranger's cloak bears the patches of multiple frontier companies, marking her as a veteran of many campaigns.

**Background:** Scout and intelligence officer who maintains Wyrmwatch's network of informants and hidden caches throughout Q'barra. Has spent years mapping dragon territories and understanding their behavioral patterns. Knows more about current draconic politics than almost anyone.

**Key Information:**
- **"The Crimson Brood has claimed the eastern valleys - their territorial markers appear every few weeks."**
- **"A new dragon calling itself 'The Collector' has been gathering artifacts from ruined cities."**
- **"The old ley lines are changing. Something is affecting the very magical structure of the land."**

**Services:**
- **Territory Maps:** Detailed charts of known dragon domains and safe routes (100 gp per region)
- **Behavioral Analysis:** Intelligence on specific dragons' tactics and weaknesses
- **Cache Locations:** Hidden supply drops throughout Q'barra for emergencies
- **Guide Services:** Personal escort through dangerous territory (negotiable rates)

---

### **Thane Korvin Deepdelver**
*Medium humanoid (dwarf), neutral good*

**Appearance:** Stocky mountain dwarf with intricate braids in his beard and hands permanently stained by cave minerals. His armor is reinforced with strange metallic inlays that seem to shift color in different light. Carries a massive warhammer that hums with barely contained energy.

**Background:** Former deep miner who discovered something profound in the deepest tunnels - evidence that dragons are not the original rulers of this land. Now serves as Wyrmwatch's chief engineer and keeper of dangerous knowledge about pre-draconic civilizations.

**Key Information:**
- **"The dragons fear what lies beneath. Their hoards are built on older treasures they dare not touch."**
- **"Ancient wardstones still function in the deep places - but their power is failing."**
- **"I've seen markings that predate the dragons by millennia. They conquered this land, but they didn't create it."**

**Services:**
- **Ancient Lore:** Knowledge of pre-draconic civilizations and their defenses (150 gp per consultation)
- **Wardstone Repair:** Restoration of ancient magical defenses (materials + 500 gp)
- **Deep Tunneling:** Access to underground routes that avoid aerial detection
- **Artifact Analysis:** Identification and understanding of pre-draconic relics

## Roleplay Opportunities

- **Historical Context:** Understanding the deeper history behind current events

## GM Notes

- Disciplined hierarchy offers structure but may conflict with party independence
- Veterans' experience invaluable for surviving encounters with dragons

### **Historical Significance**
- Pre-draconic artifacts and knowledge provide long-term story hooks
- Ancient defenses might be key to eventually challenging draconic rule
- Understanding true history reveals that dragons are conquerors, not rightful rulers

### **Strategic Position**
- Wyrmwatch serves as intelligence hub for entire region
- Communication network connects distant outposts and resistance cells
- Elevated position provides early warning of approaching threats
