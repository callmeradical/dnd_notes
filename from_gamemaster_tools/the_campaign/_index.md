## Campaign Hook

**What is this campaign about?** *What is the goal?*

Welcome to Bastion, The Last City. A megacity that operates on clockwork
machinery surrounded by the Endless Sea, a desert with no end.

Intrigue and adventure are easy to find in Bastion.  We will start in this
adventure just days after the patriarch of one of the oldest families in
Bastion is assassinated. Tensions are high amongst the various merchant guilds
as they scramble to usurp each other in the chaos that follows.

The party will impact the world around them as a war erupts, choosing sides and
uncovering something even more dire than the war, is it the end of Bastion
itself.

## The Six Truths of Your World

**What makes this campaign unique?**

1. Merchant houses engage in corporate espionage.
2. A war long ago threatened to destroy the world Bastion is the sole surviving
   city.
3. A mysterious villain pulls the strings from the shadows.
4. Bastion itself is breaking down.
5. Ruins exist everywhere, and fantastic things are to be found.
6. The city of Bastion is a place of civilization.

## Campaign Fronts

**What are the major moving forces in this campaign?**

### Kalarel The Vile (Based on Halaster Blackcloak), Kalarel Candiano

**Goal:** Looking to seize an army by using the souls of the dead to create
magical tokens that drive people mad, he is ultimately looking for revenge for
his own death. In that revenge he wants to destroy Bastion.

#### Grim Portents

1. The effects of the red gold.
2. The abundance of red gold in other places.
3. Distribution and adoption of this currency by Ordine

### Julia Marivaldi

**Goal:** To restore rule of Bastion to the Royal Family, her family.

1. Overwhelming force in Invernale
2. Begins hunting down Alunno, and elminating with extreme prejudice.
3. Begins circulating red gold, with her likeness and the house crest on the back.

#### Grim Portents

1. War breaking out with Alunno in Invernale, and occupies the town, begins
   marching on Seven Hills.
2. Using occidental technology to do so, believing she is just.
3. Destruction of an entire plate.

### Syrino Alunno

**Goal:** Solidify control of Precinct 6, and eliminate the other merchant houses.

#### Grim Portents

1. Incredible firepower, tanks and other weapons of war in the battle against Marivaldi.
2. Retreats to Monte Di Madre, believing the vault of Berronar's Hearth has what they seek.
3. Completely loses control of Precinct 6: Cagliari

### Sico Sordillo

**Goal:** To amass wealth by plundering the ruins of Cagliari. He is willing to
get this done by bribery or coercion.

#### Grim Portents

1. Begins buying up adventuring parties, mass advertisement of work.
2. Attempts to buy the Iron Lass
3. Ends up dead, after a deal with a Marivaldian agent. (Agazio)

### Bastion Itself

Bastion itself is a front and the characters must deal with the collapse of
their home and the last hold of civilization.

**Goal:** Bastion is breaking down and is failing.

#### Grim Portents

1. Cagliari stops moving
2. The plate above stops moving
3. The highest plates also stop moving.
4. The Spire becomes unstable and eventually plummet into the land of the Endless Sea.
