# Akane Hiroshi

**Role:** Bodyguard and trusted lieutenant to [[Lord Ichiro Kagemura]].

**Appearance:** A lithe woman in lacquered green-and-silver armor, with a calm but calculating demeanor. She carries a yari (spear) and a wakizashi.

**Personality:** Loyal and pragmatic, Akane quietly observes the mourners, looking for potential threats or political opportunities.

**Notable Action:** During the funeral, she keeps close to Kagemura but occasionally exchanges meaningful glances with [[Hiroto Masanori]], hinting at an unspoken rivalry or shared history tied to Lady Hana’s retinue.

## The Rivalry between Akane and Masanori

1. **Shared History:**
   - Masanori and Akane were once sparring partners in their youth, both serving rival lords in the same region.
   - During a skirmish, Akane defeated Masanori and left him humiliated. Worse, her intervention contributed to his lord's downfall, marking the beginning of Masanori's fall from grace.
   - Masanori holds Akane responsible for his current status as a ronin-turned-retainer.
