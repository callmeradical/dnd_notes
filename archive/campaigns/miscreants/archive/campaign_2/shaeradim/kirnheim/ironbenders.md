# Ironbender's Armaments

## Location

On Ashgrove Lane, in a formerly arcane district of narrow alleys and
elemental forges. The street outside is lined with a wrought-iron fence.

## Description

The shop is a two-storey tower of conjured stone, with a smooth stone floor.
It is built around a huge fallen meteorite.

## Shopkeepers

The shopkeepers are a pair of old male dwarves named Dunbin and Forovin. Dunbin is Curiously suspicious of visitors and patrons of his shop. Forovin, on the otherhand, makes tankards in his spare time, and is actually a weretiger, his brother is suspicious because of his condition and is protective.


### Dunbin
A 179 years old male mountain dwarf. He has cropped, straight,
brown hair and red eyes. He has rough white skin. He stands 4'0" tall and has
a muscular build. He has a triangular, typical face with a medium van dyke
beard.He has an unfinished piercing on his nose, a large piercing on his nose
and a shocking piercing on his left eyebrow.

### Forovin
He is a 179 years old male mountain dwarf and the second born of the twins. He has cropped, straight, brown hair and
red eyes. He has rough white skin. He stands 4'0" tall and has a muscular
build. Like his brother he has a triangular, typical face with a long beard.

Forovin wears a charm that prevents his transformation and worships Selune. It is moonstone carved into the shape of a tiger's paw.
