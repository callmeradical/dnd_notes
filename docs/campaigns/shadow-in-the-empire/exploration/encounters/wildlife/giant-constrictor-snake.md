# Giant Constrictor Snake

## Giant Constrictor Snake
*Huge beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 60 (8d12+8)  
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 19(+4) | 14(+2) | 12(+1) | 1(-5) | 10(+0) | 3(-4) |

**Skills** Perception +2  
**Senses** blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 11 (2d6+4) piercing damage.

**Constrict.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 13 (2d8+4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 16). Until this grapple ends, the creature is restrained, and the snake can't constrict another target.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 giant constrictor snake  
**Environment:** Jungle canopy, near rivers, swampy areas  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random wildlife encounter (#3 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Ambush predator that strikes from above
- Patient hunter that can remain motionless for hours

**Mannerisms**

- Coils draped over thick branches, nearly invisible among vines
- Tongue flicks out to taste the air for prey scents
- Muscles ripple visibly under scaled hide when preparing to strike

**Tactics**

- Drops from canopy onto the rear guard or isolated party member
- Uses bite attack first, then immediately attempts to constrict
- Tries to drag constricted prey up into the canopy or toward water
- Focuses on one target until they're unconscious before switching
- Retreats up into trees if reduced to 15 hit points or less
- May release prey and flee if attacked by multiple opponents

**Environment Notes**

- Shed skin pieces (10-15 feet long) may be found near its territory
- Broken branches 20+ feet up indicate snake's passage through canopy
- Small bones and equipment from previous victims scattered below favorite hunting perches
- Disturbed areas where large coils have rested on the ground

**Special Considerations**

- Climbing characters may encounter the snake in its element
- Swimming characters are at particular disadvantage against this foe
- Constricted characters need immediate help or face unconsciousness

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine snake's hunting position: coiled among thick vines 20-30 feet up
- Choose target: rear guard or most isolated party member
- Note lighting conditions: dense canopy creates shadows even in daylight
- Consider party marching order and whether anyone is watching for canopy threats

**Opening Scene Options**

**Canopy Ambush:**
> *The jungle canopy blocks much of the sunlight, creating a cathedral of green shadows. Suddenly, what you took for a massive vine coiled around a thick branch begins to uncoil. Forty feet of scaled muscle slides silently downward, massive triangular head weaving as a forked tongue tastes the air.*

**Ground Level Encounter:**
> *Near the muddy riverbank, what appeared to be a fallen log suddenly shifts. Coils thicker than a man's torso unwind from around a massive tree trunk as a giant constrictor snake raises its head, yellow eyes fixed on the party with predatory focus.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: The Drop (Surprise Round)**
- Snake attempts stealth check to remain unnoticed until striking
- Drops from canopy onto chosen target or emerges from hiding
- Uses bite attack immediately, aiming for grapple on next turn
- **Perception Check (DC 15):** Notice the snake before it strikes
- **Acrobatics Check (DC 14):** Dodge away from falling snake if noticed

**Phase 2: Constriction Focus**
- Snake's priority is to constrict the bitten target
- Attempts to drag prey toward water or up into canopy
- Ignores other attackers unless they deal significant damage
- **Strength Check (DC 16):** Escape from constriction
- **Athletics Check (DC 13):** Pursue snake if it drags prey away

**Phase 3: Defensive Retreat**
- At 15 HP or less, snake releases prey and retreats
- Uses its climbing speed to escape up into the canopy
- May drop unconscious prey to ensure its own survival
- Could return later if still in territory and opportunity presents

**Key Mechanics to Emphasize**
- **Constrict:** Describe the crushing pressure and difficulty breathing
- **Size Advantage:** Snake can grapple multiple creatures due to its huge size
- **Environmental Control:** Uses height and water to its advantage
- **Patient Predator:** May observe party before striking if they camp nearby

**Environmental Usage**
- **Canopy Fighting:** Climbing characters face the snake in its element
- **Water Advantage:** Snake excels in rivers and swamps, +2 AC from half-cover
- **Difficult Terrain:** Thick underbrush slows rescuers but not the snake
- **Vertical Movement:** Can drag prey up trees or into deep water

**Skill Integration**
- **Animal Handling (DC 16):** Calm snake if it's not actively hunting
- **Athletics (DC 14):** Climb trees to fight snake on equal terms
- **Medicine (DC 12):** Treat constriction damage and unconsciousness
- **Nature (DC 11):** Recognize snake behavior patterns and likely tactics
- **Survival (DC 13):** Track snake to its lair or predict its movements

**Combat Considerations**

**Grappling Mechanics**
- Constricted creatures are restrained (disadvantage on attacks, advantage for attackers)
- Each turn starts with automatic damage from constriction
- Multiple party members can attempt to free grappled ally
- Unconscious characters may be swallowed if campaign uses that optional rule

**Positional Tactics**
- Snake uses reach advantage to bite from 10 feet away
- Constriction requires adjacent positioning (5 feet)
- Attempts to position between party and constricted victim
- May coil around trees or rocks for additional cover

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Snake is Defeated**
- 40 feet of snake hide can be crafted into leather armor or rope
- Snake's lair may contain remains of previous victims and their equipment
- Other predators attracted to the scent of blood and combat
- Local ecosystem shift as smaller predators move into snake's territory

**If Snake Escapes**
- May return to stalk the party if they remain in its territory
- Could ambush during rest periods or river crossings
- Tracks lead to lair with potential treasure from previous victims
- Reputation among local tribes as skilled hunters if they drove it off

**Story Integration**
- Snake may have killed important NPC - tracks lead to remains
- Local tribes might pay bounty for proof of snake's death
- Druids could be upset about killing a natural apex predator
- Snake's unusual aggression might indicate territorial pressure from larger threat

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Snake is well-fed, attempts to flee after initial attack fails
- **Harder:** Multiple snakes (mated pair) or add poison to bite attack
- **Environmental:** Combat during storm, in deep water, or at night
- **Social:** Local guides refuse to enter known snake territory

**Special Situations**

**If Party Camps in Snake Territory**
- Snake observes for 1d4 hours before attacking
- Targets lone sentries or sleeping party members
- May attempt to constrict and drag away unconscious victim
- **Perception Check (DC 18):** Notice being watched during rest

**Water Combat Modifications**
- Snake gains advantage on grapple checks in water
- Swimming characters have disadvantage on weapon attacks
- Constricted characters may drown if underwater
- Snake can hold breath much longer than most party members

**Treasure Considerations**
- Snake's lair contains indigestible items from previous meals
- 2d4 × 10 gp worth of metal objects, weapons, and jewelry
- Possible potion or scroll in waterproof container
- Equipment may show signs of acid damage from digestive process
