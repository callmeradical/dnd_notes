## Highlights

A hexblade warlock with a penchant for gambling and finer things.

  * Very fine cloathes.
  * Black tapered duster
  * Black Suit beneath
  * Stark White shirt
  * Black Hair with a shock of gray from one corner of his forehead all the way back to his neckline
  * Piercing blue eyes
  * He is a gambler, a sporting man, he traffics information about horse trades with others for the object of their desire
  * Speaks kind of like Clint Eastwood

## Location(s)

His first appearance was at the Alunno outpost by The Titan's Walk, south of the roughs.
