## Create a strong start
- Opening situation in motion: Takashima Lower City is hit by a night raid of displaced creatures and opportunists.
- Immediate focus: save trapped civilians while identifying whether this is invasion or panic cascade.
- Why now: the party just returned with Takumi; the city is vulnerable and watch command is fractured.
- First decision/action prompt: market rescue under attack or push to the gate line before it collapses.

### E.A.S.E.
- **Environment:** Temple bells ring over lantern-lit alleys as rain turns ash to black slurry; market stalls burn beside shrine walls and bridge timbers split under stampeding bodies.
- **Atmosphere:** The city feels cornered and breathless, with everyone one mistake away from panic breaking into rout.
- **Senses:** Smoke and fish oil choke the air; wet wood pops in the fire; wind drives sparks across rooftops while distant screams cut through shouted guard orders.
- **Events:** A wounded guard points down the lower ward as kobolds drag civilians from a collapsed stall and an ogre hammers toward the bridge line: "Monsters in the Lower City! Protect the people!"

### Read Aloud
> Temple bells hammer the night as rain turns ash into black mud between your boots. Fire races across the fish market, shrine ribbons burn in the wind, and bridge supports groan over the canal. Smoke and fish oil sting your throat while guards shout contradictory orders through the chaos. Then a bloodied watchman stumbles into view, points down the lower ward, and screams, "Monsters in the Lower City! Protect the people!" You see kobolds hauling trapped civilians from a collapsed stall while, beyond them, an ogre barrels toward the bridge with splintered timber in its fists. What do you do?

## Define secrets and clues
- This is not a planned invasion; it is displacement chaos weaponized by opportunists.
- A Kodama-linked purification fire in Shinraku Grove triggered the creature exodus.
- Kuroda the Smoke-Eater deliberately amplified panic to mask targeted operations.
- A cracked shrine bell sounds wrong near Oni-tainted influence.
- A half-burned lotus token marks a Hidden Lotus observer path.
- The ogre's wounds contain black resin and scorched leaves from grove terrain.
- Kobolds stole ward charms, indicating fear of sanctified boundaries.
- Fox spirits observed key intersections before the first major breach.
- Bridge supports show pre-cut sabotage, not just impact damage.
- A missing evacuation ledger suggests civilians were intentionally left exposed.

## Detailed Findings (Use in Play)

### Decoded Ledger: Obsidian Circle Corrupt Guard Network
- Multiple watch payments are masked as "emergency district stabilization" disbursements.
- Payees map to a rotating set of lower-gate and middle-terrace watch posts.
- Night transfer windows align with reduced patrol intervals and staged "response delays."
- At least one payment route references movement tied to "stone below" and "first chain" operations.
- The inn drop confirms the ledger was decoded successfully and is now actionable intelligence.

### Vault Entrance Leads (Current)
- A likely service ingress exists near the middle-terrace guard infrastructure (controlled access, watch-dependent).
- A second probable route is tied to older shrine or waterworks architecture below Takashima.
- A false or trapped route may be intentionally seeded through guard rumor channels.
- Reliable entry likely requires either guard schedule disruption, Hidden Lotus route support, or both.

### One Tree / One Well / Pure Water Thread
- References indicate "pure water" is not common supply water; it is treated as ritually protected source water.
- Notes imply a relationship between "The Stream," "The Tree," and a deeper vault complex.
- Access appears permission-gated by either spiritual rite, lineage marker, or both.
- The party should treat this as both a resource objective and a lore key for the larger conflict.

### Political-Security Shift
- Shigeo's son is currently assigned to guard the daimyo.
- This increases direct risk to family ties if the party openly pressures guard corruption channels.
- It also creates a potential high-value inside line if trust can be maintained.

### Goliath Enslavement Thread
- Reports indicate goliaths are being held in daimyo-controlled labor conditions (functionally enslaved).
- This can connect to logistics, fortification labor, or concealed transport tied to current power structures.
- Any public move on this issue may trigger immediate political blowback.
- Private confirmation and witness protection should be prioritized before open accusation.

#### Beat: Reiko Notices Shigeo's Oily Gloves
- Reiko clocks Shigeo's hands the moment he reaches for coin.
- Her tone shifts from merchant-neutral to professionally curious.

**Suggested Dialogue**

**What This Adds**

#### Correction: Gloves Identification

## Strong Start Trigger: Ledger Payment at Dusk
- The decoded ledger identifies a **scheduled payment drop just after dusk, this evening**.
- The handoff location is tied to lower-city guard corruption channels and is expected to involve Circle-linked intermediaries.
- This is the session fuse: the party can surveil, intercept, impersonate, or follow the money.
- As the payment window opens, city pressure spikes and the strong start scene kicks in immediately.

### Immediate Countdown
- **Dusk Bell:** handlers move into position.
- **+15 minutes:** payment courier arrives.
- **+30 minutes:** funds transfer and dispersal to secondary runners.
- **+45 minutes:** evidence fragments; targets vanish into preplanned routes.

### Read-Aloud Insert (Optional)
> The dusk bell fades and rain begins to needle the stone. In your hands, the decoded ledger leaves no doubt: a payment moves tonight, just after dark. Not tomorrow. Not maybe. Tonight. Across the lower ward, runners are already taking position near the market roads and canal bridges. If you move now, you can catch the handoff. If you hesitate, the money disappears into the city - and with it, your cleanest lead.


## Kanji Side Operation: Rob Someone

### Best Targets During the Dusk Payment Window
- **Corrupt pay-runner (recommended):** carrying marked payout satchel and route tokens.
- **Circle intermediary clerk:** carrying cipher scraps and contact phrases.
- **Watch quartermaster aide:** carrying gate-pass seals and shift rosters.

### What Kanji Can Steal
- Coin purse (short-term gain, low intel value).
- **Ledger strip with code marks** (high-value clue; advances corruption plot).
- **Vault-adjacent key token** (opens one controlled door/checkpoint once).
- Signet or pass-stamp (enables short impersonation scene).

### Fast Resolution (3-Beat Skill Sequence)
- **Beat 1 - Identify mark:** Perception or Insight.
- **Beat 2 - Lift item:** Sleight of Hand (Gloves of Thievery applies).
- **Beat 3 - Exit clean:** Stealth, Deception, or Athletics (crowd break).

### Heat and Consequences
- **Clean success:** no immediate alarm; Kanji gains item and one follow opportunity.
- **Mixed success:** item gained but a witness notices; +1 city heat with the watch.
- **Failure:** alarm call; nearby guards lock down lanes and force a chase or social cover.

### If You Want the Robbery to Matter Immediately
- The stolen ledger strip includes tonight's fallback meeting point if the main payment goes bad.
- The stolen pass-stamp grants one short window to approach a guarded vault-access corridor.
- The stolen token links directly to the "stone below / first chain" route language.


### Kanji Plan: Rob the Dropper, Then Scope the Bag
- **Intent:** Kanji targets the man who drops the payment bag, steals from him personally, then watches the bag to identify pickup chain before intervening.

#### Step 1 - Pre-Drop Positioning (5-10 minutes before handoff)
- Kanji takes elevated or cross-lane cover with clear line of sight to the drop point.
- One ally shadows likely escape lanes; one ally stays near crowd noise for distraction.
- Check: Stealth or Deception to blend into pre-handoff movement.

#### Step 2 - The Lift on the Dropper (at drop moment)
- Trigger the lift exactly as the dropper breaks contact with the bag.
- Priority steals from the dropper: route chit, backup token, whistle, seal-ring, or personal coin.
- Check: Sleight of Hand (advantage if distraction lands, Gloves of Thievery applies).

#### Step 3 - Scope the Bag (do not touch yet)
- Kanji observes who approaches, who hesitates, and who signals before pickup.
- Look for countersigns (hand gesture, phrase, lantern tilt, sleeve knot color).
- Check: Perception/Insight for behavior patterns; Investigation for physical tells on the bag.

#### Step 4 - Pickup Decision Point
- **Option A: Follow pickup runner** to secondary node (best intel, lower immediate reward).
- **Option B: Swap bag** with weighted decoy (high risk, high disruption).
- **Option C: Let pickup happen, mark recipient** for later hit (slow burn, safest short-term).

#### What Kanji Learns If It Works
- Identity or description of at least one real collector in the corruption chain.
- A second-location handoff point tied to lower-ward canals or guard-adjacent alleys.
- Whether the bag is money, forged papers, shrine materials, or mixed payload.

#### Complications (Pick 1-2)
- A false pickup actor tests for surveillance before the real retrieval.
- The dropper carries a panic whistle linked to nearby plainclothes guards.
- A Hidden Lotus watcher is also observing and may interfere if misread as hostile.
- Rain smears ink marks on route chits unless protected immediately.

#### Failure / Partial Outcomes
- **Lift fails, surveillance succeeds:** Kanji is noticed by the dropper, but still identifies pickup chain.
- **Lift succeeds, surveillance fails:** Kanji gets item but loses bag trail in crowd shift.
- **Both fail:** guard attention spikes; convert to chase scene through market lanes.


## Encounter Insert: Ettin in the Square (Twin-Maw Goro)

- **Role:** Siege brute / area denial in open plaza.
- **Look:** Two soot-smeared heads, iron-shrine bells tied to one neck, one arm wrapped in scorched guard banners.
- **Behavior:** One head rages and charges; the other scans for weak targets and shouts directions to kobolds.

### Tactics
- Opens by hurling burning debris at clustered PCs/NPCs.
- Uses reach and bulk to block lane exits.
- Focuses targets carrying obvious symbols, banners, or magic items.
- If bloodied, grabs a cart/stall as improvised cover and fights defensively.

### Battlefield Actions (Square-Specific)
- **Debris Hurl (Recharge 5-6):** ranged flaming stone (Dex save DC 14; fail: 2d10 bludgeoning + 2d6 fire, success: half; in tight lane/crowd also prone on fail).
- **Stomp Shock:** creatures within 10 ft make Dex save DC 13 or fall prone.
- **Bell-Roar (1/encounter):** both heads howl; nearby minions may immediately reposition.

### Weak Points / Player Hooks
- Blindside one head: attacks from opposite flanks gain tactical advantage (or +2 to hit).
- Cut the bell cords: silences Bell-Roar and rattles the cautious head.
- Fire/smoke irritation: Perception drops when smoke is thick.

### Motivation
- Not loyal to the city raiders; it is displaced, hurt, and being directed by fear and pain.
- Can be redirected by loud food/scent lure, illusion of forest path, or dominant show of force.

### Loot / Clue
- Charred leaves embedded in skin.
- Resin-marked chain token in belt wrap.
- Half-burned route scrap pointing toward the lower-gate handoff lanes.
