# Skill Challenge - Navigating the Living Maze of Hisui no Mori

**Navigating the Living Maze of Hisui no Mori** is a skill challenge tailored for **level 1 characters** in **5th Edition D&D**, incorporating a **Moderate Difficulty** framework and clearer guidelines for low-level play.

The **Hisui no Mori (Jade Forest)** is a magical labyrinth where the paths shift like a living puzzle. To reach the forest's edge and avoid dangerous delays, the players must successfully navigate the mystical terrain using teamwork and creativity.

## Challenge Setup

- **Goal:** Successfully navigate the maze by achieving **4 successes before 3 failures**.
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (DCs 10–12 to account for low-level characters).
- **Environment:** The forest shifts with illusory walls, hidden paths, and mischievous yokai.
- **Time Pressure:** Optional—Add urgency by setting a 6–8 turn limit before a major storm or spiritual surge occurs.

## Primary Skills

These are the most direct ways to progress through the maze. Use them to drive the narrative forward.

### Survival (DC 12)

- **Purpose:** Identify animal trails, spiritual disturbances, or natural landmarks.
- **Success Example:** "You notice faint kodama footprints, leading toward a clearing untouched by the shifting forest."
- **Failure Example:** "The tracks loop back into a dead end overgrown with thorny vines."

### Perception (DC 10)

- **Purpose:** Detect subtle changes in the environment, such as shimmering lights or distant sounds.
- **Success Example:** "You spot faint glowing motes hovering in a line, leading toward open paths."
- **Failure Example:** "You overlook an illusory wall, wasting time retracing your steps."

### Arcana (DC 12)

- **Purpose:** Sense and analyze magical energies shaping the maze.
- **Success Example:** "The magical flow of ley lines forms a visible path where none existed."
- **Failure Example:** "You misinterpret a shifting energy pattern, leading the group into a disorienting vortex."

## Secondary Skills

Secondary skills offer creative or situational advantages but typically don't directly count toward success unless cleverly justified.

### Insight (DC 10)

- **Purpose:** Interpret signs from forest spirits or understand yokai behavior.
- **Success Example:** "You recognize a bird call as a warning, avoiding a collapsing path."
- **Failure Example:** "You misread the spirits' intent, angering them and causing paths to close behind you."

### Nature (DC 12)

- **Purpose:** Identify useful flora and fauna or safe routes based on environmental clues.
- **Success Example:** "You recognize moss growing only on trees near clearings and follow the pattern."
- **Failure Example:** "You mistake a toxic mushroom grove for an edible one, delaying progress as the group recovers."

### Deception (DC 12) or Persuasion (DC 11)

- **Purpose:** Trick or negotiate with mischievous yokai for guidance.
- **Success Example:** "You convince a giggling kitsune to reveal a cryptic but useful clue."
- **Failure Example:** "The yokai tricks you in return, leading the group in circles."

## Complications for Failures

Introduce complications for each failure to heighten tension and consequences:

1. **Shifting Paths:** "The forest twists violently, adding an extra required success to escape."
2. **Hostile Encounter:** The party stumbles into a hostile creature (e.g., 1–2 Shadow Wolves or a mischievous kodama swarm). Resolve with a quick combat or roleplay encounter.
3. **Spiritual Fatigue:** "The forest saps your strength. All characters suffer disadvantage on the next check unless you take a rest, losing precious time."

## Completion Outcomes

### Success (4 Successes Before 3 Failures)

The players navigate the maze, reaching the Ryuseki Peaks unscathed. Reward them with one or more of the following:

- **Forest Blessing:** Advantage on Survival or Perception checks in forested areas for the next in-game day.
- **Kami Relic:** A small magical token (e.g., a charm granting a +1 bonus to a single saving throw).
- **Narrative Advantage:** Favor from a shrine fox or other spiritual being.

### Failure (3 Failures Before 4 Successes)

The group becomes lost, leading to one or more of these consequences:

- **Combat Encounter:** A dangerous yokai or Shadow Wolves ambush the party.
- **Exhaustion:** Characters suffer a level of **exhaustion**.
- **Narrative Delay:** The group loses valuable time, affecting other objectives.

## Optional Additions

1. **Assistance Rules:** Allow players to assist others by granting **advantage** on a roll. This consumes their action for the round and requires a narrative explanation (e.g., "I guide them through the thorny vines using my rope").
2. **Dynamic Environment:** Adjust the environment after 2–3 turns to keep the challenge engaging. Example: "A sudden downpour floods low-lying paths, making Acrobatics checks necessary to avoid slipping."
3. **Random Yokai Encounters:** On failures, introduce whimsical yokai that can hinder or help based on player interactions.
