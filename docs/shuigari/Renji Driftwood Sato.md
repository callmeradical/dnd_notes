# Renji “Driftwood” Sato

**Campaign Setting:** Shuigari  
**Adventure:** The Twice Cursed – Act I – Master Hideo’s Funeral  
#npc #dungeonsanddragons #tlwos

## Profile
- Minor member of the Grey Tide serving as scout and smuggler.
- Resourceful and conflicted; loyal to kindness (especially Hideo) but survival-driven.

## Backstory
Renji was once a starving drifter hunted by a local lord’s samurai after stealing rice for his family. [Master Hideo Nakata](Master%20Hideo%20Nakata.md) hid Renji in his forge, provided an alibi, and persuaded the pursuers to leave. Hideo’s lesson—“A sharp blade may cut, but a tempered heart saves lives”—stuck with Renji, though living up to it proved difficult.

## Current Status
Joined the Grey Tide for freedom and survival yet carries guilt over betraying Hideo’s trust. Stationed near Takashima, Renji scouts smuggling routes and tracks patrol movements.

## Role in the Campaign
- **At Hideo’s Funeral:** Possibly attends in disguise, leaving a driftwood sword carving in tribute.
- **Potential Hooks:**
  - Seeking redemption by sharing Grey Tide intel or leads on the stolen katana.
  - His presence may draw samurai or rival factions, forcing players to choose sides.
- **Ally or Betrayer:** Could grow into a subtle ally, or betray the party if cornered and forced to protect the Grey Tide.
- **Encounters:** Acts as first contact when investigating the Grey Tide, offering information if the party gains his trust; may be caught during a Shogunate crackdown, presenting a rescue-or-not dilemma.
