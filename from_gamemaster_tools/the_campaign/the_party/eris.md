Backstory Epilogue:

Eris’s mother, Emeris, also known as The Emerald Angel, was a young and upcoming Cortesan of exceptional beauty (Companion/Geisha/Bene Gesserit level). She was applauded as a skilled dancer, musician, and singer from a very young age. Her beauty and ability made her famous among the elite society and she had her choice of patrons. 

It was therefore something of a scandal when she became pregnant, because she had the means to avoid such a situation, which meant she chose to have a child. The most prominent rumors surrounding her pregnancy where that she had fallen in love, she was blackmailing a royal in high standing,  or a very wealthy patron had payed to father a child with her. However, the father’s identity remained a mystery. 

Sadly, tragedy followed birth as Emeris passed in the moments of her daughters first cries. Her death was mourned like that of a celebrity, and there were whispers as to what would become of the child. While the father was unknown, the father’s possible power and wealth was enough to encourage Madam Pearl, the owner of the brothel that had raised and trained Emeris, to step forward and take in the babe. 

This proved to be a wise decision on her part as she was soon contacted by a private solicitor. The solicitor made her an offer, she would be paid to look after the child and make sure she was raised well, but quietly and out of societies view. The sooner everyone forgot that Emeris had a child, the better. It would not do for someone to go poking their nose and trying to find out who the child’s father is. 

Backstory: 

Eris grew up in one of the most prominent brothels in Cagliari, but her upbringing was decidedly different from the other girls or bastards growing up there. She was sent to the best schools, but it did not agree with her as she was often bullied for just being the bastard child of a rich dead prostitute. So while book learning was never her bag, she had other artistic and street skills that she eventually used to swindly the rich kids at her school. 

She did odd jobs and errands around the brothel as a child so she had a very unsheltered and full education of how the world worked. She mixed with the very highest and lowest of society. 

She had inherited her mother’s gift with music and she did do some musical performances within the brothel. She was called The Little Angel. 

When she was 16 she tried to confront Madam Pearl about her mystery benefactor and who her father was, but she got no answers. She decided to run off with a troop of traveling performers and travel the districts. 

There was a rather impressive tiefling, Cassius Nox, who pursued her and she ended up falling for him in a sort of whirlwind romance, but it did not end well. She found out he was a hitman for the Alunna who was interested in recruiting her. He professed to really love her, but he also expected her to eventually work alongside him, killing and sleeping with whoever the Alluna told her to, just like him. She was not interested in that life, but she doubted he would just let her go, so she slipped away from him and her troop in the dead of night. 

20 years old now, she took on another identity as a solo performer called Eratrix and made a little name for herself as a traveling Bard, but she had a grudge against the Alluna.
