# Crashed Lyrandar Airship

## Marooned Airship Captain
*Medium humanoid (half-elf), chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (Studded Leather)  
**Hit Points** 58 (9d8+18)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12(+1) | 16(+3) | 14(+2) | 13(+1) | 11(+0) | 16(+3) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Cha +6  
**Skills** Deception +6, Insight +3, Perception +3, Persuasion +6  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Elvish  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Dragonmark of Storm (Lesser).** The captain can cast *gust of wind* (save DC 14) once per long rest.

**Spellcasting.** The captain is a 3rd-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following sorcerer spells prepared:

- **Cantrips (at will):** mage hand, minor illusion, prestidigitation  
- **1st level (4 slots):** charm person, shield, magic missile  
- **2nd level (2 slots):** misty step, suggestion  

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The captain makes two attacks with its rapier or two ranged attacks with its light crossbow.

**Rapier.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) piercing damage.

**Light Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) piercing damage.

### Encounter Information

**Environment:** Jungle clearing with crashed airship wreckage  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random lost world discovery (#6 on d8)  
**Discovery Method:** DC 12 Perception to notice unnatural clearing and wreckage

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Charming but desperate survivor who's been stranded for months
- Maintains aristocratic bearing despite reduced circumstances
- Alternates between manic optimism and deep depression

**Ideals**

- Freedom: The sky belongs to everyone, not just the dragonmarked houses
- Innovation: New routes and discoveries advance civilization
- Survival: Whatever it takes to stay alive and get home

**Bonds**

- Loyal to House Lyrandar despite current circumstances
- Responsible for airship's cargo and the crew who didn't survive
- Desperate to return to family and restore damaged reputation

**Flaws**

- Pride: Refuses to admit mistakes that led to the crash
- Paranoia: Suspects everyone of wanting to steal remaining cargo
- Desperation: Willing to lie, cheat, or betray others to secure rescue

**Mannerisms**

- Constantly adjusts torn but still-fine clothing
- Gestures grandly when describing airship's former glory
- Eyes dart frequently to hidden cargo stash locations

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Contact:**
> "Thank the Sovereign Host! Civilized people at last! I am Captain Mira d'Lyrandar, formerly of the *Wind's Promise*. I don't suppose you have an airship nearby? No? Well, perhaps we can arrange something mutually beneficial..."

**When Discussing the Crash:**
> "Terrible storm came from nowhere—unnatural, I tell you. Lightning like I've never seen, and the winds... it was as if the sky itself turned against us. Lost half my crew in the crash."

**When Desperate:**
> "I'll pay handsomely for passage out of this green hell. Gold, gems, magical items—I have access to House Lyrandar's resources. Just get me home!"

### Knowledge

**What She Knows**
- Detailed knowledge of regional weather patterns and aerial navigation
- Information about other crashed or missing Lyrandar vessels
- Rumors about unusual storm activity and possible supernatural causes
- Location of hidden cargo caches from the wreckage

**What She Offers**
- Payment for escort to civilization (promises more than she can deliver)
- Valuable cargo items as trade goods or payment
- Maps of aerial routes and weather patterns for the region
- House Lyrandar connections and potential future business

**What She Conceals**
- Crash was partly due to her inexperience and poor decision-making
- Some crew members survived but died due to her selfish choices
- Most valuable cargo already traded away for basic survival supplies
- Growing magical instability from prolonged exposure to crashed airship's energy

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine how long captain has been stranded (2d4+2 months)
- Roll for captain's current mental state: desperate but coherent vs. paranoid and unstable
- Note remaining cargo and supplies available in wreckage
- Consider whether other survivors are present (alive or dead)

**Opening Scene Options**

**Desperate Greeting:**
> *In a clearing where ancient trees have been sheared off at impossible heights, the twisted remains of an airship lie scattered. Among the wreckage, a figure in torn but once-fine clothing waves frantically. "Thank the Host! People! Actual people!" The half-elf's voice carries a note of hysteria beneath the relief.*

**Salvage Discovery:**
> *The massive wooden hull dominates the jungle clearing, its brass fittings tarnished green. Movement near the wreckage catches your eye—someone has clearly been living here, makeshift shelters built from salvaged sail cloth. As you approach, a woman emerges with a crossbow, then lowers it with visible relief.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Desperate Relief (Social Primary)**
- Captain overwhelmed by joy at seeing other people
- Attempts to present herself as still-respectable Lyrandar nobility
- Offers immediate hospitality with remaining supplies
- **Insight Check (DC 13):** Notice captain is more desperate than she pretends
- **Medicine Check (DC 12):** Recognize signs of prolonged stress and poor nutrition
- **Investigation Check (DC 14):** Spot inconsistencies in captain's account of the crash

**Phase 2: Negotiation and Revelation**
- Captain proposes various deals for rescue or partnership
- Gradually reveals more about crash circumstances and current situation
- May become more manipulative if initial charm doesn't work
- **Deception Check (DC 15):** See through captain's exaggerated promises
- **Persuasion Check (DC 12):** Get captain to reveal hidden cargo locations
- **History Check (DC 13):** Know House Lyrandar protocols and typical airship procedures

**Phase 3: Alliance, Conflict, or Departure**
- Successful negotiations lead to trade partnership and information exchange
- Failed diplomacy may result in captain attempting theft or sabotage
- Captain's mental state may deteriorate if she feels abandoned again

**Wreckage Exploration**

**Salvageable Components**
- **Navigation Instruments:** Worth 200-500 gp to right buyers
- **Elemental Engine Parts:** Magical components worth 1,000+ gp but difficult to transport
- **House Lyrandar Documents:** Trade routes, weather data, diplomatic correspondence
- **Personal Effects:** Captain's belongings and crew possessions

**Hidden Cargo Caches**
- **Investigation Check (DC 15):** Find captain's primary stash
- **Investigation Check (DC 18):** Locate secondary caches she's forgotten or overlooked
- Contents: 3d6 × 100 gp in trade goods, 1d3 minor magical items, important documents

**Structural Hazards**
- **Unstable Wreckage:** Dexterity saves to avoid falling debris
- **Magical Discharge:** Random magical effects from damaged elemental engine
- **Trapped Sections:** Areas accessible only with proper tools or magic

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Offering immediate aid (food, medicine, or comfort)
- Showing respect for House Lyrandar and airship service
- Promising concrete plans for getting captain to civilization
- Demonstrating magical abilities that could aid in rescue

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Criticizing captain's decisions or competence
- Showing obvious interest in cargo while ignoring captain's plight
- Refusing to help or discuss rescue possibilities
- Mentioning rival dragonmarked houses or competing transportation

**Captain's Mental State**
- **Stable Days:** Charming, helpful, makes reasonable deals
- **Paranoid Episodes:** Suspicious, defensive, may attack if threatened
- **Depressive Periods:** Withdrawn, fatalistic, reveals dangerous truths
- **Manic Phases:** Overly generous, makes impossible promises

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Captain shares all knowledge including hidden locations and house contacts
- Provides valuable cargo as payment for rescue efforts
- Offers genuine House Lyrandar introduction and future business opportunities
- Becomes reliable ally who remembers party's kindness

**Success**
- Fair trade agreements for information and salvageable goods
- Captain cooperates without revealing all secrets
- Basic assistance with regional navigation and weather knowledge

**Failure**
- Captain becomes suspicious and defensive about remaining assets
- Limited cooperation, may attempt to follow party without invitation
- Possible theft attempts during night or when party is distracted

**Critical Failure**
- Captain attempts to rob party or sabotage their efforts
- Paranoid breakdown leads to combat or dangerous magical discharge
- False information provided about regional hazards or opportunities

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Captain has information about party's quest destination
- Rescue mission becomes ongoing subplot with house politics
- Crashed airship location becomes base of operations
- Other Lyrandar vessels missing in same area create larger mystery

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Captain is stable and grateful, wreckage easily salvaged
- **Harder:** Multiple survivors with competing agendas, dangerous magical hazards
- **Environmental:** Crash site under threat from approaching dangers or weather
- **Political:** House Lyrandar considers captain expendable, complicates rescue

**Treasure Possibilities**
- 2d4 × 100 gp in salvageable trade goods and components
- 1d4 magical items from airship's stores or passenger luggage  
- Valuable maps and documents worth 200-800 gp to appropriate buyers
- Future business opportunities with House Lyrandar worth thousands of gp
