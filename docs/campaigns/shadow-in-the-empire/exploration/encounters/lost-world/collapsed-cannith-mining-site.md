# Collapsed Cannith Mining Site

## Trapped Miner
*Medium humanoid (human), neutral good*

**Armor Class** 11  
**Hit Points** 13 (3d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13(+1) | 12(+1) | 11(+0) | 14(+2) | 16(+3) | 10(+0) |

**Skills** Investigation +4, Nature +5, Perception +5, Survival +5  
**Senses** passive Perception 15  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

**Mining Pick.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8+1) piercing damage.

**Improvised Sling.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4+1) bludgeoning damage.

---

### Encounter Information

**Environment:** Collapsed dragonshard mine with unstable tunnels and equipment  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random lost world discovery (#2 on d8)  
**Discovery Method:** DC 14 Investigation or Survival to notice artificial earth disturbance and mining equipment

### Mining Site Description

**Surface Features**
- Large excavation partially filled with debris and jungle growth
- Rusted mining equipment scattered around collapsed tunnel entrances
- Wooden support beams protruding from cave-ins
- Tailings piles now covered with vegetation

**Underground Network**
- Partially accessible tunnel system with unstable ceiling supports
- Flooded lower levels due to underground springs
- Remaining dragonshard deposits visible in tunnel walls
- Equipment and supplies trapped behind collapsed sections

**Current Hazards**
- Unstable tunnel ceilings requiring Dexterity saves to avoid falling debris
- Toxic gas pockets from stagnant water and rotting organic matter
- Sharp metal debris and broken support timbers creating difficult terrain
- Potential for further collapse if structural integrity is compromised

### Roleplay Information

**Trapped Miner Personality**
- Experienced dragonshard prospector caught in recent tunnel collapse
- Maintains professional knowledge despite desperate circumstances
- Grateful for rescue but protective of mining claims and discoveries

**Current Situation**
- Trapped for 2d4+1 days in air pocket within collapsed tunnel
- Has limited food and water, growing increasingly desperate
- Possesses valuable knowledge about dragonshard deposits and mining techniques
- May have witnessed what caused the recent collapse

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Calls for Help:**
> "Hello? Is someone there? I'm trapped down here! Please, I can hear voices—I've been trapped for days!"

**When Rescued:**
> "Thank the Host above! I thought I was going to die down here. The name's Gareth—I work for House Cannith's mining division. Worked, anyway. Not sure there's much left to work."

**About the Collapse:**
> "Wasn't natural, that collapse. We hit a chamber, something old. The walls were covered in markings, and then... things got strange. Shadows moved wrong. That's when the whole place came down."

### Knowledge

**What the Miner Knows**
- Location of valuable dragonshard deposits throughout the tunnel system
- Mining techniques for safely extracting dragonshards without damage
- House Cannith mining operations and their typical procedures
- What happened during the collapse: supernatural interference or sabotage

**Technical Expertise**
- Can identify different types of dragonshards and their market values
- Knows how to stabilize tunnels and clear debris safely
- Understanding of mining equipment operation and maintenance
- Awareness of geological hazards and safe excavation practices

**Hidden Information**
- Location of sealed supply caches and valuable equipment
- Maps showing extent of tunnel network and richest deposits
- Personal stash of high-quality dragonshards hidden before collapse
- Evidence of what caused the supernatural disturbance

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine miner's condition: recently trapped vs. near death from exposure
- Note extent of tunnel collapse and accessibility of different areas
- Consider what caused collapse: accident, sabotage, or supernatural interference
- Roll for remaining dragonshard deposits and their accessibility

**Opening Scene Options**

**Surface Discovery:**
> *Rusted mining equipment emerges from the jungle growth ahead—wheels, cables, and the skeletal remains of wooden scaffolding. The ground shows clear signs of excavation and recent disturbance. As you investigate, a voice echoes faintly from beneath the debris: "Help! Someone help me! I'm trapped down here!"*

**Equipment Investigation:**
> *The mining operation clearly ended abruptly. Tools lie scattered where they were dropped, and a massive cave-in has sealed the main tunnel entrance. House Cannith markings are visible on much of the equipment, though jungle growth has begun to reclaim the site. Then you hear it—a weak voice calling from somewhere within the collapsed tunnels.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Rescue Operation (Problem-Solving Primary)**
- Party must locate trapped miner and plan rescue approach
- Requires careful clearing of debris to avoid triggering further collapse
- Time pressure as miner's condition deteriorates
- **Investigation Check (DC 12):** Locate source of miner's voice in tunnel system
- **Athletics Check (DC 15):** Clear debris without causing additional collapse
- **Medicine Check (DC 10):** Assess miner's condition and provide immediate aid

**Phase 2: Information Exchange**
- Rescued miner shares knowledge about site and House Cannith operations
- Reveals circumstances of collapse and potential supernatural involvement
- Offers to guide party through accessible tunnel areas
- **Insight Check (DC 11):** Recognize miner is withholding information about personal stash
- **Persuasion Check (DC 13):** Gain miner's trust and full cooperation
- **Arcana Check (DC 14):** Understand supernatural elements miner describes

**Phase 3: Site Exploration and Excavation**
- Party can explore accessible tunnels with miner's guidance
- Opportunity to recover valuable dragonshards and equipment
- Risk of further collapse or supernatural encounters
- **Survival Check (DC 13):** Navigate tunnel system safely with unstable conditions

**Rescue Mechanics**

**Debris Clearing**
- **Athletics Check (DC 15):** Move large debris safely
- **Investigation Check (DC 12):** Determine structurally safe removal sequence
- **Thieves' Tools Check (DC 13):** Dismantle equipment blocking access
- Time required: 1d4+1 hours for full rescue operation

**Structural Stability**
- Each failed Athletics check risks triggering additional collapse
- Dexterity saves (DC 14) required to avoid falling debris damage
- Magic like Mage Hand or Telekinesis provides advantage on clearing checks
- Engineering knowledge (Intelligence + Tool Proficiency) grants bonus

**Environmental Hazards**

**Toxic Gas Pockets**
- **Constitution Save (DC 12):** Avoid poisoning from stagnant air
- Detected with successful Survival check (DC 13)
- Avoided by creating ventilation or using appropriate spells
- Causes 1d4 poison damage and potential unconsciousness

**Unstable Tunnels**
- **Perception Check (DC 14):** Notice warning signs of impending collapse
- **Acrobatics Check (DC 15):** Move quickly through dangerous areas
- Loud noises or heavy impacts may trigger collapse
- Each collapse affects 20-foot radius, 3d6 bludgeoning damage

**Flooding Risk**
- Lower tunnels partially filled with groundwater
- **Swimming Check:** Required to access flooded areas
- Water level rising during rainy season
- May contain valuable equipment but poses drowning risk

**Treasure and Resources**

**Accessible Dragonshards**
- 2d6 Eberron dragonshards worth 50-200 gp each
- 1d4 Siberys dragonshards worth 500-1,000 gp each
- 1d3 Khyber dragonshards worth 100-400 gp each (dangerous to handle)
- Requires proper extraction techniques to avoid damage

**Mining Equipment**
- Professional tools worth 200-500 gp if recovered and transported
- Specialized dragonshard detection and extraction devices
- Maps and survey documents showing regional deposits
- House Cannith operational procedures and contact information

**Hidden Caches**
- **Investigation Check (DC 16):** Find miner's personal stash
- 1d4+1 high-quality dragonshards worth 300-800 gp each
- Emergency supplies and personal effects
- Evidence about what caused supernatural disturbance

**Long-term Opportunities**

**Restoration Project**
- Party could invest in reopening mine with proper safety measures
- Requires 2,000-5,000 gp investment and 2d4 months work
- Provides ongoing income of 500-1,500 gp per month
- Attracts House Cannith attention (positive or negative)

**Information Value**
- Miner's knowledge valuable to other mining operations
- Supernatural cause of collapse may be relevant to larger quest
- Maps and geological data worth significant amounts to right buyers
- House Cannith may pay substantial reward for operational intelligence

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Supernatural disturbance connected to party's main quest
- Other Cannith operations in region facing similar mysterious problems
- Miner becomes ongoing ally with technical expertise
- Discovery of ancient chamber beneath mine leads to larger adventure

**Social Outcomes**

**If Miner Rescued Successfully**
- Grateful ally with specialized knowledge and House connections
- Willing to share information and provide technical assistance
- May offer ongoing business partnership for mining operations
- Positive reputation with House Cannith mining division

**If Rescue Attempted but Failed**
- Tragic loss but party gains access to mining site and equipment
- Other miners or House representatives may investigate later
- Moral weight of failed rescue affects party dynamics
- Site remains dangerous but potentially profitable

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Miner in better condition, tunnels more stable
- **Harder:** Multiple trapped miners, supernatural guardian awakened
- **Environmental:** Active flooding or additional geological hazards
- **Political:** House Cannith considers site strategically important
