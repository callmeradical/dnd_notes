# Tenka - The Last Oniwaban (Traitor)

## Identity & Title

**Name:** Tenka  
**Title:** The Last Oniwaban (alongside Aoshi, though he lost the right to the name)  
**Other Names:** The Traitor, The Fallen Guardian, The One Who Broke

## The Betrayal

### What He Did
Tenka betrayed the Oniwaban clan to the Obsidian Circle, leading to:
- The systematic massacre of the entire clan
- The exposure of clan secrets and techniques
- The loss of the vault's primary guardians
- The death of everyone Aoshi knew and loved

### Why He Survived
Tenka is the only Oniwaban besides Aoshi to survive the massacre because:
- He orchestrated it
- He gave the Circle the information they needed
- He stood aside (or participated) while his brothers and sisters died
- He earned his life through betrayal

## Current Status

### In Takashima
Tenka is currently in Takashima, positioned to:
- Guard the vault for the Circle (or himself)
- Prevent others from accessing it
- Eliminate Aoshi if they appear
- Serve his own mysterious agenda

Tenka and Kazuya are **counterparts** - parallel villains who serve as mirrors:

**The Duality:**
- Tenka: Traitor to clan/family for [reason]
- Kazuya: Traitor to assassin brotherhood for revenge

**Potential Dynamic:**
1. **Allied** - Work together to access vault
2. **Rivals** - Competing for vault's contents
3. **Uneasy Partners** - Use each other but don't trust
4. **Unaware** - Don't know about each other initially

**The Mirror:**
Both villains force their PC counterparts to confront betrayal:
- Aoshi must face Tenka (clan betrayer)
- Fox must face Kazuya (brotherhood betrayer)
- Both PCs must decide: Kill? Redeem? Understand?

## The Why - Motivation for Betrayal

**Possible Reasons (Choose one or combine):**

### Option 1: Corrupted by Shindarasu
- Exposed to corruption through vault duty
- Shindarasu whispered promises of power
- Memory manipulation made him believe betrayal was right path
- Wasn't fully willing - corrupted over time

**Pros:** Offers redemption possibility, ties to main villain
**Cons:** Reduces personal agency, might feel like excuse

### Option 2: Ambition & Power
- Wanted power/knowledge the clan kept from him
- Believed Oniwaban way was outdated/wrong
- Circle offered what clan wouldn't
- Fully willing and intentional

**Pros:** Clear villain, no moral ambiguity
**Cons:** Less complex, harder to make sympathetic

### Option 3: Survival & Cowardice
- Circle threatened him specifically
- Chose his life over clan's
- Regrets it now but can't undo it
- Living in guilt and self-hatred

**Pros:** Tragic villain, redemption possible
**Cons:** Makes him weaker, less threatening

### Option 4: Philosophical Disagreement
- Believed vault should be used, not guarded
- Thought Oniwaban secrecy was wrong
- Genuinely believes Circle's goals are right
- Willing traitor for "greater good"

**Pros:** Makes him ideological threat
**Cons:** Requires fleshing out Circle philosophy

### Option 5: Protecting Someone/Something
- Circle held leverage over someone he loved
- Had to betray clan to save [person/place/thing]
- Still believes it was right choice
- Would do it again

**Pros:** Complex motivations, moral gray area
**Cons:** Requires developing the leverage

### Option 6: The Visions Made Him Do It
- Shikigo or Oni Babamon spoke to him first
- Visions told him to betray the clan
- Believes he's following divine will
- Aoshi's visions are the same source

**Pros:** Ties to Aoshi's storyline directly
**Cons:** Makes visions purely evil (removes ambiguity)

## Combat Capabilities

### Oniwaban Techniques
As a former guardian, Tenka possesses:
- All traditional Oniwaban combat arts
- Oni-enhanced strength/abilities
- Vault-specific knowledge and counters
- Decades of experience

### Advantages Over Aoshi
- **Experience:** Older, more battles fought
- **Knowledge:** Knows Aoshi's training (he may have trained them)
- **Preparation:** Expects Aoshi might come
- **Resources:** Circle backing, equipment, position
- **Ruthlessness:** No moral limits anymore

### Aoshi's Advantages
- **Righteousness:** Fighting for fallen clan
- **Purity:** Untainted by corruption (yet)
- **Hunger:** Needs this more than Tenka does
- **Allies:** Has the party backing them
- **Youth:** Possibly younger, faster (if Tenka is older)

### Signature Abilities

**Oniwaban Secret Arts:**
- **Oni's Wrath:** Enhanced strength from oni heritage
- **Shadow Guardian Form:** Defensive kata from vault duty
- **Ancestral Techniques:** Clan-specific martial arts
- **Vault Walker:** Knows vault layout and traps

**Corrupted/Enhanced by Circle:**
- **Tsurogami's Blessing:** Additional power from the Woven Tyrant
- **Memory Theft:** Can steal techniques by observing them
- **Shadow Step:** Teleportation through shadows
- **Soulsteel Weapon:** May wield corrupted Oniwaban blade

Tenka knows:

## The Shikigo/Oni Babamon Connection

### Are the Visions Related?

**Possibility 1: Same Source, Different Messages**
- Shikigo speaks to both Aoshi and Tenka
- Gave Tenka one path (betrayal)
- Giving Aoshi different path (redemption?)
- Testing both to see who succeeds

**Possibility 2: Opposing Forces**
- Shikigo guides Aoshi (true guardian spirit)
- Oni Babamon corrupted Tenka (false/evil entity)
- The vision conflict represents larger cosmic struggle
- Aoshi must reject false visions to succeed

**Possibility 3: Tenka Never Had Visions**
- He's lying about divine guidance
- Using it as justification/excuse
- Trying to confuse/corrupt Aoshi
- The visions are unique to Aoshi

**Possibility 4: Corruption of True Visions**
- Tenka received real visions but misinterpreted them
- Circle's corruption twisted his understanding
- Shindarasu interfered with the message
- Aoshi risks the same corruption

## Potential Redemption Arc

**If redemption is possible, what would it require?**

**Stage 1: Recognition**
- Tenka must acknowledge what he did was wrong
- Not just for survival or pragmatism - actually wrong
- Face the weight of all those deaths

**Stage 2: Truth**
- Reveal his true motivations honestly
- No more lies, no more justifications
- Accept Aoshi's (and party's) judgment

**Stage 3: Atonement**
- Active steps to undo damage
- Help stop Circle's vault plan
- Restore Oniwaban honor somehow
- Probably requires sacrifice

**Stage 4: Acceptance**
- Even if redeemed, can never be Oniwaban again
- Must live with what he did
- Aoshi may never forgive him
- Some sins leave permanent scars

**Stage 5: New Purpose**
- If he survives, what does he do?
- Become new guardian (elsewhere)?
- Serve Aoshi's mission?
- Wandering ronin seeking redemption?
- Death may be the only true atonement
