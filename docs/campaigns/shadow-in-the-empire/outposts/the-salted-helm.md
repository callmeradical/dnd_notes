# The Salted Helm

**Location:** Near Castle Garodya  
**Type:** Half-sunken dockside tavern turned survival camp  
**Proprietor:** Captain Branka Ironhide

---

## Outpost Description

### **Physical Layout**
The Salted Helm sits partially submerged in a tidal marsh, its sagging wooden structure held up by improvised stilts and salvaged ship timbers. The tavern's original figureheads and nautical decorations now serve as both charm and functional weather vanes. Rope bridges connect the main building to several floating platforms that serve as sleeping quarters and storage.

### **Atmosphere**
Salt spray mingles with jungle humidity, creating a perpetual haze around the outpost. The sound of creaking wood and lapping water provides constant background noise. At high tide, the lower floors flood, forcing everyone to the upper levels. Storm warnings come from Branka's collection of figureheads, which she swears whisper predictions in the wind.

---

## Proprietor

### **Captain Branka Ironhide**
*Medium humanoid (mountain dwarf), chaotic neutral*

**Appearance:** One-eyed dwarf with salt-and-pepper braids woven with small bones and shells. Missing her left eye to a sahuagin attack, she wears a patch made from dragonshard-studded leather. Her hands are permanently stained with tar and rope burn scars.

**Personality:** Superstitious but practical, Branka treats her figureheads as crew members and often addresses them by name. She's generous with advice about water travel but charges premium prices for supplies. Has an encyclopedic knowledge of Q'barra's waterways and their seasonal changes.

**Services Offered:**
- Navigation charts and tide tables
- Weather predictions (surprisingly accurate)
- Boat repairs and waterproofing services
- Safe harbor during storms (for paying customers)

## Patrons & Rumors

### **Silo the Drunk**
*Medium humanoid (half-orc), chaotic neutral*

**Appearance:** Massive half-orc sailor with trembling hands and bloodshot eyes. Wears a tattered naval uniform from an unknown fleet. Constantly clutches a bottle and flinches at sudden movements.

**Background:** Former ship's mate who claims to have seen something terrible in Castle Garodya's ruins. Most patrons dismiss him as a drunk, but his terror is genuine and specific.

**Key Information:**
- **"I saw it sleeping in the vaults, I tell you! Coiled around melted gold like a cat in sunbeams!"**
- **"The heat... you can feel it from the water. Not natural fire, dragon fire!"**
- **"It dreams, and its dreams make the water boil. I've seen fish float up dead, cooked from below."**

**Potential Services:** Will guide party to outer perimeter of Castle Garodya for 50 gp, but refuses to get closer than a mile from the ruins.

---

### **Nimra Tideborn**
*Medium humanoid (elf), neutral*

**Appearance:** Sea elf with intricate tattoos that shift color with her mood. Wears practical diving gear and carries waterproof map cases. Her hair is bleached white from saltwater and sun exposure.

**Background:** Professional scavenger who specializes in underwater ruins and shipwrecks. Claims elven heritage gives her advantage in reading old maps and understanding ancient maritime routes.

**Key Information:**
- **"I've got charts showing the bay before the dragon came - some vaults might still be sealed!"**
- **"The old Galifan navy had a secret anchorage near Garodya. Never been properly explored."**
- **"Water levels changed after the Mourning. Whole sections of coast are different now."**

**Services & Goods:**
- **Salvaged Maps:** 25-100 gp each (DC 15 Investigation to verify accuracy)
- **Underwater Exploration:** 20 gp per day plus half-share of treasure
- **Diving Equipment Rental:** 5 gp per day (grants swim speed 30 ft)

## Roleplay Opportunities

### **The Figureheads' Whispers**
Branka's collection of broken figureheads creates atmospheric roleplay opportunities:
- **Storm Warnings:** Characters may hear whispers during weather changes
- **Navigation Aid:** Figureheads point toward safe channels during fog
- **Supernatural Encounters:** One figurehead may contain trapped spirit seeking rest

### **Tidal Challenges**
The outpost's flooding creates natural time pressure and environmental challenges:
- **High Tide Meetings:** Important negotiations conducted on upper floors during floods
- **Low Tide Exploration:** Access to submerged storage areas and hidden caches
- **Storm Survival:** Party may need to weather dangerous storms at the outpost

### **Maritime Mysteries**
Water-based adventure hooks naturally emerge from the setting:
- **Sunken Treasures:** Maps leading to pre-war shipwrecks
- **Sea Monster Sightings:** Reports of unusual creatures in nearby waters  
- **Smuggling Networks:** Underground economy using waterways to avoid wyrm attention

## Story Integration

### **Connection to Castle Garodya**
- Silo's dragon sighting provides advance warning about the sleeping wyrm
- Nimra's maps may reveal alternate approaches to the castle ruins
- Branka's weather knowledge helps party time their approach during optimal conditions

### **Regional Intelligence**
- Water travel routes connecting to other destinations
- Seasonal patterns affecting overland travel
- Communication network with other coastal outposts and settlements

### **Long-term Relationships**
- Successful storm shelter builds trust with Branka
- Proving Silo's claims helps establish party's credibility
- Nimra becomes valuable recurring contact for coastal exploration

## GM Notes

### **Encounter Opportunities**
- **Storm Arrival:** Party caught in sudden weather, must shelter at outpost
- **Rival Scavengers:** Competition for Nimra's maps or services
- **Supernatural Phenomena:** Figureheads react to approaching supernatural threats

### **Economic Dynamics**
- Prices fluctuate with weather and seasonal trade patterns  
- Bartering accepted - information often more valuable than gold
- Regular customers receive discounts and priority service

### **Environmental Factors**
- Tidal schedules affect outpost accessibility and services
- Storm season increases prices but also brings more desperate travelers
- Proximity to Castle Garodya creates subtle draconic influence effects
