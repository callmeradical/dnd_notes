# Haruki "The Boar" Takeda

## Overview
A broad-shouldered, boisterous member of [[The Starcall Table]] gambling group, known for his love of drinking and his loud, gregarious personality. His nickname "The Boar" likely reflects both his physical build and his charging, straightforward approach to life.

## Appearance
- Broad-shouldered and physically imposing
- Build suggests strength and endurance
- Presence that fills a room (or a campsite)

## Personality
- Boisterous and loud
- Enjoys drinking and social gatherings
- Prefers straightforward games over subtle contests
- His laughter is distinctive - booming and echoing across camp
- Gregarious and friendly in his own way

## Notable Moments
During the storytelling wager between [[Shigeo]] and [[Kaoru Sugimoto]], Haruki quickly folded, recognizing that contests of subtle wordplay and emotional resonance weren't his style. He preferred games he could approach more directly - or at least games where he could make noise and have another drink.

## Connections
**The Starcall Table:**
- [[Kaoru Sugimoto]] - The elegant merchant and skilled storyteller
- [[Daisuke "Silver Pockets" Inoue]] - The charming younger gambler
- [[Aiko Hayashi]] - The quiet warrior

## Campaign Notes
- First encountered: Along the Silver Road during the caravan journey to Toriyama
- Witnessed the famous storytelling wager while enjoying drinks
- Continues to travel with the Starcall Table
