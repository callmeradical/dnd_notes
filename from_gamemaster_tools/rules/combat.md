## Spell Damage
Can be considered non-lethal force. Meaning players have the agency to decide
how effective their spell is on a killing blow.  

## Casting Spells 
### Observing the Effect of a Friendly Spell:

At this point, the party has been traveling a sufficiently long enough time,
you know the effects of your spells and knows what to watch for in combat.

### Bonus Action spells A spell cast with a bonus action is especially swift.
You must use a bonus action on your turn to cast the spell, provided that you
haven't already taken a bonus action this turn. You can't cast another spell
during the same turn, except for a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action.

### Material Components

When a specific reagent is called out for a specific spell, such as a pearl
worth 25gp, that reagent needs to be purchased or at least be present.

## (NOT IN EFFECT) Called Shots 

If you want to call your shot for additional effect. You must hit with both d20
rolls for your attack. If the attack hits it is a critical, and an additional
effect is applied depending on the circumstance. A called shot is always a
critical.
