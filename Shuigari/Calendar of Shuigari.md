# Calendar of Shuigari

Shuigari follows a lunisolar calendar guided by the three moons—Dendaro, Intara, and Tsukuyomi. Each year contains **twelve months of thirty days**, divided into three ten-day **decads**. Seasonal transitions are timed to celestial alignments, and every month carries a dominant spiritual theme that shapes rituals, markets, and travel.

## Months at a Glance

### Spring
- **Kaminatsu — “Spring’s Blessing”**: Renewal rites, temple petitions for bountiful crops, first pilgrimages of the year.
- **Haranami — “Waves of Chaos”**: Unpredictable winds; farmers placate storm kami before sowing.
- **Sakuraji — “Cherry Bloom”**: Hanami feasts, poetry competitions, vows of harmony.

### Summer
- **Tenka — “Heaven’s Fire”**: Offerings to fire kami; warriors seek omens before campaigns.
- **Hisakata — “Endless Sky”**: Explorers, merchants, and sky-priests hold navigation festivals.
- **Kaijō — “Ocean’s Heart”**: Coastal communities honor Ryujin with regattas and tide-blessing ceremonies.

### Autumn
- **Momiji — “Maple Leaf”**: Harvest festivals, ancestor offerings, communal storehouse feasts.
- **Tsukinami — “Moonlight Path”**: Moonlit meditations and divinations—also the month when *The Last Whisper of Steel* begins.
- **Kuroame — “Black Rain”**: Period of mourning and spiritual cleansing; shrines host purification rites.

### Winter
- **Yukizora — “Snow Sky”**: Sacred snow ceremonies, vows of quiet reflection.
- **Fubuki — “Winter Storm”**: Communities gather around shared hearths; storytellers recount heroic endurance.
- **Setsunai — “Frozen Heart”**: Year-end audits, reconciliations, and spiritual renewal before the cycle resets.

## Annual Festivals & Observances
- **Spring Equinox (1 Kaminatsu):** Renewal rites for farmers; priests bless seedlings with jade bells.
- **Cherry Blossom Feast (mid-Sakuraji):** Hanami banquets, courtship promises, lantern-lit river floats.
- **Summer Solstice (5 Tenka):** Fire dancers and blacksmiths reenact the forging of Soulsteel.
- **Ocean’s Gratitude (Kaijō 15):** Fishing fleets parade decorated boats; offerings returned to the tide.
- **Moonlit Reverence (Tsukinami 21):** Held in honor of [Tsukuyomi](The%20worship%20of%20Tsukuyomi%20in%20Shuigari.md); divinations performed with mirrored pools.
- **Night of Falling Leaves (Kuroame 9):** Memorial for those lost in the past year; paper leaves released on the wind.
- **First Snow Vigil (Yukizora 3):** Children scatter rice along village borders to welcome protective kami.
- **Veil of Embers (Fubuki 27):** Hearth ash is carried to shrines symbolising the burdens of the year.
- **Last Flame Ceremony (Setsunai 30):** Final lantern extinguished at midnight to invite dawn’s renewal.
