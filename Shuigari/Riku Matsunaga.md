# Riku Matsunaga

**Role:** Young, zealous samurai sworn to [[Lord Ichiro Kagemura]].  
**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered with slightly mismatched lacquered armor that marks his fresh promotion; carries both katana and ashigaru bow.  
**Personality:** Earnest and outspoken, eager to prove himself but lacks political tact.  
**Notable Action:** During Hideo’s funeral rites he loudly questions the yokai presence, earning sharp reprimands from Kagemura and [[Akane Hiroshi]]. The outburst offers the party an opening to de-escalate tensions—or exploit them.
