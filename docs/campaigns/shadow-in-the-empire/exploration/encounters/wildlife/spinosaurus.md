# Spinosaurus

## Spinosaurus
*Gargantuan beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 15 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 231 (14d20+84)  
**Speed** 50 ft., swim 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 27(+8) | 9(-1) | 23(+6) | 2(-4) | 12(+1) | 9(-1) |

**Skills** Perception +7  
**Senses** passive Perception 17  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 13 (10,000 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +5

### Features

**Keen Smell.** The spinosaurus has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

**Siege Monster.** The spinosaurus deals double damage to objects and structures.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The spinosaurus makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 30 (4d10+8) piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw or be swallowed by the spinosaurus. A swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the spinosaurus, and it takes 21 (6d6) acid damage at the start of each of the spinosaurus's turns. If the spinosaurus takes 40 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the spinosaurus must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which fall prone in a space within 10 feet of the spinosaurus. If the spinosaurus dies, a swallowed creature is no longer restrained by it and can escape from the corpse using 20 feet of movement, exiting prone.

**Tail.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +13 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (3d8+8) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of the spinosaurus's choice that is within 120 feet of the spinosaurus and aware of it must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the spinosaurus's Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 spinosaurus (territorial, never in groups)  
**Environment:** Large rivers, coastal areas, massive swamps  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random wildlife encounter (#5 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Apex predator that views everything in its territory as potential food or threat
- Extremely territorial, will attack anything that doesn't immediately flee
- Patient when hunting, can remain motionless in water for hours

**Mannerisms**

- Massive sail fin visible above jungle canopy when standing
- Creates thunderous splashing when moving through water
- Deep, rumbling growl that can be heard for miles
- Leaves massive claw marks on trees to mark territory

**Tactics**

- Uses aquatic advantage, staying in deep water when possible
- Attempts to swallow smaller creatures whole with massive jaws
- Uses tail sweep to knock groups of enemies prone
- Employs Frightful Presence to scatter groups before attacking
- Pursues fleeing creatures relentlessly within its territory
- Retreats only if reduced to 50 hit points or less

**Environment Notes**

- Territory extends 5-10 miles along major waterways
- Massive footprints (3 feet long) in mud and sand near water
- Partially eaten large creatures left on riverbanks
- Fish and smaller aquatic life flee the area when present
- Trees broken or pushed aside to create hunting paths to water

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine encounter location: major river crossing, coastal area, or large swamp
- Note party's method of travel: boat, raft, swimming, or attempting to cross
- Consider visibility: spinosaurus sail fin is visible from great distance in open areas
- Roll for creature's current activity: hunting, sunning itself, or defending territory

**Opening Scene Options**

**River Crossing Encounter:**
> *As you approach the wide river, something massive shifts in the deeper water. What you initially took for a fallen tree trunk suddenly reveals itself as a creature of prehistoric nightmares. A spinosaurus, fifty feet from nose to tail, raises its crocodilian head from the murky water. The massive sail on its back catches the sunlight as it fixes ancient eyes upon your group.*

**Territorial Challenge:**
> *The jungle falls unnaturally silent as thunderous footsteps approach through the trees. Ancient hardwoods bend and snap like twigs before the approach of something immense. When the spinosaurus emerges from the treeline, its massive sail fin towering twenty feet above its back, you realize you've entered the domain of the jungle's undisputed master.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Territorial Display**
- Spinosaurus uses Frightful Presence to establish dominance
- Positions itself between party and escape route (usually water)
- Attempts to swallow the largest or most threatening target
- **Wisdom Save (DC 17):** Resist being frightened by Frightful Presence
- **Perception Check (DC 15):** Notice escape routes before creature blocks them

**Phase 2: Apex Predator Assault**
- Uses Multiattack to bite and tail sweep each turn
- Focuses on swallowing targets that fail Dexterity saves
- Uses massive size and reach to control battlefield positioning
- **Dexterity Save (DC 21):** Avoid being swallowed when bitten
- **Strength Save (DC 21):** Avoid being knocked prone by tail attack

**Phase 3: Territorial Pursuit**
- Pursues fleeing creatures within its territory (5-10 miles)
- Uses superior swimming speed to cut off water-based escapes
- May retreat if reduced to 50 HP, but will remember the intrusion
- **Survival Check (DC 18):** Navigate around creature's territory boundaries

**Environmental Combat**

**Aquatic Advantage**
- Spinosaurus prefers fighting in deep water where it can swim
- Party members swimming have disadvantage on weapon attacks
- Creature can use water depth for cover and concealment
- Massive splashing creates difficult terrain around combat area

**Size and Reach Benefits**
- 15-foot reach on bite attacks, 20-foot reach on tail attacks
- Can attack multiple targets with tail sweep if positioned correctly
- Siege Monster trait destroys environmental cover easily
- Large size makes it difficult to hide from or evade

**Skill Integration**
- **Animal Handling (DC 22):** Attempt to calm or redirect creature (very difficult)
- **Athletics (DC 18):** Climb onto creature's back to avoid some attacks
- **Medicine (DC 15):** Treat acid damage from being swallowed
- **Nature (DC 16):** Understand creature's territorial behavior and likely retreat routes
- **Survival (DC 15):** Track creature's movement patterns and avoid future encounters

**Swallowing Mechanics**
- Swallowed creatures have total cover but take ongoing acid damage
- Multiple party members can be swallowed (stomach is huge)
- Dealing 40+ damage from inside forces Constitution save for regurgitation
- Healing and support spells can keep swallowed allies alive longer

**Social Resolution (Nearly Impossible)**
- Spinosaurus is not naturally aggressive toward humans unless threatened
- **Massive Food Offering:** Fresh large creature carcass might distract temporarily
- **Territorial Respect:** Immediately fleeing the area prevents combat
- **Fire/Magic Display:** Powerful magical effects might encourage retreat

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Spinosaurus Defeated**
- Massive carcass provides resources: hide, bones, teeth worth 500+ gp
- Other apex predators move into vacant territory, potentially more dangerous
- Local ecosystem changes significantly with removal of top predator
- Reputation as legendary monster slayers spreads throughout region

**If Party Retreats**
- Creature remembers the intrusion and becomes more aggressive
- Territory boundaries expand as creature becomes more confident
- Future encounters in the area are more likely and dangerous
- Local guides refuse to enter the creature's hunting grounds

**Story Integration**
- Local settlement plagued by spinosaurus attacks on river traffic
- Creature guards access to important location party needs to reach
- Ancient ruins partially submerged in creature's territory
- Rival adventuring party already attempted to kill creature and failed

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Creature is old/injured, reduce HP and damage
- **Harder:** Pair of spinosaurus (extremely rare) or add environmental hazards
- **Environmental:** Combat during storm, high tide, or in treacherous swamp
- **Tactical:** Creature uses lair actions in its established territory

**Treasure Considerations**
- Previous victims' equipment scattered along riverbank
- 1d4 magic items from powerful adventurers who challenged creature
- Valuable trade goods from destroyed river vessels
- Rare dragonshard deposits in creature's underwater lair
