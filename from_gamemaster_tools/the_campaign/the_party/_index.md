## Humble Beginnings
A tavern is being established to serve as a front. Foundry House Alunno, the
most powerful merchant house in Cagliari they have their hands in just about
everything.

A Selvaggio operative is putting together a team to overthrow
Alunno. They see this as the most opportune time to create power play in
Cagliari, right after the patriarch of the Marivaldi family has been
assassinated.

* Marcus (Turok) Affiliated with Nascosto, and they have been
hired to keep tabs on the Selvaggio operation.
* Brandi (Lakelyn) and Jimmy (Oloric) Foundry House Alunno is primarily the offending and intruding party.
* Garza (Dr. Eli) and Margarita (Isla) Alunno is the house that runs the
university and responsible for ruining your reputation.
* Brian (Taklinn) The operative is a dwarf, and former cleric of “The Keepers” has some information
about the mission that Taklinn was on when his party memebers fell.
* Bethany (Lore Bard) Been contacted by an associate of an associate, reportedly offering
a big pay day?

## Base of Operations

The Iron Lass is a tavern that is run in the Seven Hills district of Cagliari.

### Notes

* A Tavern, A Blacksmith, and Living Quarters, with Workshop in the rear
* Type of Dwelling will grant the party different abilities, have to decide this after the party is formed.
* Every 10 weeks the Establishment generates revenue (1000gp/lvl)

### Artisans and Units

* Blacksmith
    * Upkeep of the tavern/dwelling
    * Crafting arms and armor
    * For each level of the stronghold crafting becomes 10% cheaper, max out at 50%.
* Scriving
    * Scribe
    * Moneychanger, record keeper, diplomat, creation of vellum, inks, spell scrolls.
* Units
