# Stonecoil Refuge

**Location:** Foothills of the Stormhorn Mountains  
**Type:** Fortified mountain camp carved into canyon wall  
**Proprietor:** Rurik Stonecoil

---

## Outpost Description

### **Physical Layout**
Carved directly into the canyon wall, Stonecoil Refuge resembles a vertical fortress more than a traditional outpost. Multiple levels connected by rope bridges and carved steps provide defensible positions with commanding views of the approaches. The main trading area occupies a large natural cave, while sleeping quarters are individual cells carved deeper into the rock.

### **Atmosphere**
The constant sound of wind through canyon passages creates an eerie backdrop to all activities. Rurik's painted dragon murals cover every available wall surface - crude but unsettling representations that seem to watch visitors with their painted eyes. The location's defensive advantages come at the cost of claustrophobic living conditions.

### **Defensive Features**
- Multiple levels with interlocking fields of fire
- Narrow approaches easily defended by small numbers
- Hidden weapon caches throughout the complex
- Emergency escape routes through canyon passages
- Natural camouflage makes refuge nearly invisible from distance

## Proprietor

### **Rurik Stonecoil**
*Medium humanoid (mountain dwarf), lawful neutral*

**Appearance:** Stocky dwarf with prematurely white beard and hands stained with paint and stone dust. His eyes dart constantly between visitors and potential threats. Wears practical mountain gear reinforced with metal plates and carries multiple weapons within easy reach.

**Personality:** Paranoid but competent, Rurik treats every visitor as a potential threat until proven otherwise. Speaks in short, clipped sentences and demands payment upfront for everything. Despite his suspicion, he provides quality equipment and reliable service to those who earn his grudging respect.

**Background:** Retired mercenary who fought in multiple conflicts throughout Khorvaire. Chose the isolated canyon location specifically for its defensive advantages and distance from political complications. His painted dragons are both artistic expression and psychological deterrent.

**Services Offered:**
- Mercenary intelligence and contact networks
- Mountain survival consultation and equipment
- Defensive planning and fortification advice
- Secure storage for valuable goods

## Patrons & Rumors

### **Veyla of the Ash Peaks**
*Medium humanoid (tiefling), chaotic neutral*

**Appearance:** Striking tiefling with ash-gray skin and horns filed to sharp points. Wears practical mercenary gear marked with scorch marks and battle damage. Her eyes glow faintly red when she's angry or excited, and her tail moves constantly, betraying her emotional state.

**Background:** Professional sellsword who specializes in dangerous mountain operations. Claims heritage from the Ash Peaks region but avoids discussing her past in detail. Known for completing contracts that others consider suicidal.

**Key Information:**
- **"The Stormhorns are under siege by wyrm cultists - proper siege, not just raids."**
- **"They've got organization, equipment, and something big backing them up."**
- **"Pay's good for anyone willing to break the siege lines, but it's bloody work."**

**Services:**
- **Military Contract:** Leading counter-siege operations (100 gp per day plus combat bonuses)
- **Tactical Intelligence:** Detailed information about cult forces and positions (75 gp)
- **Combat Training:** Advanced fighting techniques for mountain warfare (50 gp per day)
- **Equipment Procurement:** Access to professional military gear (20% markup)

---

### **Brother Alrik**
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful good*

**Appearance:** Middle-aged human priest with weathered features and callused hands from mountain travel. Wears simple robes patched with donations from various communities. Carries a walking staff topped with a silver holy symbol that catches the light.

**Background:** Traveling priest who provides medical care and spiritual comfort throughout the mountain communities. Claims divine visions guide him to those in need, though others suggest he simply has excellent information networks.

**Key Information:**
- **"There's a hidden shrine in the mountains that grants visions of the dragons' downfall."**
- **"The visions come to the faithful, but the path is treacherous and guarded."**
- **"I've seen the signs - the ancient powers are stirring, and change comes soon."**

**Services:**
- **Healing Services:** Magical and mundane medical care (donation basis, typically 25 gp)
- **Spiritual Guidance:** Religious consultation and ceremony (free to faithful)
- **Divine Divination:** Commune with divine powers for guidance (100 gp)
- **Sacred Site Information:** Directions to hidden religious locations (50 gp)

## Roleplay Opportunities

### **Paranoid Hospitality**
Rurik's suspicious nature creates unique interaction dynamics:
- **Trust Building:** Gradual warming as party proves reliability
- **Security Protocols:** Detailed procedures for staying at the refuge
- **Information Trading:** Rurik values intelligence as much as coin

### **Mercenary Culture**
The refuge attracts professional soldiers creating military atmosphere:
- **War Stories:** Exchange of combat experiences and tactical knowledge
- **Equipment Comparison:** Professional assessment of gear and weapons
- **Contract Negotiations:** Business discussions about military employment

### **Mountain Mysticism**
Brother Alrik introduces religious and supernatural elements:
- **Vision Quests:** Spiritual journeys to sacred mountain sites
- **Divine Intervention:** Religious ceremonies and blessing rituals
- **Prophetic Warnings:** Cryptic guidance about future events

- Strategic analysis of regional conflicts and their implications
