# Shikigami

*Small construct, neutral*

**Armor Class:** 13
**Hit Points:** 24 (7d6)
**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 8 (-1) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills:** Stealth +5, Perception +4
**Damage Resistances:** Piercing, Slashing
**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Paralyzed
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
**Languages:** Understands Common and the language of its creator but cannot speak
**Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

## Traits

**Construct Nature.**
The Shikigami doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.

**Paper Form.**
The Shikigami can squeeze through spaces as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing. It is immune to effects that would grapple or restrain it unless the effect specifically targets constructs.

**Origami Mimicry.**
As a bonus action, the Shikigami can fold its body into a shape resembling an animal, tool, or simple object, such as a bird, a dagger, or a key. It remains in this form until it uses a bonus action to change back. A successful **Intelligence (Investigation) check (DC 13)** reveals its true nature.

**Magic Resistance.**
The Shikigami has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Innate Spellcasting.**
The Shikigami's innate spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 12). It can cast the following spells, requiring no components:

- At will: *minor illusion*
- 1/day each: *blur*, *silent image*

## Actions

**Multiattack.**
The Shikigami makes two attacks with its Paper Slashes.

**Paper Slash.**
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage.

**Paper Barrage (Recharge 5–6).**
The Shikigami launches a flurry of razor-sharp paper fragments in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Reactions

**Fold and Flutter.**
When the Shikigami is hit by a melee attack, it can use its reaction to fold into a bird-like shape and fly 15 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

## Tactics

- The Shikigami relies on its **Origami Mimicry** to hide or deceive its enemies, striking from unexpected angles.
- In combat, it uses **Paper Barrage** to weaken groups of enemies before retreating with **Fold and Flutter** to reposition.
- It's often used as a spy or distraction, taking advantage of its small size and deceptive nature.
