# Pteranodon Flock

## Pteranodon
*Medium beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 13 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 13 (3d8)  
**Speed** 10 ft., fly 60 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12(+1) | 15(+2) | 10(+0) | 2(-4) | 9(-1) | 5(-3) |

**Skills** Perception +1  
**Senses** passive Perception 11  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Flyby.** The pteranodon doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

### Actions

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (2d4+1) piercing damage.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 2d4+2 pteranodons (4-10 in flock)  
**Environment:** Coastal areas, high cliffs, jungle clearings  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random wildlife encounter (#7 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Opportunistic scavengers that prefer easy targets over dangerous prey
- Highly social, communicate through screeches and aerial displays
- Curious about shiny objects and will investigate unusual items

**Mannerisms**

- Distinctive keening cries echo across long distances
- Perform aerial acrobatics when excited or agitated  
- Tilt heads sideways to get better look at potential threats or food
- Wings create distinctive whistling sound during dive attacks

**Tactics**

- Use coordinated dive attacks from different angles
- Target isolated party members or those carrying visible food/shiny objects
- Employ hit-and-run tactics, using Flyby to avoid retaliation
- Focus on grabbing items and flying away rather than prolonged combat
- Retreat if more than half the flock is killed or seriously injured
- May follow party at distance waiting for opportunity

**Environment Notes**

- Often seen soaring on thermal currents above jungle canopy
- Nests built on inaccessible cliff faces or tall tree platforms
- Leave distinctive guano deposits on rocks and trees below nesting areas
- Presence indicates nearby water sources or large animal populations

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 2d4+2 for flock size (4-10 pteranodons)
- Determine encounter location: open area where they can maneuver effectively
- Note party's visible equipment: shiny items, food, or interesting objects
- Consider time of day: pteranodons most active during morning and evening

**Opening Scene Options**

**Scavenger Investigation:**
> *High-pitched screeches echo from above as shadows pass over your group. Looking up, you see a flock of pteranodons circling lazily on outstretched wings. Their keen eyes are clearly evaluating your group—and your possessions. One breaks formation and swoops lower, tilting its crested head to get a better look.*

**Territorial Dispute:**
> *The peaceful jungle clearing erupts in a cacophony of screeches and wing beats. A flock of pteranodons bursts from their roosts in the tall trees, clearly agitated by your presence in their feeding area. Their fifteen-foot wingspans cast moving shadows across the ground as they wheel overhead, working themselves into aggressive displays.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Evaluation and Testing**
- Pteranodons circle overhead assessing party threat level
- May make mock dive attacks to test reactions
- Focus attention on shiny objects, food, or small items
- **Perception Check (DC 12):** Notice pteranodons before they attack
- **Animal Handling Check (DC 15):** Discourage aggressive behavior with confident displays

**Phase 2: Coordinated Harassment**
- Multiple pteranodons dive from different angles
- Use Flyby attacks to grab items and retreat without retaliation
- Target spellcasters, ranged attackers, or isolated party members
- **Acrobatics Check (DC 13):** Avoid being knocked prone by diving pteranodon
- **Sleight of Hand Check (DC 14):** Secure valuable items from grabbing attempts

**Phase 3: Escalation or Retreat**
- If initial attacks successful, more pteranodons join the assault
- If facing serious resistance, flock retreats to high perches
- May continue following party at distance looking for opportunities
- **Intimidation Check (DC 12):** Drive off pteranodons with aggressive displays

**Flyby Tactics Deep Dive**

**Aerial Superiority**
- Pteranodons control vertical space and timing of engagement
- Can attack and withdraw without triggering opportunity attacks
- Use height advantage to spot and coordinate on vulnerable targets
- Difficult to pin down with melee attacks

**Grab and Go Strategy**
- Primary goal is theft, not combat
- Target backpacks, weapon sheaths, spell component pouches
- May grab small party members (halflings, gnomes) and carry them briefly
- Drop stolen items if attacked successfully

**Environmental Usage**
- **Open Areas:** Pteranodons excel, plenty of maneuvering room
- **Dense Forest:** Limited flight paths, easier to defend against
- **Cliff Areas:** Can retreat to inaccessible perches
- **Water Sources:** Natural gathering areas for both prey and pteranodons

**Skill Integration**
- **Athletics (DC 14):** Jump to attack low-flying pteranodons
- **Acrobatics (DC 12):** Maintain footing during dive attacks
- **Nature (DC 11):** Understand pteranodon behavior and predict actions
- **Perception (DC 13):** Track pteranodons' aerial movements and coordinate defense

**Combat Countermeasures**

**Ranged Combat Advantages**
- Bows, crossbows, and ranged spells most effective
- Flying creatures make difficult but not impossible targets
- Area effect spells can catch multiple pteranodons in formation

**Defensive Formations**
- Stay close together to prevent isolation
- Protect spellcasters and ranged attackers in center
- Use shields and armor to deflect dive attacks
- Secure loose equipment to prevent theft

**Social Resolution Options**

**Feeding Alternative**
- Offer food to distract from theft attempts
- Fresh meat or fish particularly appealing
- May satisfy flock's curiosity and encourage departure

**Intimidation Display**
- Large fires or loud noises can discourage attacks
- Killing one pteranodon may cause others to retreat
- Confident, aggressive posturing shows strength

**Item Management**
- Secure shiny or valuable items in closed containers
- Remove tempting targets from sight
- Consider sacrificing less valuable items to satisfy flock

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Pteranodons Defeated**
- Wing membranes valuable for crafting gliding equipment
- Hollow bones useful for tool-making or instrument crafting
- Other scavengers attracted to pteranodon carcasses
- Nesting sites may contain accumulated shiny objects

**If Flock Retreats**
- May continue following party at distance
- Could return with larger flock if initial encounter was profitable
- Pteranodons remember successful hunting grounds
- Other aerial predators may investigate the disturbance

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Pteranodons have stolen important item party needs to recover
- Flock nesting area blocks access to cliff-side ruins
- Local settlement plagued by pteranodon raids on supplies
- Unusual behavior indicates environmental changes or larger predator

**Treasure Recovery**
- Pteranodon nests contain 2d6 × 10 gp worth of shiny objects
- Previous theft victims' equipment scattered around nesting area
- Possible magical items that caught pteranodons' attention
- Maps or documents in weather-resistant containers

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce flock size, make them more easily discouraged
- **Harder:** Include larger pteranodon variant or add environmental hazards
- **Environmental:** Combat during storm (disadvantage on ranged attacks) or near dangerous cliffs
- **Social:** Pteranodons trained by local inhabitants as messengers or scouts
