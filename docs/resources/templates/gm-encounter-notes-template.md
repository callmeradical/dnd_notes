## Usage Guidelines

**For New Encounters:**
1. Copy this template structure
2. Replace bracketed placeholders with encounter-specific content
3. Remove sections that don't apply to your encounter type
4. Add encounter-specific sections as needed

**Key Principles:**
- Provide multiple approach options (social, combat, environmental)
- Include skill check opportunities with clear DCs and outcomes
- Consider both success and failure states
- Plan for different party compositions and strategies
- Connect encounters to larger campaign narrative

**Section Priority:**
- **Essential:** Pre-Encounter Setup, Opening Scene, Encounter Phases
- **Important:** Combat Tactics, Social Guidelines, Aftermath
- **Optional:** Scaling Options, Story Integration (add as relevant)
