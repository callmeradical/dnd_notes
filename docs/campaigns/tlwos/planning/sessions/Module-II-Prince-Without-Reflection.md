# Module II: The Prince Without Reflection (Seed)

## Core Premise

The sky-strike event at Takashima palace is an oni traveling to the prince, who is an anomaly of fate.
The oni is a pact-holder warlock of Tsurogami (Woven Tyrant frame).

## Hidden Cause (GM Truth)

- The oni mage had been sealed in a great tree in the forest.
- The current Daimyo imprisoned/enslaved goliaths and forced them to clear-cut northern mountain forest for boatbuilding.
- That forced clear-cut violated core goliath beliefs and created the breach condition for oni release/return.

## Player-Facing Symptom Layer

- Palace-adjacent reality pressure intensifies.
- Creature behavior in the city appears fear-driven and directionally inconsistent.
- Prince stability is linked to reflection, identity, and consent pressure.
- The prince is currently enthralled (GM state; reveal pacing at table discretion).

## Arc Utility

- Connects street-level monster crisis to political palace arc.
- Connects ecological desecration to mythic antagonist operations.
- Creates a morally complex faction thread around coerced goliaths (victims and vectors).
