# Session 023: Middle Terrace Breach

**Arc:** The Iron Court (Arc 2)  
**Session Number:** 23  
**Date Played:** 2026-04-08  
**Session Type:** Urban crisis, escalation, live transition into palace arc  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS / LIVE CONTINUITY

---

## Arc Continuity (Confirmed)

- The party previously returned to Takashima after rescuing the blacksmith's daughter.
- The dragonborn blacksmith (the daughter's father) died.
- A **Bheur Hag** (table-spoken as "bhuer hag") was killed by the party.
- Back in the city, the party regrouped with Kaoru and the Hidden Lotus Society, received payment, and resumed searching for Obsidian Circle members.
- The lower city was besieged by goblins, ogres, and ettins.
- During the chaos, an oni infiltrator entered and was killing civilians; the party engaged and dealt with the threat.
- This conflict confirmed key Takemura lore: the Twice-Cursed Samurai is bound to his blade and operates as a demon slayer.

## City-Scale Escalation

- A massive bubble-like field emerged from the forest and encompassed Takashima.
- A lightning bolt / falling-star event crossed the sky and settled in the palace.
- Hostile creatures that had been pressing the city abruptly reversed course and fled.
- Party interpretation: whatever scared the forest host now resides in the palace, and the young prince is in immediate danger.

## Undercity Sequence (Just Completed)

- The party was attacked by banshees and fled below into the vaults under Takashima.
- The banshees could not cross a hidden boundary effect (mithal, unknown to PCs at the time).
- The party reached the One Tree and the stream, restored the flow, and broke the kitsune village slumber.
- A festival/restoration sequence followed in Kokuei Shuzo's zone.
- After a long rest, the village appeared empty again; the party navigated back through tunnels and resurfaced in the middle terrace.

## Where Play Is Now

- The party has re-entered the middle terrace.
- They have just fought a basilisk.
- Additional creatures are visible roaming the streets.
- This basilisk engagement is treated as the first of multiple middle-terrace threat encounters before palace approach.

## Active Threads

- Survive current middle-terrace pressure and gain entrance to the citadel (top tier of the city/palace).
- Protect/locate the prince in the palace.
- Identify the entity currently anchoring palace fear-pressure.
- Track whether roaming creatures are coordinated, displaced, or both.
- Continue Obsidian Circle hunt under active city instability.
- Resolve Krixarion's snowglobe pressure on Yasei as it intersects with palace events.

## GM-Only Planned Reveal

- The sky/lightning event was the oni traveling to the prince because the prince is an anomaly of fate.
- The oni is a pact-holder warlock of Tsurogami (Woven Tyrant).
- The oni had been sealed in a great tree in the forest.
- The current Daimyo has imprisoned/enslaved goliaths and forced clear-cutting in the northern mountain forest for boatbuilding.
- That coerced clear-cut created the breach condition that enabled the oni mage's return.
- The prince is enthralled.
