# Valenar: History & Legacy

## Ancient Era: The Dhakaani Empire (Before -3000 YK)

**The First Conquerors**

Long before elves set foot in what would become Valenar, the region belonged to the Dhakaani Empire—the mighty goblinoid civilization that dominated Khorvaire for millennia.

**Dhakaani Legacy:**
- **Fortress-Cities**: Massive underground complexes carved into the bedrock, engineered to withstand siege and time
- **The Kech Volaar**: "Keepers of the Word"—scholar-priests who maintained libraries of ancient lore and battle-hymns
- **Temples of the Six Kings**: Ritual sites dedicated to the Dhakaani warrior-heroes, built on ley line convergences
- **The Iron Roads**: Ancient highways of worked stone that still run beneath Valenar, connecting the old empire

**Major Dhakaani Sites in Valenar:**

### Ja'aram Qua (The Sword-City)
- **Location**: Deep beneath what is now Taer Shantare
- **Purpose**: Military academy and armory for the Dhakaani legions
- **Status**: Sealed chambers still exist, filled with ancient weapons, training grounds, and war-forges
- **Rumor**: Some say the deepest vaults contain the Ghaal'dar—legendary Dhakaani siege engines that could break mountains

### Khaar Vanon (The Hall of Silence)
- **Location**: Beneath the ruins of Jal Paeridor
- **Purpose**: Archive of the Kech Volaar, repository of historical records and magical research
- **Status**: Partially collapsed during the wyrm assault, but sections remain intact
- **Rumor**: Scholars claim Khaar Vanon held maps of every ley line in Khorvaire, now buried under floating ruins

### Dhakaan Khor (The Six Crowns)
- **Location**: Underground temple complex beneath Taer Valaestas
- **Purpose**: Religious and political center dedicated to the Six Warrior-Kings of Dhakaani legend
- **Status**: Rediscovered by the Valenar elves, partially converted into war-shrines
- **Current State**: Balaur has claimed it as his throne room—the Six Crowns now serve as pedestals for trophies of conquest

**The Fall**: When the daelkyr invasion shattered the Dhakaani Empire (circa -9000 YK), Valenar's goblinoid cities were abandoned. The remaining Dhakaani sealed their vaults and retreated underground or scattered to other regions.

## The Dragon's Age: Giants & Elves (-40000 YK to -24000 YK)

**Before the Dhakaani, the Giants Ruled**

Even older than the goblinoids, the giants of Xen'drik briefly held colonies in what would become Valenar during their age of empire.

**Giant Remnants:**
- **Titan-Stones**: Monolithic markers along the coast of the Bay of Swords, carved with giant runes predicting "the return of wings and fire"
- **The Forge of Kor'vulath**: A massive volcanic caldera used by giant smiths to forge dragonshard weapons
- **Status**: Balaur has reopened the Forge of Kor'vulath, using it as the heart of his naval forges

---

## The Age of Dhakaan (-9000 YK to -3000 YK)

The goblinoids claimed Valenar from the scattered giant remnants and built their empire. For six thousand years, Dhakaani legions marched from these lands.

**Key Developments:**
- Construction of the Iron Roads
- Establishment of Kech Volaar libraries
- Creation of Dhakaan Khor temple complex
- Forging of legendary Dhakaani blades (many lost, some still hidden in sealed vaults)

---

## The Silence (-3000 YK to -2600 YK)

After the daelkyr wars, Valenar lay largely empty. Scattered tribes of shifters, humans, and nomadic communities passed through but never settled permanently.

**Why?**

The land was said to be cursed—haunted by daelkyr corruption and the ghosts of fallen Dhakaani. Few dared to claim it.

---

## The Valenar Elves Arrive (-2600 YK to Present)

**The Valaes Tairn Migration**

The Valenar elves are descendants of the Valaes Tairn—warrior-clans from Aerenal who served as mercenaries and raiders. Unlike their Aereni cousins, they rejected the Undying Court and ancestor worship in favor of a warrior culture.

**The Claiming (-2600 YK):**
- Valenar warbands swept into the region, claiming it through conquest
- They discovered the Dhakaani ruins and repurposed them
- Taer Valaestas was founded atop Dhakaan Khor, the ancient goblin temple

**Philosophy:**
- **Patron Ancestors**: Valenar warriors seek to emulate legendary heroes, earning glory to surpass their deeds
- **Warband Culture**: Honor through combat, raid, and conquest
- **Elf & Horse**: The Valenar bond with their Valenar steeds (magically enhanced warhorses) is sacred

**Major Valenar Settlements:**

### Taer Valaestas (The City of Blades)
- **Founded**: -2600 YK
- **Built Upon**: Dhakaan Khor (Dhakaani temple complex)
- **Culture**: Center of Valenar government and martial tradition
- **Temples**:
  - **The Hall of Heroes**: Shrines to legendary Valenar warriors (now converted to honor "You keep what you kill")
  - **The Deepforge**: Repurposed Dhakaani war-forge, now used to craft Valenar steel
  - **Balaur's Throne**: The Six Crowns chamber, where Balaur sits in judgment

### Taer Shantare (The Border Watch)
- **Founded**: 894 YK (during the Last War)
- **Built Upon**: Ja'aram Qua (Dhakaani military academy)
- **Purpose**: Originally a Brelish fortress, claimed by Valenar after the Treaty of Thronehold
- **Current State**: Balaur's forward conquest-bastion, built atop ancient Dhakaani war-training grounds

### Ravar Orioth (The Sea's Edge)
- **Founded**: -1800 YK
- **Built Upon**: Giant titan-stones and Kor'vulath forge
- **Purpose**: Coastal trading port and naval base
- **Current State**: Balaur's naval crucible, where the Forge of Kor'vulath burns again

## Temples & Sacred Sites

### Valenar-Era Temples (Pre-Balaur)

#### The Hall of Heroes (Taer Valaestas)
- **Dedicated To**: Legendary Valenar warriors (Vadallia, Shaeras Vadallia, Belaerion, others)
- **Function**: Shrines where warriors would meditate before battle, seeking to emulate their patron ancestor
- **Current State**: Statues defaced or replaced with trophies. Altars now display kill-counts and conquered weapons

#### The Windspear Shrine (Near Taer Shantare)
- **Dedicated To**: Cavalry and the bond between elf and steed
- **Function**: Sacred ground where Valenar warriors would oath-bind with their mounts
- **Current State**: Converted into Balaur's arena—the bond of horse and rider replaced with trial by combat

#### The Circle of Blades (Ravar Orioth)
- **Dedicated To**: The first Valenar raiders who claimed the coast
- **Function**: Memorial site with carved standing stones bearing the names of the fallen
- **Current State**: Stones now serve as anvils for Balaur's forges, names obscured by soot and hammer-strikes

### Dhakaani Temples (Ancient, Rediscovered)

#### Dhakaan Khor - The Six Crowns (Beneath Taer Valaestas)
- **Dedicated To**: Six legendary Dhakaani Warrior-Kings
- **Original Function**: Coronation site and war-council chamber
- **Valenar Use**: Discovered and partially converted to Valenar shrines
- **Current State**: Balaur's throne room—the six crown-pedestals now hold trophies from his greatest kills

#### Temple of Jhazaal Dhakaan (Beneath Taer Shantare)
- **Dedicated To**: Jhazaal Dhakaan, legendary general who never lost a battle
- **Original Function**: Military shrine where generals would receive blessings before campaigns
- **Current State**: Sealed by Dhakaani before their retreat, unopened for millennia—rumored to contain Jhazaal's war-maps and strategic texts

#### The Vault of Ancestors (Near Keth River)
- **Dedicated To**: The Kech Volaar dirge-singers who preserved goblinoid history
- **Original Function**: Burial site for Dhakaani scholars, filled with funerary texts and preserved records
- **Current State**: Unknown—lost to time and overgrown jungle

## Religious Traditions

### Pre-Balaur Valenar Religion

**Patron Ancestor Worship:**
- Valenar warriors choose a legendary hero to emulate
- They seek glory in battle to honor and surpass their patron's deeds
- No formal priesthood—each warrior maintains their own relationship with their chosen ancestor

**Sacred Practices:**
- **The Blooding**: A warrior's first kill is dedicated to their patron ancestor
- **The Naming**: Upon achieving a legendary deed, a warrior earns the right to take their patron's name
- **The Ride**: Cavalry charges are treated as sacred acts, offerings of speed and violence

### Balaur's Cult of Conquest

**Core Tenets:**
1. **"You Keep What You Kill"** - All possessions, rank, and power are earned through defeating the previous holder
2. **Strength Judges Weakness** - The strong have the right and duty to rule; the weak serve or die
3. **War is Sacred** - Combat is the highest form of expression; peace is stagnation
4. **The Challenge** - Anyone may challenge anyone; to refuse is to forfeit honor and position
5. **Mortals Are Worthy** - Balaur's unique belief: mortals possess qualities dragons lack—curiosity, drive, the urgency of short lives

**Rituals:**
- **The Arena Oath**: New members prove themselves in three arena bouts (Balaur often watches personally)
- **The Taking**: After killing an enemy, the victor claims one item as trophy
- **The Judgment**: Balaur observes trials and judges the worthy—he respects clever tactics as much as raw strength
- **The Brand**: Those who prove themselves receive Balaur's brand—a burn-scar in the shape of a dragon wreathed in adamantine (he applies it personally)
- **The Walk Among Us**: Balaur regularly appears in knight form, training, dueling, and working alongside his followers

**No Formal Temples**: Balaur's followers don't build shrines—they claim existing sites through conquest and convert them to arenas, forges, or war-halls. Balaur prefers functional spaces where mortals can prove themselves.

## Balaur's Philosophy: The Dragon Among Mortals

Unlike his draconic brethren who view mortals as "lesser beings," Balaur sees what others dismiss.

**What Balaur Values in Mortals:**
- **Curiosity**: The drive to explore, to question, to challenge—mortals never accept limits
- **Ambition**: The hunger to become more than they are, to surpass their own legends
- **Drive**: The relentless push forward despite short lifespans, making every moment count
- **Adaptability**: Mortals improvise, innovate, and overcome in ways dragons—bound by age and pride—cannot
- **Worthy Opposition**: A mortal who earns their victory is worth more than a hundred cowering subjects

**Why He Walks Among Them:**

Balaur prefers his elven knight form not out of deception, but because he genuinely enjoys mortal company. He:
- **Duels champions** - Testing himself against mortal skill and cunning
- **Works the forges** - Crafting alongside Valenar smiths, appreciating their artistry
- **Leads from the front** - Fighting beside his warriors, not commanding from a distant throne
- **Respects earned glory** - A mortal who defeats him fairly would claim his throne, and he would accept it

**What Other Wyrms Think:**

To other dragons, Balaur's fascination with mortals is weakness or eccentricity. They see "lesser beings" where he sees potential equals. This makes Balaur an outsider among his own kind—content to rule mortals who strive, rather than dominate those who merely submit.

## Lost Sites & Mysteries

### The Iron Crown Vault
- **Legend**: Hidden treasury where the Dhakaani stored their most powerful artifacts
- **Location**: Unknown, rumored to be somewhere beneath Valenar
- **Contents**: The Iron Crown of Dhakaan, legendary weapons, ancient texts
- **Current Status**: Unfound—Balaur seeks it, believing it would grant legitimacy to his rule

### The Deeproads
- **Nature**: Ancient Dhakaani tunnel network connecting all major sites
- **Status**: Mostly collapsed or flooded
- **Danger**: Sections still open house aberrations left from the daelkyr wars
- **Opportunity**: Could allow covert movement beneath Balaur's territory

### The Last Stand of Vadallia
- **Legend**: The site where the legendary Valenar hero Vadallia made her final stand against overwhelming odds
- **Location**: Somewhere in the wilderness near Taer Shantare
- **Significance**: Said to be blessed ground where Valenar warriors feel no fear
- **Current State**: Lost during Balaur's conquest, pilgrims seek it still

## Cultural Layers

Valenar is a land of **conquest built upon conquest**:

1. **Giant foundations** - Titan-stones, volcanic forges, ancient runes
2. **Dhakaani empire** - Fortresses, temples, sealed vaults, Iron Roads
3. **Valenar elves** - Martial culture, ancestor worship, cavalry tradition
4. **Balaur's conquest** - "You keep what you kill," meritocracy of violence, war-cult

Each layer remains visible—giant stones still stand, Dhakaani vaults still hide beneath cities, Valenar shrines are converted but not destroyed, and Balaur's rule is written in brands and blood.
