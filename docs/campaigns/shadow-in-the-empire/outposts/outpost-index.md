# Q'barra Outposts & Survival Camps

These outposts dot the wilds of Q'barra, offering **supplies, rumors, and colorful NPCs** for a level 13 party navigating the jungles. They are tenuous footholds, not safe havens, each full of hints about nearby destinations.

---

## Outpost Locations

### [The Salted Helm](the-salted-helm.md) *(near Castle Garodya)*
**Half-sunken dockside tavern turned camp**  
Proprietor: Captain Branka, one-eyed dwarf privateer  
Specializes in: Marine supplies, storm warnings, bay navigation

### [The Brass Anvil](the-brass-anvil.md) *(fringe of Newthrone)*
**Ruined smithy rebuilt into barracks and trading post**  
Proprietor: Tikka, grizzled gnome smith  
Specializes in: Weapons, tools, mercenary contacts

### [Shardspike Camp](shardspike-camp.md) *(approach to Pitchwall)*
**Ramshackle mining camp, now smuggler outpost**  
Proprietor: Overseer Vorn, slick half-elf with chained spinosaurus  
Specializes in: Dragonshards, mining gear, black market goods

### [The Silver Fang Lodge](the-silver-fang-lodge.md) *(near Duskwing territory)*
**Dragonborn hunter's lodge and waystation**  
Proprietor: Kethra Flamehide, scarred dragonborn huntress  
Specializes in: Survival gear, dragonborn customs, hunting supplies

### [Stonecoil Refuge](stonecoil-refuge.md) *(foothills of the Stormhorns)*
**Fortified mountain camp carved into canyon wall**  
Proprietor: Rurik Stonecoil, paranoid dwarf mercenary  
Specializes in: Mountain gear, defensive supplies, mercenary information

### [Emberwatch Post](emberwatch-post.md) *(near Flamebrow lands)*
**Watchtower rebuilt from old colonial stone**  
Proprietor: Sister Malra, human priestess of draconic spirits  
Specializes in: Fire-resistant gear, spiritual guidance, cult intelligence

### [Wyrmwatch Tower](wyrmwatch-tower.md) *(deep in the Q'barran wilds)*
**Ancient colonial watchtower complex and military outpost**  
Proprietor: Captain Dareth Ironward, veteran Brelish ranger  
Specializes in: Dragon surveillance, military equipment, tactical intelligence

## Usage Guidelines

### **For GMs:**
- Outposts provide **gear, food, rumors, and social color**, not true safety
- NPCs should feel **strange and weathered**, marked by survival in wyrm-haunted lands
- Use rumors to hint at **upcoming themes** (wyrm cults, trials, pacts, political fractures)
- Camps give parties chances to resupply and roleplay while seeding future storylines

### **Resupply Opportunities:**
- **Basic Supplies:** Available at all outposts (food, water, basic equipment)
- **Specialized Gear:** Each outpost has unique inventory reflecting local needs
- **Information Trading:** Rumors and local knowledge as valuable as physical goods
- **Services:** Equipment repair, medical aid, guide services (for a price)

### **Social Dynamics:**
- Outpost inhabitants are **survivors first**, traders second
- Trust must be earned through demonstration of capability and respect
- Payment expected upfront - credit worthless in collapsed economy
- Information flows both ways - party's presence becomes news quickly
