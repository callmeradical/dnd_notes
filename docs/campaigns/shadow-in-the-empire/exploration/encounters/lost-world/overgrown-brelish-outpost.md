# Overgrown Brelish Outpost

## Outpost Survivor
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class** 12 (Leather Armor)  
**Hit Points** 27 (5d8+5)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13(+1) | 14(+2) | 12(+1) | 10(+0) | 15(+2) | 8(-1) |

**Skills** Nature +4, Perception +4, Stealth +4, Survival +6  
**Senses** passive Perception 14  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

**Shortsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage.

**Shortbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage.

**Improvised Trap (1/Day).** The survivor can quickly set a snare or pit trap. Creatures moving through a 5-foot square must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) damage and be restrained (snare) or knocked prone (pit).

---

### Encounter Information

**Environment:** Abandoned colonial outpost reclaimed by jungle growth  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random lost world discovery (#1 on d8)  
**Discovery Method:** DC 13 Survival or Investigation to notice artificial structures among vegetation

### Outpost Description

**Physical Structure**
- Stone foundation and partially collapsed walls overgrown with vines
- Wooden palisade rotted and fallen in most sections
- Central keep partially intact but roof caved in from tree growth
- Wells, storage buildings, and barracks identifiable by foundation remnants

**Current State**
- Jungle has reclaimed most of the compound
- Large trees grow through building floors and walls
- Useful materials scattered among ruins: tools, weapons, furniture
- Defensive positions still offer tactical advantages despite decay

### Roleplay Information

**Survivor Personality**
- Last remaining member of the outpost garrison
- Half-mad from isolation but possesses valuable survival knowledge
- Suspicious of newcomers but desperate for human contact

**Mannerisms**
- Talks to imaginary garrison members from memory
- Maintains military routines and protocols despite solitude
- Shows visitors around ruins as if buildings were still intact

**Voice/Dialogue**

**Initial Contact:**
> "Who goes there? State your business at Fort Valor! Oh... you're real, aren't you? Not like the others. The captain said no one would come, but I kept the watch. Always kept the watch."

**When Discussing the Outpost:**
> "Fine garrison we had here. Forty good soldiers, until the sickness came. Then the attacks. Now it's just me and the jungle, but I maintain the perimeter. Someone has to."

**When Asked About Survival:**
> "You learn things out here. Which plants heal, which kill. Where the clean water flows. The jungle teaches you, if you listen. Most don't listen."

### Knowledge

**What the Survivor Knows**
- Detailed knowledge of local flora and fauna
- Location of clean water sources and safe camping spots
- Hazardous areas to avoid: unstable ruins, poisonous plants, predator territories
- Recent changes in local wildlife behavior and seasonal patterns

**What Happened Here**
- Outpost established during early colonial expansion
- Garrison succumbed to tropical disease and coordinated monster attacks
- Supply lines cut, reinforcements never arrived
- Survivor remained out of duty, madness, and inability to navigate alone

**Useful Resources**
- Hidden supply caches containing preserved food and medicine
- Weapons and equipment stored in sealed containers
- Maps showing colonial supply routes and other outpost locations
- Personal effects and documents revealing colonial history

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine survivor's current mental state: lucid day vs. confused episode
- Note outpost's original size and garrison strength for exploration context
- Consider season and weather: ruins more dangerous during rains
- Roll for recent events: predator attacks, illness, or supply discoveries

**Opening Scene Options**

**Ruins Discovery:**
> *Ancient stonework emerges from the jungle tangle—too regular to be natural. As you clear away vines and debris, the outline of a colonial fort becomes visible. Wooden palisades have rotted away, but stone foundations mark where buildings once stood. Then you see movement among the ruins—a figure in patched colonial uniform watching you with suspicious eyes.*

**Survivor Encounter:**
> *A man in ragged military garb steps from concealment among the overgrown ruins. His uniform bears the faded colors of Breland, and his eyes hold the particular intensity of someone who has been alone too long. "Halt! This is Fort Valor, outpost of His Majesty's colonial forces. State your business here."*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Initial Contact (Social Primary)**
- Survivor challenges party according to military protocols
- May not immediately recognize that years have passed
- Tests party's intentions and trustworthiness through questioning
- **Medicine Check (DC 12):** Recognize signs of prolonged isolation and stress
- **History Check (DC 13):** Know about colonial outposts and their typical fate
- **Persuasion Check (DC 14):** Gain survivor's trust through patient conversation

**Phase 2: Reality and Revelation**
- Survivor gradually accepts that outpost is abandoned and years have passed
- Shares knowledge about local area and survival techniques
- May break down emotionally or become defensive about his vigil
- **Insight Check (DC 11):** Understand survivor's psychological fragility
- **Nature Check (DC 12):** Appreciate value of survivor's wilderness knowledge
- **Investigation Check (DC 15):** Locate hidden supplies and equipment caches

**Phase 3: Alliance, Departure, or Care**
- Successful interaction may lead to survivor guiding party or sharing resources
- Failed approach might result in survivor's complete breakdown or aggression
- Party may need to decide survivor's fate: rescue, leave supplies, or force departure

**Outpost Exploration**

**Salvageable Resources**
- **Armory Ruins:** 2d4 weapons in fair condition, some ammunition
- **Supply Cache:** Preserved rations, medical supplies, basic tools
- **Officer's Quarters:** Maps, documents, personal effects worth 50-200 gp
- **Well:** Clean water source, possibly the only reliable one in area

**Hidden Areas**
- **Investigation Check (DC 14):** Find survivor's secret supply stashes
- **Investigation Check (DC 16):** Locate sealed storage rooms in basement levels
- **Survival Check (DC 12):** Identify survivor's hunting blinds and safe sleeping areas

**Environmental Hazards**
- **Unstable Structures:** Dexterity saves to avoid falling debris
- **Poisonous Plants:** Constitution saves against toxic exposure
- **Hidden Predators:** Animals that hunt around ruins
- **Disease Risk:** Contaminated areas requiring Constitution saves

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Showing respect for military service and sacrifice
- Offering food, medicine, or basic comfort items
- Listening patiently to survivor's stories and memories
- Demonstrating genuine concern for survivor's wellbeing

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Dismissing survivor as mad or irrelevant
- Criticizing military decisions or colonial policies
- Rushing interaction or showing impatience
- Taking resources without asking or offering compensation

**Survivor's Mental State Variations**
- **Lucid Periods:** Coherent, helpful, realistic about situation
- **Memory Episodes:** Relives past events, talks to imaginary comrades
- **Paranoid Phases:** Suspicious, defensive, may attack if threatened
- **Depressive Cycles:** Withdrawn, fatalistic, reveals dangerous information

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Survivor shares all knowledge including secret caches and safe routes
- Offers to guide party through dangerous local terrain
- Provides ongoing wilderness survival training and consultation
- May choose to leave ruins and return to civilization with party

**Success**
- Basic cooperation and information sharing
- Access to survivor's resources in exchange for supplies or company
- Neutral relations allowing future visits and trade

**Failure**
- Survivor becomes suspicious and defensive about resources
- Limited help provided, may follow party without invitation
- Possible breakdown requiring party intervention

**Critical Failure**
- Complete psychological breakdown or aggressive defense of territory
- Survivor attempts to drive party away or trap them in ruins
- False information provided about regional hazards

**Long-term Considerations**

**If Survivor Rescued**
- Valuable wilderness guide with unmatched local knowledge
- Potential ongoing NPC ally with survival expertise
- May recover mentally with proper care and social contact
- Could establish new settlement or trading post at ruins

**If Survivor Left**
- Outpost remains as potential base of operations
- Survivor continues maintaining watch and gathering resources
- May become recurring NPC providing area information
- Resources slowly replenish through survivor's foraging and crafting

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Survivor witnessed events relevant to party's quest
- Other colonial outposts in similar state create exploration opportunities
- Documents found reveal larger colonial conspiracy or treasure location
- Survivor's rescue becomes ongoing subplot with political implications

**Base of Operations Potential**
- Defensible position with good sight lines
- Reliable water source and basic shelter
- Central location for regional exploration
- Existing supply caches and storage facilities

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Survivor is stable and immediately helpful
- **Harder:** Multiple survivors with competing psychological states
- **Environmental:** Ruins actively threatened by predators or environmental hazards
- **Political:** Colonial authorities still interested in outpost for strategic reasons

**Treasure and Resources**
- 1d4 × 50 gp in colonial-era coins and small valuables
- Military equipment worth 100-300 gp in working condition
- Maps and documents providing regional intelligence
- Ongoing access to survivor's wilderness expertise and local knowledge
