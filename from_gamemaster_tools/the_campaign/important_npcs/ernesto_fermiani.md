## Highlights

Bureaucrat and strategist, Ernesto is a company man through and through. he has
worked for Alunno since he finished at the university

## Description

* Blonde hair like the color of sand
* Green eyes
* Tan skin
* Fine breastplate sporting the Alunno logo teepo
* Puffy shirt and spadded leg armor and boots that stop just below the knee

A member of house Zelante, possibly a pilot, maverick of sorts.
