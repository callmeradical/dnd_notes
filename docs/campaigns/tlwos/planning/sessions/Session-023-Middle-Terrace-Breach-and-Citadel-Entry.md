# Session 023: Middle Terrace Breach (Chris Perkins Method)

## Hook / Recap

The party returned from the undercity after restoring the One Tree and resurfaced in Takashima's middle terrace. They just survived a basilisk fight and can see more hostile creatures roaming the streets. Takashima is in a Shadow Register state (partial Shadowfell overlay), with city behavior and shadow flow bending toward the palace. The immediate objective is to survive the terrace pressure and secure entry to the citadel before the prince's condition worsens.

## Player Characters (Single Line Each)

- **Aoshi** - Goal: defend honor through action; Background: disciplined warrior in conflict with corrupted authority.
- **Fox** - Goal: gain leverage without being controlled; Background: stealth-focused operator tied to hidden networks.
- **Shigeo** - Goal: protect people and keep family standing; Background: public-facing strongman with local social weight.
- **Thornbringer** - Goal: stop the spiritual/ecological imbalance; Background: primal outsider sensitive to nature corruption.
- **Kanji** - Goal: expose the truth behind coordinated chaos; Background: cunning investigator with infiltration instincts.
- **Yasei** - Goal: interpret omens and prevent spiritual collapse; Background: spirit-attuned wanderer carrying a cursed snowglobe thread.

## Locations (5)

- **Middle Terrace Arterial** - Collapsed market lanes, prayer ropes snapping, moving shadows and roaming monsters.
- **Lift Gate Checkpoint** - Official route upward; watch bureaucracy still functioning but unnaturally synchronized.
- **Shrine Maintenance Ascent** - Old pilgrim path that partially resists the overlay if proper rites are observed.
- **Waterworks Service Route** - Damp mechanical bypass with broken lifts, flooded channels, and unstable access ladders.
- **Citadel Threshold Court** - Palace approach where protocol, geometry, and shadow pressure become overtly supernatural.

## NPCs (6)

- **Kaoru Sugimoto (Contact)** - Calm fixer; Trait: precise; Bond: Hidden Lotus survival; Ideal: controlled resistance; Flaw: withholds until leverage exists; Motivation: get the party inside without exposing the network.
- **Takeo Ren (Gate Captain)** - Exhausted but dutiful officer; Trait: procedural; Bond: city watch oath; Ideal: order protects lives; Flaw: vulnerable to institutional manipulation; Motivation: keep movement lawful while sensing something is deeply wrong.
- **Kazuki Takemura (Patron-Ally)** - Twice-cursed demon slayer; Trait: severe; Bond: cursed blade burden; Ideal: duty over comfort; Flaw: fatalistic isolation; Motivation: prevent palace corruption from becoming irreversible.
- **Palace Warden Masanobu (Antagonistic Functionary)** - Polite compliance enforcer; Trait: courteous; Bond: palace office; Ideal: stability through obedience; Flaw: no moral flexibility; Motivation: route all entrants into controllable terms.
- **Hidden Lotus Runner Emi (Field Asset)** - Fast messenger and observer; Trait: alert; Bond: Kaoru's chain; Ideal: truth before rumor; Flaw: overextends under pressure; Motivation: deliver route intel before it is compromised.
- **Street Elder Hina (Civilian Anchor)** - Community elder coordinating shelter; Trait: steady; Bond: neighborhood shrine; Ideal: mutual duty; Flaw: refuses evacuation; Motivation: keep civilians alive long enough for a safe corridor.

## Encounters (6 Possible)

- **1) Monster Surge at the Arterial (Combat):** roaming pack hits a civilian knot; success opens movement and goodwill.
- **2) Contradictory Orders at Lift Gate (Social):** decode lawful-but-poisoned access terms; success reduces Oni attention.
- **3) Shrine Path Purification (Exploration + Skill Challenge):** perform rite while navigating unstable terrain; success lowers shadow pressure.
- **4) Waterworks Drop-Lift (Trap):** collapsing chain-lift and flood gate release; disarm or improvise escape route.
- **5) Warden Ledger Cipher (Puzzle):** interpret route marks and pass language to identify the least compromised entry vector.
- **6) Citadel Threshold Choice (Social/Combat Pivot):** accept a formal cost, force passage, or bypass into a harder fight; outcome determines Session 024 posture.
