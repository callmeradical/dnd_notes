# Lizardfolk Hunting Party

## Lizardfolk Scout (Leader)
*Medium humanoid (lizardfolk), neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (Natural Armor, Shield)  
**Hit Points** 22 (4d8+4)  
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15(+2) | 10(+0) | 13(+1) | 7(-2) | 12(+1) | 7(-2) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4, Survival +5  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Draconic  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Hold Breath.** The lizardfolk can hold its breath for 15 minutes.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The lizardfolk makes two melee attacks, each one with a different weapon.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage.

**Heavy Club.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6+2) bludgeoning damage.

**Javelin.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage.

**Spiked Shield.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4+2) piercing damage.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 Scout + 2d4 Lizardfolk  
**Environment:** Swamps, riverbanks, jungle waterways  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random hostile faction encounter (#1 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Practical and survival-focused, views all creatures as potential food or threat
- Speaks in short, clipped sentences when using Common

**Ideals**

- Survival of the Tribe: All actions serve the greater good of their clan
- Natural Order: Believes in eating or being eaten, predator and prey

**Bonds**

- Fiercely loyal to their tribe and hunting pack
- Knows every waterway and safe crossing for miles around their territory

**Flaws**

- Suspicious of all outsiders and air-breathers
- Quick to assume hostile intent from strangers

**Mannerisms**

- Constantly scents the air with forked tongue movements
- Eyes track movement like a predator watching prey
- Speaks Draconic in hissing, guttural tones to pack members

**Voice/Dialogue**

- **Common:** "You cross our waters. You pay toll or become food."  
- **Draconic:** *Hissing commands to surround and flank intruders*  
- **Threat Display:** Bares teeth, puffs throat, raises spear menacingly  

### Tactics

**Combat Approach**

- Uses aquatic advantage - leads intruders toward water
- Scout coordinates pack using Draconic commands
- Attempts to surround enemies using superior numbers
- Uses javelins from range before closing to melee
- Retreats underwater if battle goes poorly

**Preferred Strategies**

- Ambush from reedy shallows or muddy riverbanks  
- Drive enemies into deeper water where swimming is required
- Use natural camouflage to set up crossfire with javelins
- Focus fire on heavily armored opponents first

**Retreat Conditions**

- Flees when reduced to half numbers or when scout falls
- Always retreats toward tribal territory to warn others
- May offer information or safe passage if clearly outmatched

### Knowledge

**What They Know**

- All waterways, safe crossings, and seasonal flood patterns
- Locations of other predators and dangerous areas
- Recent territorial changes due to wyrm activity
- Status of nearby Dragonborn clans (rivals or allies)

**Trade Possibilities**

- Will trade safe passage for weapons, especially metal ones
- Information about water routes in exchange for preserved foods
- May serve as guides if treated with respect and paid well

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Roll 2d4 for hunting party size (3-8 lizardfolk plus scout leader)
- Determine encounter location: river crossing, muddy trail, swamp edge
- Consider weather: rain makes lizardfolk more confident, drought makes them desperate
- Check if party has been respectful to nature - affects initial reaction

**Opening Scene Options**

**River Crossing Encounter:**
> *As you approach the murky water's edge, shapes emerge from the reedy shallows. Scaled humanoids with crude spears and shields, their reptilian eyes tracking your every movement. The largest one, bearing tribal scars across his snout, steps forward and speaks in broken Common: "You cross our waters. You pay toll or become food."*

**Trail Ambush:**
> *Mud squelches under your feet as you navigate the boggy path. Suddenly, the "logs" in the water around you shift and rise—lizardfolk warriors, camouflaged and ready. Javelins glint in the humid air as they surround your group.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Initial Contact (Social)**
- Scout assesses party threat level and potential value
- Lizardfolk communicate in Draconic (allow Draconic speakers to understand)
- Demand toll, tribute, or explanation for trespassing
- **Intimidation Check (DC 14):** Aggressive posturing might cow smaller groups
- **Persuasion Check (DC 12):** Respectful approach opens negotiation
- **Deception Check (DC 15):** Claiming tribal connections or sacred mission

**Phase 2: Negotiation**
- Lizardfolk respect strength but also pragmatism
- They want: metal weapons, preserved food, shiny objects, information
- They offer: safe passage, water route knowledge, guidance, trade goods
- **Insight Check (DC 11):** Recognize they're more cautious than aggressive
- **Nature Check (DC 13):** Understand their territorial but not malicious behavior

**Phase 3: Combat (If Negotiation Fails)**
- Lizardfolk attempt to drive party toward water
- Use javelin volleys before closing to melee
- Scout coordinates using Draconic commands
- Fighting retreat toward tribal territory if losing

**Social Interaction Guidelines**

**Positive Modifiers (+2 to social rolls)**
- Speaking Draconic (shows cultural respect)
- Offering food or metal weapons
- Showing previous tribal tokens or safe passage items
- Demonstrating nature magic (druid/ranger abilities)

**Negative Modifiers (-2 to social rolls)**
- Aggressive posturing without backing it up
- Harming local wildlife needlessly
- Wearing obvious wealth while claiming poverty
- Attempting to lie about easily verifiable facts

**Key Cultural Notes**
- Lizardfolk value practicality over ceremony
- They respect those who respect the natural order
- Metal weapons are precious - they use bone and stone tools
- They view most "warm-bloods" as soft but potentially useful

**Negotiation Outcomes**

**Great Success (Beat DC by 5+)**
- Safe passage through all tribal territory
- Lizardfolk guide for 1 day
- Warning about specific dangers ahead
- Possible future alliance

**Success**
- Safe passage for this encounter
- Basic information about area hazards
- Neutral reputation with tribe

**Failure**
- Must pay higher toll or face combat
- Lizardfolk remain suspicious of future encounters

**Critical Failure**
- Immediate combat
- Word spreads to other hunting parties about hostile outsiders

**Combat Tactics Deep Dive**

**Environmental Advantages**
- Lizardfolk excel in water - use swimming speed advantage
- Know location of every muddy patch and hidden pool
- Can hold breath for extended periods during underwater combat
- Use natural camouflage among reeds and water plants

**Group Coordination**
- Scout uses Survival skill to direct party into difficult terrain
- Hunters coordinate javelin volleys on scout's signal
- Wounded lizardfolk retreat toward water for healing advantage
- Pack mentality: fight harder when scout is threatened

**Skill Challenges During Combat**
- **Athletics (DC 12):** Navigate muddy, unstable ground
- **Perception (DC 14):** Track lizardfolk movement in murky water
- **Survival (DC 13):** Anticipate lizardfolk tactical movements
- **Acrobatics (DC 11):** Maintain footing on slippery surfaces

**Non-Combat Resolutions**

**Mutual Benefit Scenarios**
- Party needs water route information, lizardfolk need protection from larger predator
- Exchange of goods: party's metal for lizardfolk's local knowledge
- Hiring lizardfolk as guides through dangerous swampland
- Joint defense against common threat (rival tribe, dragon cult, etc.)

**Aftermath Considerations**

**If Combat Occurred**
- Noise may attract other predators or tribal reinforcements
- Lizardfolk survivors will warn their tribe about dangerous outsiders
- Area becomes hostile to party for future encounters
- Other hunting parties may seek revenge

**If Resolved Peacefully**
- Gain tribal token providing safe passage
- Information about water routes saves 1d4 days travel time
- Possible future trading opportunities
- Positive reputation may help with other lizardfolk encounters

**Story Integration**
- Hunting party may have encountered recent dangers (dragon sightings, rival tribes)
- Scout may carry tribal messages or warnings about regional threats
- Successful interaction opens possibilities for larger tribal involvement
- Failed interaction creates ongoing antagonist subplot

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce numbers, make them more willing to negotiate
- **Harder:** Add tribal shaman with spell support, or increase numbers
- **Environmental:** Encounter during storm, at night, or in deep swamp
