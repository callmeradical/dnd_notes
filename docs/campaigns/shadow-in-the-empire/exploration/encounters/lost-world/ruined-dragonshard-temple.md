# Ruined Dragonshard Temple

## Siberys Shard Guardian
*Large construct, neutral*

**Armor Class** 17 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 114 (12d10+48)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 20(+5) | 8(-1) | 19(+4) | 3(-4) | 11(+0) | 1(-5) |

**Damage Immunities** poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** understands Draconic but can't speak  
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Features

**Immutable Form.** The guardian is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

**Magic Resistance.** The guardian has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Dragonshard Powered.** While within 60 feet of a large dragonshard formation, the guardian regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The guardian makes two slam attacks.

**Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning damage.

**Shard Blast (Recharge 5-6).** The guardian projects energy from dragonshard formations within 60 feet. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Slow (1/Day).** The guardian casts *slow* (save DC 13) as a 3rd-level spell, targeting up to 6 creatures within 60 feet.

---

### Encounter Information

**Environment:** Ancient temple with exposed dragonshard veins in walls and floor  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random lost world discovery (#5 on d8)  
**Discovery Method:** DC 16 Arcana or Investigation to notice magical emanations from ruins

### Temple Description

**Physical Structure**
- Partially collapsed stone temple with draconic architectural elements
- Walls and floor shot through with glowing dragonshard veins
- Central altar contains massive Siberys dragonshard formation
- Entrance partially blocked by fallen stones and jungle growth

**Magical Properties**
- **Detect Magic** reveals overwhelming abjuration and divination auras
- **Identify** on dragonshards shows they're connected to ancient warding spells
- **Arcana DC 18:** Recognize temple as pre-Galifar draconic construction
- Ambient magic makes spellcasting more powerful but unstable

### Roleplay Information

**Guardian Behavior**
- Activates only when someone approaches within 30 feet of central altar
- Follows simple programming: protect temple and dragonshard formations
- Cannot be reasoned with but responds to specific draconic commands

**Temple Atmosphere**
- Constant low humming from active dragonshard formations
- Multicolored light refracts through crystal formations creating prismatic effects
- Air shimmers with visible magical energy
- Ancient draconic runes pulse with soft light on remaining walls

**Environmental Effects**
- **Enhanced Magic:** Spell damage dice increased by +1 per die within temple
- **Wild Magic:** Critical failures on spell attacks trigger wild magic surges
- **Healing Crystals:** Dragonshard formations can be used to power healing (1/day per formation)
- **Scrying Block:** Temple interior cannot be viewed by divination magic from outside

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine temple's current state: mostly intact vs. heavily ruined
- Note how many dragonshard formations are still active (2d4+2)
- Consider whether other creatures have made lairs in the ruins
- Check party's knowledge of draconic culture and dragonshard properties

**Opening Scene Options**

**Magical Discovery:**
> *Through the jungle canopy ahead, an unnatural glow emanates from ancient stone ruins. As you push through the vegetation, a magnificent sight unfolds—a temple whose walls pulse with veins of crystalline light. Dragonshard formations jut from the structure like frozen lightning, their radiance casting rainbow patterns through the humid air.*

**Guardian Awakening:**
> *Your footsteps echo in the ruined temple as you approach the central altar. The massive Siberys dragonshard formation atop it pulses more brightly with each step. Suddenly, stone grinds against stone as an ancient construct unfolds from what you had taken for a collapsed pillar. Glowing crystal eyes fix upon you with implacable purpose.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: Temple Exploration**
- Party investigates ruins and discovers magical properties
- Dragonshard formations can be studied or harvested carefully
- Guardian remains dormant until central altar area is approached
- **Arcana Check (DC 15):** Understand dragonshard formation purposes and powers
- **Investigation Check (DC 13):** Find hidden chambers or treasure caches
- **History Check (DC 16):** Recognize temple's age and cultural significance

**Phase 2: Guardian Activation**
- Guardian activates when anyone approaches within 30 feet of central altar
- Uses Shard Blast and Slow abilities to control battlefield
- Regenerates health while near dragonshard formations
- **Initiative:** Guardian acts first if it surprises party
- **Draconic Commands:** Speaking Draconic might influence guardian behavior

**Phase 3: Resolution**
- Guardian fights until destroyed or given proper stand-down command
- Successful deactivation allows peaceful exploration and harvesting
- Destroyed guardian cannot be reactivated without major magical ritual

**Combat Tactics Deep Dive**

**Dragonshard Synergy**
- Guardian positions itself near largest formations for regeneration
- Uses Shard Blast ability by channeling energy through crystals
- Attempts to prevent party from damaging or removing dragonshard formations
- Becomes less effective if formations are destroyed (no regeneration)

**Battlefield Control**
- Uses Slow spell to limit party mobility and action economy
- Shard Blast forces positioning decisions and spreads out party
- Large size allows it to block access to altar and most valuable formations
- Magic resistance makes it difficult to disable with spells

**Environmental Tactics**
- **Enhanced Magic:** Party spellcasters deal increased damage but risk wild magic
- **Crystal Cover:** Dragonshard formations provide partial cover but can be damaged
- **Unstable Magic:** Critical spell failures may cause unexpected effects
- **Healing Opportunities:** Party can use crystal formations for emergency healing

**Deactivation Methods**

**Draconic Command Words**
- **Arcana Check (DC 20):** Deduce proper command sequence from temple inscriptions
- **History Check (DC 18):** Recall ancient draconic guardian protocols
- Speaking fluent Draconic allows attempt at command (Persuasion DC 16)

**Magical Bypass**
- **Dispel Magic** at 5th level or higher can temporarily shut down guardian
- **Command** spell might work if cast in Draconic (DC 15 Charisma save)
- Destroying central dragonshard formation deactivates guardian permanently

**Treasure and Harvesting**

**Dragonshard Collection**
- **Investigation Check (DC 12):** Locate harvestable shard formations
- **Arcana Check (DC 15):** Extract shards without damaging magical properties
- **Thieves' Tools Check (DC 14):** Remove shards from stone settings safely
- Total value: 2d4 × 500 gp in various dragonshard types

**Temple Treasures**
- Hidden chamber behind altar: 1d4 × 100 gp in ancient coins
- Ritual components worth 200-600 gp to appropriate buyers
- Draconic texts and scrolls containing historical information
- 1d3 magical items powered by dragonshard technology

**Knowledge Rewards**
- Ancient maps showing dragonshard deposit locations
- Historical information about pre-human draconic civilizations
- Ritual instructions for creating dragonshard-powered magical items
- Warnings about ancient threats still sealed in the region

**Long-term Consequences**

**If Temple Preserved**
- Can serve as base of operations with magical benefits
- Attracts scholars and treasure hunters seeking dragonshard access
- May become pilgrimage site for those studying draconic magic
- Provides ongoing magical resources for crafting and research

**If Temple Stripped**
- Loss of protective wards may release bound entities
- Other guardians in region become more aggressive
- Environmental magical balance disrupted, affecting local creatures
- Reputation among dragon-allied factions suffers

**Story Integration Hooks**
- Temple contains prophecy or map relevant to party's quest
- Dragonshard formations are keys to larger magical working
- Guardian contains memories of ancient draconic civilization
- Temple's destruction or preservation affects regional magical balance

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Reduce guardian's regeneration, fewer active formations
- **Harder:** Multiple guardians or additional magical hazards
- **Environmental:** Temple partially flooded or structurally unstable
- **Social:** Local dragonborn or kobold tribes consider temple sacred
