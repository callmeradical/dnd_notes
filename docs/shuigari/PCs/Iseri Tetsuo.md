# Iseri Tetsuo

## Player Information
**Player:** Dane  
**Introduced:** Arc 2, Road to Takashima

## Background

### Early Life
Iseri Tetsuo grew up in a modest carpenter's home, keenly aware of the injustice surrounding him. His parents never spoke openly about their struggles, but young Tetsuo saw how heavy-handed taxes and underpaying commissions from their leaders kept honest craftspeople from ever getting ahead.

What started as childhood pranks on tax collectors and corrupt officials became something more. Local business owners, grateful for the small rebellions, would reward him with trinkets and snacks. This validation planted a seed - the understanding that he could make a difference, however small, against the system that crushed people like his parents.

### The Descent into Shadow
As Tetsuo grew older, pranks gave way to training. He developed martial skills with a singular purpose: to make a bigger impact against corruption and exploitation. The boy who once loosened wagon wheels evolved into a young man who could do real damage to those he saw as oppressors.

At 19, everything changed. Tetsuo lured a drunken collections officer into the wilderness for what he intended as another lesson. The confrontation turned physical. It ended with Tetsuo standing over his first kill.

### Life in the Shadows
After that night, there was no going back. Tetsuo retreated deeper into the shadows, working from darkness to avoid capture. His crusade against corrupt officials continued, but now the stakes were life and death - both for his targets and for himself.
