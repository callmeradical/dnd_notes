# Military Structure of Shuigari

War looms over Shuigari. The Shogunate, provincial armies, and local militias all respond differently to the Obsidian Circle’s threat. Use this overview to ground political manoeuvring, mass-combat encounters, or requests for aid.

## Shogunate High Command
- **Shogun’s Legion:** Elite troops stationed in Hikosan. Composed of battle-hardened samurai and onmyōji. Deploys only when the Shogun declares a formal campaign.
- **Council of Blades:** Strategic body made up of daimyo envoys. Currently gridlocked by debates over succession and Circle infiltration.
- **Imperial Heralds:** Messengers capable of invoking martial law in prefectures. Protecting or intercepting them can shift control of a region.

## Daimyo Armies
Each prefecture maintains its own forces. Loyalty varies based on politics and resource strain.
- **Takashima Vanguard:** Mountain-trained infantry, Soulsteel-armoured shock troops, and refugee auxiliaries. Suffering shortages due to Circle sabotage.
- **Harado River Guard:** Naval patrols and riverboat rangers securing trade routes. Often clash with the Crimson Maw.
- **Hikosan Magistrate Guard:** Urban peacekeepers enforcing Shogunate law; efficient but stretched thin by court intrigue.

## Local Militias & Auxiliaries
- **Village Ashigaru:** Peasant levies defending shrines and farmlands. Motivated by survival; morale improves when supported by PCs.
- **Okugōri Scouts:** Deep Province skirmishers who excel in guerrilla warfare. Rarely cooperate without significant bargaining.
- **Temple Sentinels:** Shrine guardians trained in defensive rituals. Provide buffs or healing if their sanctums remain intact.

## Samurai Culture & Expectations
- Samurai serve under daimyo but increasingly question oaths as power vacuums appear. Honour duels, hostage exchanges, and political marriages remain tools of war. PCs with samurai ties may face pressure to choose sides or enforce questionable orders.

## Campaign Applications
- **Reinforcement Tracks:** Earning favour with a faction grants access to extra troops or defensive fortifications during major battles.
- **Espionage Hooks:** Circle spies pose as heralds or Quartermasters; unmasking them prevents supply-route collapse.
- **Mass-Combat Scenes:** Use faction alliances to justify abstracted mass combat while PCs handle strike missions, ritual defence, or assassination.

Understanding the hierarchy helps explain why aid is scarce, why certain missions are deniable, and how political leverage can turn the tide without every skirmish becoming a pitched battle.
