## Session 005: The Coming War

The party heard sounds coming from the tunnels beyond as loud booms shook the
ruins. They were cut off from Agazio in the ruins as a boulder blocked the
path. After wards shadows, undead creatures of some sort attacked the party
sapping their strength. A number of the party had fallen unconcious by
Taklinn's holy light shone through and ended the assault.

After shattering the boulder, Dr. Eins gave the party a way forward, arriving
in a lab, some sort of observatory.

Our heroes then went upward and into a manor house at the top of the hill.
Airships flew by as it reported the messages of Julia Marivaldi. A great
battle was fought, with seemingly little resistance.

* After arriving back at the temple, Agazio seemed to have a mortal wound
inflicted by an aether crystal. He was fine with being turned in. 
* Takklin went out and flagged down one of the scouting ships that was hovering above
the city.
* A man named Mazzio paid for turning in the dragonborn.
* Taklinn volunteered information about a relic the queen has been searching for.
* The party boards the airship, only to be apparently taken hostage.


## Session 004: Beneath Invernale

The party awoke in the temple to find that things were definitely amiss in the
district. As airships floated overhead spotlights poured down from above. A
manhunt was underway. Finding another way out of the temple and hopefully out
Invernale, the party decided to follow the dragonborn into the old passageways
beneath the temple. Finding a fortress of sorts beneath ground. The dragonborn
list his footing and nearly plunged to his death.  Venturing further in you
found old battlements, undead, and kobolds. The kobolds seemed to be looking
for something. Capturing one of them things got violent when you all attempted
to talk to the leader. Killing your captive, they turned their weapons on you.

After dispersing the kobolds you looked at the nearby table, gathering quite a
few relics, and even one that seems to match the description of the relic you
were looking for. 

Dr. Eli looked ahead to see where there might be another way out. Discovering
some kobold prisoners. He pulled out some of the red gold and it seemed to
drive them into a frenzy. After shoving the coins down the throats of the
kobolds, they were placated and eventually began decomposing, with their corpse
turning into aether crystals.

### Events

- Airships above Invernale
- Agazio almost died
- Discovered the ruins of a fortress beneath Invernale
- Remains of a long forgotten battle inside the fortress
- Fought a kobold taskmaster and his goons
- Found that some of the kobolds seemed to be captives

## Session 003: Invernale (Wrafton's Inn)

The party started in Wrafton's Inn located off the market square of the
district of Invernale. A fight broke out between a small group of freelance
bounty hunters and a Dragonborn. The Dragonborn cut down one of the men, and
within seconds, the door to the inn swung open. Flanked by small hooded
figures, a man named Kalarel entered and brought about a presence of fear with
him. Another of the freelancers attempted to do harm, either to Kalarel or the
Paladin, and instead ended up killing himself.

After a small exchange of words and coins, Kalarel and his minions made their
exit from Wrafton's bringing the bodies of the dead with them. The Dragonborn
followed. Takklin had taken to the streets himself to chase him down, and with
the help of Lakelyn, the party successfully tracked the Dragonborn taking
refuge at a nearby temple.

There the party met Agazio Agosta, the Dragonborn paladin, and Sister Linora of
The Temple of She with Authority over Life and Death.

Meanwhile, Dr. Eins was nowhere to be found, some of the party had set out to
see him back at the tavern. Takklin and Oloric played a less than impressive
game of cat and mouse with Oloric eventually catching up. Takklin saw a coin
purse on the side of the street, upon inspection it was filled with rocks, and
the two of them did battle with a drow assassin. After killing the drow and
leaving him dead in the street, they proceeded back to Wrafton's Inn.

A sickly looking potbellied man stood at the bar yelling about how great his
ale was, something about Iceblood Brewing Company.

As the night went on, more of the party retired back to the temple as lodging,
but not before Randor (Dr. Eins), had his weasel steal some items from the pack
of Ernesto, an apparently important person in House Alunno. Randor took his
leave shortly after more Alunno guards began spilling in.

Among some of the belongings on the assassin, Takklin had found a note with
some marks that were given as a bounty for the death of operatives working for
the merchant house known as Macellaio, specifically Takklin.

From Ernesto, a letter detailing the plans of House Macellaio to find this
relic before Alunno, mysteriously signed with a single letter, the letter C.



### Events
- Met Kalarel and heard some information about him
- Met Agazio Agosta, Dragonborn paladin
- Got directions to the ruins
- Met a few interesting NPCs
- Takklin and Oloric did battle with a drow assassin
- Good times had at Wrafton's
