# Module I (Continued): The Root of Balance Part II - The Gentle Boundary

This continuation starts at the exact moment the tree has been restored. It is aftermath, transition, and next-arc ignition.

## Area VI (Continued): The Tree Remembers

### VI.3: The Moment After

**Read Aloud**

> The light settles.
>
> Not fading - completing.
>
> The movement within the tree slows, then steadies, like a breath finally taken after being held too long.
>
> For the first time since you entered these depths...
>
> nothing is waiting to happen.

**DM Notes**

- Pause here and let players speak.
- Do not introduce new threats yet.
- This is release of tension, not escalation.

### VI.4: The Mithal Engages

**Subtle Changes** (introduce gradually)

- Sound dampens slightly.
- Shadows soften.
- Light has no visible origin.
- Breath feels easier.

**Mechanical Effects** (light touch)

Within this area:

- The first hostile action each scene hesitates (disadvantage or delayed initiative).
- Insight checks gain advantage.
- Deception checks suffer disadvantage.

**Read Aloud**

> It takes you a moment to understand what feels different.
>
> Nothing is pushing against you anymore.
>
> The world is not resisting... or demanding.
>
> It is simply allowing.

## Area VII: The Boundary of Hikari

### VII.1: The Soft Transition

**Read Aloud**

> A lantern flickers.
>
> But the light it casts does not move.
>
> A drop of water falls -
>
> and the sound comes a moment too late.
>
> When you look at one another...
>
> you are not entirely where you were.

**Progression** (do not rush)

Over several minutes:

- Reflections appear on dry surfaces.
- Movement feels slightly lagged.
- Air carries a faint shimmer.

**Reveal** (if players question it)

Nothing has changed. They are only noticing what was already present.

### VII.2: The Village in Hikari

**Differences**

- Kitsune forms are less stable.
- Tails are visible in reflection.
- Eyes reflect light unnaturally.
- Buildings feel slightly larger inside than outside.
- Sound carries emotionally, not physically.

**NPC Behavior**

Kitsune are calm, unhurried, and unsurprised.

## Area VIII: Kokuei Shuzo (Awakened)

### VIII.1: The Living Brewery

**Read Aloud**

> The brewery is no longer still.
>
> Liquid moves within sealed barrels - without being touched.
>
> Steam curls into shapes that almost resemble language.
>
> The air tastes faintly... sweet.

### VIII.2: Lady Kogane's Response

**Dialogue**

> "You have done what we could not."
>
> "You carried without claiming.
> You returned without taking."

She looks toward the tree.

> "The boundary is open now."
>
> "Which means... it can be crossed."

## Area IX: The Tree Revealed (Hikari State)

### IX.1: The True Form

**Read Aloud**

> The tree is no longer the size you remember.
>
> It cannot be.
>
> Its roots remain before you...
>
> but its branches -
>
> do not end.

### IX.2: Player Interaction (Optional)

If a player touches the tree, give a vision:

- Water flowing upward.
- The hydra intercepting it.
- The flow restoring.

**Mechanical Reward**

- Gain insight into future imbalance or oni mage influence.

## Area X: Residual Imbalance

### X.1: The Disturbance

**Read Aloud**

> For a moment -
>
> one branch dims.
>
> Not dying.
>
> Resisting.

**DM Note**

This is the bridge to the next arc.

Interpret as either:

- The hydra's influence is not fully gone.
- Something else is disrupting balance elsewhere.

## Area XI: The Upper City Thread

### XI.1: Kogane's Warning

**Dialogue**

> "Balance below... does not restore balance above."

She looks upward.

> "There is a prince who no longer stands in his own reflection."

If asked:

> "He is being shaped."
>
> "Not broken.
>
> Directed."

### XI.2: The Oni Mage

Reference only. Do not fully reveal.

**Dialogue**

> "The one above does not rage like the dead below."
>
> "He convinces."
>
> "He bends what is already willing."

## Area XII: Gifts of the Boundary

### XII.1: Cup of Gentle Return

**Effect (1/day)**

- Create a small zone where the first hostile action falters.
- One ally rerolls a failed check.

**Flavor**

> "You cannot carry this place with you.
>
> But it may remember you... briefly."

## Area XIII: Departure

### XIII.1: The Ascent

**Read Aloud**

> As you climb, the light cools.
>
> Shadows sharpen.
>
> Sound returns to where it belongs.
>
> And something else returns with it -
>
> expectation.

**End Session Line**

Kogane, behind them:

> "Above... things forget more easily."
>
> "Try not to."
