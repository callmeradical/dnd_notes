# Arc 2 Personal Backstory Questions (Knife Theory)

*"Knife theory" in storytelling: The more you care about a character, the more it hurts when bad things happen to them. These questions are designed to create deep connections between each character and the world, making stakes personal and emotionally resonant.*

*Give these questions to players individually. Their answers will create hooks, NPCs, locations, and complications that tie directly into their character's heart.*

---

## Fox - The Masked Assassin

### Identity & The Mask

1. **Why did you first put on the mask?** Was it to hide from someone, to become someone new, or to kill the person you were before?

2. **What does the person beneath the mask look like?** Describe your real face - is there a scar, a birthmark, something that makes you recognizable? Something you hide?

3. **Has anyone in the current party seen your face?** If so, who and why? If not, who do you most want to show - or who are you most afraid will see?

4. **What name were you given at birth?** Not the name "Fox" - your real name. Do you remember it? Have you forbidden yourself from thinking it?

### The Kenshin Wound

5. **What did "Kenshin" mean to you before you learned he wasn't real?** Describe your favorite memory with him - the one that felt most true. What does it mean that this never happened?

6. **When you discovered Kenshin was a false memory, what was the first emotion you felt?** Rage? Grief? Betrayal? Relief? Why that one specifically?

7. **If you could choose, would you keep the Kenshin memories even knowing they're false?** Why or why not? What does that say about you?

8. **Are there other memories you now question?** What if your childhood home wasn't real? What if the reason you became an assassin was planted? How do you know what's true?

### Master Hideo

9. **When did you first meet Master Hideo?** What was the exact moment you realized he was important to you?

10. **What did Master Hideo give you - physically or metaphorically?** A weapon? Training? A purpose? A reason to live? Be specific.

11. **What was the last thing you said to Master Hideo?** If you could go back, what would you say instead?

12. **What question about Master Hideo will haunt you forever if you don't find the answer?** Why did he die? What was he protecting? Did he know you cared?

### The Assassin's Life

13. **Who was your first kill?** Did they deserve it? Did you hesitate? Do you remember their face?

14. **What job or kill do you most regret?** What would you do differently if you could go back?

15. **Is there someone you were supposed to kill but didn't?** What happened to them? Are they still alive? Do they know what you almost did?

16. **Who taught you to kill?** Are they still alive? Do you owe them? Fear them? Love them? Hate them?

### Unfinished Business

17. **Is there someone looking for the person beneath your mask?** A family member? An old friend? Someone you wronged? Someone who wronged you?

18. **What's the one place from your past you can never return to?** Why? What would happen if you did?

19. **If the mask came off permanently - if everyone knew who Fox really is - what's the first consequence that terrifies you?**

20. **What truth about yourself would you kill to keep hidden?** Not from enemies - from your friends. From the party. What can they never know?

## Shigeo - The Braggart Brawler

### The Curse of Greed

1. **What specific moment triggered Greed's Embrace?** What did you take, hoard, or refuse to share? Was it worth it?

2. **Before the curse, what was your relationship with money and wealth?** Were you always greedy, or did something make you this way?

3. **What's the most expensive thing you own?** Why do you keep it instead of selling it? What would you do if someone tried to take it?

4. **If you had to choose between saving a party member and securing a legendary treasure, which would you choose?** Be honest. What does that say about you?

### Pride & Bravado

5. **What's the lie you tell most often about yourself?** A fight you won? A feat you accomplished? A person you impressed?

6. **What's the ONE story about yourself that's actually true but nobody believes?** Why don't they believe it?

7. **Who do you most want to impress?** Someone in the party? Someone from your past? Someone you'll never see again? Why them specifically?

8. **What's the one insult or criticism that cuts deepest?** Not just any insult - the specific words that make you want to prove yourself or shut down completely.

### The Storyteller

9. **Why did you tell the story of Atsuko instead of something more impressive?** What made you choose humility over spectacle in that moment?

10. **Was Atsuko a real person?** Did you know her? Was she someone you admired? Someone you failed? Someone you wish you could be?

11. **Kaoru saw something in you during that contest.** What do you think she saw? Do you deserve that respect?

12. **If you could only be remembered for one true deed instead of a hundred boasts, what would you want it to be?**

### Family & Origin

13. **Where did you learn to fight?** The streets? A master? Trial by fire? Did someone teach you, or did you teach yourself?

14. **Do you have family?** Are they alive? Do they know what you've become? Would they be proud or ashamed?

15. **What's the one thing from your childhood home you wish you still had?** Why that specific thing?

16. **Was there ever someone who believed in you before you learned to brag?** What happened to them?

### The Weight of the Curse

17. **How much longer can you survive with Greed's Embrace draining you?** Do you feel yourself getting weaker? Are you afraid of dying to your own greed?

18. **What would you sacrifice to break the curse?** Your wealth? Your pride? A relationship? Your memories?

19. **If you died tomorrow, what would you want done with your possessions?** Who gets the Great Tortoise charm? Do you trust anyone with what you've accumulated?

20. **Deep down, do you believe you deserve this curse?** Why or why not?

## Aoshi - Last Oni Waban

### The Last of Your Kind

1. **How did you become the last Oni Waban?** Did the others die? Abandon the way? Were you the only one chosen? Tell the story.

2. **Do you know the names of the other Oni Waban who came before you?** Speak them. How do you honor their memory?

3. **What happened on the day you realized you were truly alone?** The moment you understood there would be no more Oni Waban after you. What did that feel like?

4. **Can the Oni Waban tradition die with you, or is there a way to pass it on?** If you could train someone, who would it be? Would you even want to burden another with this?

### The Oni Heritage

5. **What is your relationship to oni?** Are you part-oni? Do you serve them? Fight them? Protect them? Fear them? How much oni blood runs in your veins?

6. **What do people see when they look at you?** Do your oni features show? Do you hide them? Are you proud or ashamed of what you are?

7. **Have you ever been called a monster?** Who said it? Were they right? How did you respond?

8. **What's the most "oni" thing about you - the trait you can't hide or suppress?** Rage? Strength? Appearance? Hunger? How do you control it?

### The Visions

9. **When did you first see Shikigo?** Describe the vision in detail. What were you doing? What did it feel like? What did you think it meant?

10. **Who or what is Shikigo to you?** Ancestor? God? Demon? Guide? Warning? Do you want to find them, or are you afraid of what you'll discover?

11. **The vision of Oni Babamon - was it different from Shikigo?** How? What did Oni Babamon show you or tell you? Why two visions?

12. **Do you think these visions are real, or could they be tricks?** Corruption? False memories like Fox's Kenshin? How do you know?

### Guardian's Duty

13. **What were the Oni Waban created to guard?** A place? A person? A secret? A seal? Be specific.

14. **Have you failed in your duty yet?** If so, how? If not, what's the closest you've come?

15. **What happens if you die before passing on the Waban legacy?** Is something unleashed? Does a protection fail? Will people suffer?

16. **Is there someone or something you've been tasked to kill?** By the visions? By the Waban tradition? Have you told anyone?

### Unfinished Oaths

17. **What oath did you swear when you became an Oni Waban?** Can you still remember the exact words? Do you still believe in them?

18. **Have you ever broken an oath?** What were the consequences - for you and for others?

19. **If completing your Waban duty requires you to die, will you do it?** Without hesitation? What if it meant abandoning the party?

20. **What would it take for you to forsake the Oni Waban path?** What line would need to be crossed for you to walk away from everything you are?

## Thornbringer - The Druid

### The Well of Life

1. **When did you first hear of the Well of Life?** Who told you? A vision? A mentor? An ancient text? Why did it immediately matter to you?

2. **What do you believe the Well of Life can do?** Heal? Restore? Grant power? Bring back the dead? Be specific about your hope.

3. **Why do YOU need to find it?** Not "because it's important" - what personal wound, loss, or need drives you to seek it?

4. **The failed Insight check - what do you think you missed?** In that moment, what was just beyond your understanding? Does it haunt you?

### The One Tree Vision

5. **Describe the vision of the One Tree seedling exactly as you saw it.** What was the seedling growing from? Who was holding it? What did it smell like? What feeling did it give you?

6. **Do you think you're meant to plant the seedling, find it, or become it?** What does the vision want from you?

7. **Has the One Tree appeared in your dreams since?** What does it show you? Is it the same each time, or is it changing?

8. **If you found the Well but not the seedling - or the seedling but not the Well - which matters more?** Could you walk away from one to pursue the other?

### Nature & Balance

9. **What made you become a druid?** Was it a choice, a calling, or something forced upon you? Tell the story.

10. **What's the most beautiful natural place you've ever seen?** Is it still there? If you returned, would it still be beautiful, or has corruption reached it?

11. **What's the worst thing you've seen humans do to nature?** Did you stop it? Did you avenge it? Do you still see it in your mind?

12. **If you had to choose between saving a city of people or an ancient forest, which would you choose?** Why? Would you be able to live with that choice?

### The Fox-Kami

13. **Why do you believe fox-kami know the Well's location?** Is it intuition? Something you read? Something the One Tree vision suggested?

14. **Have you ever encountered a fox-kami before?** If so, what happened? If not, how will you recognize one when you see it?

15. **What are you willing to offer fox-kami in exchange for the knowledge?** Service? Sacrifice? Secrets? Your own memories?

16. **What if the fox-kami demand something terrible in trade?** A life? Betraying the party? Abandoning your quest? Would you pay the price?

### Loss & Regret

17. **What part of nature did you fail to protect?** A grove? An animal? A kami? How did you fail, and do you still carry that shame?

18. **Is there someone you couldn't save?** Someone who died while you watched nature take its course? Do you accept that death, or does it torment you?

19. **What natural disaster or corruption have you witnessed that you were powerless to stop?** Fire? Flood? Corruption? Deforestation? Describe it. Does it fuel you or break you?

20. **If finding the Well of Life means you can never return to nature - trapped in cities, cut off from the wild - would you still seek it?** What would that cost you?

## Kanji - The Haunted Acrobat

### Akemi Takahashi

1. **Who was Akemi Takahashi to you?** Friend? Lover? Family? Partner? Rival? Be honest about the relationship.

2. **What happened to Akemi?** Tell the story. What was your role in it? Were you there when it happened? Did you cause it, fail to prevent it, or survive when they didn't?

3. **What was the last thing Akemi said to you?** Or the last thing you said to them? Can you still hear their voice?

4. **How did you resolve the guilt?** What did you do, say, or realize that let you move forward? Was it earned, or are you lying to yourself?

### The Ghost That Haunted

5. **Before you resolved the guilt, did you actually see Akemi's ghost?** In dreams? In mirrors? In your peripheral vision? Or was it only in your mind?

6. **What did the "ghost" say to you?** Accusations? Pleas? Forgiveness? What words cut deepest?

7. **When you finally let go, did the ghost disappear, or is it still there - just quieter?** Be honest.

8. **Is there someone else's ghost waiting to haunt you?** Another person you've wronged or failed? Are you just between hauntings?

### The Chasm Crossing

9. **Describe the chasm.** How deep? How wide? What was at the bottom? What made it so dangerous?

10. **What exactly did you do to become the "Hero of the Chasm Crossing"?** Step by step. What was at stake? Who were you saving?

11. **At what point during the crossing did you think you were going to die?** What went through your mind in that moment?

12. **Did being called a "hero" change how you see yourself?** Do you believe it? Does it feel like redemption, or does it feel like a performance?

### The Acrobat's Past

13. **Where did you learn your acrobatic skills?** A circus? A troupe? The streets? A monastery? Who taught you?

14. **Was there a time when your skills weren't enough?** When you fell, failed, or missed? What were the consequences?

15. **Do you perform for audiences, or do you only use your skills to survive?** If you performed, what was your act? Do you miss it?

16. **What's the most dangerous stunt you've ever successfully pulled off?** Not the chasm - something before. Why did you attempt it?

### Guilt & Redemption

17. **Before Akemi, was there someone else you failed?** Is Akemi part of a pattern, or were they unique?

18. **What do you think you deserve?** Punishment? Forgiveness? Another chance? Peace? Do you believe you've earned it?

19. **If you could go back and save Akemi by sacrificing yourself, would you?** Without hesitation? What does that say about whether you've truly let go?

20. **The party calls you a hero, but what do you call yourself?** When you're alone, when no one's watching, what word describes who you think you are?

## Yasei - Servant of Hikari

### The Deep Village

1. **Describe your home.** The Deep Village of Hikari - what did it look like? Sound like? Smell like? What made it special?

2. **When did the kami first go silent in Hikari?** Was it sudden or gradual? What was the first prayer that went unanswered?

3. **Where were you when Yora-no-kiba attacked?** Were you there? Did you flee? Were you sent away before it happened? Tell the truth.

4. **Who did you leave behind?** Names. Faces. Specific people you still think about. Family? Friends? A love? A mentor?

### Servant of Hikari

5. **What does it mean to be a "Servant of Hikari"?** What are your specific duties? Who gave you this title? Can it be taken away?

6. **Is Hikari a deity, a kami, or just the village's name?** What is the spiritual force you serve? What does it want from you?

7. **Were you chosen to be a servant, or were you born into it?** Did you have a choice? Would you have chosen this path if given the option?

8. **What ritual or ceremony did you perform most often in Hikari?** Describe it in detail. Do you still perform it, even though the kami don't answer?

### The Hope You Carry

9. **Who told you to "carry the village's hope"?** Were those their exact words? What did they mean by it?

10. **What hope are you carrying, specifically?** That survivors will be found? That the village can be rebuilt? That the kami will return? Be precise.

11. **What happens if you fail?** If there are no survivors? If the kami never return? If Hikari is truly gone? What becomes of you?

12. **Do you actually have hope, or are you just pretending?** Be honest. Do you believe Hikari can be saved, or are you carrying a corpse?

### Yora-no-kiba

13. **What is Yora-no-kiba village?** Why did they attack Hikari? What grievance, greed, or corruption drove them?

14. **Do you know anyone from Yora-no-kiba personally?** Did you have friends, family, or trading partners there before the attack? Has someone you knew betrayed you?

15. **What will you do if you meet someone from Yora-no-kiba?** Kill them on sight? Demand answers? Break down? Try to understand?

16. **Is the attack on Hikari part of something larger?** The Obsidian Circle? Political conflict? Ancient feuds? Or was it just violence for violence's sake?

### Faith & Silence

17. **When you pray now, do you still expect an answer?** What does it feel like to pray into silence? Rage? Despair? Determination? Emptiness?

18. **What was the last miracle or blessing you witnessed before the kami went silent?** Something small or large - proof that Hikari once listened. Do you cling to that memory?

19. **Do you blame yourself for the kami's silence?** Did you fail in your duties? Sin in some way? Make a mistake? Or do you know it's not your fault?

20. **If the kami of Hikari spoke to you right now, what would you ask?** Not what they would say - what would YOU ask? What question burns inside you?

### The Weight of Exile

21. **How long can you keep carrying hope for people who might be dead?** When do you let go and live your own life? Or is your life tied to Hikari forever?

22. **If you discovered every villager died in the attack, would you still continue your quest?** What would be the point? Revenge? Justice? Honoring their memory? Or would you finally break?

23. **Do you fear returning to Hikari?** Not because of danger - because of what you might find? Empty ruins? Mass graves? Evidence of your failure?

24. **What would it take for you to consider yourself free of your duty?** Can you ever stop being a Servant of Hikari? Would you want to?

## Iseri Tetsuo - The Vigilante

### The First Kill

1. **What was the name of the collections officer you killed?** Do you remember his face? His last words? The exact moment he stopped breathing?

2. **Did he deserve to die?** Be honest. Was he truly evil, or just a drunk doing his job? Does the answer matter?

3. **How did it feel?** Not afterwards - in the moment. The actual act of killing. Did you hesitate? Did it feel right? Wrong? Easy? Horrible?

4. **Did anyone witness it?** Does someone know what you did? Are you running from justice, revenge, or both?

### The Carpenter's Son

5. **What are your parents' names?** Say them. Are they still alive? Do they know what you've become?

6. **Do they know you killed for them?** That the violence was meant to ease their burden? Would they be proud or horrified?

7. **What was your childhood home like?** Describe it. Can you ever go back? Would your parents even recognize the person you've become?

8. **What did your father teach you?** Not violence - what skills from carpentry did you learn? Do you still carry those skills, or have you buried them?

### The Path of Shadows

9. **After the first kill, what did you do next?** Did you wait? Run? Kill again? When did vigilante justice become your life?

10. **How many have you killed?** Give an honest number. Do you remember them all, or have they blurred together?

11. **What's the worst thing you've done in the name of justice?** Not killing - something else. Something that made you question if you're still the hero.

12. **Is there someone you killed who you now realize didn't deserve it?** A mistake? Bad information? Rage clouding judgment? Does their face haunt you?

### The Troll Encounter

13. **Why were you on the road to Takashima?** Were you chasing someone? Running from someone? What brought you to that exact place?

14. **Who are the four dead bodies around the troll?** Were they with you? After you? Innocent travelers? Do you know their names?

15. **How did you end up restrained by the troll?** Did you lose a fight? Get captured protecting someone? Make a mistake? Tell the truth.

16. **When you wake up with the party, what's your first thought?** Gratitude? Suspicion? Anger at being vulnerable? Planning escape?

### Justice & Methods

17. **What's the difference between you and a murderer?** Is there one? Do you choose your targets carefully, or has the line blurred?

18. **Have you ever let a corrupt official live?** Why or why not? What convinced you to show mercy or refuse it?

19. **What would you do if you discovered the party disagreed with your methods?** Would you defend yourself? Change? Leave? Kill to protect your mission?

20. **If the law came for you - legitimate justice, not corrupt officials - would you surrender or fight?** What if the party had to choose between you and the law?

### Unfinished Business

21. **Is there a specific person you're hunting?** Someone who wronged your family? A symbol of the corrupt system? Give a name.

22. **What will you do when that person is dead?** Will you stop? Find a new target? Is this about justice, or is it about the hunt itself?

23. **Do you believe you'll survive this path?** Do you expect to grow old, or is this a suicide mission you've dressed up as righteousness?

24. **What would it take for you to put down the blade and go back to carpentry?** Could you? Would you even want to? Or is the violent life all you have left?

---

*These questions are designed to hurt. They should make players uncomfortable, force hard truths, and create hooks that tie directly into the world. Use their answers to build NPCs, locations, moral dilemmas, and story beats that make the campaign personal.*

*Remember: The more it hurts, the more it matters.*
