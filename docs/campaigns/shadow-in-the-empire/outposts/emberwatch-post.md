# Emberwatch Post

**Location:** Near Flamebrow Territory  
**Type:** Watchtower rebuilt from colonial stone  
**Proprietor:** Sister Malra Ashborn

---

## Outpost Description

### **Physical Layout**
Built around the remains of a colonial watchtower, Emberwatch Post combines old stone construction with newer wooden additions. The original tower provides elevated observation points, while ground-level buildings serve as living quarters and trading space. At night, red lanterns throughout the complex create an otherworldly hellish glow visible for miles.

### **Atmosphere**
The constant smell of burning incense mingles with the acrid smoke from various alchemical preparations. Red lantern light casts dancing shadows that seem to move independently of their sources. Everything has a reddish tinge in the evening light, creating an unsettling but strangely beautiful environment.

### **Notable Features**
- Colonial stone tower with modern wooden additions
- Red lanterns throughout create distinctive nighttime appearance
- Multiple shrines with burning incense and draconic iconography
- Elevated platforms provide clear views of approaching Flamebrow territory

## Proprietor

### **Sister Malra Ashborn**
*Medium humanoid (human), neutral*

**Appearance:** Middle-aged human woman with prematurely gray hair and hands stained red from constant incense handling. Wears simple robes decorated with ember and flame motifs. Her eyes reflect the red lantern light, creating an unsettling but not malevolent presence.

**Personality:** Deeply spiritual but pragmatic about survival in wyrm-dominated lands. Speaks of draconic spirits as real entities requiring respect and appeasement. Maintains careful neutrality between traditional religious practices and the harsh realities of draconic rule.

**Background:** Former Flamebrow clan member who left due to philosophical differences but maintains complex relationship with draconic spirituality. Established the post as neutral ground where various factions can meet and trade.

**Services Offered:**
- Spiritual consultation and draconic ceremony
- Fire-resistant equipment preparation and blessing
- Neutral ground for factional meetings and negotiations
- Cultural intelligence about wyrm cult practices

## Patrons & Rumors

### **Jorlan Emberchild**
*Medium humanoid (dragonborn), lawful evil*

**Appearance:** Young dragonborn with bright copper scales that seem to shimmer with inner heat. Wears traditional Flamebrow clan regalia mixed with newer draconic cult symbols. His breath creates visible heat distortion even in normal conversation.

**Background:** Flamebrow clan member who serves as unofficial diplomat and recruiter for the clan's new draconic allegiances. Genuinely believes that partnership with a red wyrm represents the clan's best path to prosperity and survival.

**Key Information:**
- **"The Flamebrows have found the path to true power through alliance with the Red Lord."**
- **"Soon, the pact will be sealed, and our enemies will burn before our righteous flames."**
- **"Join us willingly, and share in the glory. Oppose us, and face the consequences."**

**Services:**
- **Clan Introduction:** Formal presentation to Flamebrow leadership (if loyalty demonstrated)
- **Cultural Education:** Detailed instruction in Flamebrow customs and draconic worship
- **Equipment Enhancement:** Access to clan-blessed gear with fire properties
- **Political Intelligence:** Information about clan politics and future plans

---

### **Kessa Hollow-Eyes**
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic neutral*

**Appearance:** Gaunt human woman with empty eye sockets that seem to contain flickering flames instead of normal eyes. Her fingers are permanently stained with herb residue, and she moves with the careful precision of someone who navigates by senses other than sight.

**Background:** Former clan herbalist who lost her sight in a ritual gone wrong but gained the ability to see through flames and perceive draconic magic. Now serves as reluctant oracle and advisor to those seeking understanding of draconic influence.

**Key Information:**
- **"Through the flames, I see the truth - the Flamebrows plan sacrifice of their own blood."**
- **"The pact requires more than loyalty. It demands transformation, body and soul."**
- **"Some prices are too high, even for power. The Red Lord's gifts come with chains."**

**Services:**
- **Fire Scrying:** Vision through flames reveals distant events (50 gp per session)
- **Draconic Detection:** Identify wyrm cult influence and corruption (25 gp per assessment)
- **Herbal Remedies:** Specialized treatments for fire damage and draconic curses
- **Prophetic Warnings:** Cryptic guidance about dangerous draconic encounters
