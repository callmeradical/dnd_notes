## Session Recap

The party answered Master Hideo's summons at the Whispering Willow and met Kazuki Takemura, who revealed he ended Hideo's life at the smith's request. Kazuki presented a steel tablet containing Hideo's prophecy, naming the party and a list of allies and threats. He hired them to infiltrate the Upper Citadel, protect the boy heir, and find the "first chain" before the Festival of Ascending Iron.

At the Iron Rest Inn, the party met Mama Tora and reunited with the Starcall Table. Kaoru revealed herself as a Hidden Lotus Society leader and asked for help finding a missing blacksmith's daughter. The party confronted Master of the Watch Saan at the guard tower, discovered Kento, and stole a ledger containing a ciphered message. The session ended with a plan to retrieve the cipher key book and return the ledger before the theft is discovered.
