# The Silver Fang Lodge

**Location:** Near Duskwing Territory  
**Type:** Dragonborn hunter's lodge and waystation  
**Proprietor:** Kethra Flamehide

---

## Outpost Description

### **Physical Layout**
Built in traditional dragonborn architectural style with stone foundations and curved wooden structures that echo draconic forms. The main lodge features a central hearth surrounded by trophy displays - dragon bones, dire beast hides, and preserved specimens from Q'barra's dangerous wildlife. Sleeping quarters occupy wings extending from the main hall like sprawling claws.

### **Atmosphere**
The lodge maintains ancient dragonborn customs despite the harsh frontier conditions. Ceremonial weapons hang alongside practical hunting gear. The constant smell of smoking meat mingles with incense burned to honor draconic ancestors. Everything is arranged with martial precision but decorated with traditional artistry.

### **Cultural Elements**
- Dragon skull entrance arch (from long-dead ancestor dragon)
- Weapon racks displaying both ceremonial and functional arms
- Trophy wall showcasing successful hunts and defeated enemies
- Shrine to draconic ancestors with active incense offerings

---

## Proprietor

### **Kethra Flamehide**
*Medium humanoid (dragonborn), lawful neutral*

**Appearance:** Scarred dragonborn huntress with bronze-scaled hide marked by claw marks and burn scars from decades of dangerous hunts. Wears practical leather armor reinforced with metal studs and dragon scales. Her eyes hold the steady gaze of a predator who's survived by being faster and smarter than her prey.

**Personality:** Respectful of strength and competence, dismissive of weakness or cowardice. Speaks directly and expects the same from others. Values traditional dragonborn honor but adapts pragmatically to frontier necessities. Takes pride in providing quality gear and reliable information to worthy customers.

**Background:** Former member of a dragonborn clan's hunting elite who established the lodge to serve travelers while maintaining traditional customs. Survived the clan wars through skill and now offers her expertise to those who respect dragonborn ways.

**Services Offered:**
- Wilderness survival training and consultation
- Custom hunting equipment and modifications  
- Cultural guidance for interacting with dragonborn clans
- Tracking services for dangerous creatures

## Patrons & Rumors

### **Tazrik Bonecarver**
*Medium humanoid (dragonborn), neutral*

**Appearance:** Elderly dragonborn artisan with silver scales and rheumy eyes that still gleam with intelligence. His claws are worn down from decades of careful carving work. Wears simple robes decorated with bone ornaments of his own creation.

**Background:** Master craftsman who specializes in bone and horn carving. Maintains connections throughout dragonborn society and knows the ancient customs that govern clan interactions. His work is respected across tribal boundaries.

**Key Information:**
- **"The Duskwings test all outsiders by combat - it's their way of measuring worth."**
- **"The Heirloom Trial isn't just about fighting. It tests courage, honor, and understanding of our ways."**
- **"Fail their test and they'll mark you as unworthy. That mark follows you to other clans."**

**Services:**
- **Cultural Education:** Detailed instruction in dragonborn customs and expectations (50 gp)
- **Tribal Introductions:** Formal presentations to clan leaders (if party proves worthy)
- **Custom Carvings:** Bone and horn artwork with cultural significance (25-100 gp)
- **Diplomatic Advice:** Strategy for navigating complex tribal politics

---

### **Old Marrik**
*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic neutral*

**Appearance:** Ancient human trapper with wild gray hair and deep-set eyes that dart constantly between shadows. Wears patched fur clothing and carries pouches filled with carved teeth, animal bones, and mysterious trinkets. His fingers are stained from various alchemical processes.

**Background:** Hermit trapper who's survived decades in dragonborn territory through caution, skill, and careful respect for tribal customs. Claims to have witnessed ancient rituals and supernatural events that most dismiss as the ravings of an old man.

**Key Information:**
- **"Seen them performing rites under the twin moons - rites not meant for mortal eyes."**
- **"They call on powers older than the clans, older than the dragons themselves."**
- **"The night when both moons are dark, that's when the ancient pacts are renewed."**

**Services:**
- **Wilderness Guide:** Knows secret paths through dangerous territory (20 gp per day)
- **Ritual Components:** Rare materials for magical ceremonies (varying prices)
- **Supernatural Intelligence:** Information about mystical events and locations
- **Tooth Currency:** Accepts carved teeth as payment (traditional dragonborn trade medium)

## Roleplay Opportunities

### **Dragonborn Honor Culture**
The lodge provides immersion in dragonborn social customs:
- **Formal Greetings:** Proper protocols for showing respect
- **Weapon Etiquette:** Traditional ways of displaying arms without threat
- **Trophy Sharing:** Stories of great hunts and dangerous encounters

### **Cultural Exchange**
Interactions bridge the gap between different societies:
- **Language Lessons:** Learning key Draconic phrases and their significance
- **Custom Explanations:** Understanding why certain behaviors offend or honor
- **Clan Politics:** Navigation of complex inter-tribal relationships

### **Preparation for Trials**
The lodge serves as training ground for upcoming challenges:
- **Combat Preparation:** Physical readiness for clan trials
- **Mental Conditioning:** Understanding what dragonborn value in outsiders
- **Equipment Selection:** Choosing gear that shows respect for traditions
