# Important Landmarks

## Temple of the Three Moons

&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Temple of the Three Moons.&lt;/strong&gt; Hidden deep within the mist-shrouded forests of Minashima, the Temple of the Three Moons is an ancient, enigmatic sanctuary devoted to the celestial bodies of Atellas. The temple is located on a craggy hilltop, surrounded by towering cedar trees and a maze of crumbling stone pathways that lead up to the main structure. The temple’s architecture features towering spires and grand arches, each adorned with carvings depicting the phases of the moons, the cycles of the stars, and intricate celestial patterns. Worshipers of the moons, known as the &lt;em&gt;Seekers of the Hidden Moon&lt;/em&gt;, guard the temple and maintain its rituals. The most devout among them believe in the existence of a mysterious third moon that is hidden from the eyes of mortals—a celestial body known as &lt;strong&gt;Tsukuyomi&lt;/strong&gt;, the Lost Moon.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;h2&gt;Lore and Cultural Importance&lt;/h2&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;The people of Shuigari revere the two moons, &lt;strong&gt;Dendaro&lt;/strong&gt; and &lt;strong&gt;Intara&lt;/strong&gt;, as divine beings that influence tides, time, and fate. However, the temple’s adherents believe that there is a third moon, Tsukuyomi, which was cast into the void by the sun, &lt;em&gt;Rafellos&lt;/em&gt;, during an ancient conflict between the celestial bodies. This myth speaks of how the three moons were once aligned in perfect harmony, governing the balance of magic and destiny. But when Rafellos grew jealous of their combined power, he banished Tsukuyomi into the shadows. The temple’s priests teach that Tsukuyomi still exists, hidden in a veil of darkness, and only reveals itself to those who have been touched by the stars’ light.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;Legends say that those who receive Tsukuyomi’s blessing can see beyond the veil of reality, glimpsing truths unseen by other mortals. They become &lt;strong&gt;Moonbound&lt;/strong&gt;, able to walk the thin boundary between the material world and the astral plane. It is believed that when all three moons are aligned, a cosmic gate will open, granting the faithful passage to a hidden realm where the moon’s lost power is said to dwell. The temple’s rituals often involve star-gazing, moonlit ceremonies, and blood offerings made at midnight in hopes of gaining Tsukuyomi’s favor. Despite these beliefs, many consider the Seekers of the Hidden Moon heretics, and the temple has been sacked and abandoned numerous times throughout history—only to be rebuilt by the devout who continue their vigil.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;h2&gt;Temple of the Three Moons Adventures&lt;/h2&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;The Hidden Moon’s Prophecy (Low Level).&lt;/strong&gt; The characters are approached by a lone monk, a Seeker of the Hidden Moon, who begs them to help recover a sacred relic known as the &lt;em&gt;Orb of the Third Eye&lt;/em&gt; from a rival faction that considers the temple’s beliefs to be blasphemy. This orb is said to have the power to reveal the location of Tsukuyomi during the next celestial alignment. The characters must infiltrate an enemy camp hidden in the dense forest, retrieve the relic, and return it to the temple before the next full moon. Along the way, they will face religious zealots, ancient moon spirits, and even illusions conjured by the lost moon’s power.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;The Night of Three Shadows (Mid Level).&lt;/strong&gt; Whispers spread through the temple’s inner sanctum that the celestial alignment is approaching—an event that only occurs once every hundred years. However, not all the Seekers are aligned in their beliefs. A faction within the temple believes that when Tsukuyomi’s shadow falls upon the land, it will bring not enlightenment, but madness. The characters are hired by the temple’s high priest to quell this dissent and ensure that the ritual is completed. But as they investigate, they discover that the dissenters might be right. The characters must decide whether to protect the temple’s legacy and risk unleashing a dark force or prevent the ritual and face the wrath of the devout Seekers.&lt;/p&gt;

## Mount Hoshigami

**Mount Hoshigami.** The largest and most sacred peak in the Ryuseki range, Mount Hoshigami is an active volcano that dominates the landscape of central Shugane. The mountain’s jagged slopes and steaming vents are a constant reminder of the island’s elemental power. Revered as the dwelling place of the fire kami, *Izanami no Kagutsuchi*, the volcano is surrounded by both awe and fear. Its eruptions are viewed as divine messages, and its ash-laden winds can either bring fertility to the land or blight the crops, depending on the kami’s favor. Pilgrims often journey to the base of the mountain to pay their respects at the various shrines scattered along its lower slopes, but few dare to ascend to the caldera itself, where the air is thick with sulfur and the spirits of fire roam freely.<br />Lore and Cultural Importance<br />In the mythology of Shugane, Mount Hoshigami is believed to be the earthly manifestation of the fire kami, **Kagutsuchi**. It is said that long ago, Kagutsuchi was born from the ashes of the creation flame that shaped the world. His first cries ignited the heavens and scorched the earth, giving rise to the volcano that still smolders today. The people of Shugane view Kagutsuchi as both a protector and a destroyer—a force that must be revered and appeased to ensure the safety and prosperity of the island. The annual **Rite of Flames** is performed by the monks of Hoshigami Shrine, during which offerings of precious metals and rare herbs are cast into the molten heart of the volcano to honor Kagutsuchi and seek his blessing for the coming year.<br />Legends speak of a time when the balance between the elements was disturbed, causing Kagutsuchi’s rage to erupt violently. Entire villages were buried in ash, and the sky turned dark for three days. It was only after a group of brave warriors ventured into the heart of the volcano and presented a blade forged from the purest steel as an offering that Kagutsuchi’s wrath was quelled. Since then, the sword—known as the *Flamebearer’s Blade*—has been kept as a sacred relic, and it is said that whoever wields it can command the fire spirits that dwell within the mountain.<br />Mount Hoshigami Adventures<br />**The Blade in the Ashes (Low Level).** A local daimyo’s shrine has recently been buried by a minor eruption, and the kami’s wrath is believed to be the cause. The characters are hired to recover a sacred relic—a sword forged in the fire of Kagutsuchi—from the ruins before the lava flow consumes it entirely. However, they soon discover that the relic is guarded by fierce *fire spirits* that will not let it go without a fight. Will they risk the kami’s ire to claim the blade, or find another way to appease the restless spirits?<br />**Wrath of the Fire Kami (Mid Level).** Strange tremors have been felt across the plains, and molten cracks are forming along the slopes of Mount Hoshigami. The monks of the Hoshigami Shrine fear that Kagutsuchi’s rage has been stirred, and they beseech the characters to help them perform the *Rite of Flames* before a catastrophic eruption destroys the surrounding villages. The adventurers must brave the treacherous ascent to the caldera, battling volatile elementals and avoiding sudden lava flows, all while racing against time to complete the ritual. If they succeed, they may gain Kagutsuchi’s favor—or suffer his eternal wrath if they fail.

## Shrine of the Falling Stars

&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Shrine of the Falling Stars.&lt;/strong&gt; Perched halfway up the fiery slopes of Mount Hoshigami, the Shrine of the Falling Stars is a place of both serenity and danger, where the boundaries between the heavens and the mortal world blur. Built on a rocky outcrop that juts out over a sea of molten lava, the shrine is dedicated to the celestial kami &lt;em&gt;Hoshitsukiyo&lt;/em&gt;, the kami of stars and fate. Its architecture is marked by towering stone spires etched with constellations, and luminous lanterns that glow with an otherworldly blue light. On clear nights, the shrine’s position high above the clouds allows for a perfect view of the night sky, and it is believed that the stars shine brighter here than anywhere else on Shugane. During the sacred festival of &lt;strong&gt;Tsukimi-no-Matsuri&lt;/strong&gt;, the monks of the shrine light a massive beacon that can be seen for miles around, symbolizing the connection between the earth and the heavens.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;h2&gt;Lore and Cultural Importance&lt;/h2&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;The Shrine of the Falling Stars is shrouded in myth and legend, and many believe that it was built on the spot where a fragment of the celestial heavens fell to earth centuries ago. According to the ancient texts, a falling star descended upon Mount Hoshigami, carrying with it the essence of the kami Hoshitsukiyo. The impact of the star was so powerful that it split the mountain and left a permanent scar—a crater now known as the &lt;em&gt;Star’s Wound&lt;/em&gt;—that glows faintly with a soft, silvery light. Pilgrims from across Shuigari make the treacherous journey to this remote shrine to offer prayers and meditate on their destinies, hoping to receive visions of their future or gain Hoshitsukiyo’s guidance.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;It is said that on nights when the sky is clear and the constellations align, Hoshitsukiyo descends to the shrine in the form of a radiant stag with a coat of shimmering stardust. Those blessed with the vision of the celestial stag are believed to be marked by fate, destined for greatness—or tragedy. As such, the shrine is not only a place of worship but also one of omens and portents, where samurai, monks, and even daimyo seek answers to questions that can shape the course of their lives.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;h2&gt;Shrine of the Falling Stars Adventures&lt;/h2&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Echoes of a Fallen Star (Low Level).&lt;/strong&gt; Strange tremors have been shaking the shrine, and the Star’s Wound has begun to glow brighter than ever before. The monks believe that a shard of the original celestial star, long thought lost, is reawakening. They hire the characters to venture into the treacherous depths of the Star’s Wound to retrieve the shard before its celestial energy causes another eruption. However, the shard is guarded by &lt;em&gt;star-wrought yokai&lt;/em&gt;—creatures born from the very essence of the falling star—making this recovery mission fraught with peril.&lt;/p&gt;<br />&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;The Celestial Prophecy (Mid Level).&lt;/strong&gt; A powerful daimyo has received a vision of the celestial stag and now believes he is fated to conquer the island. The monks of the Shrine of the Falling Stars, fearing that this destiny could bring ruin to Shugane, hire the characters to recover the &lt;em&gt;Celestial Mirror&lt;/em&gt;, an artifact said to show the true path of fate. The mirror is hidden within the shrine’s deepest sanctum, guarded by enchanted puzzles and celestial guardians that test the purity of one’s soul. If the characters succeed, they may change the course of fate itself—but if they fail, the daimyo’s ambitions may plunge the island into war.&lt;/p&gt;

## Flame Temple

**Flame Temple.** Nestled in the shadow of Mount Kurai, the Flame Temple is an ancient structure built directly into the volcanic rock. This sacred site is one of the most revered locations on the island of Torinokai, dedicated to honoring the fire kami, *Izanami no Kagutsuchi*. The temple’s architecture is a marvel of engineering, with walls lined in shimmering obsidian and a central chamber illuminated by a vast brazier that never extinguishes, fueled by the mountain’s own magma. Priests and monks who serve here are known as the *Ember Keepers*, tending to the eternal flame and performing rituals to keep the fiery wrath of the volcano at bay. The temple is a place of both beauty and danger, where the air is thick with the scent of sulfur and the presence of restless fire spirits lingers in the shadows.<br />Lore and Cultural Importance<br />The Flame Temple was founded centuries ago by a legendary warrior-monk known as **Shirohime the Firebearer**, who sought to tame the chaotic power of Mount Kurai. According to legend, Shirohime ventured deep into the heart of the volcano, where she battled a mighty *fire dragon* that threatened to consume the island in flame. After a fierce struggle, she struck a pact with the beast, binding it to the temple’s flame in exchange for its protection over Torinokai. This dragon, known as the *Crimson Wyrm*, is believed to still slumber beneath the temple, and its power is said to be the source of the temple’s unending flame. The **Rite of Burning Resolve** is held every decade, during which a chosen warrior meditates before the brazier for three days and nights, testing their resolve and seeking the fire kami’s blessing.<br />To the people of Torinokai, the Flame Temple is both a symbol of strength and a reminder of the delicate balance that must be maintained between the destructive and nurturing aspects of fire. Those who seek the temple’s blessing often come bearing offerings of rare metals and gems, believing that Kagutsuchi’s favor can bring great fortune or temper the soul with courage. However, it is also a place of great peril—many have entered the inner sanctum seeking enlightenment, only to be consumed by the temple’s insatiable flames.<br />Flame Temple Adventures<br />**Trial of the Firebearer (Low Level).** A young monk has gone missing after attempting the *Trial of the Firebearer*, a rite of passage meant to prove one&#39;s worthiness to serve as an Ember Keeper. The characters are asked to venture into the lower chambers of the Flame Temple to find the missing monk, where they must face *fire-wreathed guardians* and solve the ancient puzzles that guard the path. Will they rescue the aspirant, or uncover a darker secret hidden deep within the temple’s fiery depths?<br />**The Slumbering Wyrm (Mid Level).** Strange tremors and bursts of flame have been erupting around Mount Kurai, causing panic among the villagers. The Ember Keepers fear that the *Crimson Wyrm* is awakening from its centuries-long slumber. They beseech the adventurers to delve into the heart of the temple, venturing through treacherous lava tunnels to reach the beast’s lair. The characters must either find a way to pacify the wyrm or seal it back into its fiery prison before its rage consumes the entire island.
