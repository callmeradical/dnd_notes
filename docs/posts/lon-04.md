## During the Session (recap)

We are in the Quay, where two forces stand opposed at the base of the tower:
the Crown Alchemical Society and the Penny Moors. At the summit, Dalan Korr
and Whistle Jack meet to negotiate. • The Crown’s crimes are laid bare. •
Korr confesses — every soul is precious, yet the Society’s work has left
people living only half-lives. • Whistle Jack demands common ground before
he’ll trust the Lanterns.

A place at the table is offered — but it feels more like a curse than a
reward. The only sane choice seems to be destruction of the weapon.

I gave up Rook’s reputation to succeed on persuasion, to prove sincerity.
Whistle Jack presses us: “What do we all share?”
• Korr’s truth: every soul has worth.
• Korr’s sorrow: we are only living half-lives.

Finally, Vareth and Thistle step forward to persuade them both. The Lanterns
propose a compromise — to remove the weapon from Londra entirely, take it
outside the city.

The moment teeters on success. Hands reach across the divide.

And then — the bells toll.

The filtration towers overload. Whistle Jack retracts his hand. A sharp whistle
cuts the air, followed by a deep rumble that shakes the tower’s foundation. Fog
hisses from ruptured seams, swelling through the tower and pouring into the
streets. CAS and Penny Moor alike scream as the mists close in.

Korr stands stricken, face ashen: “We were so close.”

We flee down from the tower into the fog-choked streets. The Lanterns dive into
the sewers as the bomb billows above. Rook manages to disarm the device.

But a new threat emerges — a CAS exosuit, massive and looming in the dark. Its
voice carries: “The Fog is death. Division is a veil between worlds… and all
veils burn.”
