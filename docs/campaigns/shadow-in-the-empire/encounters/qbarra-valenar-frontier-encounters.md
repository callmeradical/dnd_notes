# Into Valenar: Treasure & Thematic Encounters

The following Points of Interest reflect how Q'barra and Valenar have been reshaped in the wake of the dragons' return. Each location provides actionable hooks for exploration and discovery. The wyrms' influence is active and omnipresent—storms that never end, ash that burns like prophecy, and mortals caught in the divine machinery of the new age.

---

## Hightower (Dragonwatch Isles)

**Encounter/Theme:** Once a Lyrandar fortress of skyships, Hightower now crackles with arcane lightning. A blue wyrm coils around its ruined spire, using the tower's focusing lens to channel storms across the sea.

**Rumors (d6):**

| d6 | When | Rumor |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Before | "Hightower was the jewel of House Lyrandar's skyship fleet—they could launch three vessels at once from those docks." |
| 2 | Before | "The elementals bound in that tower's ring screamed for weeks before the wyrms came. Some say they knew." |
| 3 | After | "Every storm in the archipelago starts at Hightower now. The blue one uses it like a conductor's baton." |
| 4 | After | "Salvagers who get too close report their metal tools magnetizing, dragging them toward the spire." |
| 5 | After | "They say the wyrm speaks through the lightning—gives commands to its cult in thunder-tongue." |
| 6 | After | "A Lyrandar heir is still alive in the tower's undercroft, bargaining with the dragon for safe passage." |

**Treasure Hoard:**
- Lyrandar flight harness (300 gp, grants Feather Fall 1/day)
- Lightning-infused dragonshard (+1 spell focus for lightning spells)
- Smoldering core of the tower's elemental ring (powers a submersible or airship engine for 3 days)
- 700 gp in stormglass shards recovered from the upper decks

## Draconath (Trade Town)

**Encounter/Theme:** Once a crossroads for Valenar and Q'barran trade, Draconath is now a marketplace under siege. Rival wyrm cults vie for dominance; each offers protection to travelers, none can be trusted.

**Rumors (d6):**

| d6 | When | Rumor |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Before | "Draconath was neutral ground—Valenar and Q'barran merchants both honored the market truce." |
| 2 | Before | "You could buy anything there: Valenar steel, Cannith tools, dragonshard dust from the deep mines." |
| 3 | After | "Three wyrm cults control different quarters of Draconath. Cross the wrong street and you're an enemy." |
| 4 | After | "The Sun-Wyrm faction wears golden masks. The Shadow-Scaled wear black. Never trust the Unbound." |
| 5 | After | "Merchants play the cults against each other—pay tribute to all three and hope none find out." |
| 6 | After | "A secret resistance hides in the undercity, smuggling refugees out through the old aqueducts." |

- False idol mask of the Sun-Wyrm (acts as disguise self, 1/day)

- Cache of smuggled Valenar steel (four +1 shortswords)
- Ledgers detailing secret wyrm cult hierarchies

## Taer Shantare (Border Fortress)

**Dragon Lord:** Balaur, the Beast of War

**Encounter/Theme:** Once a Last War fortress, Taer Shantare has been reforged as Balaur's forward bastion. The walls no longer ward against dragonfire—they thrum like siege drums, resonating with the rhythm of war machines being assembled within. Valenar elves and dragonborn drill in the courtyard under the watchful eye of their dragon lord, who walks among them as an elven knight in adamantine plate. Here, the strong judge the weak. Officers earn rank through trial by combat. The fortress doesn't defend—it produces conquerors who keep what they kill.

**Rumors (d6):**

| d6 | When | Rumor |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Before | "Taer Shantare was built during the Last War—a fortress designed to withstand siege engines." |
| 2 | Before | "Valenar claimed it after the war ended. Their warbands used it to raid Q'barran settlements." |
| 3 | After | "The walls thrum like war drums now. You can hear them from miles away—a promise of conquest." |
| 4 | After | "Balaur walks among them as an elven knight. He earned the Valenar's respect by defeating their greatest champion." |
| 5 | After | "Officers are chosen through combat. Kill your superior in the arena and you take their rank. You keep what you kill." |
| 6 | After | "Weakness is purged. The strong rise. Balaur doesn't coddle—he forges warriors worthy of conquest." |

**Treasure Hoard:**
- War-trophies vambraces (+1 AC, grants resistance to fire damage, engraved with names of the defeated, requires attunement)
- 1,000 gp in claimed weapons and armor, each bearing previous owner's sigil
- Kill-counter banner (displays the bearer's confirmed kills in battle, grants advantage on Intimidation)
- Arena champion's circlet (once per day, issue a challenge that cannot be refused without loss of honor)

## Jal Paeridor (Ruined City-State)

**Dragon Lord:** Balaur, the Beast of War (staging ground)

**Encounter/Theme:** Once a proud city of scholars, Jal Paeridor was obliterated in the first wyrm wars. Its floating shards still hover midair, held aloft by residual draconic magic. Balaur claimed these ruins through conquest and uses them as a proving ground. Warriors test themselves on the floating platforms—navigating deadly terrain, fighting in zero-gravity conditions, proving their worth. Those who survive the Trials of the Shattered City earn rank and glory. Those who fail become warnings, their bodies left suspended in the air as testament to weakness. Balaur walks the ruins in knight form, observing, judging.

**Rumors (d6):**

| d6 | When | Rumor |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Before | "Jal Paeridor was a city of towers and archives—scholars came from across Khorvaire to study there." |
| 2 | Before | "They claimed to have mapped every ley line in eastern Khorvaire. Their knowledge was unmatched." |
| 3 | After | "The city shattered when five wyrms descended at once. The ruins still float, suspended in midair." |
| 4 | After | "Warriors undergo the Trials of the Shattered City to prove themselves. Most don't return." |
| 5 | After | "Bodies float among the ruins—failures left as warnings. Balaur judges all, and weakness is punished." |
| 6 | After | "They say if you kill Balaur there, in his proving ground, you could claim his throne. None have tried twice." |

**Treasure Hoard:**
- Shard of suspended gravity (functions as feather token: anchor)
- 900 gp in skyshard fragments and scavenged war materials
- Prototype siege weapon core (unstable, requires attunement, grants +2 to attack rolls with siege weapons)
- Map of Balaur's experimental weapon deployments (reveals weaknesses in his war machine designs)

## Ravar Orioth & The Bay of Swords (Maritime Zone)

**Dragon Lord:** Balaur, the Beast of War

**Encounter/Theme:** The Bay of Swords has become Balaur's conquest-forges. Every ship that falls to his fleet is dragged here, melted down, and reforged—its strength added to his armada. "You keep what you kill" applies even to vessels. Wrecks harvested from the blade-coral spires become warships. Conquered crews who prove their worth in combat are given a choice: serve and share in the spoils, or die. The bay churns with Valenar shipwrights, dragonborn metallurgists, and warriors who earned their place through strength. Balaur oversees it all in knight form, his adamantine plate marking him as supreme predator.

**Rumors (d6):**

| d6 | When | Rumor |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Before | "The Bay of Swords was named for the blade-shaped coral formations—beautiful but deadly." |
| 2 | Before | "Skilled captains could navigate it. House Lyrandar ran trade routes through there for years." |
| 3 | After | "Every ship they sink is reforged into their fleet. They don't just defeat enemies—they consume them." |
| 4 | After | "Captured crews fight in the arena. Win three bouts and you can join the fleet. Lose once and you're forge fuel." |
| 5 | After | "Balaur appears in knight form to judge combat. The strong earn their place. The weak feed the fires." |
| 6 | After | "They brand each ship with kill-marks—one for every vessel it's destroyed. Some hulls are covered in scars." |

**Treasure Hoard:**
- Conqueror's hull plate (shield +2, salvaged from a legendary warship, etched with its kill-count, requires attunement)
- 1,200 gp in claimed naval trophies—figureheads, captain's wheels, ship bells from defeated vessels
- Brand of the fleet (hot-iron brand that marks warriors who prove themselves in arena combat, grants entry to Balaur's forces)
- Naval kill-ledger (leather-bound book listing every ship destroyed by Balaur's fleet, their captains, and who claimed the kill)

## Taer Valaestas (Valenar Capital)

**Dragon Lord:** Balaur, the Beast of War

**Encounter/Theme:** Taer Valaestas is Balaur's throne and the heart of his war-cult. The ancestral banners now bear a new creed: "You keep what you kill." The High Council earned their seats through conquest—each killed their predecessor or claimed their position in battle. Balaur appears as an elven knight in adamantine plate, a form he won by besting the Valenar's greatest warrior in single combat. The Valenar worship strength, and Balaur embodies it. The city is a meritocracy of violence: challenge and triumph, or serve those stronger. Judgment belongs to the strong.

**Rumors (d6):**

| d6 | When | Rumor |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Before | "Taer Valaestas was the heart of Valenar culture—where the greatest warriors proved their worth." |
| 2 | Before | "The High Council answered to no one. They claimed descent from the ancient elven heroes." |
| 3 | After | "Balaur earned his throne by killing the previous Valenar high lord. They respect strength—he proved his." |
| 4 | After | "The new creed: 'You keep what you kill.' Every position, every title, every treasure is won through conquest." |
| 5 | After | "Challenge anyone, even Balaur. Win and you take their place. Lose and you die. That's the law." |
| 6 | After | "The Valenar don't worship him—they follow him because he's the strongest. If he weakens, he falls." |

**Treasure Hoard:**
- Banner of the Conqueror (advantage on Intimidation checks, inscribed with "You keep what you kill" in Draconic)
- 1,500 gp in trophy armor—pieces claimed from defeated enemies, each etched with kill-marks
- Balaur's challenge coin (present this to any warrior to demand trial by combat, they cannot refuse without losing honor)
- Adamantine warhelm of the vanquished (AC +1, resistance to critical hits, bears the name of its previous owner who fell in the arena)

## GM Guidance

### Active Dominion
Every site reflects the dragons' ongoing reshaping of Q'barra. They are not relics—they are territories in motion.

### Exploration Hooks
Each location can spark a mission: negotiation, sabotage, scavenging, or rescue.

### Treasure as Story
Rewards are not mere currency; most are remnants of the old world or tools of adaptation in the new.

### Recurring Impact
Actions in one region may provoke wyrm retaliation or allegiance shifts elsewhere. The dragons are watching.
