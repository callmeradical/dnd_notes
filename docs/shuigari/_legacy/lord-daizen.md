Finalized Details for the Villain and Sect



Charismatic Noble and Hidden Tyrant

Public Persona:

	•	Appearance:
	•	Clothing: Wears elegant, dark indigo robes trimmed in gold, with understated jewelry.
	•	Physical Traits: His graying hair and faintly lined face exude wisdom and calm. His piercing black eyes seem to see through people, unsettling even seasoned diplomats.
	•	Personality:
	•	Daizen’s demeanor is serene, his voice soothing, his words calculated to inspire trust.
	•	He often speaks in parables, offering wisdom that hides subtle manipulations.
	•	Reputation:
	•	Known as a stabilizing figure in Takashima, Daizen’s mediation has resolved feuds and strengthened alliances between clans. His philanthropic works include funding temples, aiding the poor, and supporting local artisans.

True Role as the Sect Leader:

	•	Private Personality:
	•	Daizen’s calm hides an unrelenting ambition and a ruthless belief in the necessity of Tsurogami’s unbinding.
	•	He views his actions as morally justified, considering himself a visionary who must act beyond the petty constraints of honor or mortality.
	•	Abilities:
	•	Master Manipulator: Daizen uses his charm, wealth, and political connections to keep the Obsidian Circle’s operations hidden.
	•	Psionic Talents: He wields subtle mental abilities, such as Suggestion, Dominate Person, and Detect Thoughts, to bend people to his will.
	•	Aberration Tamer: Daizen commands two Umbrawraiths, large aberrations bound to him through Tsurogami’s influence.

Umbrawraiths

Large aberrations bound to Tsurogami’s shadowed power
	•	Appearance:
	•	Resembling eyeless panthers with tendrils of black mist extending from their spines. Their sleek, muscular forms shimmer like liquid shadow, and their claws drip with corrosive ichor.
	•	Abilities:
	•	Shadow Phase: They can pass through solid objects as though incorporeal.
	•	Psychic Shriek: A devastating psychic attack that stuns and frightens creatures within range.
	•	Eclipse Bind: Tendrils lash out, restraining a target and draining their vitality.
