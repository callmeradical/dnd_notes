## The Big Boss: General Kaien

### General Kaien, the Smiling Storm
**CR 7 | AC 17 | HP 145 | Speed 40 ft**  
**Legendary Resistance (1/day)**  
**Resistances:** Lightning, Psychic | **Immunities:** Frightened

**Appearance:** Layered silk robes (ink and stormcloud shades), wide-brimmed hat, luminous eyes, arcs of silent lightning across skin

**Personality:** Theatrical, cruel but charismatic, views combat as choreography and war as divine theater

**Philosophy:** "Every stitch in the world was sewn wrong. I simply carry the scissors."

#### Sequential Storm Actions (3-Round Cycle)

**Round 1 - Summon the Sandstorm:**
- 60 ft radius storm
- Ranged attacks have disadvantage
- DC 15 CON save or blinded until end of next turn
- Begins summoning storm-charge

**Round 2 - Lightning Surge:**
- Chain lightning through sand
- All enemies in storm: DC 15 DEX or 4d6 lightning
- Fail by 5+: Shocked (disadvantage on next attack and save)

**Round 3 - Glass Reign:**
- Crystallized glass shards rain down
- DC 15 DEX or 3d6 piercing + 2d6 lightning + blinded
- Fail by 5+: Roll 1d2, on 1 = permanent blindness (magical healing needed)
- Terrain becomes difficult (10+ ft movement = 1d6 piercing)

#### Combat Actions

**Stormcoil Strike**
- +7 to hit, reach 10 ft
- 2d6+4 slashing + 2d6 lightning
- **Crit:** DC 15 CON or stunned + forget minor memory

**Command the Wraiths**
- All minions +2 attack, auto move 20 ft
- Choose: summon 1d2 more minions OR current ones take second attack

#### Legendary Actions (3 per round)

1. **Lightning Pulse (1 LA):** Explode minion, 10 ft radius, 2d6 lightning DC 13 DEX
2. **Sand Veil (2 LA):** Heavily obscured until next turn, +2 AC
3. **Threadcut Step (3 LA):** Teleport 30 ft with thunder crack, path creatures DC 14 STR or prone

#### Lair Actions (Initiative 20)

1. **Shifting Sands:** Movement halved, dash requires DC 12 DEX or prone
2. **Flare Pulse:** Line of sight DC 13 CON or blinded until end of next turn
3. **Static Surge (1/encounter):** Casters DC 15 CON or lose concentration + 2d6 lightning

#### Minions: Shadeblade Wraiths
- **1d4 summoned per round**
- AC 13 | 1 HP | Speed 40 ft
- **Shadow Fang:** +4 to hit, 1d6 piercing + 2d6 poison DC 13 CON
- **Fail by 5+:** Paralyzed 1 minute (repeat save at end of turn)
- Immune to AoE and minion rules

## Campaign Themes

### Core Themes
- **Memory and Identity:** Loss threatens individuals and world
- **Sacrifice and Duty:** Binders gave up everything
- **Corruption and Redemption:** Characters struggle with darkness
- **Truth vs. Erasure:** Circle seeks to unmake history

- Threads and weaving (Tsurogami's metaphor)
- Shells and cracks (protection that fails)
- Storms and calm (disruption of peace)
- Mirrors and reflection (truth-telling)
- Greed and hunger (spiritual emptiness)

- Sacrifice everything to seal evil, or destroy it?
- Can corrupted spirits be redeemed?
- Cost of forgetting vs. burden of remembering?
- Use weapons that bind spirits in suffering?
- Individual desire vs. collective good?
- Is unmaking a flawed world justified?
