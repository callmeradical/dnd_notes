# Eryssyl

A Demiplane of Dread trapped beneath frost, illusion, and possession.

## Core Premise

The primary campaign-facing vector is the cursed snowglobe. It crosses worlds, seeds possession logic, and can pull victims into Krixarion's narrative gravity even outside Eryssyl.

Eryssyl is a snowbound Demiplane of Dread centered on the city of Hailspire and overshadowed by Frostclaw Peak, lair of the ancient white dragon Krixarion the Hoarfrost.

Krixarion is not merely a monster in a mountain. It is the hidden architect of the kingdom's decline.

For years, the dragon has extended its influence across Eryssyl through fear, enchantment, and a warped sense of beauty. It collects not only treasure, but people, places, and power. Villagers vanish and return as frozen statues. Ancient wards fail. The royal family of Hailspire is slowly being turned into living jewels in the dragon's collection. Entire communities are divided between resistance, appeasement, and worship.

At the same time, an older power stirs.

The ancient protections that once bound the dragon are failing, and the unraveling of those wards has drawn the attention of forces beyond the realm. A celestial pursuer, Atriel Solis, descends upon Eryssyl to demand restitution for something Krixarion has stolen. To the people of the kingdom, this angel may be salvation or apocalypse.

The player characters arrive at a kingdom on the edge of spiritual, political, and supernatural collapse. Their choices determine whether Eryssyl is freed, shattered, or remade.

### The Snowglobe Curse Across Worlds

The cursed snowglobe tied to Krixarion is not confined to Eryssyl. It appears across worlds as a recurring cursed artifact, carrying traces of the dragon's will and the logic of possession from one setting into another.

Use this as the canonical interpretation:

- Eryssyl is the Demiplane of Dread origin point.
- The snowglobe is the mobile curse vector.
- Other settings can receive the artifact without inheriting all of Eryssyl's geography or timeline.
- Contact with the globe creates story-pressure toward possession, curation, and fate distortion.

This allows Eryssyl to function as both a self-contained horror realm and a cross-setting contamination source.

## The Truth of Eryssyl

### What Eryssyl Is

Eryssyl is a cold and isolated realm, almost dreamlike in its borders, where winter has become metaphysical. Snow does not simply fall here; it preserves, silences, and remembers.

The kingdom is held together by three fragile things:

- The Crown in Hailspire
- The old warding powers tied to Frostclaw Peak
- The belief of the people that survival is still possible

All three are failing.

### What Krixarion Wants

Krixarion does not think like a conqueror. It thinks like a curator.

It believes beautiful things should be possessed, preserved, and immobilized forever. It sees:

- Frozen villagers as perfect keepsakes
- Noble bloodlines as crown jewels
- Whole settlements as pieces in a display
- The kingdom itself as part of its hoard

This is why its influence feels strange and theatrical rather than purely destructive. Krixarion does not simply raze. It collects.

### The Hidden Court Intrigue

Krixarion has infiltrated the royal court of Hailspire in the guise of High Magister Caldrath Myrvayne, a trusted advisor whose subtle enchantments have already begun to unravel the minds and wills of the royal family.

- The queen speaks in riddles.
- The prince wanders sleepless and obsessed.
- The king's judgment is fading.

These are not random symptoms. The dragon is preparing the royal family to become the greatest treasures in its collection: willing, dependent, and spiritually broken before being taken.

### The Older Binding

Long before the current age, the powers of the mountain and forest bound Krixarion through a system of ancient warding sites, rituals, and guardian compacts. Chief among them is the Frozen Circle, a ring of standing stones near Coldhaven.

These bindings have eroded over centuries. Some guardians are dead. Some have been corrupted by frost magic. Some have been forgotten entirely.

If the Circle is restored, Krixarion can be weakened, restrained, or stripped of its hold over the kingdom.

If it is destroyed, Eryssyl may become wholly the dragon's domain.

### The Extraplanar Crisis

Krixarion has also stolen something beyond mortal right, perhaps a relic, oath-bound soul, divine shard, or sanctified treasure, from a celestial order. This theft draws Atriel Solis, an angelic pursuer whose arrival threatens to turn a struggling kingdom into a battlefield between divine wrath and draconic cunning.

Atriel does not come to save Eryssyl. Atriel comes to balance a theft.

If the dragon is not surrendered, the kingdom may be judged complicit.

## Tone

Eryssyl should feel like:

- A winter fairy tale rotting from the inside
- A kingdom in quiet panic
- Beauty wrapped around horror
- Survival shaped by superstition
- Ancient pacts and dream logic beneath political collapse

This is courtly gothic winter fantasy with folklore, dread, and sacred ruins beneath the snow.

## Regional Structure

### Hailspire

The capital and seat of the royal court.

This is where the political story lives:

- Royal manipulation
- Hidden enchantments
- Fear of scandal and collapse
- Loyalists, opportunists, and spies

If Hailspire falls fully under Krixarion's control, the rest of the kingdom follows.

### Frostclaw Peak

The mountain lair of Krixarion and the physical-spiritual center of corruption.

Source of:

- The dragon's active power
- Frozen disappearances
- Corruption of ancient mountain guardians
- Access to deeper truth of Eryssyl's curse

### Frostmere

A grieving river hamlet at the edge of the Whispering Forest.

Human-cost entry point:

- Missing villagers
- Lifelike frozen corpses
- Whispering river omens
- Signs old spirits are waking in distress

### Coldhaven

A settlement pressed against Frostclaw Peak.

Represents resignation and corruption. The Whispered Ones preach that survival comes through reverence.

Key nearby features:

- The Mountain Gate (route to deeper lair-complex)
- The Frozen Circle (remnant of old bindings)

### Silverpine Village

A forest settlement at the southern edge of the Whispering Forest.

Represents resistance, old pacts, and memory:

- Forest guardians
- Shrine traditions
- Warding lanterns
- Hints that something older than Krixarion is stirring

## Main Story Arc

### Act I: The Frost Spreads

The party enters through a frontier settlement, naturally Frostmere.

Early truths:

- Villagers are being chosen, not randomly taken
- Dragon influence extends beyond the peak
- Warding sites are failing
- Communities are isolated but share one threat

### Act II: The Kingdom Is Already Infected

Regional investigation reaches Coldhaven, Silverpine, and Hailspire.

Revelations:

- Dragon-venerating drift in Coldhaven
- Weakening forest wards in Silverpine
- Royal family under magical influence in Hailspire
- High Magister Caldrath tied to all of it

Central turn:

The dragon is not outside the kingdom. It is inside its institutions.

### Act III: The Old Wards or the Dragon's Hoard

Two campaign vectors can run in parallel.

Path A, Restore the Frozen Circle:

- Exploration
- Ritual recovery
- Corrupted guardians
- Mountain and forest lore

Path B, Infiltrate the Hoard:

- Greed and temptation
- Cursed objects
- Living ice servants
- Trapped treasures
- Dragon psychology reveals

### Act IV: Judgment Comes to Eryssyl

Atriel Solis descends.

Consequences:

- Some see deliverance
- Some see another tyrant
- Court panic
- Cult prophecy claims
- Dragon blame-shifting

Central question:

Does Eryssyl deserve saving if it has lived under the dragon's bargain for so long?

### Act V: Fate of the Frozen Kingdom

Final threads converge:

- Royal enchantment
- State of the Frozen Circle
- Hamlet loyalties
- Forest response
- Atriel's judgment
- Krixarion's final move

Possible finales:

- Ritual battle at the Frozen Circle
- Court unmasking in Hailspire
- Descent into Frostclaw Peak
- Three-way confrontation: party, dragon, angel
- Kill, rebind, or bargain outcome

## Key Factions

### Krixarion's Influence

A web of fear, enchantment, servants, and admirers:

- Frost-touched beasts and elementals
- Corrupted guardians
- Court manipulation through Caldrath
- Cult sympathizers in Coldhaven
- Opportunists and treasure-seekers

### Crown of Hailspire

Weakening authority still carrying symbolic weight.

Potentially includes:

- Loyal guards like Captain Raelus Thornwright
- Suspicious courtiers
- Compromised advisors
- Royals who are victims, liabilities, or puppets

### The Whispered Ones

Dragon-venerating movement growing in and beyond Coldhaven.

Beliefs:

- Krixarion is divine or inevitable
- Survival requires obedience
- Old wards are blasphemous or futile

### The Old Keepers

A legacy rather than a fully intact faction.

Fragments survive in:

- Selene's scholarship
- Silverpine shrine traditions
- The Frozen Circle
- Ruins and warding sites
- Spirits that remember the covenant

### Atriel Solis

A faction of one backed by celestial authority.

Useful because it complicates morality: the dragon is terrible, but the angel is not automatically humane.

## Important NPC Roles

- Merris Greycloak: local grief and suspicion in Frostmere
- Reeve Haldrik Ironspur: fearful appeasement in Coldhaven
- Old Tilly: prophetic and unsettling voice
- Thane Brynja Wolfsdottir: resistance in Silverpine
- Eldric Vale: pathfinder into deep forest truths
- Sister Alunne: spiritual bridge to wounded forest powers
- Selene Tharwin: Frozen Circle scholar-practitioner
- Captain Raelus Thornwright: reliable bridge into court
- Sareth Dalthorn: greed and unreliable opportunity
- High Magister Caldrath Myrvayne: Krixarion's court identity
- Atriel Solis: judgment made manifest

## Thematic Throughline

Core theme:

What do people become under long-term domination by beauty, fear, and survival?

Location answers:

- Frostmere: grief and superstition
- Coldhaven: submission and worship
- Silverpine: resistance and memory
- Hailspire: denial and corruption
- Frostclaw Peak: possession as philosophy

Competing models of order:

- Krixarion: preserve and possess
- The Crown: maintain appearances
- Old wards: bind and balance
- Atriel: judge and reclaim
- The party: choose what salvation can mean

## Clean Campaign Summary

Eryssyl is a winter kingdom trapped under the influence of the white dragon Krixarion, whose obsession with possession turns people, politics, and beauty itself into part of its hoard. As villages suffer disappearances, the royal court falls under enchantment, and ancient wards fail, the adventurers uncover a truth buried beneath frost and folklore: the dragon has already infiltrated the soul of the kingdom. To save Eryssyl, they must navigate broken hamlets, cults, old guardian magic, a corrupted court, and the arrival of a celestial pursuer whose judgment may destroy the realm as surely as the dragon's greed.

## One-Paragraph DM Version

Eryssyl is a snowbound kingdom centered on Hailspire and overshadowed by Frostclaw Peak, where Krixarion the Hoarfrost secretly manipulates both crown and countryside. Villagers vanish and return frozen like art objects, ancient warding stones fail, cults rise in isolated settlements, and the royal family is slowly enchanted into the dragon's possession. As the players move from haunted hamlets to court intrigue to forgotten ritual sites, they uncover the truth: the dragon does not merely rule through terror, but through curation. When a celestial being arrives to demand repayment for something Krixarion stole, the party must decide whether Eryssyl can still be saved, or whether it has already become part of the dragon's hoard.
