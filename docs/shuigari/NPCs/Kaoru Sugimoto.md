## Overview
A wealthy merchant who travels alone, part of the Starcall Table gambling group encountered during the caravan journey along the Silver Road to Toriyama. Despite her understated appearance, she possesses more wealth than her companions and carries herself with effortless composure and quiet confidence.

### The Storytelling Wager (Night before the Great Stone Gate)
During a night at the Starcall Table, Shigeo wagered the Great Tortoise charm in a storytelling contest. Kaoru immediately wagered 100 gold kobans, taking the bait while others at the table folded.

**The Contest:**
- Shigeo told the tale of Atsuko, a woman from Kagemura who defied tradition to open trade routes - a grounded, believable story of quiet perseverance
- Kaoru countered with the story of Chiyo, the "Weaver of Blades" - a weaver's daughter who became a legendary warrior capable of commanding the wind itself
- Shigeo won by emphasizing believability and humility over Kaoru's more fantastical tale

**The Coin Trick:**
After conceding defeat graciously, Kaoru performed a dramatic flourish - flipping a gold koban into the air and miming a slicing motion with her hand. The coin landed on the table in two perfectly clean halves, leaving the crowd questioning whether it was sleight of hand or something more.

Her parting words: "I promise that next time, I'll not be so easily swayed. I've always liked stories with dramatic endings, haven't you?"

## Speculation & Mysteries
- The precision of her coin-splitting trick suggests either incredible sleight of hand or actual supernatural ability
- Her story of Chiyo the "Weaver of Blades" may have been more autobiographical than it appeared
- Her true wealth and purpose for traveling alone on the Silver Road remain unclear
- Is she simply a skilled merchant, or is there more to her identity?
