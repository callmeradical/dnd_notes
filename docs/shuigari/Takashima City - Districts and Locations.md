# Takashima City - Districts & Locations Guide

*A comprehensive directory of shops, locations, and inhabitants organized by the Three Tiers*

---

## THE UPPER CITADEL (Ue-shiro)

*Population: ~8,000 | Access: Restricted to nobility, elite warriors, master craftsmen*

### SHOPS & SERVICES

#### The First Forge (Dai-ichi Kajiba) - WORKSHOP
**Proprietor:** Smith Master Isamu (Ancient Dwarf)
**Specialty:** Legendary kami-forged weapons
**Access:** Forbidden except to master smiths with Lady Arashi's direct approval
**Notable:** Entry protected by both mundane and spiritual wards

#### The Crimson Armoury - ARMORER
**Proprietor:** Commander Taiga Steelshod (Half-Orc)
**Specialty:** Elite armor for the Mountain Guard's officers
**Wares:** Enchanted plate, blessed bracers, commander insignia
**Cost:** Premium - officers only, court commissions

#### The Sharpened Quill - SCRIBE
**Proprietor:** Master Archivist Fumiko (Human)
**Specialty:** Military records, clan genealogies, treaty documents
**Services:** Official correspondence, command orders, historical research
**Notable:** Repository for Arashi clan battle records dating back 300 years

### LOCATIONS

#### Ironpeak Keep - FORTRESS
**Ruler:** Lady Ryoko Arashi
**Key Areas:**
- Throne Room and Hall of Judgment
- The Hall of Blades (legendary weapon gallery)
- War Council Chamber
- Private armories and treasure vaults
- Lady Arashi's personal quarters

#### Shrine of the Iron Mountain (Tetsuzan-jinja) - CHURCH
**Keeper:** High Priestess Tsumura (Dwarf)
**Dedicated to:** Mountain kami, spirits of iron and stone
**Services:** Blessings for miners/smiths, dispute arbitration, spiritual counsel
**Notable:** Maintains wards on deepest mine levels
**Architecture:** Massive temple complex carved into mountainside

#### The Gardens of Ancestors - GARDENS
**Caretaker:** Elder Botanist Hana (Elf)
**Features:** Memorial stones for fallen warriors, meditation pools, cherry trees
**Access:** Open to clan members and honored guests
**Notable:** Annual ceremony during March of the Ancestors

#### Officers' Mess Hall - TAVERN
**Proprietor:** Quartermaster Roku (Dwarf)
**Clientele:** Mountain Guard officers, master smiths, visiting dignitaries
**Specialty:** Mountain Yak stew, Yak Butter Tea, strong sake
**Atmosphere:** Martial camaraderie, war stories, tactical discussions

### INHABITANTS

**Noble Residents:**
- Lady Ryoko Arashi - Daimyo
- General Kenta Arashi - Military Commander and Heir
- High Priestess Tsumura - Spiritual Advisor
- Smith Master Isamu - Master of the First Forge
- Warden-Captain Hira Steelwind - Commander of Forge Wardens

**Guards & Sentries:**
- Elite Mountain Guard - Iron Wall Company (50 stationed at keep)
- Forge Wardens (security detail for First Forge)
- Shrine Guardians - warrior-monks protecting Tetsuzan-jinja

## THE MIDDLE TERRACE (Naka-dan)

*Population: ~45,000 | The beating heart of commerce and craft*

### SMITHS' QUARTER (Kajiya-machi)

#### MASTERFORGES (Legendary Weapons)

**Ashen Phoenix Forge - WEAPON SHOP**
**Proprietor:** Master Smith Takeshi (Human)
**Specialty:** Katanas with ancestral blessings
**Services:** Custom legendary blades (6 month wait), reforging broken heirlooms
**Cost:** 500-2000 gold for commissioned work
**Notable:** Apprenticed under Smith Master Isamu for 40 years

**Moonblade Smithy - WEAPON SHOP**
**Proprietor:** Master Smith Yuki "Winterhand" (Dwarf)
**Specialty:** Naginatas and pole weapons
**Services:** Women's weapons, ceremonial blades, teaching smithcraft to daughters
**Cost:** 300-1500 gold
**Notable:** First female master smith in Takashima, trains only women apprentices

**Dragon's Breath Workshop - WEAPON SHOP**
**Proprietor:** Master Smith Kaito (Half-Dragon)
**Specialty:** Fire-tempered blades, dragon-touched steel
**Services:** Weapons for dragon-blooded warriors, flame enchantments
**Cost:** 400-1800 gold
**Notable:** Exhales flame directly onto blades during tempering process

#### WARFORGES (Military Equipment)

**The Iron Battalion - ARMORER**
**Proprietor:** Foreman Goro (Orc)
**Specialty:** Mass production of military-grade equipment
**Services:** Mountain Guard contracts, bulk orders, replacement parts
**Cost:** Standard rates - 50-200 gold
**Wares:** Yari, basic katanas, ashigaru armor

**Hammer & Anvil Collective - WEAPON SHOP**
**Proprietor:** Guild Master Taro (Human)
**Specialty:** Training weapons, guard equipment
**Services:** Quick repairs (while-you-wait), bulk commissions
**Cost:** 20-100 gold
**Notable:** Employs 30 journeyman smiths

#### SPECIALIST WORKSHOPS

**Kagamura Precision Tools - WORKSHOP**
**Proprietor:** Craftmaster Hiro (Gnome)
**Specialty:** Smith's tools, mining equipment, precision instruments
**Services:** Custom tool orders, equipment repair, calibration
**Cost:** 10-150 gold
**Notable:** Supplies all other forges in city

**The Tempered Shell - ARMORER**
**Proprietor:** Artisan Mei (Human)
**Specialty:** Decorative armor, ceremonial pieces, noble commissions
**Services:** Court armor, parade gear, display pieces
**Cost:** 200-800 gold
**Notable:** Intricate engraving and gold inlay work

**Ghostblade Enchanters - WORKSHOP**
**Proprietor:** Enchanter Akari (Elf)
**Specialty:** Weapon enchantments, magical tempering
**Services:** Blessing ceremonies, minor enchantments, curse removal
**Cost:** 100-500 gold (enchantment only)
**Notable:** One of few enchanters permitted in Smiths' Quarter

### THE IRON MARKET - MARKETPLACE

**Metalworks Plaza - Central Market**
**Market Master:** Overseer Daichi (Dwarf)

**Key Stalls:**

**Ore Trader Ichiro's Stand - MERCHANT**
**Proprietor:** Trader Ichiro (Human)
**Wares:** Raw iron ore, coal, charcoal, rare metals
**Services:** Bulk ore contracts, delivery to forges
**Notable:** Has exclusive contracts with three mines

**The Crucible - MERCHANT**
**Proprietor:** Alchemist Sora (Tiefling)
**Wares:** Tempering oils, quenching salts, forge additives
**Services:** Custom tempering formulas, metallurgy consultation
**Notable:** Secret recipes passed down five generations

**Mountain Herbs & Remedies - APOTHECARY**
**Proprietor:** Herbalist Grandmother Kiku (Human)
**Wares:** Medicinal herbs, burn salves, stamina tonics
**Services:** Healing treatments for forge workers
**Notable:** Knows every mountain plant's properties

**Traveler's Rest Supply - OUTFITTERS**
**Proprietor:** Merchant Hideaki (Halfling)
**Wares:** Rope, pitons, climbing gear, trail rations
**Services:** Mountain expedition outfitting
**Notable:** Also deals in "unofficial" maps of sealed mine areas

**The Singing Steel - MERCHANT**
**Proprietor:** Trader Yori (Bard, Human)
**Wares:** Finished weapons from various forges (resale)
**Services:** Brokerage for commissioned work, quality appraisal
**Notable:** Can "sing" a blade's quality by tapping it

### RESIDENTIAL DISTRICTS

#### The Forge Quarter Inn - INN**
**Proprietor:** Innkeeper Mama Tora (Dwarf)
**Rooms:** 20 rooms - mostly craftsmen and visiting merchants
**Rates:** 5 silver/night (common), 1 gold/night (private)
**Tavern:** The Hammered Heart
**Specialty:** Takashima Iron Stew, Blacksmith's Pie, strong dwarvish ale
**Atmosphere:** Loud, smoky, friendly to craftfolk

**The Copper Lantern - INN**
**Proprietor:** Host Kenji (Human)
**Rooms:** 15 rooms - quieter, more refined
**Rates:** 1 gold/night (common), 3 gold/night (suite)
**Tavern:** The Quiet Quench
**Specialty:** Mountain sake, refined fish dishes
**Atmosphere:** Respectable, popular with guild masters

#### The Baker's Dozen - BAKERY**
**Proprietor:** Baker Yumi (Halfling)
**Specialty:** Mountain grain bread, meat pies for workers
**Hours:** Opens 4am for morning shift miners/smiths
**Cost:** 2 copper (loaf), 5 copper (pie)
**Notable:** Bakes 300 loaves daily, all sold by noon

### TEMPLE DISTRICT

**Temple of the Three Moons - CHURCH**
**High Priest:** Sorin (Human)
**Dedicated to:** Tsukuyomi, Dendaro, Intara
**Services:** Moon blessings, celestial guidance, divination
**Architecture:** Three spires representing each moon
**Notable:** One of few temples where kami still (weakly) respond

**Shrine of Takemikazuchi - CHURCH**
**Keeper:** War-Monk Takeo (Human)
**Dedicated to:** God of thunder and conquest
**Services:** Warrior blessings, martial training, oath-taking
**Notable:** All Mountain Guard swear oaths here

**Hall of Honored Ancestors - CHURCH**
**Caretaker:** Priestess Hanae (Elf)
**Purpose:** Ancestor veneration, death rites, memorial services
**Services:** Funeral ceremonies, ancestor consultation rituals
**Notable:** Contains genealogy records for every major clan

### OTHER SERVICES

**The Healer's Crucible - HEALER**
**Proprietor:** Dr. Sato (Human)
**Specialty:** Burn treatments, broken bones, forge injuries
**Services:** Surgery, long-term care, herbal remedies
**Cost:** 1 gold/treatment (subsidized by guilds)

**The Mended Boot - WORKSHOP**
**Proprietor:** Cobbler Old Jin (Halfling)
**Specialty:** Sturdy mountain boots, leather repair

**Scribe Tomoe's Letters - SCRIBE**
**Proprietor:** Scribe Tomoe (Human)
**Services:** Letter writing for illiterate workers, reading services
**Cost:** 5 copper/letter
**Notable:** Knows everyone's family news in Lower Terrace

## THE LOWER YARDS (Shita-niwa)

*Population: ~27,000 | The working heart - miners, laborers, refugees*

### SHOPS & SERVICES

**The Bent Nail - TAVERN**
**Proprietor:** Burly Hana (Half-Orc)
**Clientele:** Miners fresh from shifts
**Specialty:** Cheap sake, mystery meat stew, gambling
**Atmosphere:** Rough, fights common, dangerous after dark
**Notable:** Black market ore deals happen in back room

**Miner's Mercy - HEALER**
**Proprietor:** Sister Ayame (Cleric, Human)
**Specialty:** Lung disease, crushing injuries, cave-ins
**Services:** Free healing for miners (temple-funded)
**Notable:** Campaigns for better mine safety, ignored by owners

**The Rusty Pickaxe - OUTFITTERS**
**Proprietor:** Merchant Genzo (Dwarf)
**Wares:** Mining tools, headlamps, rope, helmets
**Services:** Equipment rental for new miners
**Cost:** Low-quality but affordable
**Notable:** Will extend credit to desperate workers

**Widow Tanaka's Stall - MERCHANT**
**Proprietor:** Widow Tanaka (Human, elderly)
**Wares:** Preserved foods, dried fish, simple clothing
**Services:** Charity for destitute families
**Cost:** Extremely cheap, often trades for favors
**Notable:** Lost husband and two sons in mine collapse 20 years ago

### LOCATIONS

**The Miners' Gate - GATEHOUSE**
**Watch Captain:** Sergeant Kuro (Human)
**Purpose:** Primary entrance from mountain passes
**Security:** Moderate - checks for contraband
**Traffic:** Caravans, refugees, new workers

**The Deep Mines (Fukai Kōzan) - MINE**
**Multiple Entrances:** Scattered throughout Lower Yards
**Shift Foreman:** Boss Tetsu (Dwarf, harsh)
**Operations:** 24-hour shifts in rotating crews
**Hazards:** Cave-ins, gas pockets, sealed sections
**Notable:** Deepest levels sealed 50 years ago after "incident"

**Worker's Commons - COMMUNAL HALL**
**Manager:** Elder Keiko (Human)
**Purpose:** Shared meals, community meetings, shelters
**Services:** Soup kitchen, message board, job postings
**Capacity:** Can house 200 overflow refugees

**The Soot Market - MARKETPLACE (Unofficial)**
**Location:** Open plaza near mine entrances
**Vendors:** Unlicensed sellers, desperate traders
**Wares:** Scavenged goods, stolen ore, questionable food
**Security:** None - buyer beware
**Notable:** Circle recruiters operate here disguised as merchants

**The Broken Shaft - CULTIST LAIR (Hidden)**
**Location:** Collapsed mine tunnel converted to meeting space
**Occupants:** Obsidian Circle agents
**Purpose:** Recruitment, weapons cache, planning
**Access:** Through "respectable" merchant fronts
**Notable:** Players may discover during investigation

### INHABITANTS

**Miners & Workers:**
- Boss Tetsu - Cruel shift foreman, takes bribes
- Young Toshi - New miner (17), desperate to prove himself
- Old Riku - Veteran miner, knows sealed areas

**Refugees:**
- The Displaced Hundred - Recent arrivals from southern conflicts
- Speaker: Taro the Ash-Marked - Organizes refugee mutual aid

**Criminals:**
- The Ore Rats - Gang stealing from mines
- Leader: "Digger" Kenzo - Former miner turned thief
- Madame Silk - Runs brothel/gambling den in converted warehouse

**Priests & Healers:**
- Sister Ayame - Free clinic operator
- Wandering Monk Jun - Ministers to dying miners

**Guards:**
- Sergeant Kuro - Miners' Gate watch captain (sympathetic)
- The Rust Company - Private security for mine owners (brutal)

**Town Crier:**
- Young Kaito - Announces shift changes, safety warnings

## SPECIAL LOCATIONS (Throughout City)

### The Hidden Veil - UNDERGROUND NETWORK
**Controller:** Unknown ("The Veiled Master")
**Access Points:** Throughout all three tiers
**Purpose:** Smuggling, espionage, Circle operations
**Routes:** Secret passages connecting major districts
**Notable:** Obsidian Circle uses extensively; Kazuya may operate here

### The Shadow Market - BLACK MARKET
**Location:** Moves nightly between Lower/Middle Terrace
**Fence:** "No-Eyes" Michio (actually has eyes, nickname ironic)
**Wares:** Stolen goods, contraband ore, forged documents, cursed items
**Access:** Need introduction from trusted source
**Security:** Heavily guarded, violent consequences for snitches

### The Whispering Stones Network - SCATTERED
**Description:** Obsidian slivers with runes placed throughout city
**Purpose:** Circle communication, surveillance, coordination
**Locations:** Temples, markets, guild halls, guard posts
**Detection:** DC 18 Investigation or Arcana to notice
**Removal:** Dangerous - alerts Circle agents

### Frostwood Border Outpost - OUTPOST
**Location:** 2 hours north, mountain pass to Nagano
**Commander:** Captain Toshiro (Human)
**Garrison:** 50 soldiers, rotating patrols
**Purpose:** Border security, traveler aid, yokai watch
**Notable:** Players passed through en route to Takashima

## DISTRICT MOOD & THEMES

### Upper Citadel
- **Mood:** Martial discipline, quiet power, tradition
- **Sounds:** War drums during drills, ceremonial gongs, war councils
- **Smells:** Incense from shrines, oil on armor, mountain air
- **Danger Level:** Low (heavy security) but political intrigue

### Middle Terrace - Smiths' Quarter
- **Mood:** Industrious chaos, pride, competition
- **Sounds:** Hammers on anvil, roaring forges, bellows, guild songs
- **Smells:** Hot metal, coal smoke, sweat, quenching steam
- **Danger Level:** Moderate (theft, sabotage, accidents)

### Middle Terrace - Temple District
- **Mood:** Anxious faith, spiritual crisis, failing magic
- **Sounds:** Prayers, bells, worried congregations
- **Smells:** Incense, candle wax, fear
- **Danger Level:** Low but infiltrated

### Lower Yards
- **Mood:** Desperation, exhaustion, quiet anger
- **Sounds:** Mine warnings, coughing, arguments, grinding poverty
- **Smells:** Sweat, ore dust, unwashed bodies, despair
- **Danger Level:** High (violence, Circle recruitment, collapse risks)

## GM NOTES

6. Cryptic warning from High Priestess Tsumura

1. Forge accident - help needed
2. Guild dispute turning violent
3. Pickpocket attempt
4. Merchant offering suspicious bargain
5. Circle agent disguised as trader
6. Festival preparation chaos

**Lower Yards:**
1. Mine collapse - rescue effort
2. Refugee begging for food/work
3. Gang shaking down merchant
4. Circle recruiter preaching
5. Sick miner collapses
6. Riot over working conditions

### Notable Daily Schedules

**Forges:** 
- First shift: 4am-noon
- Second shift: noon-8pm  
- Third shift: 8pm-4am
- Hottest (best) work: noon-8pm

**Mines:**
- Similar 3-shift rotation
- Most dangerous: overnight shift
- Monthly: Blessing ceremony before descent

**Markets:**
- Iron Market: 6am-sunset
- Shadow Market: 10pm-3am (rotating locations)
- Soot Market: Noon-sunset

**Guard Changes:**
- Upper Citadel: Every 6 hours, ceremonial
- Middle Terrace: Every 8 hours
- Lower Yards: Every 12 hours (understaffed)
