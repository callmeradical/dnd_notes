# Taro the Hunter

- **Quote:** “He made me a blade for yokai.”  
- **Role:** Northern tracker indebted to [Master Hideo Nakata](Master%20Hideo%20Nakata.md) for forging a spirit-slaying knife that saved him from a Frostwood wolf.  
- **Hook:** Can guide the party through yokai-haunted trails in exchange for help repaying Hideo’s legacy—either by protecting his kin or reclaiming a stolen blade.
