# Lord Ichiro Kagemura

**Title:** Minor lord of a border fief under Daimyo Ryoko Arashi.

**Appearance:** A tall, stern man with a lined face and a sharp goatee. He wears dark green and silver robes, the colors of his house, with a modest katana at his side.

**Personality:** Ambitious but cautious, he is known for balancing diplomacy with subtle manipulation. His presence at the funeral suggests he has a vested interest in maintaining—or disrupting—the power dynamics left by Hideo's death.

**Motivation:**
- Publicly, he claims to honor Hideo's legacy.
- Privately, he seeks allies to secure his position against rivals in the Arashi clan.
