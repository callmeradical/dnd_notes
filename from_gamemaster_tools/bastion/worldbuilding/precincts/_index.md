## Bari

This is the hub of the wealthy and powerful where courtly games and espionage
play out among visitors gawking at the impressive architecture. The precinct is
also known as the Guildpact Precinct.

## Brescia

Many professionals live here in clean and orderly neighborhoods, in the shadow
of New Prahv, while mob bosses coerce residents in order to enrich themselves
and influence local politics.

## Verona

In the Greenbelt, nature has encroached into the urban environment to varying
degrees, and folk here provide bountiful sustenance and domesticated beasts for
most of the district.

## Udine

Constantly in a state of turmoil, the scarred streets of Precinct Four are a
proving ground for soldiers and marauders alike. Visitors here had best be
spoiling for a fight.

## Ancona

Precinct Five is where the learned folk of the Tenth District gather to discuss
theory or to put their knowledge to practical use in the precinct's many
schools, libraries, and laboratories.

## Cagliari

In the hardscrabble neighborhoods of Precinct Six, the working folk manages to
eke out a living by toiling at warehouses, docks, and factories controlled by
callous employers. When night comes, the residents hide indoors to avoid
becoming prey to creatures that stalk the darkness.

### Additional Notes

Nascosto keeps it’s headquarters in precinct six but operates all over bastion.

## Isernia

The realm of house Candiano, the plate passes by unnoticed and exists in a bend
in reality that obscures it. After the characters defeat lady Candiano and
repair the engine of Bastion, they will find a map that leads beyond the
Endless Sea.

### Additional Notes

A plate/district that is not talked about anymore. No one knows why the
precincts jump from Six to Eight.

## Imperia
The prison district. This is where hard labor and confinement is common place.
All residents of precinct eight are convicts and are unable to travel outside
of precinct eight. This is enforced by guards from the merchant houses (Ordine,
Macaellaio, and Zelante) and was formerly owned and occupied by the royal
family (Marivaldi), the old palace is now nothing more than a prison.

### Additional Notes

A prisoner that is able to come and go as he/she pleases, a member of House
Nascosto.
