## Session Recap

Session 022 opened with an immediate banshee clash at the underground threshold, then shifted into traversal and discovery as the party moved through hidden pipes into the undercity region beneath Takashima. The Brewery of Ninefold Reflections (Kokuei Shuzo) and its connected kitsune enclave became central to the session's geography. The undead barrier created a hard tactical boundary that prevented both hostile spirits and Takemura from pressing forward through the same route. The session's core pressure point was the Rokurokubi hydra encounter, with Krixarion's snowglobe influence escalating Yasei's personal stakes for what comes next.
