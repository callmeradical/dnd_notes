# Adventure: The Twice Cursed – Act III, Labyrinth of the Twice-Cursed

Act III thrusts the party into the Ryuseki Peaks—an astral-touched fortress reshaped by the Twice-Cursed Samurai’s fractured soul. The domain mixes treacherous mountain travel with a puzzle-laden dungeon crawl leading to the campaign’s first major confrontation.

## The Ryuseki Peaks
- **Reality Warped:** Cliffs curve back on themselves, time dilates, and sudden planar rifts spew aurora-colored winds.
- **Opposition:** Spectral tengu, animated basalt guardians, and lingering echoes of Soulsteel experiments challenge progress.
- **Environmental Hazards:** Thin air, ice storms, and shifting gravity zones demand Constitution and Dexterity saves alongside clever spell use.

## Labyrinth Highlights
- **The Blade Room:** Chamber of shattered Soulsteel prototypes. Investigating reveals memories of Hideo’s apprentices or grants advantage on forging-related checks.
- **Hall of Reflections:** Mirror-lined hall manifesting illusions of the party’s regrets. Failures produce hostile mirror doubles.
- **Chamber of Echoes:** Acoustically warped arena where voices accuse and taunt. Solving the sound puzzle requires Performance or Insight.
- **Planar Distortion Nodes:** Shortcuts that risk teleporting characters into side chambers haunted by yokai or free-elemental hazards.

## Climactic Encounter: The Twice-Cursed Samurai
### Phase 1 — Entrance & Parley
- Samurai appears amid crimson light, offering riddles about honor and sacrifice.
- Dialogue seeds: “Hideo’s death was a gift. Do you understand why?” / “Will you carry his burden, or break beneath it?”

### Phase 2 — Duel within the Throne Hall
- **Fractured Spirit aura:** Attacks deal slashing + necrotic; failed DC 13 Charisma saves sap Charisma/Wisdom temporarily.
- **Shifting Arena:**  
  - *Round 2:* Illusions obscure line of sight (Perception DC 14).  
  - *Round 3:* Platforms float; Athletics/Acrobatics (DC 12) to maneuver safely.  
  - *Ongoing:* Phantom Strike (Recharge 5–6) teleports the Samurai and lashes nearby foes.
- **Challenge of Honor:** Samurai marks one hero, focusing pressure and testing resolve.

### Phase 3 — Resolution Paths
- **Defeat:** Samurai kneels, revealing Hideo orchestrated his own death to become his grandson’s guardian spirit. Provides lore, a map fragment, or a Soulsteel shard.
- **Surrender:** If convinced through honor or empathy, the Samurai yields and offers their cursed blade (powerful yet burdensome) along with reluctant cooperation.

## Rewards & Fallout
- **Truth of Hideo’s Sacrifice:** The party learns why the funeral mattered and how the prophecy hinges on protecting the boy.
- **Labyrinth Spoils:** Soulsteel fragments, planar reagents, or ritual instructions to restore corrupted shrines.
- **Continuing Threads:** The Obsidian Circle’s influence lingers; the Raven Yokai’s omen points toward future threats in the peaks or Frostwood.

Act III should feel like the crucible that tempers the party—testing their convictions before they step into the wider politics and spiritual stakes of Shuigari.
