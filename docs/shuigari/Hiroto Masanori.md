# Hiroto Masanori

**Title/Role:** Ronin turned retainer for Lady Hana Arashi.

**Appearance:** A rugged man with a harsh demeanor, Masanori wears mismatched armor, a symbol of his fallen honor. His katana is a Soulsteel blade forged by Hideo, which he wields with pride and skill.

**Personality:** Ruthless and deeply embittered, Masanori has a sharp tongue and little patience for perceived insults. His loyalty to Lady Hana stems from her recognizing his skill and giving him a chance to rebuild his status.

## The Rivalry between Masanori and [Akane Hiroshi](Akane%20Hiroshi.md)

1. **Shared History:**
   - Masanori and Akane were once sparring partners in their youth, both serving rival lords in the same region.
   - During a skirmish, Akane defeated Masanori and left him humiliated. Worse, her intervention contributed to his lord's downfall, marking the beginning of Masanori's fall from grace.
   - Masanori holds Akane responsible for his current status as a ronin-turned-retainer.
