# Nagano

Nagano sits at the edge of the Frostwood within [Takashima Prefecture](Takashima%20Prefecture.md). It is a modest forest village that balances reverence for [Master Hideo Nakata](Master%20Hideo%20Nakata.md)’s legacy with the practical needs of travelers, hunters, and shrine-keepers. The settlement acts as the party’s launch point for *The Last Whisper of Steel*, providing resources, rumors, and a steady pulse of local drama.

## Services for Adventurers

### Rewards & Contacts
- **Mayor Hiroshi Takeda:** Assigns civic contracts and pays bounties through his steward at Takeda Manor.
- **The Path of Blades:** A small adventurers’ hall run by **Kana Yoshiro**, a retired swordswoman who tracks quests and mediates disputes.
- **Temple of the Three Moons:** **Reiko Matsuda** grants blessings, relics, or divine favors for services rendered to the kami.

### Rest Options
- **The Jade Lantern Inn:** Mid-tier rooms, warm baths, and gossip courtesy of proprietors **Yuto and Aiko Mori**.
- **Temple Sanctuary:** Communal mats for pilgrims and those who aid the shrine.
- **Village Hospitality:** Locals such as [Aya the Weaver](Aya%20the%20Weaver.md) or [Taro the Hunter](Taro%20the%20Hunter.md) occasionally host travelers in exchange for stories or favors.
- **Frostwood Camps:** Militia-protected campsite just outside the village walls for those preferring the open air.

### Resupply & Craftsmanship
- **Market District:** Everyday gear, provisions, and minor talismans traded beneath silk awnings.
- **Haruto’s Forge:** Crafts functional blades and repairs Soulsteel heirlooms with reverence.
- **Ayame’s Apothecary:** Stocks healing salves, herbal poultices, and rare Frostwood botanicals.
- **Temple Blessings:** Priests inscribe protective charms or sanctify equipment prior to expeditions.

### Research & Lore
- **Temple Library:** Scrolls on regional kami, yokai sightings, and ceremonial calendars; Reiko serves as guide.
- **Storyteller’s Hearth:** Alehouse where **Renji “Driftwood” Sato** gathers rumors from smugglers and hunters.
- **Village Elders:** **Yumi Harada** and other retirees share oral histories and guidance on surviving the Frostwood.

### Recreation & Downtime
- **Spring Equinox Festival:** Seasonal games, dances, and offerings to the Earth Kami; ideal for roleplay interludes.
- **Frostwood Dojo:** Sparring lessons with [Riku Matsunaga](Riku%20Matsunaga.md) for those wanting a training montage.
- **Jade Lantern Games Night:** Dice, cards, and storytelling contests—wagers often pay in favors, not coin.
- **Artisan Workshops:** Rotating demonstrations in weaving, pottery, and smithing; participants craft keepsakes or gifts.

## Key NPCs
- **Hiroshi Takeda:** Pragmatic mayor safeguarding Nagano’s autonomy.
- **Kana Yoshiro:** Guildmaster who keeps tabs on factions moving through the region.
- **Reiko Matsuda:** Head priest balancing kami diplomacy with political pressure.
- **Haruto the Blacksmith:** Devoted apprentice of Hideo, anxious to uphold his mentor’s standards.
- **Renji “Driftwood” Sato:** Informant and conflicted member of [The Grey Tide](The%20Grey%20Tide.md).

## Unique Features
- **Whispering Glade:** Resting place of Master Hideo; the funeral scene and many visions originate here.
- **Frostwood Outpost:** Frontier watch post managed by Nagano’s militia; doubles as quest hub for wilderness threats.
- **Weaver’s Market:** A decadal market bringing artisans from surrounding villages and traveling merchants under a single canopy.

## Market Faces
- **Hana Yamaguchi:** Master barterer; general goods and trail supplies.
- **Kenta “Two Coins”:** Vendor of charms and dubious relics—great for plot hooks.
- **Akiko Tanaka:** Fine textiles co-created with Aya; outfits dignitaries and performers.
- **Isamu Takahashi:** Grizzled armourer producing reliable kits for guards and travelers.
- **Mariko Fujimoto:** Sells fresh produce, herbs, and festival delicacies.

Traveling caravans rotate through the Weaver’s Market every ten days, bringing lumber from Hagane, preserved mountain fare from Futaba, Frostiron ingots from Shirogane, and luxurious silks from Shimizu. Treat the market as a living backdrop—prices shift with political tension, and the right merchant always seems to know a secret worth chasing.
