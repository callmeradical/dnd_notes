# Kaoru's Arrival - Calling In The Bargain (REVISED)

**Context:** Party entered Takashima with Kaoru's help at the gates. She's part of the **Wild Lotus Society** (ghost hunters and demon slayers). They agreed to investigate the haunted tea house in exchange for her assistance.

**Timing:** After sake scene with Mama Tora, before midnight  
**Purpose:** Kaoru arrives to collect on the bargain  
**Key: This isn't a new quest - they already agreed to this**

---

## THE KNOCK (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> You're on your second (third? fourth?) cup of sake when Mama Tora's head snaps toward the door. Her hand moves to the iron poker by instinct.
>
> Someone is knocking.
>
> Not pounding. Not urgent. Just... persistent. Three measured knocks. Pause. Three more.
>
> "Nobody knocks on that door after dark," Mama Tora says quietly. She stands, poker in hand. "Not for two years."
>
> The knock comes again. Three. Pause. Three.
>
> Old Hiroshi looks up from his soup. "That's... that's the old merchant code. 'Friend seeking shelter.'"
>
> But then - a voice calls through the door. A woman's voice, cultured and warm, with a hint of amusement:
>
> "Mama Tora! I bring your promised investigators. The ones who owe me a debt. May I enter, or must I collect payment on the cursed street itself?"
>
> (Beat)
>
> You recognize that voice.
>
> **Kaoru Sugimoto. The woman from the Starcall Table. The one who helped you enter Takashima.**

---

## KAORU ENTERS (Read Aloud - 3 minutes)

> Mama Tora's eyes widen in recognition. She lowers the poker and opens the door.
>
> Kaoru enters, and she's exactly as you remember - perhaps forty years old, elegant but practical, dark intelligent eyes that miss nothing. She wears traveling clothes in deep indigo and gray, and she carries a leather satchel across her shoulder.
>
> But something's different. At the city gates, she played the role of worried refugee. Here, on this cursed street, in this haunted inn, she moves with absolute confidence. No fear. No hesitation.
>
> She surveys the room - notes each of you, Old Hiroshi, the empty tables, the protective charms hanging from the beams. A slight smile touches her lips.
>
> "Mama Tora," she says warmly, moving to embrace the older woman. "It's been too long."
>
> Tora actually returns the embrace - brief but genuine. "Kaoru. You brought them."
>
> "As promised." Kaoru turns to you, that smile deepening. "And you came. I wasn't certain you would. Most people hear 'haunted inn on cursed street' and suddenly remember urgent business elsewhere."
>
> She removes her cloak and sets down her satchel. From within, you catch a glimpse of equipment: blessed salt, purification talismans, silver bells, and what looks like a very old, very dangerous dagger.
>
> "At the gates, when I helped you enter Takashima, we made a bargain. You needed access. I needed investigators - real ones, not another failed priest or frightened exorcist. People who don't run when things get strange."
>
> She gestures to the empty inn around you.
>
> "Well. You're sitting in a cursed inn, drinking sake while waiting for midnight and the weeping woman. I'd say that qualifies."

---

## MAMA TORA'S RELIEF (Read Aloud - 1 minute)

> Mama Tora refills everyone's sake cups with shaking hands - relief, not fear.
>
> "Two and a half years," she says quietly. "Two and a half years of priests failing. Exorcists fleeing. Shrine maidens making excuses. And Kaoru here..." (She gestures to the younger woman) "...she tried herself. Twice. Nearly died the second time."
>
> Kaoru touches her side unconsciously - an old wound?
>
> "The spirit is bound somehow. Trapped. I couldn't break the binding alone. Couldn't even identify what kind of binding it is. That's when I knew..." (She meets your eyes) "...I needed people who've faced worse than ghosts. Who've stood against the Obsidian Circle and won."
>
> "Word of your rescue of Kazuo Himura reached me before you even entered the city. When you appeared at the gates as refugees from Nagano, I knew. This was fate. Or fortune. Or desperation." (A wry smile) "Probably all three."

---

## KAORU'S BRIEFING (Read Aloud - 5 minutes)

> Kaoru sits at your table, pours herself sake, and begins:
>
> "Here's what we know. The facts, without speculation:
>
> **The Haunting:**
> - Started two and a half years ago
> - Every night, midnight to dawn
> - A woman weeping, heartbroken, desperate
> - Sounds come from multiple locations - inside the inn, from the street, from the abandoned tea house two doors down
> - Objects move. Doors open. Cold spots appear. But no one has been physically harmed.
> - Yet.
>
> **The Source:**
> - The old tea house - The Jade Blossom - closed three years ago
> - Owner was Master Chen. His daughter was Yuki, eighteen years old
> - The week Master Chen's wife died, Yuki vanished
> - Official story: she ran away with a merchant boy
> - Master Chen closed the tea house, moved away, was never seen again
> - Six months later, the weeping began
>
> **The Problem:**
> - The tea house is warded. Heavily. Someone sealed something inside.
> - The wards aren't keeping something out - they're keeping something IN
> - Every priest who's tried to enter either fled or collapsed unconscious
> - I tried twice. The second time..." (She touches her side again) "...something tried to pull me through. If I'd stayed another minute, I'd be whatever's in there, weeping with her.
>
> **What I Suspect:**
> - Yuki didn't run away. She was murdered.
> - Her spirit is bound to the tea house, unable to move on
> - Someone did this deliberately - trapped her, sealed her
> - The Obsidian Circle has been operating in Takashima for years
> - The timing is too convenient
>
> **What I Need From You:**
> - Enter the tea house
> - Find out what happened to Yuki
> - Break whatever binding holds her
> - Give her peace
> - And if her death was murder..." (Her voice goes cold) "...find who did it."

---

## THE WILD LOTUS SOCIETY (If Asked)

**"Who are you really?"**

> Kaoru's smile is sardonic.
>
> "You saw me split a coin at the Starcall Table. Shigeo thought it was a trick. It wasn't."
>
> She pulls out the dagger from her satchel - the blade gleams with spiritual energy.
>
> "I'm a member of the Wild Lotus Society. We hunt ghosts, slay demons, cleanse corruption. We're the people who go where priests can't or won't. We do the dirty work of keeping the spiritual world from devouring the mortal one."
>
> "I've faced yurei, oni, cursed objects, corrupted shrines. I've seen things that would make samurai weep and priests reconsider their vocation."
>
> "And this tea house? This weeping woman? She's the ONLY spirit I've ever encountered that I couldn't handle. That's why I need you. Because if Wild Lotus training and blessed steel couldn't break her binding... then this is bigger than a simple haunting."

**"Why should we trust you?"**

> "You don't have to. But consider: I helped you enter Takashima when the gates were locked to refugees. I've told you everything I know about this haunting. I'm sitting in a cursed inn at night, which most of Takashima won't do."
>
> "And more than that - you've fought the Circle. So have I. We want the same thing: Takashima safe, the Circle exposed, the innocent protected."
>
> "Besides..." (She refills her sake) "...if I wanted to betray you, I'd have done it at the gates. Much easier. Much safer. Much less sake involved."

---

## THE PAYMENT (Read Aloud - 2 minutes)

> Mama Tora speaks up, her voice rough with emotion:
>
> "I've told Kaoru already. If you solve this - if you stop the weeping, if you give Yuki peace, if you make this street safe again - I'll pay everything I have. One hundred gold pieces. It's all that's left."
>
> Kaoru adds:
>
> "And the Wild Lotus Society will pay another hundred if you can prove who killed her and why. Murder of the innocent is our jurisdiction. We'll handle the justice."
>
> "But more than gold..." (She looks at each of you seriously) "...you'll have allies. Real ones. Mama Tora has lived in Takashima for sixty years. She knows everyone, hears everything. The Wild Lotus has eyes throughout the prefecture. We fight the same enemies you do."
>
> "Help us. And we'll help you with whatever brought you to Takashima. Fair exchange."

---

## THE JADE BLOSSOM TEA HOUSE - DETAILS

### Location

> "Two doors east," Mama Tora says. "You can see it from the window."
>
> (If they look):
> Through the half-open shutters, you see a two-story building shrouded in darkness. The windows are boarded. The door is sealed with prayer strips, now faded and torn. Chalk wards cover every surface you can see. Some are standard protective symbols. Others... others are unfamiliar, wrong somehow, as if drawn by someone who understood the form but twisted the meaning.
>
> Even from here, even through walls and distance, you feel it. A weight. A presence. A grief so profound it warps the air around it.

### What They Can See Tonight

> "The weeping starts at midnight," Kaoru says. "You'll hear it clearly from here. Listen carefully - sometimes she almost forms words. Sometimes she's begging. Sometimes she's accusing. Sometimes she's just... lost."
>
> "If you want to investigate tonight, we'll go together. The wards weaken between midnight and dawn - that's when the binding is thinnest. That's when we have the best chance of entering."
>
> "Or..." (She studies you) "...we can wait until daylight, prepare properly, and enter when you're ready. The spirit has waited two and a half years. She'll wait one more night."

---

## INVESTIGATION OPTIONS

### Option 1: Go Tonight (Immediate)

**Advantages:**
- Strike while party is together and prepared
- Midnight is when binding is weakest
- Get it over with quickly

**Disadvantages:**
- Party may be tired or low on resources
- Midnight investigation is dangerous
- Less time to prepare or gather information

**Kaoru's Advice:**
> "I recommend tonight if you're ready. The longer we wait, the more time for word to spread. If someone sealed Yuki deliberately, they may not want us investigating. Better to act fast."

### Option 2: Investigate Tomorrow (Careful)

**Advantages:**
- Long rest to recover resources
- Daylight for external investigation
- Time to gather information from neighbors
- Prepare equipment and spells

**Disadvantages:**
- Spirit binding may strengthen during day
- Risk of Circle interference if they learn of investigation
- Another night of Mama Tora's suffering

**Kaoru's Advice:**
> "Caution has its place. If you're not ready - truly ready - then waiting is wise. Rushing into spiritual combat unprepared gets people killed. Or worse."

### Option 3: Gather Information First (Thorough)

**Available Sources:**
- Old Hiroshi (was here when tea house closed)
- Neighborhood survivors (few remain)
- City records (at administration building)
- Shrine priests (may have insight into binding type)
- Former tea house customers (track them down)

**Kaoru's Help:**
> "I can guide you to sources. The Wild Lotus knows who to ask and how to ask without drawing attention. But it will take time - a day, maybe two. And time is something Yuki doesn't have much of left."

## MAMA TORA'S PLEA (If They Hesitate)

> Mama Tora sets down her sake cup. Her hands are steady but her voice shakes:
>
> "I'm not a coward. I've run this inn for thirty-five years. I've survived mountain winters, riots, plagues, bandits. I watched my husband die and kept going. I've faced everything life could throw at me."
>
> "But that weeping..." (Her voice breaks) "Every night. Every single night. A girl's voice, crying for help, and I can't help her. Can't reach her. Can't even tell her she's not alone anymore."
>
> "Kuro - my husband - he always said: 'The measure of a person is what they do when no one's watching.' Well. No one's watching this street anymore. No one cares about a cursed inn and a dead girl's ghost."
>
> "Except you. And Kaoru. And maybe... maybe that's enough."
>
> (She refills everyone's cups)
>
> "So please. When you're ready - tonight, tomorrow, whenever - help her. Help us both. Give Yuki peace. Give me back my inn. Give this street back its life."
>
> "That's all I'm asking."

---

## THE MIDNIGHT BELLS

> Temple bells begin to ring in the distance.
>
> One. Two. Three. Four...
>
> Mama Tora goes very still. Old Hiroshi pulls his blanket tighter. Kaoru's hand moves to her blessed dagger.
>
> ...Nine. Ten. Eleven. Twelve.
>
> Silence.
>
> And then...

### If They Choose to Investigate Tonight:

> The weeping begins.
>
> Soft at first. Distant. But growing clearer with each sob. A young woman's voice, speaking through tears:
>
> "Help me... please... someone... anyone... I'm so cold... so alone... please don't leave me here... don't forget me... don't let me disappear..."
>
> Kaoru stands, buckling on her equipment. "That's her. That's Yuki. Time to see what's keeping her trapped."
>
> Mama Tora hands you lanterns, blessed salt, extra sake (for courage), and her blessing: "Bring her home. Even if home is just peace."

### If They Choose to Wait:

> The weeping begins.
>
> Soft at first. Distant. But growing clearer with each sob. A young woman's voice, speaking through tears:
>
> "Help me... please... someone... anyone... I'm so cold... so alone... please don't leave me here... don't forget me... don't let me disappear..."
>
> Kaoru closes her eyes, listening. Memorizing. A professional studying her prey.
>
> "Tomorrow then. But listen tonight. Learn her voice. Understand her pain. When we face her, we'll need to be guides as much as exorcists. She's not our enemy. She's our client. Remember that."
>
> The weeping continues until dawn.

## KAORU'S EQUIPMENT (What She Brings)

- **Blessed Silver Dagger** (+1 dagger, deals radiant damage to undead/fiends)
- **Purification Talismans** (can suppress spiritual effects for 1 minute each)
- **Spirit Binding Rope** (can contain incorporeal creatures temporarily)
- **Blessed Salt** (creates protective circles)
- **Silver Bells** (reveal invisible spirits)
- **Jade Compass** (points toward strongest spiritual presence)
- **Wild Lotus Training** (advantage on saves against fear and possession)

**In Combat:**
- Level 7 Rogue (Phantom subclass) / Level 3 Monk (Open Hand)
- AC 16 (Unarmored Defense)
- HP 65
- Sneak Attack 4d6
- Can cast Detect Evil and Good, Protection from Evil and Good
- Resistance to necrotic damage
- Evasion

**Roleplay:**
- Will fight beside party
- Protects them from spiritual attacks
- Knows yurei weaknesses
- But can't solve this alone (already tried twice)

## SCENE RESOLUTION

### If Party Accepts Tonight:

> Kaoru nods with satisfaction. "Good. Then let's prepare."
>
> (She distributes equipment, explains the tea house layout, shares what she knows about ward breaking)
>
> "Remember: Yuki is the victim, not the villain. Whatever's keeping her bound is the real enemy. Find the binding's anchor - an object, a symbol, a ritual component - and destroy it. That should free her."
>
> "And if we encounter whoever sealed her in there... well. The Wild Lotus doesn't believe in mercy for those who torture the dead."
>
> Mama Tora: "I'll keep the fire burning. If you need to retreat, this inn is warded. It won't keep her out forever, but it'll slow her down. Come back if you need to."

### If Party Asks for Time:

> Kaoru accepts gracefully. "Wisdom over haste. I can respect that."
>
> "I'll be back tomorrow at sunset. Bring questions, and I'll bring answers. Bring a plan, and I'll bring support. Together, we'll free her."
>
> (Stands to leave)
>
> "But know this: every night she weeps, she weakens. Eventually, the binding will consume what's left of her soul entirely. When that happens, she won't be Yuki anymore. She'll be something worse. Something hungry."
>
> "So don't take too long."

### Either Way:

> As Kaoru prepares to leave (or prepares to investigate), she adds:
>
> "One more thing. At the Starcall Table, Shigeo won that wager fair and square. Beat me at my own game with a story about quiet perseverance."
>
> (She looks at all of you)
>
> "Yuki's story ended in tragedy. But maybe... maybe you can give it a better epilogue. That's what you do, isn't it? Change endings that should have been different."
>
> "Show me I was right to trust you. Show Mama Tora her hope wasn't misplaced. Show Yuki that someone finally came."

> When Mama Tora sees her name on Hideo's tablet - "Daughter of iron, widow of courage, keeper of cursed street" - she breaks.
>
> Not loudly. She doesn't wail or sob. She just... stops. Sets down her sake. Stares at the inscription.
>
> "Kuro died three years ago. Same night Hideo wrote this. Same night Hideo died. He knew I'd end up here, alone, keeping this inn alive out of pure spite."
>
> (Her voice cracks)
>
> "And he knew you'd come. He sent you. Somehow, a dead smith sent you to help a stubborn old woman and a dead girl no one else remembers."
>
> (She refills everyone's cups with shaking hands)
>
> "If Hideo trusted you... if Kaoru trusts you... if you're sitting here despite the weeping and the warnings and the fear..."

>
