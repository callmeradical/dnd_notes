# Arc 2 Opening: The Road to Takashima

## Session Setup

### Opening Encounter: The Six-Armed Troll

**Location:** Road to Takashima  
**Discovery:** The party encounters a six-armed troll with an unconscious man restrained. Four dead bodies litter the ground.

**The Scene:**
- [[Iseri Tetsuo]] is unconscious, held by the troll
- Four corpses surround them (identities unknown)
- Combat or negotiation possible
- Tetsuo's condition critical

**Key Questions:**
- Who are the dead?
- What happened here?
- Is Tetsuo a victim or perpetrator?

### Getting to Takashima

**Options:**
1. Take Tetsuo directly to the city (if stable enough)
2. Make camp and tend to wounds before traveling
3. Skip to the city gates regardless

**Outcome:** Either way, the story moves to Takashima's gates

## The Gates of Takashima

### The Situation
Takashima's gates are heavily guarded. Guards are turning away most travelers - the city is dealing with an influx of refugees from the south. Getting inside won't be easy for a random group of travelers, especially if they're carrying an unconscious stranger.

Long lines of desperate people wait, hoping for entry. The guards are tired, suspicious, and have little patience.

### The Reunion

In the crowd waiting to enter, the party spots a familiar face: [[Kaoru Sugimoto]], along with the surviving members of [[The Starcall Table]] from Nagano.

**Present:**
- [[Kaoru Sugimoto]] - as composed as ever despite the chaos
- [[Daisuke "Silver Pockets" Inoue]] - looking less charming in refugee lines
- [[Haruki "The Boar" Takeda]] - uncharacteristically subdued
- [[Aiko Hayashi]] - armor showing signs of recent conflict

**What happened in Nagano?** The Starcall Table's presence here, looking worse for wear, suggests something drove them from their usual routes. They're survivors, not just travelers.

### Kaoru's Offer

Kaoru recognizes the party. She has connections - she can get them into the city. But it will cost them.

**Her Price:** A favor. She needs capable people, and the party has proven themselves before.

**The Bargain:** Entry to Takashima in exchange for completing a task once inside.

## Inside Takashima

### The Wild Lotus Society

Once inside the city, Kaoru reveals her true affiliations. She's not just a merchant and gambler - she's a member of the **Wild Lotus Society**, a group of ghost hunters and demon slayers who work on behalf of the citizenry.

**The Wild Lotus Society:**
- Hunt supernatural threats
- Work outside official channels
- Serve the common people
- Mix of warriors, spiritualists, and investigators
- Kaoru's role within them suggests her coin trick was no mere sleight of hand

### The Task: The Haunted Tea House

Kaoru needs the party to investigate an old tea house. According to reports, a vengeful spirit haunts the place, driving away anyone who enters. The owners are desperate - their livelihood is gone, and the neighborhood lives in fear.

**The Assignment:**
- Clear out the spirit from the old tea house
- Determine what it wants (if anything)
- Restore peace to the location
- Report back to the Wild Lotus Society

**What Kaoru Knows:**
- Multiple witnesses have seen a figure in samurai armor
- Objects move on their own
- Temperature drops when the spirit manifests
- No one has been killed (yet), but several have been injured
- The haunting began recently - something triggered it

## The Tea House Investigation

### The Truth

The "vengeful spirit" is the **Twice Cursed Samurai**. But the party will discover he's not the monster the rumors suggest.

**Key Discovery:** The Twice Cursed Samurai did NOT murder Master Hideo.

**The Real Story:** [To be revealed during investigation]
- What actually happened to Master Hideo?
- Why is the Twice Cursed Samurai here?
- What is he protecting or seeking?
- How does this connect to larger events in Takashima?

### Investigation Hooks
- Witnesses in the neighborhood
- The tea house owners' account
- Physical evidence in the tea house
- Spiritual/magical traces
- Connection to Master Hideo
- The Twice Cursed Samurai's own testimony (if they can communicate)

## Key NPCs

- [[Iseri Tetsuo]] - New party member, mysterious background
- [[Kaoru Sugimoto]] - Wild Lotus Society member, party's entry ticket
- [[Daisuke "Silver Pockets" Inoue]] - Starcall Table survivor
- [[Haruki "The Boar" Takeda]] - Starcall Table survivor
- [[Aiko Hayashi]] - Starcall Table survivor
- **Twice Cursed Samurai** - The "spirit" haunting the tea house
- **Master Hideo** - Murder victim (or is he?)

## Questions for Development

1. What drove the Starcall Table from Nagano to Takashima as refugees?
2. What are Iseri Tetsuo's immediate goals upon waking?
3. How does Kaoru present the Wild Lotus Society to the party?
4. What clues lead the party to question the "vengeful spirit" narrative?
5. What is the Twice Cursed Samurai's real agenda?
6. How does this tea house investigation connect to the larger Arc 2 plot?
7. Are there other Wild Lotus Society members the party will meet?

## Session Flow

1. **Opening Combat/Encounter** - Troll and Tetsuo
2. **Recovery/Travel** - Getting to know Tetsuo (or carrying his unconscious body)
3. **The Gates** - Refugee crisis, blocked entry
4. **Reunion** - Starcall Table, specifically Kaoru
5. **The Bargain** - Entry for service
6. **Inside Takashima** - Wild Lotus Society revelation
7. **The Assignment** - Haunted tea house mission
8. **Investigation Begins** - First steps toward the truth
