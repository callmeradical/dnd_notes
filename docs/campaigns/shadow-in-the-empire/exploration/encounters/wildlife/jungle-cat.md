# Jungle Cat

## Jungle Cat (Tiger)
*Large beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 37 (5d10+10)  
**Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 17(+3) | 15(+2) | 14(+2) | 3(-4) | 12(+1) | 8(-1) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Features

**Keen Smell.** The tiger has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

**Pounce.** If the tiger moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a claw attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the tiger can make one bite attack against it as a bonus action.

### Actions

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10+3) piercing damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8+3) slashing damage.

---

### Encounter Information

**Number Appearing:** 1 jungle cat (rarely 2 during mating season)  
**Environment:** Dense jungle, near water sources  
**Encounter Trigger:** Random wildlife encounter (#2 on d8)

### Roleplay Information

**Personality Traits**

- Solitary and territorial apex predator
- Patient stalker that observes prey for hours before striking

**Mannerisms**

- Moves with fluid, silent grace through underbrush
- Yellow eyes reflect light, giving away position in darkness
- Low growl before attacking, tail twitches when agitated

**Tactics**

- Stalks the party for 1d4 hours before attacking, following at distance
- Prefers to attack during rest periods when guard is down
- Uses pounce attack from elevated positions (fallen logs, low branches)
- Targets spellcasters or wounded party members as easier prey
- Attempts to drag unconscious victims away to feed
- Retreats if reduced to 10 hit points or less

**Environment Notes**

- Leaves paw prints near water sources and game trails
- Territory marked by claw marks on trees 8-10 feet high
- May be attracted to the smell of rations or fresh kills

## GM Notes

### Running This Encounter

**Pre-Encounter Setup**
- Determine stalking duration: 1d4 hours before tiger reveals itself
- Note party's current activity: traveling, resting, setting up camp?
- Identify the most vulnerable target: wounded, isolated, or cloth-armored PC
- Consider lighting conditions: tigers excel in low-light situations

**The Stalking Phase**
> *Roll a few Perception checks (DC 16) during travel. On success: "You catch a glimpse of golden eyes reflecting firelight from the darkness beyond your camp, but when you look again, nothing's there."*

**Building Tension**
- **Hour 1:** Subtle signs - rustling bushes, snapped twigs, feeling of being watched
- **Hour 2:** More obvious - paw prints in mud, fresh claw marks on trees
- **Hour 3:** Direct evidence - low growl from the darkness, yellow eyes reflecting light
- **Hour 4:** The tiger makes its move when conditions are optimal

**Opening Scene**
> *The jungle seems to hold its breath. Then, from the shadows of an overhanging branch, a massive shape unfolds—muscles rippling beneath golden fur striped with black. The jungle cat's eyes lock onto [vulnerable target] with predatory focus.*

**Encounter Phases**

**Phase 1: The Ambush**
- Tiger uses its full movement to charge and pounce on chosen target
- Prioritize pounce attack for knockdown effect
- If target goes prone, use bonus action bite attack
- Position near cover for quick escape if needed

**Phase 2: Sustained Combat**
- Focus on same target until they're unconscious or heavily wounded
- Use hit-and-run tactics if facing multiple opponents
- Attempt to drag unconscious prey away from the group
- Retreat to dense cover between attacks if possible

**Phase 3: Escape or Death**
- At 10 HP or less, tiger attempts to flee
- Uses difficult terrain and superior speed to escape
- May drop unconscious prey to ensure its own survival
- Could return later if still hungry and opportunity presents

**Key Mechanics to Emphasize**
- **Pounce:** Describe the explosive leap and crushing impact
- **Keen Smell:** Tiger knows exactly where wounded PCs are
- **Stealth:** Even in combat, tiger can hide in dense jungle
- **Bite/Claw Combo:** Vivid descriptions of savage attacks

**Environmental Usage**
- **Elevated Positions:** Fallen logs, low branches for pounce attacks
- **Dense Undergrowth:** Provides cover and concealment
- **Water Sources:** Tiger may try to drag prey toward water
- **Difficult Terrain:** Uses jungle obstacles to its advantage

**Skill Integration**
- **Animal Handling (DC 18):** Might calm or redirect the tiger briefly
- **Intimidation (DC 15):** Making yourself appear larger might cause hesitation
- **Nature (DC 13):** Recognize territorial behavior, predict likely actions
- **Survival (DC 12):** Find escape routes or identify tiger's likely hiding spots
- **Medicine (DC 10):** Stabilize unconscious party members quickly

**Social Resolution (Difficult)**
- Tigers are not naturally aggressive to humans unless hungry/protecting cubs
- **Food Offering:** Fresh meat might distract tiger (requires significant portion)
- **Territorial Respect:** Backing away slowly might allow peaceful resolution
- **Fire:** Large flames may discourage attack (not guaranteed)

**Complications & Variables**
- **Cubs Nearby:** Mother tiger fights to the death, no retreat
- **Injured Tiger:** More desperate and aggressive than usual
- **Multiple Tigers:** Extremely rare, only during mating season
- **Territorial Dispute:** Tiger may be fleeing larger predator

**Aftermath Considerations**
- **Tracking:** Tiger's territory extends 2-5 miles in any direction
- **Scavenging:** Other predators attracted to combat sounds and blood
- **Trophy Potential:** Tiger hide valuable to certain NPCs (10-50 gp)
- **Reputation:** Successfully driving off tiger may impress local tribes

**Scaling Options**
- **Easier:** Tiger is well-fed, more likely to retreat or ignore party
- **Harder:** Dire tiger stats, or pregnant female protecting cubs
- **Environmental:** Combat during rainstorm, at night, or near cliff edge

**Story Hooks**
- Tiger has killed important NPC, party hired to hunt it down
- Local druids consider the tiger sacred - killing it has consequences
- Tiger is actually a cursed human, remove curse brings reward
- Tiger's behavior is unnatural - something else is driving it to hunt

**Atmospheric Details**
- Deep, rumbling purr before attack
- Massive paws leave 4-inch prints in mud
- Tail lashing back and forth when agitated
- Eyes that seem to glow in firelight
- Musty, wild smell of a large predator
