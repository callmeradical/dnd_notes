## Daily Travel Procedure

Each day is divided into **three travel periods**: morning, afternoon, and night.

### 1. Navigation

- A chosen PC (or guide) makes a **Survival check**
- **DC 15** in jungle/swamp, **DC 12** in open terrain
- Failure: party goes off-course (1d6 miles/hexes in a random direction)

### 2. Travel Pace

- **Fast Pace:** 24 miles/day, –5 passive Perception, higher ambush risk
- **Normal Pace:** 16 miles/day, balanced
- **Slow Pace:** 12 miles/day, can move stealthily, advantage on finding hidden sites

### 3. Encounters

- Roll **1d4 each period**. On a **1**, roll on the encounter tables below
- Encounters may be of the following type:

  | 1d4 | Type of Encounter   |
  | --- | ------------------- |
  | 1   | Wildlife            |
  | 2   | Terrain/Environment |
  | 3   | Hostile Factions    |
  | 4   | Lost World/Ruins    |

### 4. Foraging & Supplies

- Each PC may attempt a **Survival check (DC 15)** to forage
- Success: 1d6 rations + water. Failure: nothing found
- In swamps or mountains, increase DC to 20

### 5. Weather

- Roll once per day:
  - 1–2: Heavy Rain — travel halved, all must make Con save (DC 12) vs. Exhaustion
  - 3–5: Overcast & Humid — no mechanical effect, but sticky/uncomfortable
  - 6: Clear & Hot — Con save (DC 10) or gain 1 level of Exhaustion without
    enough water

### 6. Camping

- Safe Camp: sheltered/dry ground. No checks needed
- Unsafe Camp: swamp, open jungle, or storm. All make **Con save (DC 12–15)**
  vs. disease

## Encounter Tables

### Daily Procedure

- Roll **1d4 at morning, afternoon, night**.
- On a **1**, roll on the appropriate sub-table:
  1. **Wildlife** (beasts, dinosaurs, predators)
  2. **Terrain / Environment** (hazards, storms, navigation issues)
  3. **Hostile Faction** (lizardfolk, dragonborn, scavengers, cultists)
  4. **Lost World Discovery** (ruins, dragonshards, ancient mysteries)

## Jungle Diseases

### **Jungle Rot**

- Feet and legs blister, wounds fester
- Effect: Speed reduced by 5 ft. If untreated for 3 days, –10 ft and 1 Exhaustion

### **Wyrmfever**

- Fever, chills, delirium
- Effect: Disadvantage on Wis/Int checks. Each long rest, Con save (DC 15) or
  gain 1 Exhaustion

### **Venom Chills**

- Shivering, pale skin, red eyes
- Effect: Disadvantage on Str checks/saves. Each dawn, lose 1d4 HP permanently
  until cured

**Cures:** 1 day of rest + DC 15 Medicine check, or _Lesser Restoration_
**Prevention:** insect salves, proper camp gear, avoiding swamp camps

## Summary & GM Notes

### Travel Mechanics

- **Travel:** 16 miles/day standard; navigation checks determine direction
- **Encounters:** ~2 per 3 days (25% chance per period)
- **Supplies:** Foraging checks vital; scarcity increases difficulty
- **Weather/Disease:** Heat, storms, insects, and rot grind the party down

### Table Feel

- 25% chance per period = ~2 encounters every 3 days
- Balanced mix of danger, survival, factions, and mystery
- Travel feels alive and unpredictable without overwhelming the party
- **Tone:** Survival and attrition matter — the jungle itself is an adversary
