## Background

Yasei comes from the Deep Village of Hikari, a settlement that has fallen silent - its kami no longer answering prayers, its people scattered or worse. As a servant of Hikari (both the village and possibly a deity or spiritual force), Yasei carries the hope of their people even as they face uncertainty about their home's fate.

## Current Situation

### The Deep Village of Hikari
Yasei's home village represents both origin and ongoing concern:

**What Happened:**
- The village was attacked by forces from Yora-no-kiba
- Kami have gone silent - no longer responding to prayers or offerings
- Current condition of the village is unknown
- Survivors' fates unclear

**What It Means:**
- Yasei left behind family, friends, community
- The spiritual connection that sustained the village has been severed
- The attack suggests larger conflicts beyond the village
- Yasei's role as "servant" may have saved them or driven them away at a crucial moment

### Servant of Hikari
The title "Servant of Hikari" carries multiple possible meanings:

**Interpretations:**
1. **Religious Role:** Servant of Hikari as a deity or spiritual force
2. **Community Role:** Servant/representative of Hikari village
3. **Dual Meaning:** Both spiritual servant and village representative
4. **Special Connection:** Chosen or blessed individual with sacred duties

**Responsibilities:**
- Carry the village's hope in exile
- Seek answers about the kami's silence
- Find way to restore spiritual connection
- Potentially avenge or rescue the village

### The Kami Silence
The most troubling aspect of Hikari's fall is the silence of the kami:
- No response to prayers
- No spiritual protection during the attack
- Connection severed, not just weakened
- May be related to larger corruption (Shindarasu?) or shrine desecration

### The Hope Bearer
Yasei "carries the Deep Village's hope amidst kami silence" - this means:
- Survivors (if any) look to Yasei
- Yasei represents continuity of their people
- The burden of hope is both honor and weight
- Yasei must maintain faith even when the kami are silent
