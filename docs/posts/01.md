As we are about to be executed. The ash warden says, "The everflame welcomes
all..." - we are shadows, conspirators, actions have endangered people...
sentence is death...

We are hung.

Before pain or death, something else does... a vision, fog creeps like spilled
ink in a city without shape. The world around us is dead quiet. The city
swallowed in ash and silence, no voice but our own heartbeat. Alone, but not.
Each of us stands at a different corner with a lantern.

What is the memory that sustains you, the memory that flickers within the
lantern:

> The lantern’s flame trembles—not from wind, but from doubt. Within it, the
> memory plays again:
>
> A cold dormitory, sterile and humming with aetheric current. A younger
> Rook—too thin for his uniform, ink-stained fingers gripping a chalk
> stylus—holds his breath as Chum, still alive, barks at a floating sigil.
>
> It was just a test. One of many. But the glyph held. It held.
>
> A teacher clapped him on the shoulder. Another took notes. And for one
> breathless moment, Rook belonged. Not because he’d obeyed—but because he
> understood. Because something he made worked. And because Chum was there to
> see it.

An endless voice, speaking from every direction at once. A presence moving
towards us, the fog parts and we see a monster, a familiar one, Vareth walking
out of the ocean towards us... a deep sense of dread, only together will the
light hold."

All six ropes snap, breathless but alive! Figures appear hurling canisters into
the air, the resistance! The squares, pennymoors! "Courtesy of whistle jack!"

Grayplank... first skill challenge! We escape!! We run along the rooftops
through the wards. Some of the wards the doctor and I are familiar with.

We get to the bridge, something like a shiver, something old turns it gaze
towards us. The everflame has been a symbol that has burned and given hope to
the people, we can feel the everflame flickers, twice and then dies. Dusk has
just set...Light the mirrors, fog, gray black mist, like a living tide, creeping
towards the bridge, hunting. Coming for us...

We are in a grangebound ward.

Old lady, in the fog, a second set of eyes next to them, old four eyes, she has
been out and experienced some of the mutation effects... she may be close to
turning.

Blue glass lantern, CAS issue, able to pierce the fog, and reduce the
effectiveness of the monsters. Very few still functioning, older invention,
finnicky and difficult to maintain, one is maintained one is broken.

Sniffling, crying, suddenly there is a gasp from within the group of citizens,
the shaw that the woman was wearing falls to the ground. She fell but don't know
where she went.

As we escape through the fog to the lanterns safe house. We can feel a palpable
change happen, a temple bell rings, the emberspire sparks a thread of fire...
the everflame re-ignites and the fog recoils.

Ceremony and renewal, the everflame burns once more, we are alive once more for
now!
