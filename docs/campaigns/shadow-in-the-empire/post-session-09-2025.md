## Narrative Recap: The Black Dawn and the Road to Newthrone

The storm had not yet lifted when the party made their choice. The infernal war machine, battered from its time in Avernus and the battles since, would not carry them further along Q'barra's coast. Instead, its twisted bulk was dragged across the broken sandbars and loaded into the cavernous hold of the submarine Black Dawn — their new vessel, their new refuge, their gamble.

### **The Black Dawn**

Captain Voss, resurrected and unmasked as Captain Dreyden d'Lyrandar, watched with folded arms as the crew he once commanded reluctantly obeyed new masters. The Black Dawn was not a prize easily won — it had been baptized in blood, claimed in the chaos of a lizardfolk ambush where velociraptors tore through sailors and raiders alike. The party's deception, invoking the Devourer of the Dark Six, scattered many foes, but not before steel was drawn and lives were lost.

**Eighteen crewmen had fallen.** Their gear, their rations, their weapons — all stripped and claimed, the spoils of a desperate alliance. The submarine was theirs now, though Dreyden alone held the skill to guide it beneath the waters.

### **Salvage and Secrets**

On the shore, the wreckage of an airship still smoldered, twisted timbers and shards of elemental binding rings scattered across the beach. The party salvaged what they could: tools, shards, and timbers, feeding the hungry hold of the Black Dawn. Each fragment whispered of a once-great craft brought low, its secrets now drifting in the tide.

Below deck, the infernal war machine's presence was like a wound. Even dormant, its chains rattled in the half-light, as though resentful at being confined. The submarine's sailors muttered and avoided its shadow, and even Dreyden's steady hands on the wheel seemed tighter with it aboard.

### **To Newthrone**

With the monsoon spent and supplies secured, the Black Dawn slipped along the coast. Days of tense travel brought them to the shattered grandeur of Newthrone. The city was alive with fear and ambition, split between Collaborators, mercenaries, and cults clawing for dominance.

Here, through Gareth's introductions, the party met **Lady Mirenna**. She did not ask for coin or simple loyalty — she demanded proof of audacity. Her task: steal the **Oathfang**, a Brelish relic wielded by **High Dreamer Saltheris** of the **Dreaming Maw**.

The heist was daring. Infiltrating the dream-temple, the party paralyzed Saltheris, ripped the sword from his grasp, and fled through incense-choked halls. By the time they placed the blade into Mirenna's waiting hands, she was already plotting a duel that would humiliate her rival before all Newthrone.

### **The Present Moment**

Now, the party sits aboard the Black Dawn, supplies for 14 days stowed in the galley, Mirenna's favor tenuously granted, and the infernal war machine chained below deck like a sleeping giant. Allies are fragile — Fellion with his schemer's grin, Dreyden with his hidden name, Mirenna with her fiery ambitions. Enemies gather in whispers — the Dreaming Maw licking its wounds, Aufgeben watching from Adderport, wyrm cults tightening their grip.

The sea stretches ahead. The coast is dotted with ruins, clanholds, and the shadows of dragons. Their journey into Q'barra's wilds has only just begun.
