# The Obsidian Circle

**Type:** Clandestine Cult/Secret Society  
**Patron Deity:** [[Shindarasu]], the Primordial Shadow (also known as Tsurogami, the Woven Tyrant)  
**Symbol:** A black circle interwoven with faint, shimmering threads  
**Motto:** *"In shadows, we weave destiny's threads. In darkness, we unbind the light."*

---

## Overview

The **Obsidian Circle** is a centuries-old clandestine sect operating across Takashima and the surrounding regions of Shuigari. Fanatically devoted to **Shindarasu, the Primordial Shadow** (also called Tsurogami, the Woven Tyrant), the Circle believes it is their sacred duty to shatter the seals binding this ancient entity and unleash its power upon the world.

They envision a new world order—one forged through chaos and bound under their control. The Circle operates through secrecy, manipulation, and calculated violence, infiltrating political, spiritual, and social hierarchies while spreading discord and advancing their ultimate goal: **the Ritual of Unbinding**, which will free Shindarasu from its prison and reshape reality itself.

---

## Philosophy & Beliefs

### Core Tenets

**Shindarasu as a Force of Renewal**
- The Circle views Shindarasu not as a destroyer but as a necessary force of change
- They believe the current world is stagnant and flawed, requiring Shindarasu's touch to evolve
- "Creative destruction" will birth a perfected existence

**Balance as Weakness**
- The Circle rejects Shuigari's traditional philosophy of balance and harmony
- They see such ideals as hindrances to progress and innovation
- Stagnation must be shattered for transformation to occur

**Unity Through Fear**
- By unleashing Shindarasu, the Circle believes they can forge a world united under their rule
- Fear of Tsurogami's power will eliminate conflict and division
- The Circle sees themselves as "weavers of destiny," destined to guide this new age

**The Path to Perfection**
- Members are promised power, immortality, or ascension under Tsurogami's new order
- Chaos is embraced as the crucible of transformation
- Mortals must reject false comfort and embrace their role in the great unmaking

## History

### The Age of Sealing (Second Age)

**The War Against Tsurogami/Shindarasu**
- During the Second Age, a coalition of celestial kami, mortal heroes, and divine powers united to confront the ancient primordial entity
- Tsurogami/Shindarasu was defeated and imprisoned in the **Veil of Night**, a prison anchored by the celestial moons: Dendaro, Intara, and Tsukuyomi
- The entity was sealed beneath Takashima with powerful celestial wards

**The Cult of the Primordial**
- After the sealing, remnants of Tsurogami's followers went into hiding
- A mix of aberrations, yokai, and mortals seduced by its promises
- They began worshipping it as the "Woven Tyrant"
- Preserved rituals and knowledge for a future time when the seals would weaken
- These remnants evolved into the first iteration of the **Obsidian Circle**

### Early Influence (Late Second Age)

- The Circle operated as a secretive cult
- Gathered relics tied to Tsurogami and researched forbidden texts
- Infiltrated temples, noble courts, and yokai clans
- Sowed seeds of chaos and mistrust to weaken the celestial wards

### Early Third Age: Temporary Defeat

**First Major Resurgence**
- Shortly after the **Sundering of Ydrafor**, when spiritual and physical boundaries across Atellas were destabilized
- The Circle saw an opportunity to restore Shindarasu

**Coalition Response**
- A coalition of Shuigari daimyo and kami executed a campaign to expose and root out the sect
- Many believed the Circle was destroyed
- The true leaders and core operatives survived, retreating into the shadows

### Rebirth Under Hideo Takatsume (Recent Decades)

**Note:** There appears to be two versions of the Circle's modern leadership—one under **Hideo Takatsume** and another under **Lord Daizen Kuroda**. This may represent different eras or conflicting accounts.

**Hideo Takatsume's Leadership:**
- Discovered ancient texts tied to the Circle
- Restructured the sect into a sophisticated network
- Transformed the Circle from a fragmented cult into a powerful organization
- Gained influence in courts, temples, and mercantile guilds
- Current supreme leader of the Circle

**Lord Daizen Kuroda's Role:**
- A shrewd and charismatic nobleman
- Discovered Circle remnants while researching his family's history
- Drew to its philosophies of "creative destruction"
- May be a regional leader, predecessor, or rival faction leader
- Vision: Tsurogami's return is rebirth, not destruction

**The Three Champions of the Obsidian Circle**

These generals serve as the hands and voice of Tsurogami's will, enacting the corruption of the world through different domains of power: violence, deception, and dominion.

**1. Lady Hoshiko, the Blooded Knife** - *The Silent Executioner*
- **Domain:** Assassination, ritual murder, cleansing of "tainted" bloodlines
- **Role:** The cold and loyal blade of the Circle; eliminates threats both internal and external
- **Appearance:** Always veiled; wears immaculate ceremonial white kimono forever stained crimson at the hem
- **Abilities:** 
  - Strikes in silence; kills through veils
  - Her blade can sever memories as well as flesh
  - Ritual murder specialist
  - Executioner of heretics and bloodline purges
- **Thematic Function:** Reinforces the Circle's purity-through-death ideology
- **Campaign Role:** Arc antagonist focused on ritual bloodshed and spiritual assassination

**2. Master Genji of the Thousand Eyes** - *The Forsaken Seer*
- **Domain:** Shadows, intelligence, scrying, forbidden knowledge
- **Role:** The Circle's spy master and arcane tactician
- **Network:** Whispers and shadow familiars span all of Takashima
- **Appearance:** 
  - Robes of shifting black-and-indigo ink
  - Shadow tendrils whisper around him constantly
  - Face always obscured
- **Abilities:**
  - Sees through shadows and dreams
  - Summons fragmented horrors from the Void
  - Master of scrying and surveillance
  - Commands network of shadow spies
- **Thematic Function:** Enacts deception and psychological warfare; confronts players with truths they wish to ignore
- **Campaign Role:** Arc antagonist focused on infiltration, espionage, and mental manipulation

**3. Kaien, the Smiling Storm** - *The Tyrant's Wrath*
- **Domain:** Destruction, elemental fury, spectacle of power
- **Role:** The Circle's war leader—brash, proud, and terrifying in combat
- **Current Status:** **Already defeated by the party** (or confrontation imminent at warcamp)
- **Appearance:**
  - Wields Stormcoil polearm crackling with stormfire
  - Armor resembles lightning-caught steel
  - Layered silk robes in ink and stormcloud shades
  - Wide-brimmed hat, luminous eyes
- **Abilities:**
  - Multi-phase combatant with sequential storm attacks
  - Sequential Storm Cycle: Sandstorm → Lightning Surge → Glass Reign
  - Can manipulate terrain with lightning (creates glass from sand)
  - Commands Shadeblade Wraiths
  - Memory-erasing critical strikes
- **Philosophy:** "Every stitch in the world was sewn wrong. I simply carry the scissors."
- **Thematic Function:** Represents force, tyranny, and dominance through overwhelming martial power
- **Stats:** CR 7, AC 17, HP 145, Legendary Resistance (1/day)
- **Campaign Role:** Arc I primary antagonist; theatrical, cruel combatant who views war as divine theater

## Goals & Operations

### The Ultimate Goal: The Ritual of Unbinding

The Circle's primary objective is to perform the **Ritual of Unbinding**, which will free Shindarasu/Tsurogami from its celestial prison. This requires:

**The Boy's Blood**
- A child born under a specific celestial alignment
- Prophesied to be the key to breaking the seals
- Master Hideo's grandson (in some accounts)
- The mysterious boy heir in Takashima (in other accounts)
- His blood is the "blood key" that can unseal Shindarasu

**Sacrifices of Yokai**
- Captured yokai provide spiritual energy to weaken the seals
- The Circle binds and corrupts yokai for this purpose
- Powerful yokai sacrifices channel energy into breaking wards

**Celestial Relics**
- Artifacts tied to the three moons (Dendaro, Intara, Tsukuyomi)
- Amplify the ritual's power
- Fragments of celestial wards from the original sealing
- Ancient Soulsteel weapons forged during the Second Age

**Shrine Desecration**
- Six sacred shrines form a network of seals
- Each must be corrupted to weaken Shindarasu's prison
- The Circle targets sites like the Shrine of Daichi-no-Kame
- Shrines are "locks" powered by spiritual guardians (like the Seven Binders)

### Active Operations

**Infiltration & Political Manipulation**
- Members hold positions in courts, guilds, and temples
- Subtly steer policies and decisions in their favor
- Pit factions against each other, ensuring enemies remain divided
- Sow discord to weaken unified resistance
- Embed agents in military, government, and spiritual institutions

**Relic Hunting & Artifact Recovery**
- Search for artifacts tied to Shindarasu's sealing
- Fragments of celestial wards
- Ancient Soulsteel weapons and tools
- Forbidden texts and ritual knowledge
- Steal records from the Temple of the Three Moons

**Shrine Desecration**
- Target sacred sites to sever their connections to celestial wards
- Corrupt shrine energies with shadow rituals
- Plant Whispering Stones (obsidian slivers with runes) for communication/surveillance
- Eliminate or corrupt shrine guardians
- Break the spiritual ties binding Shindarasu

**Yokai Manipulation**
- Bind yokai to the Circle's service
- Use yokai influence to spread chaos in rural areas
- Create distractions while advancing true objectives
- Harvest yokai for ritual sacrifices
- Corrupt natural spirits and kami

**Assassination & Sabotage**
- Eliminate those who discover Circle operations
- Sabotage supply lines and military operations
- Create instability through targeted violence
- Silence witnesses and investigators
- Remove obstacles to the ritual

**Espionage & Intelligence**
- Extensive spy network using Whispering Stones
- Infiltrators in every major organization
- Monitor threats to Circle operations
- Gather information on seals and wards
- Track prophesied individuals (like the boy)

**Propaganda & Manipulation**
- Stage "miracles" using the boy heir
- Exploit failing magic to recruit followers
- Present Shindarasu's return as salvation, not destruction
- Turn public fear and desperation into support

### The Vault Beneath Takashima

**Critical Secret Location**
- Hidden beneath Takashima city
- Contains the Well of Life
- Houses the One Tree (connected to Hikari's light)
- Stores Oniwaban Archives
- Holds ancient seals and binding magic
- May be connected to Shindarasu's prison

**Circle Interest:**
- The Oniwaban were massacred to access it (via Tenka's betrayal)
- Kazuya seeks access for Circle or personal purposes
- Master Hideo's grandson may be the "blood key" to access it
- Contains secrets about the original sealing
- Could be used to corrupt the One Tree
- Hikari's fading weakens vault protections

## Methods & Tactics

### Secrecy & Misdirection
- Operate through proxies and unknowing agents
- Create false trails and scapegoats
- Hide true objectives behind mundane cover
- Use legitimate organizations as fronts

### Patience & Long-Term Planning
- Plans span years or decades
- Wait for optimal conditions (celestial alignments, political instability)
- Build influence slowly and carefully
- Strike only when certain of success

### Corruption & Seduction
- Promise power, immortality, purpose
- Exploit disillusionment and ambition
- Use fear and desperation to recruit
- Corrupt through gradual exposure to Shindarasu's influence

### Violence as Tool, Not Goal
- Assassination is calculated, not impulsive
- Sabotage creates specific conditions
- Terror serves strategic purposes
- Avoid drawing unnecessary attention

### Exploitation of Crisis
- Capitalize on failing magic across Shuigari
- Use civil war and political instability
- Exploit refugee crisis and famine
- Turn natural disasters to their advantage
