# Geography and Locations

## The Archipelago of Shuigari

The islands of Shuigari are a breathtakingly diverse archipelago, each landmass shaped by both the natural world and the spiritual forces of kami and yokai. The geography of Shuigari is a mix of towering mountain ranges, lush forests, rolling hills, and tranquil plains. Shuigari is made up of four main islands with scattered smaller islands dotting the seas around them.<br /><br />**Shugane (Main Island).** The largest island, analogous to Honshu, is the political and cultural heart of Shuigari. It is home to the capital and numerous castles of samurai lords (daimyos). Its geography features mountain ranges like the Celestial Spine, as well as fertile plains ideal for rice farming.<br /><br />**Furenhoku (Northern Island).** A colder, rugged land akin to Hokkaido, Furenhoku is dominated by thick coniferous forests and towering mountains. The people here are hardy, living in small, fortified villages. They are famed for their expert bowmen and mastery over the harsh elements.<br /><br />**Torinokai (Western Island).** Known for its volcanic activity and hot springs, this island is home to monasteries and martial arts schools. The landscape is rugged, with dramatic cliffs and forests teeming with wildlife. The people of Torinokai are renowned for their religious devotion and warrior monks.<br /><br />**Minashima (Southern Island).** A tropical, southern island, smaller but fertile, known for its unique spices and fishing culture. The people here are more relaxed and culturally distinct from the rest of Shuigari, mirroring the distinct character of Kyushu.
