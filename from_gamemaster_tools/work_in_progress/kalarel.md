# Narrative
Kalarel was a priest of The Temple of She with Authority over Life and Death.
His last living duty was to seeing over the funeral of Tribuno Candiano.
During the service in Company Candiano's Compound on Precinct 7, he should have
chose his words more carefully. Lady Candiano, raging and reeling from the
death of her husband was taken by the words in a fit of rage. He was beaten and
battered. An aetherborn (Illario the Eternal), had consumed Kalarel's soul,
leaving his body behind as a husk, and then had transferred the soul of Tribuno

## Who is He?
Kalarel was a priest. His body has been captured by the spirit of Tribuno
Candiano, a amazingly powerful scriver and mage. Lady Candiano and Him have
been working to exact revenge on the merchant houses. They are being controlled
by Illario the Eternal, an aetherborn manifestation of an occidental warlord,
looking to complete his mission of destruction.

## The Red Gold

Kalarel is attempting to build an army of crazed monsters that do his
bidding. He is doing this by subjecting them to tests and breeding brutality
and rewarding their behaviors. The red gold is the reward they are
conditioned with. A magical currency that is used to satiate them, they will
viciously go after it until they have it.

### How is it Created?
It is created  by extracting their essence and infusing it heavily with aether.
Souls that have been trapped on this plane after being subjected to prolonged
exposure to raw aether.
