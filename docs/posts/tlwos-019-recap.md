## The Whispering Willow Tea House - Meeting the Twice-Cursed

### The Summons

The party received a wooden token bearing Master Hideo's maker's mark and a single word: "Debt." Following this summons, they arrived at the Whispering Willow Tea House in the Middle Terrace, where they were led to a private alcove.

### Kazuki Takemura Arrives

A scarred ronin entered - **Kazuki Takemura**, known as the **Twice-Cursed Samurai**. His appearance was haunting: a scarred face, one eye clouded, and a cursed blade (Yomi-no-Kiba) that seemed to hunger for souls.

### The Story of Two Curses

Kazuki shared his tragic tale:

**The First Curse:**
- Once a retainer of House Arashi
- Twenty years ago, faced an impossible choice during a yokai attack
- Chose duty over 37 innocent souls (including women and children)
- Was cast out from House Arashi for this decision
- Became ronin, masterless and dishonored

**The Second Curse:**
- Dueled a yokai to protect a village
- His soul was fractured in the battle
- Bound to the cursed blade Yomi-no-Kiba
- The blade binds failed souls to it, making him half-living, half-dead
- With each kill, the curse strengthens and the blade hungers more

### Master Hideo's Death - The Truth Revealed

Kazuki revealed the shocking truth about Master Hideo's death:

- **Hideo asked Kazuki to kill him** (mercy killing before Circle torture)
- Hideo was dying from illness and knew the Obsidian Circle was coming for him
- He chose death by an honorable blade rather than torture for his secrets
- Hideo's final words: *"Guard what they seek. Trust those who grieve true."*
- Kazuki has carried the guilt of this mercy killing for three years

### Master Hideo's Steel Tablet

Kazuki revealed **Master Hideo's Last Will and Testament** - a steel tablet inscribed with acid-etched silver characters. The tablet contained:

**Front Side - Hideo's Final Words:**
- Written the night he died (3 years ago)
- Explained his choice to have Kazuki end his life
- Warning about the Obsidian Circle seeking "the First Chain"
- Prophecy: "Those who would stand against this darkness will arrive when needed"

**Back Side - The Mission and The Names:**

**Kazuki's Mission:**
- Guard the vault until worthy allies arrive
- Test them, employ them, trust them if they prove true
- Don't face the Circle alone
- "Permission to trust again"

**THE LIST OF TWENTY NAMES:**

Listed First - **"The Six Flames"** (The Party):
1. **Fox, the Masked Assassin** - "Bearer of hidden face, seeker of lost master, carrier of implanted memories"
2. **Shigeo, the Braggart Brawler** - "Marked by Greed's Embrace, louder than thunder, stronger than doubt"
3. **Aoshi, the Last Waban** - "Final guardian of fallen order, seer of visions, bearer of oni blood"
4. **Thornbringer, the Druid** - "Seeker of the Well, speaker to growth, walker between wild and steel"
5. **Kanji, the Haunted Acrobat** - "Hero of the chasm, resolver of guilt, investigator of shadows"
6. **Yasei, Servant of Hikari** - "Voice of the silenced kami, hope of the Deep Village, light in darkness"

**The Revelation:**
Master Hideo knew about the party three years before they arrived. He saw them in the forge flames - their names, their burdens, their purposes - and inscribed them in steel as those who would come when needed most.

Hideo knew about:
- Fox's implanted memories and search for their lost master
- Shigeo's curse (Greed's Embrace)
- Aoshi's visions and oni blood
- Thornbringer's quest for the Well of Life
- Kanji's guilt about Akemi Takahashi at the chasm
- Yasei's connection to Hikari and the silenced kami

Steel cannot lie. This was prophecy made manifest.

### The Proposal - Infiltrating the Upper Citadel

Kazuki hired the party for **500 gold upfront** (500 more upon completion):

**The Mission:**
1. Identify 3+ Obsidian Circle agents in the Upper Citadel
2. Protect the boy heir (grandson of Hideo) from assassination/kidnapping
3. Discover what lies in the vault beneath the citadel ("the first chain")
4. Complete before the Festival of Ascending Iron (3-day deadline)

**Intelligence Provided:**
- Ancient vault beneath Upper Citadel
- Boy heir's blood is the key to the vault seal
- Lady Hana Arashi (boy's mother) likely Circle agent (unproven)
- Festival of Ascending Iron is when Circle plans to strike

**Additional Named Allies from the Tablet:**
- Kazuo Himura (already rescued!)

- Kaoru Sugimoto (weaver of stories, seeker of truth)

## The Iron Rest Inn - Sake and Reunions

### The Cursed Street

After accepting Kazuki's employment, the party traveled to the **Iron Rest Inn** on a nearby street locals call "the cursed street." The street was eerily empty despite the late afternoon hour - shuttered buildings, protective wards on doorframes, an atmosphere of abandonment and fear.

### Mama Tora Kaneshiro

The inn's proprietor, **Mama Tora**, greeted them. A mountain of a woman (60 years old), former blacksmith's daughter, stubborn and defiant. Her husband Kuro died three years ago (same week as Hideo). The inn once thrived but now sits nearly empty due to the curse.

**When Mama Tora saw her name on Hideo's tablet:**
She froze. "Daughter of iron, widow of courage, keeper of cursed street... He knew. Somehow he knew I'd end up here, alone, holding this place together with spite and sake. If Hideo trusted you... then I trust you too."

### Sake and Stories

Mama Tora served the party her finest sake (Mountain Peak sake from her personal stock). The party bonded over drinks, sharing stories and processing the weight of Master Hideo's prophecy.

### The Starcall Table Returns

During the evening, familiar faces arrived: **The Starcall Table** members from the caravan journey to Toriyama:

- **Kaoru Sugimoto** - The elegant merchant/gambler who lost 100 gold to Shigeo in the storytelling wager
- **Daisuke "Silver Pockets" Inoue** - The charming younger gambler
- **Haruki "The Boar" Takeda** - The boisterous drinker
- **Aiko Hayashi** - The quiet, intense woman

**Kaoru's Revelation:**
When she saw her name on Hideo's tablet - "Weaver of stories, seeker of truth" - she revealed her true role:

**Leader of the Starcall Table** AND **Member of the Hidden Lotus Society** - a clandestine organization of ghost hunters and demon slayers who work outside official channels to help common folk with supernatural threats.

Her coin-splitting trick at the Starcall Table wasn't sleight of hand - it was real supernatural ability from her Hidden Lotus training.

## A New Quest - The Missing Blacksmith's Daughter

### Kaoru's Request

Kaoru explained a troubling case the Hidden Lotus Society was investigating:

**The Victim:**
- A young girl, daughter of **Kaito** (half-dragon blacksmith in the Iron Market)
- Disappeared several days ago
- No trace, no witnesses, no body

**The Problem:**
The city guard won't help. They claim lack of resources, but the truth is worse - corruption runs deep in the Mountain Guard. Captain Saan, Master of the Watch for the Middle Terrace, is particularly notorious for ignoring crimes against commoners.

**Why the Hidden Lotus Society Exists:**
Because official channels have failed. When the guard won't protect the people, someone must. The Hidden Lotus fills that gap - investigating crimes, hunting supernatural threats, and bringing justice where the system refuses to.

**The Request:**
Help find Kaito's daughter. Investigate the guard. Uncover what's really happening in Takashima's streets.

The party agreed to help.

## Guard Tower of the Middle Terrace - Infiltration

### Journey to the Guard Tower

The next morning, the party traveled to the guard tower that oversees the Middle Terrace. The imposing structure serves as headquarters for the local Mountain Guard contingent.

### A Familiar Face

Upon arrival, the party discovered **Kento** - Shigeo's eldest son from his first marriage - working as a guard at this very tower. 

Kento serves directly under **Master of the Watch Saan**, and the reunion was... complicated. Kento seemed professional but wary, caught between family loyalty and duty to his commanding officer.

### Master of the Watch Saan

The party met with **Saan**, and the meeting went poorly:

**Saan's Demeanor:**
- Dismissive, arrogant, clearly corrupt
- Showed particular disdain for Shigeo
- Made it clear he considers investigating missing commoners beneath the guard's dignity
- Implied resources are "better spent elsewhere"
- Refused to provide any meaningful help with Kaito's missing daughter

**The Atmosphere:**
The guard tower reeked of corruption. Guards lounged about, discipline was lax, and Saan's office was lavishly furnished - far beyond what a guard captain's salary should afford.

### The Heist - Stealing the Ledger

During the meeting with Saan, the party noticed several leather-bound ledgers on his desk - clearly records of some kind. Through clever distraction and expert sleight of hand (legerdemain), they managed to steal **one of five ledgers** without Saan noticing.

### The Coded Message

Upon examining the stolen ledger later, the party discovered:

**Contents:**
- Standard guard duty rosters, patrol schedules, incident reports
- **But on the last 5 pages:** A coded message
- The cipher was sophisticated - not simple substitution
- Numbers, symbols, arrangements that suggested a book cipher

**The Cipher Key Discovery:**
Sharp-eyed party members recalled seeing a specific book in Saan's office - an unusual volume that seemed out of place among military records. They realized this book is likely the **primer/key** needed to decode the message.

### The Dilemma

The session ended with the party facing a crucial decision:

**The Problem:**
- They have Saan's stolen ledger
- It will eventually be discovered missing
- They need the cipher key book from Saan's office
- But returning to the guard tower raises suspicion

**Options Being Debated:**
- Sneak back in during the night
- Create another distraction for a second theft
- Use Kento's access somehow
- Return the ledger and find another way to investigate
- Decode the message without the key book
