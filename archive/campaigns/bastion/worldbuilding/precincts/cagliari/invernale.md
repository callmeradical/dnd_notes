**This is from the book:**

> The walled heart of Invernale seem strangely empty.
Inside the walls, the few villagers you see walking around appear nervous,
pausing to give furtive glances before darting away. Invernale is a walled off
neighborhood in Cagliari. They keep to their own, and have their own militia
(neighborhood watch type of thing). A small city in a much bigger city. They
have pretty much everything they need within their walls, and the walls are
much older than the buildings inside, save for one. A tower, ancient, and
crumbling that looms over the borough. Most residents of Invernale don’t go
beyond the walls. The majority of villagers are craftsmen and priests. A small
group of them manage to grow food on very paultry farms that are the size of a
city block. Wagons occasionally head east down old King’s Road which leads to
larger markets some ways down the road through tennements and ruins. Sometimes
wagons come into Invernale, causing the villagers to excitedly gather in the
Market Square to hear news of the outside world and to buy new and exotic
goods.

### Leadership

Ernesto Padraigianni, the socalled Lord of Invernale, is descended from the
noble family that ruled the area under edict of the old
empire. Folks around Invernale were happy with Lord Padraigianni’s father’s
authority, and they have found answering to the rules of the new lord no more
arduous. On the other hand, Ernesto doesn’t have absolute power. For instance,
he has been unable to raise a force from among the villagers to deal with the
kobold problem. A problem that is more of a peculiarity... Whenever someone
dies in this district within minutes a band of kobolds comes to claim the body
under the watchful presence of the local priest, Kalarel. He pays the
proprietor for his inconvenience and the right to the body, and pays with red
platinum. Ernesto commands the Invernale Regulars, a core group of ten
soldiers who perform guard and police functions in and around the village. The
Regulars patrol in pairs. To augment this force, Padraig can muster a group of
about fifty civilians, given a day’s notice. However, he can only do this if
the village itself is threatened—the villagers have no desire to patrol the
streets, particularly at night or beyond the walls.

### Demographics
The population of Invernale is predominantly human, with a scattering of dwarf
families, and a handful of individuals of other common races, including a
couple of elves.

### Economics

Invernale is primarly an economy based on barter and trade, though Lord
Padraigianni keeps a monetary system afloat with his own coin. Adventurers
should have no trouble purchasing appropriate items for which they have
sufficient coin. Outer Gate and Walls Open by day, closed and barred at night,
the outer gate is policed by two guards regardless of the hour. They nod
amiably to locals, and point travelers (whom they assume to be traders) to
Wrafton’s Inn. Invernale’s walls have a narrow parapet extending around the
inside edge. If Invernale comes under attack, the Regulars and what militia can
be raised from among the civilians guard the walls to stave off the threat.


### Locations

##### Wrafton’s Inn

This spacious inn and tavern serves as the public house for the
neighborhood. Like alehouses everywhere, Wrafton’s Inn offers beer, wine, and,
on occasion, spirits. Meals are served for those with the coin, and beds for
travelers are available. A crowd of villagers gathers each night to drink,
gossip, sing, and play games of chance. In addition to village residents, any
travelers passing through Invernale are found here.

Anyone in Wrafton’s knows the general history of the neighborhood and the
nearby ruined keep. Only Valthrun knows that the ruined keep was built to
contain a rift into the Shadowfell.

##### Eilian the Old
This old farmer is a regular customer at Wrafton’s. Every night, Eilian takes a
seat at a table in the corner. He has a farm down in the valley along the Old
King’s Road. Eilian has an interest in Invernale’s history. He is a good source
for local information, and he loves to talk.

##### Valthrun the Prescient
Valthrun is a sage and scholar who lives in a tower within Invernale’s walls.
On occasion he shows up in Wrafton’s to socialize. Valthrun is knowledgeable
about the area, though he knows nothing of death cult activity in the vicinity.
He does know about the sealed rift beneath the ruins of the old keep, but he
doesn’t discuss such things with just any adventurer, and he never talks about
it with the villagers since he doesn’t want to cause a panic. Valthrun is a
good listener, asking just enough questions to keep whomever he speaks with
talking.

##### Lord Padraig
From time to time, the town’s lord (level 3 human warlord) visits Wrafton’s.
All those present doff their hats and call him Lord, after which he retreats to
a corner table and sips beer. He is amenable to speaking with adventurers who
approach him. He initially assumes they are dignitaries from another village or
possibly merchants scouting for new trade. Regardless, if he believes they are
merely treasure hunters, his respect diminishes unless they prove their worth.

##### Ninaran
A quiet elf hunter who usually drinks alone, Ninaran is not interested in
conversation. Stiff and bitter in demeanor, she is Kalarel’s agent in Invernale
and reports back to him about happenings in the village.

##### Delphina Moongem
Delphina turned away from the forest of her elven
roots to live an urban existence, if Invernale can truly be called such.
Delphina can be found in the square on Market Day selling wildflowers from her
cart. She is happy to tell visitors about Invernale. She collects wildflowers
north of the village, and she has seen goblins around the ruined keep visible
in the foothills to the north.

#### Market Square
Every other day or so, carts and wagons gather in the square and offer goods to
the people of Invernale. Once each week, the official Market Day acts as a
siren’s song, calling most of the villagers to shop and socialize in the
square. Farmers sell produce, hunters hawk smoked meats, villagers sell
crafts, and sometimes a trader from the east sells implements or costly goods.
On any given day, there is a 50% chance that items from the adventuring gear
list in the Player’s Handbook are available for sale here. Adventurers will
almost never find armor, weapons, implements, or magic items in the market
square, however.


#### Stables
On market days these stables are filled
with mounts and other beasts of burden used by sellers to haul wares. Trav-
elers can stable their mounts here for 2 sp per day. Rarely (10% chance), the
stable master has a riding horse or wagon for sale, as listed in the Player’s
Handbook.

#### Smithy
A dwarf named Thair Coalstriker owns the village smithy. PCs
can purchase a variety of mundane wares here, including spikes, weapons, heavy
armor, and so on. Simple weapons are readily available, but military weapons
require one day to complete, and superior weapons require a week of work.


#### Valthrun’s Tower
This five-story structure is the highest building in
Invernale. The tower is rumored to be over 300 years old, and Valthrun the
Prescient is its most recent resident. Valthrun doesn’t use much of the tower;
he inhabits the top two levels. The bottom three levels are locked and empty
except for the stairway connecting them to Valthrun’s quarters. Valthrun is
described briefly above in the section on Wrafton’s Inn. The sage and scholar
has a small selection of 1st and 2nd level rituals that he is willing to sell
once he determines the worth and merit of an adventurer. Refer to the Player’s
Handbook for a list of rituals.

#### Bairwin’s Grand Shoppe
Bairwin Wildarson has traveled the world, or so he claims, before setting up
shop in Invernale ten years ago. He has a variety of items available for sale,
including anything off the list of adventuring gear shown in the Player’s
Handbook. He occasionally has a few 1st-level magic items for sale, and he
sometimes has a couple of potions of healing available in the shop.

#### Warrior Guild
Invernale villagers can join the Warrior Guild and train, once per week,
in basic sword and shield skills. The guild trains in Market Square on days
when the market is not open.  Some citizens earn militia pay (a few silver
pieces per year) by training at least once per month and being on call for
emergencies. Rond Kelfem, captain of the Invernale Regulars, also oversees the
Warrior Guild. He can be found here, in the barracks, or on guard duty,
depending on the time of day.

#### Tenements
The large tenement structure features
apartments for the village residents who don’t own farms or who work in the
businesses within the walls. Several families also live in the homes (buildings
labeled H) on the west side of the village. Most of the villagers who live
outside the walls are farmers and crafters who bring their wares to the Market
Square to sell or trade.

#### Temple
This large stone structure is the village temple. Of the several deities
worshiped by locals, Avandra, goddess of luck and change, is the most
prominent.

#### Graveyard
Invernale has a graveyard for which Sister Linora serves as
caretaker. The graveyard is located a short distance south of the walled town.
Inner Gate This gate is closed and guarded by two Invernale guards during the
daytime. They sternly regard anyone who approaches and question all who wish to
visit Lord Padraigianni in his manor without an appointment; however, PCs who
exude an air of mystery and importance might convince the guards to let them
pass if they act amiably.

#### Invernale Siege Supplies
Though it hasn’t happened
in a couple of years, on occasion Invernale comes under attack from bands of
goblins, kobolds, or even gnolls. When such an event happens, the villagers
seek refuge inside the walled keep and subsist on the supplies of water, flour,
and other basic foodstuffs stored here. The contents of the storehouse are
protected by thick wooden walls and a sturdy lock (DC 30 Thievery check) on the
door. Lord Padraig and Sister Linora hold the only keys to the lock.

#### Invernale Barracks
This military-style barracks is home to Invernale’s ten professional
guards, The Regulars. During the day, only two can be found here; the other
eight are on patrol. At night, eight guards sleep here while the two on duty
are stationed at the outer gate. The guards in the barracks respond quickly to
any call to arms from the manor or elsewhere within the walls of Invernale.

#### Manor House
This grand manor home was built when Invernale was an outpost of the Nerath
Empire. Staffed by five servants, the manor house where Lord Padraig, his wife,
and their four sons live is a beautiful example of stone architecture in a
village otherwise composed of thatch and wood. Lord Padraig is willing to meet
with adventurers who visit Invernale if they specifically seek him out. He also
frequents Wrafton’s Inn.
