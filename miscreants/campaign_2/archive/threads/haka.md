# Haka, Emissary of the North Wind

## Nemesis


A draconic origin sorcerer (lvl 6) is amongst the caravan guards. He has watched from afar. He knows to beat him he needs to incapacitate or charm him. He is going to attempt to cast Hold Person at its highest level targeting Haka. This is to get Haka out of Rage and allow the thugs to work him over. There also needs to be a tie-in to another larger baddy in The Rise of Tiamat (Severin) that becomes his nemesis.
This is the person that had enslaved him. There will be places that Haka goes and people will greet him with hostility and anger. They will recognize him from battle and his exploits there will not be known to him.
