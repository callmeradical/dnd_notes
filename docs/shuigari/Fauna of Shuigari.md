# Fauna of Shuigari

Shuigari’s wildlife reflects the archipelago’s balance between spirit and sword. Creatures often carry cultural meaning, appear in omens, or lend their traits to local craftsmanship. Use the table below to bring the natural world to the forefront of encounters and downtime scenes.

| Creature | Type | Description | Habitat | Cultural Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ashwing Raven** | Bird | Charcoal raven with ashen wingtips and a keen mimicry call. | [Takashima Prefecture](Takashima%20Prefecture.md) highlands | Considered heralds of change; feathers anchor divination charms. |
| **Riverguard Turtle** | Reptile | Massive freshwater turtle cloaked in moss and algae. | Minashima river valleys | Villagers carve shells into ceremonial shields representing patience. |
| **Skyflash Hawk** | Bird of prey | Sleek hawk with lightning-streaked plumage and uncanny speed. | Kisato plateau and trade routes | Employed as message hawks by scholars and magistrates. |
| **Flamehorn Yak** | Mammal | Mountain yak whose horns glow faintly at night. | Takashima alpine passes | Horn shavings fuel rituals for safe travel; beasts haul forge supplies. |
| **Crimson Fang Tiger** | Mammal | Rare tiger with crimson striping and elongated sabre fangs. | Furenhoku tundra forests | Sightings are omens of either great fortune or impending disaster. |
| **Sea Blossom Dolphin** | Aquatic mammal | Playful dolphin patterned like blooming petals. | Sea of Mirrors | Fisherfolk credit pods with guiding ships through treacherous shoals. |
| **Moonlit Koi** | Fish | Silver-scaled koi that glow under moonlight. | Temple ponds across Hikosan | Released during moon festivals to honor [Tsukuyomi](The%20worship%20of%20Tsukuyomi%20in%20Shuigari.md). |
| **Amberback Boar** | Mammal | Hefty boar with amber carapace-like bristles. | Frostwood outskirts | Hunts are dedicated to local kami; tusks carved into protective talismans. |

Sprinkle these beasts into travel montages, merchandise lists, or lore dumps to reinforce Shuigari’s blend of mysticism and pragmatism.
