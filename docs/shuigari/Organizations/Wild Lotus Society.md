# Wild Lotus Society

## Overview
A group of ghost hunters and demon slayers who work on behalf of the citizenry, operating outside official channels. The Wild Lotus Society protects common people from supernatural threats that government officials often ignore or can't handle.

## Purpose & Methods
- Hunt ghosts, demons, and other supernatural threats
- Work on behalf of ordinary citizens
- Operate independently of official government structures
- Mix investigation, combat, and spiritual expertise
- Accept payment but prioritize protecting people over profit

## Known Members

### [[Kaoru Sugimoto]]
The elegant merchant and gambler revealed to be a member during the party's arrival in Takashima. Her supernatural abilities (demonstrated in the coin-splitting trick) now make more sense in context - she's a trained warrior and spirit hunter, not just a skilled performer.

**Role:** Field agent, investigator
**Skills:** Combat (possibly wind-based techniques), investigation, social manipulation
**Cover:** Travels as a merchant, uses gambling to gather information and make connections

## Operations

The Society appears to use covers and mundane occupations to move freely while investigating supernatural threats. Members maintain public personas while secretly hunting ghosts and demons.

### Known Tactics
- Maintain civilian covers (merchants, gamblers, etc.)
- Gather intelligence through social networks
- Recruit capable outsiders for specific missions
- Pay for services when needed
- Investigate before engaging

## Current Activities in Takashima

The Society has identified a haunting at an old tea house. Rather than handle it directly, [[Kaoru Sugimoto]] recruited the party (as payment for getting them into the city) to investigate and deal with the spirit.

**The Tea House Assignment:**
- Location: Old tea house in Takashima
- Threat: Supposed vengeful spirit (actually the [[Twice Cursed Samurai]])
- Reported activity: Figure in samurai armor, moving objects, temperature drops
- Status: Under investigation by the party
