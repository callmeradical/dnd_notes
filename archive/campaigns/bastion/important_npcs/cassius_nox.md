## Highlights

Use the stats for a Drow Gunslinger, make him a tiefling, two attacks/round

## Description

* Black hair
* Golden eyes
* Midnight blue skin
* Horns that follow the shape of his head to the rear
* Fine breastplate sporting the Alunno logo teepo
* Bandolier with two revolvers and a plethora of aether cartridges

## Adversary

Eris and Adonis De Muro
