## Acererak's Intervention
* Severin Silrajin is an agent of Acererak, unbeknownst to Severin.
* Acererak granted him the ability to converse with the Nine Hells, and has made a connection to Tiamat.

After this connection was made, Tiamat has told him of the Dragon Masks. Tiamat thinks herself wise and seeks to turn on Severin once released. Severin is amassing souls to over power Tiamat, and believes he will harness enough energy to control her to do his bidding.

Acererak is going to use Tiamat to fight and destroy the Raven Queen. He
will raise an army of undead from the ashes of Faerun and the material
plane. Acererak will then begin his assault on the Shadow Plane and The
Raven Queen.
